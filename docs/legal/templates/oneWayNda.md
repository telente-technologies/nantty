# Non-Disclosure Agreement (one-way)

**This Non-Disclosure Agreement** (the "**Agreement**") is between <mark>[Nantty legal entity name]</mark>, RC <mark>[RC number]</mark>, of <mark>[registered address]</mark> ("**Nantty**"), and the recipient named on the signature page (the "**Recipient**"), effective on the last date signed below (the "**Effective Date**"). Nantty may disclose confidential information to the Recipient solely to <mark>[enable the Recipient to evaluate or pursue a business relationship with Nantty]</mark> (the "**Permitted Use**").

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Confidential Information**" means any non-public information Nantty (or anyone for it) provides to the Recipient, before or after the Effective Date, in any form, that is marked or identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including user, seller and payment data, product plans, code, designs, pricing and business plans.

1.2 "**Representatives**" means the Recipient's directors, employees, lawyers, accountants and other advisers.

1.3 "**Technology**" means Nantty data, software, prototypes and other materials made available for the Permitted Use.

## 2. Obligations

The Recipient will keep Confidential Information strictly confidential, use it only for the Permitted Use, protect it with at least reasonable care, and share it only with Representatives who need to know it and are bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations. The Recipient will not reverse engineer the Technology, and will not upload any Confidential Information or Technology to a third-party artificial intelligence tool or use it to train any model, without Nantty's written consent. The Recipient is responsible for its Representatives and will notify Nantty immediately of any loss or unauthorised use or disclosure. All Confidential Information remains Nantty's property.

## 3. Exceptions

The obligations do not apply to information the Recipient can show with written records: (a) was or became public through no fault of the Recipient; (b) was lawfully known to it without confidentiality obligations before disclosure; (c) was lawfully received from a third party without confidentiality obligations; or (d) was independently developed without access to the Confidential Information.

## 4. Legally required disclosure

The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information only to the extent required by law, court order or a regulator, after giving Nantty prompt notice where legally allowed, cooperating with any effort to limit disclosure, and disclosing only the minimum required.

## 5. Personal data

Any personal data received is processed only for the Permitted Use, under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the GAID 2025. The Recipient will keep it secure, not transfer it outside Nigeria without Nantty's written consent, and report any personal data breach to Nantty within <mark>24 hours</mark>.

## 6. Return or destruction

On termination or Nantty's request, the Recipient will promptly return or destroy (and confirm in writing) all Confidential Information, except copies required by law or held in routine back-ups, which remain confidential.

## 7. Term

This Agreement lasts <mark>three (3)</mark> years from the Effective Date unless ended earlier by either Party on written notice. Obligations for information disclosed during the term continue for <mark>five (5)</mark> years after it ends, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets.

## 8. No warranty

Confidential Information is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind.

## 9. Governing law, disputes and remedies

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Disputes not settled by negotiation within <mark>30</mark> days will be resolved by arbitration under the Arbitration and Mediation Act 2023, before a sole arbitrator, seated in Lagos, in English. Nantty may seek an injunction or other urgent relief from a court at any time, because a breach may cause harm that money cannot fully compensate.

## 10. General

Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's consent, except Nantty to an affiliate or successor on notice. Notices must be in writing, including by email with confirmation of receipt. This is the entire agreement on its subject; changes must be in writing and signed. If any provision is unenforceable, the rest stays in force. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and electronically, which the Parties accept as valid under section 93 of the Evidence Act 2011 (as amended).
