# Advisor Agreement

This Advisor Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made on <mark>[date]</mark> (the "**Effective Date**") between <mark>[Nantty legal entity name]</mark>, a private company limited by shares incorporated in Nigeria, RC <mark>[RC number]</mark>, of <mark>[registered address]</mark> (the "**Company**"), and <mark>[Advisor name]</mark> of <mark>[address]</mark> (the "**Advisor**").

## 1. Services

The Advisor will advise the Company when asked, including on <mark>[describe services, e.g. payments regulation, marketplace growth, creator partnerships]</mark>, for about <mark>[number]</mark> hours a month (the "**Services**"). While this Agreement is in force, the Advisor will not, without the Company's written consent, advise or work for any business that competes with the Company in <mark>[field, e.g. social commerce or live shopping in Africa]</mark>. [Note for counsel: Nigerian courts enforce restraints only if reasonable in scope and duration; keep this limited to the term.]

## 2. Consideration: share options

2.1 Subject to approval by the Company's board of directors (the "**Board**") and, where required, its shareholders, and to the rules of the Company's <mark>[Employee and Advisor Share Option Plan]</mark> (the "**Plan**"), the Company intends to grant the Advisor an option to acquire <mark>[number]</mark> ordinary shares of the Company at an exercise price of <mark>[₦ per share / the fair value determined by the Board at the date of grant]</mark> (the "**Option**").

2.2 The Option will vest over <mark>[24]</mark> months in equal monthly instalments from the Effective Date <mark>[with a 3-month cliff]</mark>, as long as the Advisor continues to provide Services on each vesting date. Unvested options lapse when this Agreement ends. <mark>[Optional: full acceleration on a change of control.]</mark>

2.3 The Option is governed by the Plan and an option agreement. Shares issued on exercise will be allotted under CAMA and the Company's articles, and recorded in the register of members, with the return of allotment filed at the Corporate Affairs Commission. [Note for counsel: confirm the Board's authority to allot, pre-emption rights under the articles and any shareholders' agreement, and the tax treatment of options for the Advisor under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025.]

2.4 The Company will reimburse reasonable, documented out-of-pocket expenses that the Company approved in writing in advance and that are claimed within 30 days.

## 3. Intellectual property

3.1 Under the Copyright Act 2022, copyright first vests in the author unless an agreement says otherwise, so this clause is needed. The Advisor assigns to the Company, by way of present assignment of future rights, all rights in any works, inventions, designs, data and other materials created by the Advisor in performing the Services or using the Company's Proprietary Information ("**Inventions**"), including copyright, patent and design rights under the Patents and Designs Act, and trademarks.

3.2 Inventions do not include anything the Advisor created before the Effective Date or outside the Services without using Proprietary Information ("**Pre-existing IP**"). If the Advisor uses Pre-existing IP in any deliverable, the Advisor will tell the Company in writing first, and grants the Company a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, transferable licence to use it as part of that deliverable.

3.3 To the extent the law allows, the Advisor waives moral rights in the Inventions. The Advisor will sign any documents reasonably needed to record or enforce the Company's rights.

## 4. Confidentiality and data protection

4.1 All non-public information about the Company, its users, sellers, creators, technology, finances and plans that the Advisor receives or learns ("**Proprietary Information**") is confidential. The Advisor will use it only to perform the Services, protect it with reasonable care, not disclose it, and not upload it to any third-party artificial intelligence tool. The usual exceptions apply (public information, information already known, lawfully received from others, or independently developed), and disclosure required by law is allowed after notice to the Company where legally permitted.

4.2 If the Advisor receives personal data, the Advisor will process it only as the Company instructs, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the GAID 2025, keep it secure, and report any breach to the Company within <mark>24 hours</mark>.

4.3 When this Agreement ends, or on request, the Advisor will return or securely destroy all Proprietary Information, including on personal devices and cloud storage, and confirm this in writing.

4.4 Nothing in this Agreement stops the Advisor from reporting a suspected crime or regulatory breach to a competent authority.

## 5. Term and termination

Either party may end this Agreement at any time by written notice. The Company may also end it if it has not asked for Services for 12 consecutive months. Clauses 2 (for vested options) and 3 to 7 survive.

## 6. Relationship

The Advisor is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner or agent of the Company, and cannot bind the Company. The Advisor is responsible for their own taxes, and the Company may deduct withholding tax where the law requires. The Advisor is not entitled to employee benefits. The Company may name the Advisor as an advisor in its materials, but will not imply an endorsement of any investment or regulated activity without consent.

## 7. No conflicts

The Advisor confirms that this Agreement does not breach any other obligation they owe, that they will not bring or use any third party's confidential information, and that they will tell the Company of any institutional policies (for example of a university or employer) that affect the Services or Inventions.

## 8. Governing law and disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Disputes not settled by negotiation within 30 days will be resolved by arbitration under the Arbitration and Mediation Act 2023 before a sole arbitrator, seated in Lagos, in English, without affecting either party's right to seek urgent relief from a court.

## 9. General

This is the entire agreement about its subject. Changes must be in writing and signed. The Advisor may not assign it. It may be signed in counterparts and electronically.
