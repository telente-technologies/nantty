# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

**This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement** (this "**Agreement**") is made on <mark>[date]</mark> (the "**Effective Date**") between:

1. <mark>[Zudomart legal entity name]</mark>, a company incorporated in Nigeria under the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020 with RC number <mark>[RC number]</mark> and its registered office at <mark>[registered address]</mark> ("**Zudomart**"); and
2. <mark>[Counterparty name]</mark>, <mark>[a company incorporated in [country] with registration number [number] / an individual]</mark>, of <mark>[address]</mark> (the "**Other Party**"),

(each a "**Party**", together the "**Parties**"), to protect confidential information that either Party discloses solely to evaluate or pursue <mark>[describe the business relationship, e.g. a brand partnership, integration or investment]</mark> (the "**Permitted Use**").

## 1. Confidential Information

1.1 "**Confidential Information**" of a Party (the "**Disclosing Party**") means all non-public technical, commercial, financial and personal information disclosed by or for it to the other Party (the "**Receiving Party**"), before or after the Effective Date, in any form, including: product plans, source code, algorithms, designs, data, user and seller information, pricing, business plans, finances, the terms of any discussions, and any information the Receiving Party knew or reasonably should have known was confidential.

1.2 The existence and terms of this Agreement and of the Parties' discussions are Confidential Information of both Parties.

## 2. Obligations

2.1 The Receiving Party will: (a) keep the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information strictly confidential; (b) use it only for the Permitted Use; (c) protect it with at least the care it uses for its own confidential information, and never less than reasonable care; and (d) share it only with its directors, employees, professional advisers and contractors ("**Representatives**") who need to know it for the Permitted Use and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as strict as these. The Receiving Party is responsible for its Representatives' breaches.

2.2 The Receiving Party will not: (a) reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble any software, prototype or material provided; (b) upload or provide any Confidential Information to any third-party artificial intelligence or machine-learning tool, or use it to train any model, without the Disclosing Party's written consent; or (c) file any patent or design application based on the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

2.3 The Receiving Party will notify the Disclosing Party promptly, and in any case within <mark>48 hours</mark>, after discovering any loss or unauthorised use or disclosure of Confidential Information.

## 3. Exceptions

The obligations in clause 2 do not apply to information that the Receiving Party can show with written evidence: (a) was public when disclosed, or became public later through no fault of the Receiving Party; (b) was lawfully in its possession without confidentiality obligations before disclosure; (c) was lawfully received from a third party without confidentiality obligations; or (d) was independently developed by people with no access to the Confidential Information. A combination of elements is not public just because individual elements are.

## 4. Disclosure required by law

The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, a court order or a regulator (including the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, the Central Bank of Nigeria or the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission), if, where legally allowed, it gives the Disclosing Party prompt notice, cooperates (at the Disclosing Party's cost) with any attempt to limit disclosure, and discloses only the minimum required.

## 5. Personal data

If either Party discloses personal data, the Receiving Party will process it only for the Permitted Use, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (the "**NDPA**") and the GAID 2025, keep it secure, not transfer it outside Nigeria except as sections 41 to 43 of the NDPA allow, and notify the Disclosing Party of any personal data breach within <mark>24 hours</mark> so that the Disclosing Party can meet its 72-hour notification duty. Where the Parties will share personal data regularly, they will sign a data processing or data sharing agreement.

## 6. Return and destruction

On termination or on written request, the Receiving Party will promptly return or destroy (and confirm in writing that it has destroyed) the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, except copies it must keep by law or that sit in routine back-ups that cannot reasonably be deleted. Kept copies stay subject to this Agreement.

## 7. Ownership and no obligation

Confidential Information remains the Disclosing Party's property. No licence or other right is granted except the right to use it for the Permitted Use. Neither Party is obliged to disclose any particular information or to enter into any further agreement. This Agreement does not create a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment.

## 8. Feedback

Ideas or suggestions a Party gives the other about the other's products ("**Feedback**") may be used by the recipient freely, without payment, under a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free licence.

## 9. No warranty

Confidential Information is provided "as is". The Disclosing Party gives no warranty about its accuracy or completeness and is not liable for the Receiving Party's use of it.

## 10. Term

10.1 This Agreement lasts <mark>three (3)</mark> years from the Effective Date unless terminated earlier by either Party on <mark>30</mark> days' written notice.

10.2 The obligations of confidentiality continue for <mark>five (5)</mark> years after termination or expiry, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets.

## 11. Remedies

Unauthorised use or disclosure may cause harm that money cannot fully compensate. Each Party may seek an injunction or specific performance from a court of competent jurisdiction, in addition to any other remedy, without having to prove actual damage, to the extent the court allows.

## 12. Governing law and disputes

12.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

12.2 The Parties will first try to settle any dispute by negotiation between senior representatives for <mark>30</mark> days. Any dispute not settled will be finally resolved by arbitration under the Arbitration and Mediation Act 2023 before a sole arbitrator appointed <mark>[by agreement, or failing agreement within 15 days, by the Lagos Court of Arbitration]</mark>, seated in Lagos, conducted in English. Nothing in this clause prevents a Party from seeking urgent interim relief from the courts, including the Federal High Court for intellectual property matters.

## 13. General

13.1 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent, except to a successor by merger, acquisition or sale of substantially all of its assets, on written notice.

13.2 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by courier, or by email to the addresses below. Email notice is effective when sent unless the sender receives a delivery failure message.

13.3 **Entire agreement and amendments.** This Agreement is the entire agreement about its subject and replaces earlier discussions. Changes must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

13.4 **Severability and waiver.** If any provision is unenforceable, the rest stays in force and that provision is applied to the extent allowed. A failure to enforce is not a waiver.

13.5 **Stamp duty.** <mark>[Zudomart / the Parties equally]</mark> will pay any stamp duty payable on this Agreement under the Stamp Duties Act.

13.6 **Counterparts and electronic signature.** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature, which the Parties agree is valid under section 93 of the Evidence Act 2011 (as amended) and section 17 of the Cybercrimes (Prohibition, Prevention, etc.) Act 2015.
