## 1. Role and place of work

You will be employed as <mark>[Job title]</mark>, reporting to <mark>[manager title]</mark>. Your main duties are <mark>[duties]</mark>. Your place of work is <mark>[the Company's office at [address], Lagos / remote from your home in Nigeria / hybrid]</mark>. The Company may reasonably change your duties within your skills and grade after consulting you.

## 2. Start date and probation

Your employment starts on <mark>[date]</mark>. The first <mark>[three (3) / six (6)]</mark> months are a probation period, which the Company may extend once by up to <mark>[three]</mark> months with written reasons. During probation either party may end employment with <mark>[one week's]</mark> written notice or pay in lieu. You will be confirmed in writing at the end of a successful probation.

## 3. Hours

Normal working hours are <mark>[40]</mark> hours a week, <mark>[Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00 WAT]</mark>, with a daily lunch break. You may need to work reasonable additional hours for business needs. <mark>[If the role is covered by the Labour Act as a "worker": overtime is paid or given as time off in lieu as set out in the handbook.]</mark>

## 4. Pay

4.1 Your gross annual salary is <mark>₦[amount]</mark>, paid monthly in arrears on or before the <mark>[last working day]</mark> of each month into your bank account. It is made up of:

| Component | Monthly (₦) |
| --- | --- |
| Basic salary | <mark>[amount]</mark> |
| Housing allowance | <mark>[amount]</mark> |
| Transport allowance | <mark>[amount]</mark> |
| <mark>[Other allowance]</mark> | <mark>[amount]</mark> |
| **Gross monthly pay** | <mark>[amount]</mark> |

4.2 Your pay will never be less than the national minimum wage, currently ₦70,000 a month under the National Minimum Wage (Amendment) Act 2024.

4.3 The Company will deduct from your pay only what the law requires or you agree in writing to:

- **PAYE income tax** under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025, at the progressive rates in force (from 1 January 2026: 0% on the first ₦800,000 of chargeable income, rising to 25% above ₦50 million), after reliefs such as rent relief and your pension, NHF and NHIS contributions. Tax is paid to the tax authority of your state of residence.
- **Pension:** your contribution of at least 8% of your monthly basic, housing and transport allowances under the Pension Reform Act 2014, paid with the Company's contribution of at least 10% to the Pension Fund Administrator you choose. Please give us your RSA PIN.
- **National Housing Fund:** 2.5% of your basic salary under the National Housing Fund Act, where it applies. <mark>[Counsel to confirm current NHF practice for private employers.]</mark>
- **Health insurance:** your contribution, if any, under the National Health Insurance Authority Act 2022 scheme the Company provides.

4.4 The Company also pays, at its own cost: 1% of total monthly payroll to the Employees' Compensation scheme managed by the NSITF under the Employee's Compensation Act 2010, and the Industrial Training Fund contribution where the Company qualifies.

4.5 Your pay is reviewed <mark>[annually]</mark>. A review does not guarantee an increase.

## 6. Leave

- **Annual leave:** <mark>[20]</mark> working days a year with full pay, in addition to public holidays (the Labour Act minimum is 6 working days after 12 months' continuous service).
- **Sick leave:** up to 12 working days a year with full pay on a medical certificate, as section 16 of the Labour Act provides, and more at the Company's discretion.
- **Maternity leave:** at least 12 weeks under section 54 of the Labour Act, with <mark>[full]</mark> pay (the Labour Act minimum is 50% pay after 6 months' service), and time for nursing as the law provides.
- **Paternity leave:** <mark>[10]</mark> working days with full pay.
- **Compassionate leave:** <mark>[5]</mark> working days.

## 7. Benefits

<mark>[Health insurance through an HMO for you, your spouse and up to four children; a laptop and equipment; an internet or power allowance for remote work; learning budget.]</mark> Benefits follow the handbook and may change with notice.

## 8. Conduct and policies

You will follow the Company handbook, including the code of conduct, information security, data protection, anti-bribery (Corrupt Practices and Other Related Offences Act 2000), anti-harassment and social media policies. You will handle personal data of users, sellers and colleagues only as your role needs and the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 allows, and complete data protection training.

## 9. Other work

While employed full time you may not take other paid work or hold a material interest in a competing business without the Company's written consent, which will not be unreasonably refused for non-competing work outside working hours.

## 10. Confidentiality and intellectual property

Your employment is conditional on signing the Confidentiality and Intellectual Property Agreement in the Appendix. Because copyright in work made by an employee of a private company does not automatically belong to the employer under the Copyright Act 2022, that agreement assigns to the Company the rights in work you create in your employment. Do not bring or use any confidential information of a former employer.

## 11. Ending employment

11.1 After confirmation, either party may end employment by giving written notice of <mark>[one (1) month]</mark>, or pay in lieu of notice. This is at least the notice that section 11 of the Labour Act requires (one week for service of 3 months to 2 years, two weeks for 2 to 5 years, and one month for 5 years or more).

11.2 The Company may end your employment without notice for gross misconduct after a fair process in which you are told the allegation and can respond, in line with the National Industrial Court's approach to fair hearing and international labour standards.

11.3 On leaving, you will return all Company property and data, and the Company will pay your salary and accrued untaken leave to your last day and give you a certificate of service on request. <mark>[Redundancy follows section 20 of the Labour Act: consultation with you or your representatives, last-in-first-out subject to skill and merit, and severance as set out in the handbook.]</mark>

## 12. Conditions

This offer is conditional on: satisfactory references <mark>[and background check, with your consent]</mark>; proof of your identity and your right to work in Nigeria (and, for non-Nigerians, an expatriate quota position and work permit); your NIN, BVN (for payroll only), tax identification number and RSA PIN; and signing the Appendix.

## 13. Disputes and governing law

This contract is governed by Nigerian law. We will first try to resolve any grievance through the internal grievance procedure in the handbook and, if both agree, mediation. Employment disputes fall within the exclusive jurisdiction of the National Industrial Court of Nigeria under section 254C of the Constitution, and nothing in this letter limits your right to go to that court.

## 14. General

This letter and its Appendix are the entire agreement about your employment and replace earlier discussions. Changes must be in writing and signed by both of us, except handbook policies that the Company may update with notice. If any term is unenforceable, the rest stays in force. This letter may be signed electronically.

## Appendix: Confidentiality and Intellectual Property Agreement

1. **Confidential information.** During and after employment you will keep confidential, and use only for your work, all non-public information about the Company, its users, sellers, creators, partners, technology, source code, finances and plans. This does not stop you reporting suspected wrongdoing to a regulator or law enforcement, or discussing your own pay and conditions as the law allows.
2. **Assignment of IP.** You assign to the Company, by way of present assignment of future rights, all copyright, design, patent, trademark and other rights in all works, software, inventions, designs, data and materials you create, alone or with others, in the course of your employment or using Company resources ("**Work Product**"). This includes rights under the Copyright Act 2022 and the Patents and Designs Act. To the extent the law allows, you waive moral rights in Work Product.
3. **Prior inventions.** List in a schedule anything you created before employment that you want excluded. If you use any of it in Work Product, you grant the Company a perpetual, royalty-free, worldwide licence to use it.
4. **Further assurance.** You will sign documents reasonably needed to register or enforce the Company's rights, during and after employment.
5. **Return of property.** On leaving, or when asked, you will return or securely delete all Company information, including from personal devices and accounts.
6. **Non-solicitation.** For <mark>[6]</mark> months after leaving you will not solicit Company employees to leave, or solicit the Company's business partners with whom you dealt in your last 12 months to stop dealing with the Company.
