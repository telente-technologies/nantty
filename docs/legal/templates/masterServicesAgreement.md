# Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement is made on <mark>[date]</mark> (the "**Effective Date**") between <mark>[Nantty legal entity name]</mark>, RC <mark>[RC number]</mark>, of <mark>[registered address]</mark> ("**Nantty**"), and <mark>[Customer name]</mark>, <mark>[RC/registration number]</mark>, of <mark>[address]</mark> (the "**Customer**").

## 1. Definitions

- "**Agreement**": this Master Services Agreement, its schedules and every Order Form.
- "**Applicable Data Protection Law**": the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), the NDPC's General Application and Implementation Directive 2025 (GAID), and any other data protection law that applies to the processing, including the GDPR where relevant.
- "**Campaign Materials**": ads, creative, product data, brand assets and other content the Customer provides.
- "**Customer Data**": data the Customer provides, or that is generated for the Customer, under the Agreement, excluding Aggregated Data.
- "**Aggregated Data**": de-identified, aggregated data about use of the Services that does not identify the Customer or any individual.
- "**Order Form**": a document signed by both parties describing the services, deliverables, fees, term and any special terms.
- "**Platform**": the Nantty apps, websites, Nantty Ads, the seller dashboard, Creator Studio, APIs and embeddable widgets.
- "**Services**": the services described in an Order Form.

## 2. Services

2.1 Nantty will provide the Services described in each Order Form with reasonable skill and care. If an Order Form conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement controls unless the Order Form expressly says which clause it overrides.

2.2 The Customer may use the Platform and any API or widget only for its internal business and as described in the Order Form and Nantty's documentation, and must follow the Terms of Service, Ads Policy, Seller Terms and Community Guidelines published at nantty.com.

2.3 Nantty may use subcontractors, including creators engaged for a campaign, and remains responsible for them.

## 3. Customer responsibilities

3.1 The Customer will provide accurate information and Campaign Materials on time, and Nantty is not responsible for delays caused by the Customer.

3.2 The Customer confirms that the Campaign Materials: (a) are true, lawful and not misleading; (b) do not infringe anyone's rights; (c) comply with the Advertising Regulatory Council of Nigeria Act 2022 and the Nigerian Code of Advertising Practice, including any required pre-exposure approval from ARCON, which the Customer will obtain unless the Order Form says Nantty will; and (d) have any product approval needed, for example from NAFDAC or the Standards Organisation of Nigeria.

3.3 The Customer will not reverse engineer the Platform, scrape it, resell the Services, get around technical limits, or upload malicious code.

## 4. Intellectual property

4.1 Nantty and its licensors own the Platform, its technology, documentation and all improvements. No rights are granted except those expressly stated.

4.2 The Customer keeps ownership of its Campaign Materials and Customer Data and grants Nantty a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use them to provide the Services and, for public campaigns, to display them on the Platform.

4.3 Deliverables created specifically for the Customer and identified as such in an Order Form are assigned to the Customer when paid for in full, except for Nantty's pre-existing tools, templates and know-how, which Nantty licenses to the Customer for use with the deliverables. Content created by creators is licensed as set out in the Order Form.

4.4 Nantty may create and use Aggregated Data to provide and improve its services.

4.5 Feedback the Customer gives about the Platform may be used by Nantty freely.

## 5. Data protection

5.1 Each party will comply with Applicable Data Protection Law. If Nantty processes personal data for the Customer as a processor, the parties will sign the Nantty Data Processing Agreement, which prevails on data protection matters.

5.2 Where each party is a separate controller (for example, campaign reporting), each is responsible for its own compliance, including lawful basis, notices and consent. Nantty shares only aggregated campaign results unless the Order Form provides otherwise.

## 6. Fees and payment

6.1 The Customer will pay the fees in each Order Form. Unless the Order Form says otherwise, fees are invoiced <mark>[monthly in advance / on completion of milestones]</mark> and are due within <mark>30</mark> days of the invoice, in <mark>Naira</mark>.

6.2 Fees exclude VAT, which the Customer will pay at the rate under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 (currently 7.5%) where it applies. Where the law requires the Customer to deduct withholding tax, it will do so, pay it to the tax authority, and give Nantty the receipt so Nantty can claim the credit.

6.3 Late payments carry interest at <mark>[the Central Bank of Nigeria Monetary Policy Rate plus 2%]</mark> a year from the due date. If any amount is more than 30 days overdue, Nantty may suspend the Services after giving 7 days' written notice.

6.4 Nantty may increase fees for a renewal term with at least 30 days' notice before the renewal.

## 7. Warranties and disclaimers

7.1 Each party warrants that it has authority to enter into this Agreement and that doing so does not breach any other agreement or law.

7.2 Except as stated in this Agreement, and to the extent the law allows, the Services and Platform are provided "as is". Nantty does not guarantee any particular reach, sales, engagement or results, uninterrupted availability, or the conduct of third parties such as creators, users or payment partners.

## 8. Limitation of liability

8.1 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, or loss of profit, revenue, goodwill or data, however caused.

8.2 Each party's total liability under this Agreement in any 12-month period is limited to the fees paid and payable by the Customer in that period.

8.3 These limits do not apply to: liability that cannot be limited by law (including for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or fraud); breach of confidentiality; infringement or misappropriation of the other party's intellectual property; the Customer's payment obligations; or indemnities under clause 10.

## 9. Confidentiality

Each party will keep the other's confidential information confidential, use it only for this Agreement, and share it only with people who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations, subject to the usual exceptions (public information, information already known, independently developed or lawfully received) and disclosures required by law. On request or at the end of the Agreement, each party will return or destroy the other's confidential information, except copies required by law.

## 10. Indemnities

10.1 Nantty will defend the Customer against third-party claims that the Platform, used as permitted, infringes intellectual property rights in Nigeria, and pay damages finally awarded or agreed in settlement. If the Platform is or may be infringing, Nantty may get the right to continue, modify or replace it, or end the affected Services and refund prepaid fees for the unused period. This does not apply to claims caused by the Customer's materials, changes or misuse.

10.2 The Customer will defend Nantty against third-party and regulatory claims arising from the Campaign Materials, the Customer's products, or the Customer's breach of clause 3.2, and pay damages, fines and costs finally awarded or agreed in settlement.

10.3 The indemnified party must notify the other promptly, let it control the defence and settlement, and cooperate. It may join with its own lawyers at its own cost.

## 11. Term and termination

11.1 This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues until all Order Forms have ended. Each Order Form runs for its stated term and renews for equal periods unless either party gives <mark>30</mark> days' notice of non-renewal.

11.2 Either party may end this Agreement or an Order Form by written notice if the other materially breaches it and does not fix the breach within 30 days of notice, or becomes insolvent, enters liquidation or makes an arrangement with creditors.

11.3 When an Order Form ends, the Customer will pay for Services performed up to the end date. Clauses that by their nature should survive (including 4, 5, 8, 9, 10 and 12) survive.

## 12. Governing law and disputes

12.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

12.2 The parties will first try to resolve any dispute by negotiation between senior representatives for 30 days, and may agree to mediation under the Arbitration and Mediation Act 2023.

12.3 Any dispute not resolved will be finally settled by arbitration under the Arbitration and Mediation Act 2023 and the <mark>[Lagos Court of Arbitration Arbitration Rules]</mark>, by a sole arbitrator, seated in Lagos, in English. Either party may seek urgent interim relief from a court or an emergency arbitrator.

## 13. General

13.1 **Force majeure.** Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control (other than payment obligations), including power or network failures, payment partner outages, strikes, civil unrest, epidemics or government action, if it notifies the other and tries to resume.

13.2 **Anti-bribery.** Each party will comply with the Corrupt Practices and Other Related Offences Act 2000 and other anti-corruption laws.

13.3 **Publicity.** Nantty may name the Customer as a customer and use its logo under its brand guidelines, unless the Customer objects in writing.

13.4 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without consent, except to an affiliate or successor on notice.

13.5 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing, by email to the addresses in the Order Form, with a copy of any breach or termination notice by courier to the registered address.

13.6 **Relationship.** The parties are independent contractors.

13.7 **Entire agreement, changes, waiver, severability.** This Agreement is the entire agreement on its subject. Changes must be in writing and signed. A failure to enforce is not a waiver. Unenforceable provisions are applied to the extent allowed and the rest stays in force.

13.8 **Stamp duty and counterparts.** <mark>[Party]</mark> will pay stamp duty. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and electronically.
