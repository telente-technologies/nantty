# Independent Contractor Agreement

This Agreement is made on <mark>[date]</mark> between <mark>[Nantty legal entity name]</mark>, RC <mark>[RC number]</mark>, of <mark>[registered address]</mark> (the "**Company**"), and <mark>[Contractor name / business name, RC or BN number]</mark>, of <mark>[address]</mark> (the "**Contractor**").

## 1. Services and deliverables

1.1 The Contractor will provide the services and deliverables in the Schedule (the "**Services**") with reasonable skill, care and diligence, by the dates in the Schedule.

1.2 The Contractor decides how, when and where to perform the Services, provides its own equipment unless the Schedule says otherwise, and may work for other clients, except direct competitors of the Company in <mark>[field]</mark> during the term without the Company's consent.

1.3 The Contractor may use subcontractors only with the Company's written consent and remains responsible for them.

## 2. Fees, invoices and tax

2.1 The Company will pay the fees in the Schedule within <mark>30</mark> days of receiving a correct invoice, by transfer to the Contractor's bank account. Approved, receipted expenses are reimbursed.

2.2 The Contractor is responsible for its own income tax and, if registered, VAT, which it will show on its invoices. The Company will deduct withholding tax where the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 and its regulations require, pay it to the tax authority and give the Contractor the receipt. The Contractor will provide its tax identification number.

2.3 The Contractor is not entitled to salary, pension contributions, leave or other employee benefits, and is responsible for its own insurance.

## 3. Intellectual property

3.1 Under the Copyright Act 2022, copyright first vests in the author unless agreed otherwise, so this clause is essential. The Contractor assigns to the Company, by way of present assignment of future rights, all rights in all deliverables, code, designs, content, inventions and other materials created for the Company under this Agreement ("**Work Product**"), including copyright, patent and design rights and trademarks, from creation. To the extent the law allows, the Contractor waives moral rights.

3.2 The Contractor keeps its pre-existing tools and materials ("**Background IP**"), listed in the Schedule. If Background IP is included in Work Product, the Contractor grants the Company a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, transferable licence to use it as part of the Work Product.

3.3 The Contractor will not include third-party or open-source material in Work Product unless the licence allows commercial use and the Company approves it in writing.

3.4 The Contractor will sign any documents reasonably needed to perfect or register the Company's rights.

## 4. Confidentiality and data protection

4.1 The Contractor will keep confidential, and use only for the Services, all non-public information about the Company, its users, sellers, creators, partners, systems and plans, during the Agreement and for <mark>five (5)</mark> years after (trade secrets for as long as they remain secret). The usual exceptions apply (public, already known, independently developed, lawfully received) and disclosures required by law are allowed after notice where legally permitted. The Contractor will not upload Company confidential information or code to third-party artificial intelligence tools without approval.

4.2 If the Contractor processes personal data for the Company, it is a data processor under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and will: process it only on the Company's written instructions; keep it confidential and secure; not transfer it outside Nigeria without consent; report any breach within <mark>24 hours</mark>; help the Company with data subject requests; and delete or return it at the end. For significant processing the parties will also sign the Company's Data Processing Agreement.

4.3 The Contractor will follow the Company's security policies when using its systems, including multi-factor authentication and least-privilege access, and return all Company property and data at the end.

## 5. Warranties

The Contractor warrants that: it has the right to enter into this Agreement; the Work Product will be original (except approved third-party material) and will not infringe anyone's rights; it will comply with applicable law, including anti-bribery law; and it will fix defects reported within <mark>30</mark> days of delivery at no extra cost.

## 6. Liability and indemnity

6.1 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss. Each party's total liability is limited to <mark>[the total fees paid and payable under this Agreement]</mark>, except for breach of confidentiality or data protection, infringement of intellectual property, fraud, and liability that cannot be limited by law.

6.2 The Contractor will indemnify the Company against third-party claims that the Work Product infringes intellectual property rights, and against tax, pension or employment claims arising from the Contractor's own staff.

## 7. Term and termination

7.1 This Agreement starts on <mark>[date]</mark> and ends on <mark>[date / completion of the Services]</mark>.

7.2 Either party may end it on <mark>14</mark> days' written notice. Either party may end it at once if the other materially breaches it and does not remedy the breach within 7 days of notice. The Company will pay for Services properly performed up to the end date, and the Contractor will deliver all Work Product, finished or not.

7.3 Clauses 3, 4, 6 and 8 survive termination.

## 8. Governing law and disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Disputes not settled by negotiation within 30 days will be resolved by arbitration under the Arbitration and Mediation Act 2023 before a sole arbitrator, seated in Lagos, in English, without affecting either party's right to seek urgent relief from a court.

## 9. General

The Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner or agent of the Company. Neither party may assign this Agreement without consent, except the Company to an affiliate or successor. This is the entire agreement on its subject; changes must be in writing and signed. Unenforceable provisions are applied to the extent allowed. It may be signed in counterparts and electronically.
