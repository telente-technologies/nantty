- `VERIFIED`: the named behavior passed the stated automated/runtime check.
- `IMPLEMENTED_UNVERIFIED`: source exists but the relevant integration/runtime path was not exercised.
- `PARTIAL`: meaningful pieces exist but acceptance criteria remain open.
- `MISSING`: no owning implementation/contract was established in this audit.
