Profile entity rule: implementation plan

Zudomart · owner decision of 11 October 2026 · status: planned, not built yet

The rule. A personal account can own at most two linked profiles: either one Store or one Business page (never both), plus at most one Brand. Business pages show no Mart / shop icon. Brands can choose to sell or not. Never mix them up.

1. Allowed combinations

Personal account owns…Allowed?Notes
Nothing (just personal)YesDefault.
1 StoreYesSells in Mart; profile shows the Mart icon.
1 Business pageYesA non-shop organisation page; no Mart icon, no products.
1 BrandYesThe brand may sell or not (toggle).
1 Store + 1 BrandYesThe two-in-one maximum.
1 Business + 1 BrandYesThe two-in-one maximum.
Store + BusinessNoMutually exclusive: must choose one.
2+ Stores, 2+ Businesses or 2+ BrandsNoOne of each kind at most.
Store + Business + BrandNoMore than two linked profiles.

2. How brands sell

  • A brand has a sellingEnabled setting (default off). When off, the brand is a showcase only: posts, clips, lives, the Official Store badge eligibility display, and brand protection. It has no products and no Mart icon.
  • When on, the brand sells through its own brand storefront. It is a sales channel of the brand, not an extra Store, so it does not count as the account's Store slot. The owner can still separately have their one Store or one Business page.
  • Turning selling off: hide the products and storefront, keep orders and payouts working until all open orders are completed, then lock new listings.
  • Brand selling uses the same verification and money rules as stores: Flutterwave wallet, escrow in Nigeria, verification before selling outside Nigeria, and the debt limits.

3. Backend changes

3.1 Single source of truth

  • In the users service (goIdentity), add one guarded registry of an account's linked profiles: linked_profiles(account_id, kind ∈ {store, business, brand}, profile_id) with a unique (account_id, kind) constraint and a check that store and business can't both exist for the same account (a partial unique index or a trigger, plus enforcement in code).
  • Add an internal endpoint: GET /internal/accounts/{id}/linked-profiles and POST …/claim {kind}, called by catalog (stores) and users (business pages, brands) inside their create flows, so the rule is enforced server-side in one place.

3.2 Enforcement points

  • Create a store (catalog POST /stores): refuse if the account has a business page or a store, with 409 PROFILE_LIMIT_STORE_OR_BUSINESS.
  • Create a business page (users businessProfiles): refuse if the account has a store or a business page (the same code).
  • Create a brand: refuse if a brand exists, with 409 PROFILE_LIMIT_ONE_BRAND.
  • Acting-profile switch: only linked profiles the account owns; the team-member access rules stay as they are.
  • Brand selling toggle: PATCH /brands/{id} {sellingEnabled} with step-up, owner only; products and storefront visibility follow it.
  • Admin: staff can view the rule state and, with step-up plus a reason, convert a Store ↔ Business page or reassign a brand (audited).

3.3 Existing data migration

  1. Report accounts that break the rule (e.g. both a store and a business page, or several brands).
  2. Grandfather them read-only: nothing is deleted. The owner gets an in-app task, "Choose which to keep", and the extra profiles stop accepting new listings or posts until they choose. Admin can resolve it for them.
  3. Backfill linked_profiles for compliant accounts.

4. App changes (mobile, webapp, seller, studio, admin)

  • Create flows: "Open a store" and "Create a business page" become one choice screen, Store or Business, with a clear explanation that you can only have one. Once one exists, the other option is hidden and replaced with "You already have a Store / Business page".
  • Brand: "Create a brand" appears only if none exists. Brand settings get a "Sell products with this brand" switch, with confirmation and step-up.
  • Profile UI: Business pages never show the Mart / shop icon or a products tab. Stores, and brands with selling on, show it.
  • Profile switcher (acting as): shows Personal plus at most two linked profiles, grouped and labelled (Store / Business / Brand).
  • Help Centre and Terms: add an article "Stores, business pages and brands: what you can have", and update the seller terms.
  • Error screens: map the new 409 codes to clear, centred screens with a link to manage profiles.

5. Tests

  • Backend: every row of the table in section 1, plus concurrent create races (two creates at once, only one wins), conversion, the brand selling toggle with open orders, and migration of rule-breaking accounts.
  • Apps: the create choice screen, hidden options, no Mart icon on business pages, the brand toggle, and the error states.
  • Contract tests for the new endpoints and error codes.

6. Order of work

  1. Backend registry, constraint and enforcement, with tests.
  2. Migration report and grandfathering.
  3. App create flows, profile UI and brand toggle.
  4. Admin tools, Help Centre and Terms.
  5. Full QA (adversarial: double taps, races, deep links into blocked create flows).
