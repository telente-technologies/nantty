Zudomart never pays out to someone else's account. This is one of the most important protections on your wallet: even if someone got into your Zudomart account, they couldn't send your money to their own bank.

## Who this is for

Sellers and creators withdrawing money.

## How the name check works

1. When you finish [payout setup](/help/creators-and-earnings/payout-setup), we confirm your legal name. In Nigeria this comes from your BVN consent. Elsewhere it comes from your ID check with our identity partner.
2. When you add a bank account, we look up the account name with your bank. In the **Get paid** setup you'll see "This matches your legal name." or "This doesn't match".
3. When you withdraw, we check again that the payout account's name matches the name on your wallet.

Small differences, such as the order of your names or a missing middle name, are usually accepted. Very different names aren't.

## What's not allowed

- A friend's, partner's or family member's account, even if they agree
- An account belonging to a "payment agent" or anyone offering to collect money for you
- A business account in someone else's name

## If something goes wrong

- **"Withdrawals go only to a bank account in your own verified name."** The account you chose doesn't match your verified name. Add an account in your own name.
- **"This doesn't match" your legal name** during setup. "Use an account in your own name, or go back and fix your legal name."
- **"We could not find that account. Check the number and bank."** Check the 10-digit number and the bank you chose.
- **"We could not confirm this account right now. Try again shortly."** The bank check is temporarily unavailable.

## Changed your name?

If your legal name changed, for example after marriage, update it with your bank first, then contact support from **Help**. Our team can review the change.

## Stay safe

If anyone offers to "receive your payout" for you or asks you to add their account, it's a scam. See [Payment scams](/help/wallet-security/payment-scams).
