## What Zudomart will never do

- Ask for your password, card PIN, bank PIN or any code we email you
- Ask you to pay a seller, a "support agent" or anyone else outside the Zudomart app or **app.zudomart.com**
- Ask you to send money to "release", "unlock" or "verify" an order, a payout or a wallet
- Ask you to move money to a "safe" account
- Pay out to a bank account that isn't in your own verified name

Anyone asking for these is a scammer, even if they use our name or logo.

## Common scams

- **Fake payment alerts.** A buyer sends a screenshot, SMS or email saying they've paid. Only trust what you see in your Zudomart wallet or order. Money is added only after we've confirmed it with our payment partner.
- **"Pay me directly."** A seller asks you to transfer to their bank to "save fees" or "skip escrow". You lose all buyer protection. Always pay at Zudomart checkout.
- **Fake support.** Someone messages you claiming to be Zudomart support and asks for a code, your password or a transfer. Real support only reaches you through **Help** in the app or emails about your own tickets.
- **Overpayment.** A buyer "pays too much" and asks you to send back the difference. Don't. Refunds only happen inside Zudomart.
- **"Unlock your earnings."** Someone says you must pay a fee to release your balance. Payout fees are only ever taken inside Zudomart and shown before you confirm.
- **Account takeover.** If you get a "Confirm it's you" code you didn't ask for, someone may be trying to get into your account. Don't share it. Change your password.

## If something happens

1. Don't send any more money or codes.
2. Change your password and check your [active sessions](/help/account-and-login/active-sessions).
3. Remove any payout account you don't recognise. New payout accounts can't receive withdrawals for 48 hours, which gives you time.
4. Report the person or chat. See [Report a problem](/help/safety-and-reporting/report-a-problem).
5. Contact support from **Help**, and include order numbers and screenshots.

If the scam happened outside Zudomart, also contact your bank straight away.
