Being signed in isn't enough to move money. Before certain actions we ask you to confirm it's you again, even if you've been signed in for days.

## When you'll see it

- When you tap **Withdraw** to send money to your bank
- When you add a payout account, in **Payout accounts** or in the **Get paid** setup

## What you see

A sheet titled **Confirm it's you** says: "We check again before money leaves your wallet or a payout account changes."

1. Enter your **Password** and tap **Confirm**.
2. No password? Tap **No password? Use an emailed code**, then **Email me a code**. Enter the 6-digit code sent to your account email and tap **Confirm**.

To switch back, tap **Use my password instead**.

After you confirm, you won't be asked again for a few minutes, so you can finish what you started. After that, you'll be asked again.

## If it doesn't work

- **Wrong password**: try again, or use an emailed code instead.
- **Code not arriving**: check spam and promotions folders, wait a minute, and ask for a new code. The code goes only to the email on your account.
- **You closed the sheet**: nothing happened. Your withdrawal or new account wasn't sent. Start again when you're ready.

## Keep it to yourself

Your password and your codes are only for you to type into Zudomart. **Zudomart staff will never ask for them**, by phone, chat, email or social media. Anyone who asks for a code is trying to take your money. See [Payment scams](/help/wallet-security/payment-scams).

If you get a code you didn't ask for, someone may know your email or password. Change your password and check your [active sessions](/help/account-and-login/active-sessions).
