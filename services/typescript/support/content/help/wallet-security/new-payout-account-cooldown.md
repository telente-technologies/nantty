## Why there's a wait

If someone ever got into your account, the first thing they'd try is adding their own bank account and withdrawing your money. The 48-hour wait gives you time to notice and stop them.

## Adding a payout account

1. In the app, open **Wallet** > **Payout accounts**. On the web (**app.zudomart.com**), open **Wallet** > **Payout accounts & withdraw**.
2. Under **Add a bank account**, choose your bank and enter your 10-digit account number.
3. Tap **Add account** and [confirm it's you](/help/wallet-security/step-up-codes).

We check the account name with your bank. It must match your verified name. "We verify the account name with your bank. Withdrawals can only go to an account in your name."

You can save up to 5 payout accounts.

## The 48-hour wait

- The new account can receive withdrawals **48 hours after you add it**. On the web you'll see "Payout account added. You can withdraw to it after 48 hours."
- During the wait you can still withdraw to any older payout account.
- If you try earlier, you'll see "New payout accounts can receive withdrawals 48 hours after they are added."
- Removing an account and adding it again starts a new 48-hour wait.

## Common problems

- **"We could not find that account. Check the number and bank."** Check the account number and that you chose the right bank.
- **"We could not confirm this account right now. Try again shortly."** The bank check is temporarily unavailable. Try again later.
- **"You can save up to 5 payout accounts."** Remove one you no longer use first.
- **The name doesn't match.** Use an account in your own name. Zudomart never pays out to someone else's account.

## If you didn't add it

If a payout account appears that you didn't add, remove it right away, change your password and contact support from **Help**. The 48-hour wait means no withdrawal can go to it in that time.
