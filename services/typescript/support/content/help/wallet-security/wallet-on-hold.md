A hold is a pause while we check something. It isn't a penalty, and nothing is taken from you. Zudomart never holds your money. It stays in your own wallet with our licensed payment partner during the check.

## What you might see

- **"Your wallet is on hold while we check it. Contact support if this lasts."**
- **"Payouts for this account are on hold while we check its balance."**
- A withdrawal that stays pending for longer than usual, because it's waiting for review.

## Why it happens

- **A balance check.** Every day we compare your wallet with our payment partner's records. If they don't match, payouts pause until our team finds out why.
- **Unusual activity.** For example, lots of deposits and withdrawals in a short time, or signs that someone else is using your account.
- **A first withdrawal.** Your first payout gets a quick safety check: "Your first payout can take a few extra minutes while it is checked."
- **Disputes and chargebacks.** A seller with many open disputes or chargebacks may have some money held back until they're settled.
- **Legal orders.** If a court or regulator orders money to be frozen, we follow the order.

## What still works

- You can usually keep browsing, buying and selling.
- Earnings keep arriving in your wallet.
- A pending withdrawal that's rejected on review is returned to **Available to withdraw**.

## What to do

1. Wait a few hours. Most checks are quick.
2. Make sure your payout setup is finished and your payout account is in your own name. See [Payouts go only to your own name](/help/wallet-security/payout-name-match).
3. If the hold lasts more than a day, contact support from **Help**. Include your username and any withdrawal or order references.

Support may ask you to confirm recent transactions. They'll never ask for your password, PIN or codes.

## Stay safe

Nobody can "unlock" your wallet for a fee. Anyone offering to release a hold in exchange for money or codes is a scammer. See [Payment scams](/help/wallet-security/payment-scams).
