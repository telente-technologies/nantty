Limits protect your money if someone gets into your account. They apply to everyone.

## The limits

- **Up to 3 withdrawals** in any 24 hours.
- **At least 10 minutes** between withdrawals.
- **One at a time**: a new withdrawal can't start while another is still processing.
- **A daily amount cap** in any 24 hours, based on your verification level. In Nigeria it currently starts at ₦500,000 a day at level 1 (payout verified) and rises to ₦5,000,000 a day at level 2 (identity verified). Limits can change, and the app tells you if you'd go over.
- **Your own name only**: withdrawals go only to a bank account in your own verified name.
- **New accounts wait 48 hours**: see [New payout account wait](/help/wallet-security/new-payout-account-cooldown).

Every withdrawal also needs a fresh [step-up check](/help/wallet-security/step-up-codes).

## Messages you might see

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "You have reached today's number of withdrawals." | Wait until 24 hours have passed since your earliest withdrawal in that window. |
| "Wait a few minutes before another withdrawal." | Try again after 10 minutes. |
| "Wait for your current withdrawal to finish first." | Your last withdrawal is still processing. You'll be able to withdraw again once it completes or fails. |
| "This would go over your daily withdrawal limit." | Withdraw a smaller amount, or wait. Reaching level 2 raises your limit. |
| "New payout accounts can receive withdrawals 48 hours after they are added." | Choose an older account, or wait. |
| "Withdrawals go only to a bank account in your own verified name." | Add an account in your own name. |
| "Clear the amount you owe before withdrawing. Add money or wait for incoming funds." | Money you owe Nantty (from a refund or chargeback) is repaid first. |
| "This withdrawal is too small to cover the bank transfer fee." | Withdraw a larger amount. |

## Raising your limit

Finish one of the identity methods offered for your country to reach level 2. See [Verification levels](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/verification-levels).
