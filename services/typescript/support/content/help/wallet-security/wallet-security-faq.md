## Is my money safe with Nantty?

Nantty never holds your money. In Nigeria it sits in your own wallet, in your name, with our licensed payment partner. See [Who holds your money](/help/wallet-security/who-holds-your-money).

## Why am I asked to "Confirm it's you" when I'm already signed in?

Moving money needs a fresh check. Enter your password or a 6-digit code we email you. See [Step-up codes](/help/wallet-security/step-up-codes).

## I didn't get the email code. What now?

Check spam and promotions folders, wait a minute, then ask for a new code, or use your password instead.

## How much can I withdraw a day?

Up to 3 withdrawals, at least 10 minutes apart, up to a daily cap that depends on your verification level. In Nigeria it's currently ₦500,000 at level 1 and ₦5,000,000 at level 2. See [Withdrawal limits](/help/wallet-security/withdrawal-limits).

## Why can't I withdraw to the account I just added?

New payout accounts wait 48 hours. See [New payout account wait](/help/wallet-security/new-payout-account-cooldown).

## Can I withdraw to my friend's or my business partner's account?

No. Withdrawals only go to an account in your own verified name. See [Payouts go only to your own name](/help/wallet-security/payout-name-match).

## Why can't I withdraw money I just added?

Deposits are held for 24 hours, or 72 hours for your first deposit. You can still spend it on Nantty. See [Deposit holds](/help/wallet-security/deposit-holds).

## My wallet says it's on hold. Have I lost my money?

No. A hold is a pause while we check something. Your money stays in your wallet. See [Wallet or payouts on hold](/help/wallet-security/wallet-on-hold).

## Someone says they're from Nantty and wants my code. Should I give it?

Never. Nantty staff never ask for passwords, PINs or codes. See [Payment scams](/help/wallet-security/payment-scams).

## A buyer sent me a payment screenshot. Can I ship?

Only ship once the order shows as paid in Nantty. Screenshots and bank alerts can be faked.

## A payout account I don't recognise appeared. What do I do?

Remove it, change your password, sign out of other sessions and contact support from **Help**. It can't receive withdrawals for 48 hours.

## How do I make my wallet safer?

Use a strong password, turn on two-factor authentication and never share codes. See [Wallet safety basics](/help/wallet-security/wallet-safety-basics).
