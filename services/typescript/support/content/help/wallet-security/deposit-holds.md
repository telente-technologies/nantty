## Who this is for

Nigeria, where wallets can hold money. The app doesn't have an **Add money** button yet, but these rules already apply to any money deposited into your wallet. See [Adding money to your wallet](/help/payments-and-wallet/add-money-to-your-wallet).

## How long the hold lasts

- **24 hours** after each deposit.
- **72 hours** after your very first deposit.

During the hold you can still **spend** the money on Nantty, for example on an order. When you spend, money still on hold is used first. You just can't **withdraw** it to a bank until the hold ends. After that it becomes available on its own. You don't need to do anything.

## Why we hold deposits

Fraudsters use stolen bank accounts to send money into a wallet and pull it straight out again somewhere else. A short hold stops that, and protects you from having your wallet used this way.

## Other holds you might see

- **Pending (escrow)**: order money waiting for the buyer to confirm delivery.
- **Locked**: released order money in a 24-hour safety hold before it becomes available.
- **Your first withdrawal**: it may take a few extra minutes while it's checked.
- **"Your wallet is on hold while we check it."** or **"Payouts for this account are on hold while we check its balance."**: our team is checking something unusual. Contact support from **Help** if this lasts.

See [Your wallet explained](/help/payments-and-wallet/your-wallet-explained).

## If a deposit is sent back

Rarely, a deposit can't be accepted, for example if the wallet is closed or the account is suspended. We then send it back to the account it came from, before any of it is spent.
