## Your transaction list

- **In the app**: open **Wallet**. **Recent activity** shows the latest 5. Tap **All transactions** for the full list.
- **On the web**: open **Wallet** > **All transactions**.

You'll see payments, refunds, deposits and payouts, with any fees shown on the transaction. Pull down to refresh.

Coins have their own list. See [Coins](/help/payments-and-wallet/buy-coins).

## Withdrawals

On the web (**app.nantty.com**), **Wallet** > **Payout accounts & withdraw** shows your **Withdrawal history**.

## Receipts

Receipts go to your account's verified email. Checkout never asks you for an email. If you change your email, new receipts go to the new address.

Purchases made through the App Store or Google Play also get a receipt from Apple or Google.

## Sellers

Sellers get fee breakdowns, settlements and monthly statements on the web at **dashboard.nantty.com**. See [Seller payouts](/help/selling/seller-payouts).

## Something looks wrong

- **A charge you don't recognise**: check your coin activity and subscriptions first. If you still don't recognise it, contact support from **Help** and change your password.
- **A refund you expected isn't there**: in Nigeria, a refund on an escrow order unlocks the money in your own wallet. Card refunds back to your card can take a few working days with your bank. See [Disputes and refunds](/help/buying-and-orders/disputes-and-refunds).
