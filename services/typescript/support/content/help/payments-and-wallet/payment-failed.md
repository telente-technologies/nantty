We only place an order, add coins or start a plan after we've confirmed the payment with our payment partner on our own servers. That protects you from being charged twice, but it means there can be a short wait.

## What you might see after paying

- **"Confirming payment…"**: we're checking with our payment partner. Stay on the screen for a moment.
- **"Waiting for your payment"**: the secure payment window was closed before we saw a result. If you finished paying, tap **I've paid, check status**. If not, tap **Open checkout again**.
- **"Payment didn't go through"**: "No money was taken. You can try again or choose another method."

The **Payment** screen also has **Get help with this payment**, which opens a support ticket already linked to the order, and **View my orders**.

## If your bank shows a debit but there's no order

1. Don't pay again yet.
2. Tap **I've paid, check status** on the payment screen, or open **Orders** and check the order.
3. Bank transfers and USSD can take a few minutes to confirm.
4. If there's still nothing after an hour, tap **Get help with this payment** and include the time, amount and your bank's reference.

If the amount you paid doesn't match the order, we don't apply it to the order. It is refunded instead.

## Coins

- **"Your payment is still being confirmed. Your coins will appear shortly."** Don't buy again.
- **"The payment wasn't completed. You have not been charged."** Try again.

## Error messages

- **"Couldn't place your order. Please try again."** The order wasn't created and nothing was charged. Try again.
- **"The payment provider could not complete the request. Try again shortly."** or **"Our payment partner is unavailable. Try again in a few minutes."** A temporary problem on the payment side.
- **"Payments in this currency are not supported yet."** Checkout isn't available in your currency yet.
- **"This store cannot take payments yet."** The seller hasn't finished setting up. Try another store.
- **"This store is not verified yet, so its orders can only be paid through escrow."** Choose escrow for that seller.
- **"This order was cancelled, so there is nothing to pay. Start a new checkout if you still want it."**
- **"The amount paid does not match this payment."** Contact support. The money will be refunded.

## Card declined

Check that your card has enough money and is enabled for online payments, or try **Another way to pay**. Your bank decides whether to approve a card payment, so they can tell you why it was declined.

## Stay safe

Nantty support will never ask for your card PIN, CVV or one-time codes to "fix" a payment, and never asks you to pay again into a personal account.
