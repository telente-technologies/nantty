## Does Nantty hold my money?

No. In Nigeria your money sits in your own wallet with our licensed payment partner, in your name. See [Who holds your money](/help/wallet-security/who-holds-your-money).

## How can I pay?

At checkout, by card, bank transfer or USSD in the secure payment window, or with a card saved on your phone. See [Payment methods and saved cards](/help/payments-and-wallet/payment-methods).

## Can I add money to my wallet or pay from my balance?

Not in the app yet. There's no **Add money** button or pay-from-wallet option at checkout today. See [Adding money to your wallet](/help/payments-and-wallet/add-money-to-your-wallet) and [Paying from your wallet](/help/payments-and-wallet/pay-from-your-wallet).

## My bank was debited but I have no order. What now?

Don't pay again. Tap **I've paid, check status**, then contact support if it's still missing after an hour. See [Payment failed or stuck](/help/payments-and-wallet/payment-failed).

## Where's my saved card?

On the phone you saved it on, under **Settings** > **Payment Methods** > **Card**. See [Saved cards](/help/payments-and-wallet/saved-cards).

## Why are coins and Premium cheaper on the web?

Apple and Google take a fee on in-app purchases. See [App Store and Google Play purchases](/help/payments-and-wallet/app-store-purchases).

## Can I turn coins back into money?

No. Coins aren't cash. Only creators can convert coins they earned. See [Coins](/help/payments-and-wallet/buy-coins).

## Can I send coins to a friend?

Yes, from their profile, with a platform fee shown before you confirm. See [Send coins to friends](/help/payments-and-wallet/send-coins-to-friends).

## How do I cancel Premium or a creator subscription?

Premium: **Verification** > **Cancel subscription**. Creators: **Settings** > **Payment Methods** > **Subscriptions**. See [Managing subscriptions](/help/payments-and-wallet/manage-subscriptions).

## Where are my receipts?

In your verified email, and in **Wallet** > **All transactions**. See [Transactions and receipts](/help/payments-and-wallet/transactions-and-receipts).

## Why does my wallet say I owe Nantty?

A refund or chargeback couldn't be covered by your balance. It's repaid from your next earnings. See [Owed to Nantty](/help/payments-and-wallet/owed-to-nantty).

## Why is my price different from my friend's?

Prices are set per country and differ between the app and the web. See [Prices by country and currency](/help/payments-and-wallet/prices-by-country).

## How do payments work outside Nigeria?

No escrow and no stored balance. You pay the seller through checkout. See [Payments outside Nigeria](/help/payments-and-wallet/payments-outside-nigeria).

## Will Nantty ever ask for my PIN or a code?

Never. See [Payment scams](/help/wallet-security/payment-scams).
