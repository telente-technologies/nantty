In Nigeria your wallet is held in your own name by our licensed payment partner. Zudomart never holds your money. This page explains what you can and can't do with your wallet balance today.

## Who this is for

Buyers, sellers and creators in Nigeria. Outside Nigeria there's no stored balance. See [Payments outside Nigeria](/help/payments-and-wallet/payments-outside-nigeria).

## Paying from your wallet

At **Checkout**, under **Payment method**, choose **Pay from wallet** when it is shown. The option appears in the app and on the web when:

- your wallet is active in Nigeria; and
- your spendable balance covers the **full order total**, including shipping.

On the web the main button changes to **Pay from wallet**. In the app, select **Pay from wallet** above the card and secure-payment-window options.

The payment is taken from your wallet straight away. For an escrow order, the money stays locked in your own wallet until you confirm delivery, then moves to the seller. For a direct-payment order, the seller is paid right away.

If your balance is too low, the wallet option is not offered. Pay the difference into your wallet first, or choose a saved card or the secure payment window instead.

Your wallet is also used in these ways:

- **Refunds.** A refund on an escrow order before release simply unlocks the money in your wallet.
- **Earnings.** Sales and creator earnings arrive in your wallet and can be withdrawn once available. See [Withdrawals and payout timing](/help/creators-and-earnings/withdrawals).
- **Ads.** Verified sellers and creators can fund ads from their earnings in Ads Manager.

Coin packs, Premium and creator subscriptions are still paid at checkout or through the App Store or Google Play; a store purchase cannot use your wallet balance.

## Good to know

These rules apply whenever wallet money is spent:

- Only money we've confirmed with our payment partner counts.
- Deposited money that's still in its [deposit hold](/help/wallet-security/deposit-holds) can be spent, but not withdrawn.
- Ad credit and coins are separate. They can't be used to pay for products.
- Wallet payments have a daily limit: ₦50,000 (basic), ₦200,000 (mid) or ₦5,000,000 (full), depending on how far you've verified. See [Payment limits](/help/payments-and-wallet/payment-limits).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Your wallet balance is too low for this payment."** Add money to your wallet, or pay by card, bank transfer or USSD instead.
- **"This payment cannot be paid from your wallet."** Some payments, such as App Store and Google Play purchases, can't use your wallet.
- **"Your wallet is not ready yet. Finish verification first."** Finish [payout setup](/help/creators-and-earnings/payout-setup).
- **"Your wallet is on hold while we check it. Contact support if this lasts."** See [Wallet on hold](/help/wallet-security/wallet-on-hold).

## Stay safe

Never pay a seller outside Zudomart to "use your balance" or "skip fees". Only Zudomart checkout keeps your escrow protection. See [Payment scams](/help/wallet-security/payment-scams).
