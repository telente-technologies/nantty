You can give coins to someone you know, not only to creators on a live.

## Who this is for

Anyone with coins in their balance. The person you send to must be able to receive coins.

## How to send coins

1. Open the person's profile.
2. Tap the more menu, then **Send coins to** (their name).
3. Enter the number under **Coins to send**.
4. Tap **Review transfer**. You'll see the platform fee and how many coins they'll receive, for example "Platform fee 25 coins. Ada receives 75 coins".
5. Tap **Confirm: send** to finish.

If you're not signed in, you'll be asked to sign in first and then brought back.

## Good to know

- **There's a platform fee**, taken in coins from the amount you send. It's currently a quarter of the coins sent and can change. The review step always shows the exact numbers before you confirm.
- **Transfers are final.** Coins can't be pulled back once sent.
- **Coins aren't cash.** The person you send to can use them for gifts and tips but can't withdraw them.
- **Unusual transfers may be reviewed.** Our team checks transfers that look suspicious, which can delay them.
- You can see transfers you sent and received in your coin activity. See [Coins](/help/payments-and-wallet/buy-coins).

## If something goes wrong

- **"You don't have enough coins. Buy more coins and try again."** Top up your coins first.
- **"This transfer is too small to cover the platform fee."** Send more coins.
- **"You cannot send coins to yourself."**
- **"This person cannot receive coins."** Their account can't take coin transfers right now.
- **"You cannot send coins to this person."** Coin transfers between you aren't allowed right now.

## Stay safe

Never send coins to someone who promises to send back money, more coins or a prize. Coins are not a way to pay for products. Buy products only through Nantty checkout. See [Payment scams](/help/wallet-security/payment-scams).
