## Buying coins

- **In the app**: open **Wallet** and tap your coin balance, or open the coin shop from a live. Pick a pack. The price is shown on each pack.
- **On the web**: buy coins from a live room or the gift tray. **Wallet** > **Coin activity** shows your balance and the packs.

Some packs include bonus coins, shown under the pack as **+ bonus**.

Prices depend on where you buy. In Nigeria, 100 coins cost about ₦1,000 on the web and ₦1,200 in the iPhone or Android app, because Apple and Google take a fee. The web often gives you more coins for the same money. The price on the pack is what you pay.

If you see **"Coin purchases are available on nantty.com"**, buying coins isn't available in this version of the app. Buy them on the website instead. They show up in the app right away.

## After you pay

- **"Your payment is still being confirmed. Your coins will appear shortly."**: we're still checking the payment. Your coins appear once it's confirmed. Don't pay again.
- **"The payment wasn't completed. You have not been charged."**: nothing was taken. You can try again.

We only add coins after we've confirmed the payment on our servers.

## Using coins

- Send **gifts** on lives and **tips** to creators.
- **Send coins to a friend** from their profile. A platform fee in coins is shown before you confirm, along with how many coins they'll receive.

A gift or coin transfer is final once sent. Coins can't be withdrawn or turned back into money.

## Coin activity

Coin activity lists the coins you buy, send and receive. On the web (**app.nantty.com**) it's under **Wallet** > **Coin activity**. In the app, creators find it in Creator Studio.

## Refunds

If Apple or Google refunds a coin purchase, those coins are taken back from your balance. Purchases that look unusual may be checked by our team before they finish.

## For creators

Creators receive gifts as earned coins and can convert them to cash in Creator Studio, in the currency of their verified country, once they've finished payout verification. See [How creators earn](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-earnings).
