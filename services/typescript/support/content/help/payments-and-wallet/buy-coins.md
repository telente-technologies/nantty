## Buying coins

Open your wallet or the coin shop from a live. In Nigeria, 100 coins cost about ₦1,000 on the web, ₦1,200 on Android and ₦1,500 on iPhone. Bigger web packs include bonus coins. The difference is the app store fee.

## Using coins

Coins are spent on gifts. A gift is final once sent. Coins can't be withdrawn or turned back into money by viewers.

## Refunds

If Apple or Google refunds a coin purchase, the coins are removed from your balance.

Creators receive gifts as earned coins and can convert those to cash. See [How creators earn](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-earnings).
