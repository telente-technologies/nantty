## Creator subscriptions

Go to **Settings** > **Payment Methods** > **Subscriptions** to see the creators you subscribe to.

- **Cancel subscription**: tap it, then confirm **"Stop renewing this subscription?"**. Your access continues until the end of the paid period.
- **Resume subscription**: restarts renewal on a subscription you cancelled, while it's still active.
- **Manage in the App Store** or **Manage in Google Play**: subscriptions bought through Apple or Google can only be cancelled there.

To subscribe to someone new, open their profile. See [Fan subscriptions](/help/creators-and-earnings/fan-subscriptions).

## Premium and Blue checkmarks

Open your profile menu, tap **Verification**, then **Cancel subscription**. Your badge and perks stay until the end of the current billing period, and you won't be charged again. See [Premium](/help/payments-and-wallet/premium).

## Seller plans and Gold

Seller Pro, Seller Business and Gold-only plans are managed on the web at **dashboard.nantty.com**. See [Seller plans](/help/selling/seller-plans).

## Renewals

- Plans renew automatically at the full price shown when you subscribed, unless you cancel.
- Each renewal is charged only once per billing period, never twice.
- Receipts for every charge go to your account's verified email.

## Refunds

Cancelling stops future charges but doesn't refund the current period. If you were charged by mistake, contact support from **Help**. For App Store or Google Play purchases, ask Apple or Google for the refund.
