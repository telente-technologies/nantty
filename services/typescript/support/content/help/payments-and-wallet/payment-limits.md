To keep buyers and sellers safe, every payment has an upper limit. This page lists the current limits in Nigeria and what happens outside Nigeria.

## Who this is for

Anyone paying on Zudomart: buyers, creators buying coins, and advertisers topping up ads.

## The limits for one payment

| What you're paying for | Most you can pay at once |
|---|---|
| One order (card, bank transfer or USSD) | ₦5,000,000 |
| One coin purchase | ₦500,000 |
| One ads top-up (verified advertisers only) | ₦10,000,000 |
| Creator subscriptions | The price of the tier you choose |

The order limit applies to each store's order. If you check out from several stores at once, each order is checked on its own.

## Daily limits for wallet payments

When you pay from your Zudomart wallet, there's also a limit on how much you can pay in one day. It depends on how far you've verified:

| Your wallet tier | How you get it | Wallet payments per day |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | Payout account verified (L1) | ₦50,000 |
| Mid | Identity verified (L2) | ₦200,000 |
| Full | Identity and business verified | ₦5,000,000 |

The day runs from midnight to midnight, Lagos time. Card and bank payments at checkout don't count towards the wallet's daily limit.

## Outside Nigeria

The same limits apply in your local currency, converted from the naira amounts above at our current exchange rates and rounded to a round number. In some countries we set a fixed local amount instead.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Orders above ₦5,000,000 need a quick check first. Contact support to place a large order."** Contact support from **Help** with what you'd like to buy. We'll help you complete it safely.
- **"The most you can pay in one go here is …"** Choose a smaller amount, or split it into separate purchases.
- **"You can pay … more from your wallet today. Pay by card, or try again tomorrow."** You've nearly reached today's wallet limit. Pay the rest by card, or wait until tomorrow. Verifying further raises your limit. See [Verification levels](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/verification-levels).

## Good to know

Zudomart may change these limits. The figures above are the current ones.
