Apple and Google have rules for digital items sold inside their apps. Physical products, like orders from sellers, are always paid at Nantty checkout instead.

## Who this is for

Anyone buying coins, Premium or creator subscriptions in the iPhone or Android app.

## What's paid through Apple or Google

- **Coins**, when the app offers App Store or Google Play packs
- **Premium and Blue plans** bought in the app
- **Creator subscriptions** bought in the app

Some legacy Gold seller plan codes are marked eligible for store-price derivation in backend configuration, but published products and a working native purchase flow have not been confirmed. Follow the channel and product availability shown in the current app checkout. Black Government remains web-only and manually approved. Orders from sellers are never paid through Apple or Google.

## Why app prices are higher

Apple and Google apply store-specific fees and price-point rules. Store prices can differ from web prices and from one another; the prices shown in the native purchase sheet are authoritative. The repository can calculate suggested store prices from configured fee schedules, but that does not prove a product is configured or purchasable in either store. For creator subscriptions, compare the final displayed price and terms on each purchase channel before confirming.

Where web purchase is offered, the checkout displays its applicable price and terms. Do not assume web purchase is available for every plan or allowed as an alternative in every mobile app flow.

## Cancelling and refunds

- Cancel in-app subscriptions in your Apple or Google account. In **Settings** > **Payment Methods** > **Subscriptions** you'll see **Manage in the App Store** or **Manage in Google Play**.
- Ask Apple or Google for refunds on in-app purchases. Nantty can't refund them for you: "App store purchases are refunded by Apple or Google."
- If Apple or Google refunds a coin purchase, those coins are removed from your balance.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Coin purchases are available on nantty.com"**: in-app coin packs aren't available on this device. Buy on the web.
- **"Buy coins through the App Store or Google Play in the app."** That pack can only be bought inside the app.
- **"Your payment is still being confirmed. Your coins will appear shortly."** We're checking the purchase with Apple or Google. Don't buy again.
- **A purchase went through but nothing appeared.** Reopen the app and check again. If it's still missing, contact support from **Help** with the receipt Apple or Google emailed you.

## Good to know

Apple and Google receipts come from them. Nantty sends its own receipts only for purchases paid at Nantty checkout.
