Apple and Google have rules for digital items sold inside their apps. Physical products, like orders from sellers, are always paid at Zudomart checkout instead.

## Who this is for

Anyone buying coins, Premium or creator subscriptions in the iPhone or Android app.

## What's paid through Apple or Google

- **Coins**, when the app offers App Store or Google Play packs
- **Premium and Blue plans** bought in the app
- **Creator subscriptions** bought in the app

**Seller plans** and **Gold** are only sold on the web at **dashboard.zudomart.com**. Orders from sellers are never paid through Apple or Google.

## Why app prices are higher

Apple and Google take a fee on in-app purchases, so in-app prices are higher. For example, in Nigeria 100 coins cost about ₦1,000 on the web and ₦1,200 in the app, and Premium costs ₦4,500 a month on the web or ₦5,500 in the app. For creator subscriptions, the higher in-app price means the creator gets the same amount whether you subscribe in the app or on the web.

To pay less, buy on the web at **app.zudomart.com** with the same account. What you buy there shows up in the app.

## Cancelling and refunds

- Cancel in-app subscriptions in your Apple or Google account. In **Settings** > **Payment Methods** > **Subscriptions** you'll see **Manage in the App Store** or **Manage in Google Play**.
- Ask Apple or Google for refunds on in-app purchases. Zudomart can't refund them for you: "App store purchases are refunded by Apple or Google."
- If Apple or Google refunds a coin purchase, those coins are removed from your balance.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Coin purchases are available on zudomart.com"**: in-app coin packs aren't available on this device. Buy on the web.
- **"Buy coins through the App Store or Google Play in the app."** That pack can only be bought inside the app.
- **"Your payment is still being confirmed. Your coins will appear shortly."** We're checking the purchase with Apple or Google. Don't buy again.
- **A purchase went through but nothing appeared.** Reopen the app and check again. If it's still missing, contact support from **Help** with the receipt Apple or Google emailed you.

## Good to know

Apple and Google receipts come from them. Zudomart sends its own receipts only for purchases paid at Zudomart checkout.
