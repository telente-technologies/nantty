Open **Wallet** in the app, or **Wallet** on the web (**app.nantty.com**), to see your money. Each balance is kept separately, so you always know which money you can withdraw now and which is still on its way.

## Who holds the money

In Nigeria, your wallet money sits in your own wallet with our licensed payment partner. Nantty never holds it. We only record what each line is for and decide when it's safe to release. See [Who holds your money](/help/wallet-security/who-holds-your-money).

## The lines on your wallet

- **Available to withdraw**: sales and creator earnings you can withdraw now, after anything you owe.
- **Pending (escrow)**: order money held until the buyer confirms delivery.
- **Locked**: released order money in its short safety hold, 24 hours, before it becomes available.
- **Creator earnings**: gift, subscription and ad-share earnings on their way to your available balance.
- **Ad credit**: prepaid credit for Nantty ads and boosts. It can't be withdrawn.
- **Coins**: coins to send as gifts, plus coins you earned. Coins are not cash.
- **Owed to Nantty**: refunds or chargebacks taken from your next earnings. It shows in red when you owe something.

Under the lines, **Held in escrow** lists each order whose money is still waiting for delivery. Tap one to open the order.

## Other things on the wallet screen

- **Withdraw**: send available money to a bank account in your own name. See [Withdrawals and payout timing](/help/creators-and-earnings/withdrawals).
- **Coins**: your coin balance. Tap it to buy coins. See [Coins](/help/payments-and-wallet/buy-coins).
- **Payout accounts**: the bank accounts you can withdraw to.
- **Store rewards**: store credit sellers gave you, which you can only use at that seller.
- **All transactions** and **Recent activity**: payments, refunds and payouts. See [Receipts and transaction history](/help/payments-and-wallet/transactions-and-receipts).

## If you see a verification banner

A banner such as **"Verify your payout details to continue"** means you need to finish payout verification before you can withdraw. Your money stays safe in your wallet and keeps waiting for you. Tap **Get paid**, **Verify identity** or **Finish business verification**, whichever the banner shows.

## Why can't I withdraw everything?

Only **Available to withdraw** can leave your wallet. Money in **Pending (escrow)**, **Locked** or a deposit hold becomes available on its own once the hold ends. Ad credit and coins can't be withdrawn. If you owe Nantty money, that is repaid first.
