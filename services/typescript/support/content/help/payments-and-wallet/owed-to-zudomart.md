Sometimes money has to come back out of your wallet after you've been paid. If your balance can't cover it at the time, the rest shows as **Owed to Nantty**.

## Who this is for

Mostly sellers and creators. Buyers rarely see this line.

## Why you might owe money

- **A refund after release.** A buyer was refunded after the order money had already reached you. We take it back from your available balance, and anything still short becomes an amount owed.
- **A chargeback.** A buyer's bank reversed a card payment. The amount is recovered from the order's escrow if it's still held, otherwise from your available balance, and any shortfall is owed.
- **A reversed deposit.** A bank transfer that funded your wallet was reversed by the sending bank.

The wallet line reads: "Refunds or chargebacks taken from your next earnings." It shows in red when you owe something.

## How it's repaid

- New earnings repay what you owe first. You don't need to do anything.
- You can't withdraw while you still owe money.

Owing a small amount doesn't stop you selling or earning. If sellers owe more than their limit (₦10,000 unverified, ₦50,000 payout-verified, ₦200,000 identity-verified or trusted), new listings and new orders pause until it's repaid; orders you already have carry on. See [Selling when you owe Nantty](/help/selling/selling-limit-when-you-owe). If something looks unusual our team may also put payouts on hold while they check. See [Wallet on hold](/help/wallet-security/wallet-on-hold).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Clear the amount you owe before withdrawing. Add money or wait for incoming funds."** Wait for new earnings to cover it. Adding money isn't available in the app yet. See [Adding money to your wallet](/help/payments-and-wallet/add-money-to-your-wallet).
- **You don't recognise the amount.** Open **All transactions** to see the refund or chargeback, then the order it belongs to. Sellers can see refunds and statements on **dashboard.nantty.com**.
- **You think the refund or chargeback was wrong.** Contact support from **Help** with the order number. If a chargeback is won, the amount is returned to you.

## Good to know

Chargebacks can arrive weeks after a sale, because buyers' banks allow time to dispute. Keeping delivery proof, such as tracking numbers and photos, helps us fight them for you. See [Responding to a dispute](/help/selling/respond-to-a-dispute).
