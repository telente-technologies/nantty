Zudomart works differently outside Nigeria because wallets that hold money are only available in Nigeria for now. Zudomart never holds your money anywhere.

## Who this is for

Buyers and sellers in Ghana, Kenya, South Africa and every other country outside Nigeria.

## How buying works

- You pay at checkout in the secure payment window. The methods depend on your currency. For example, card and mobile money in Ghana, card and M-Pesa in Kenya, and card in most other countries.
- Orders are **direct payments**. There's no escrow, so the money goes to the seller once the payment settles.
- You're protected by our dispute and refund rules and by the seller's identity verification, not by escrow. Check the seller's ratings before you buy. See [Buying outside Nigeria](/help/buying-and-orders/buying-outside-nigeria).

## How selling works

- **You must verify your identity before you can sell.** That means a government ID check with our identity partner, plus a payout bank or mobile-money account in your own name. Until then you can set up your store, but you can't publish listings, sell on lives, take orders or run ads.
- There's no unverified selling and no sales cap outside Nigeria.
- Your share of each sale is paid straight to your payout account once the buyer's payment settles. Nothing waits in a Zudomart balance. See [Selling outside Nigeria](/help/selling/selling-outside-nigeria).

## Your wallet

- There's no stored cash balance outside Nigeria, and you can't add money to a wallet.
- Coins, ad credit and Premium work the same everywhere.
- Prices are set per country. See [Prices by country and currency](/help/payments-and-wallet/prices-by-country).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Verify your payout details to continue. Nothing you have earned is lost while you verify."** Finish the identity check and add your payout account. See [Setting up payouts](/help/creators-and-earnings/payout-setup).
- **"Payments in this currency are not supported yet."** Checkout can't take your currency yet.
- **"Withdrawals in this currency are not supported yet."** Withdrawals aren't available in your currency yet.

## Stay safe

Because there's no escrow outside Nigeria, never agree to pay a seller outside Zudomart checkout. Paying through checkout keeps your dispute and refund rights. See [Payment scams](/help/wallet-security/payment-scams).
