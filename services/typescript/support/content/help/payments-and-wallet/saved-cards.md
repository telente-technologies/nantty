A saved card lets you skip typing your card details at checkout. It lives on your phone, not on Zudomart's servers.

## Who this is for

Anyone paying by card in the Zudomart app. Your phone needs fingerprint or face unlock turned on.

## How to save a card

1. At **Checkout**, choose **Secure payment window**.
2. Tick **Save my card on this device for next time**.
3. Tap **Place order and pay** and pay by card in the secure window.

Next time, your card appears under **Payment method** as the brand and last 4 digits, with "Saved on this device. Confirmed with your fingerprint or face." When you pay with it, your phone asks you to "Confirm to pay with your saved card".

## How to see or remove it

1. Go to **Settings** > **Payment Methods** > **Card**.
2. You'll see the brand, last 4 digits and expiry date.
3. Tap **Remove from this device**.

## Good to know

- **One phone only.** The card is saved on the phone you used. A new phone, or reinstalling the app, means saving it again.
- **We never see your card number.** You type it only in the secure payment window run by our payment partner. Only a secure token is kept, in your phone's protected storage.
- **Subscriptions are different.** Plans that renew automatically on the web, such as Premium or seller plans, use a card held by our payment partner for renewals. If that card is missing you'll see "Add a card to keep your plan."
- To use a different card, choose **Another way to pay** at checkout.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Your card is still being saved. Try again in a moment."** Wait a few seconds and try again.
- **"This card can no longer be added to the device. Save it again."** Pay once more with **Save my card on this device for next time** ticked.
- **"This card cannot be used with your account."** Use another card or another way to pay.
- **The fingerprint or face prompt doesn't appear.** Check that fingerprint or face unlock is set up in your phone's settings.

## Stay safe

Zudomart will never ask you to read out your card number, CVV, PIN or a one-time code. Only enter card details in the secure payment window that opens from Zudomart checkout. See [Payment scams](/help/wallet-security/payment-scams).
