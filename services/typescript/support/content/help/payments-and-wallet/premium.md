Blue Premium is the top Blue plan for personal profiles, including creators.

## Perks

- Everything in Blue Verified: the blue badge on your profile and posts (after the identity check), priority ranking in comments and clips up to 10 minutes
- Fewer ads in your feed
- One free post boost each month. After that you pay for boosts as usual
- 6 streak restores a month instead of 3
- Priority support

Premium also helps fund a pool shared with the creators Premium members watch.

## Price

In Nigeria it costs ₦4,500 a month on the web or ₦5,500 in the app. Paying yearly on the web saves 20%. Prices are set per country, and the price shown before you pay is the one you're charged. See [Checkmark and plan prices by country](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/checkmark-prices).

## Getting Premium

1. Open your profile menu and tap **Get verified** (or **Verification** if you already have a badge), then tap **Get the blue badge**.
2. Under **Choose a plan**, pick Premium and pay.
3. Finish the identity check: your legal name, a photo of your ID and a selfie holding your ID.

Paying never buys a badge on its own. If the identity check isn't approved, Premium's other perks keep running and the badge part of your payment is refunded.

## Trials and cancelling

- Free trials are offered on the web with a card.
- To cancel, open **Verification** from your profile menu and tap **Cancel subscription**. Your badge and perks stay until the end of the current billing period, and you won't be charged again.
- If you bought Premium through the App Store or Google Play, cancel it in your Apple or Google account.

See [Managing subscriptions](/help/payments-and-wallet/manage-subscriptions).
