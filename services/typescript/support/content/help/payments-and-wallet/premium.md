Blue Premium is for eligible personal profiles, including creators. It uses the same Blue checkmark as Blue Basic; payment does not replace identity verification or moderation review.

## Perks

- Blue badge after identity verification is approved.
- No platform-controlled ad inventory while the subscription is active. See [How many ads you see on each plan](/help/ads-and-boosts/ads-by-plan).
- Unlimited scheduled feed posts up to 90 days ahead while entitled.
- Up to five linked business profiles, subject to ownership rules; only one store may be owned.
- Advanced analytics/export and priority-support response apply only where available and shown as entitlements for your account.

The subscription does not grant Creator Program approval, live access, moderation exceptions or financial privileges.

## Price

The Nigeria fresh-install web source default is ₦20,000/month. Effective prices in existing databases and store prices can differ. The signed-in checkout shows the applicable amount, discount, channel and renewal terms. See [Checkmark and plan prices by country](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/checkmark-prices).

## Getting Premium

1. Open your profile menu and tap **Get verified** (or **Verification** if you already have a badge), then tap **Get the blue badge**.
2. Under **Choose a plan**, pick Premium and pay.
3. Complete the identity checks requested for your application. The checkmark appears only after approval.

Paying never buys a badge on its own. Identity approval is separate from subscription payment; refunds follow the terms shown at checkout and applicable policy.

## Trials and cancelling

- Trial availability is conditional and shown in checkout when offered.
- To cancel, open **Verification** from your profile menu and tap **Cancel subscription**. Your badge and perks stay until the end of the current billing period, and you won't be charged again.
- When Premium ends, profile and already-accepted scheduled-post behavior follows the effective account policy. Check current plan details before relying on a particular profile-retention or bundle rule.
- If you bought Premium through the App Store or Google Play, cancel it in your Apple or Google account.

See [Managing subscriptions](/help/payments-and-wallet/manage-subscriptions).
