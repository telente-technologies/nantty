Premium is the top Blue tier.

## Perks

- Premium badge (after ID verification)
- Fewer ads
- 6 streak restores a month instead of 3
- Boost credit

In Nigeria it costs ₦4,500 a month on the web or ₦5,500 in the app, and yearly on the web saves 20%. Premium helps fund a pool shared with the creators Premium members watch.

## Trials and cancelling

Free trials are offered on the web with a saved card. Cancel any time; you keep Premium until the end of the paid period. App store subscriptions are cancelled in your Apple or Google account.
