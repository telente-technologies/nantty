## Can I just pay for a checkmark?

No. Payment does not grant a checkmark: identity or business approval is separate. Refund terms are shown during checkout and depend on the purchase channel. See [Blue, Gold and Black checkmarks](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/checkmarks-explained).

## Which checkmark should I get?

Blue for people and creators, Gold for businesses and brands, Black for government bodies only.

## Why can't I apply yet?

Eligibility requirements can differ by account type and are shown in the verification application. See [The Blue checkmark](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/blue-checkmark).

## How much does it cost?

The applicable price is shown in the signed-in checkout, which reflects country, plan, billing interval and purchase channel. Repository defaults are not a quote. See [Checkmark and plan prices by country](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/checkmark-prices).

## Why is it cheaper on the web?

Apple and Google take a fee on in-app purchases.

## What identity information does Nantty keep?

Nantty does not store raw ID numbers, ID images or face templates. We keep the verification result and necessary audit details, including the ID type, country and a keyed fingerprint used to prevent the same ID being reused across accounts. The method varies by country: a photo-ID flow may involve a selfie/liveness check, while an ID-number flow is completed on Smile ID's secure hosted page. See [How identity checks work](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/identity-check).

## How long does verification take?

Usually a minute or two. If a person needs to review it, up to 3 days.

## What happens if I'm rejected?

You're refunded and can try again after 30 days. See [If your checkmark is rejected](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/checkmark-rejected).

## What are L0, L1 and L2?

Money verification levels: verified email, verified payout details, and an approved identity check. See [Verification levels](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/verification-levels).

## Do I need a checkmark to sell or get paid?

No. You need payout verification (L1) to withdraw, not a checkmark. Gold does unlock extras for verified stores. See [Verification levels](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/verification-levels).

## How do I cancel?

**Profile > More > Verification > Cancel subscription**, or in your app store if you paid there. See [Checkmark renewals and cancelling](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/checkmark-renewals-and-cancelling).

## Can I lose my checkmark?

Yes, if your plan ends or you break the rules, for example by impersonating someone. You'll be told why and how to appeal.
