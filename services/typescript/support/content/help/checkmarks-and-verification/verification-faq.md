## Can I just pay for a checkmark?

No. You pay, then we verify your identity or business. If we can't, you're refunded. See [Blue, Gold and Black checkmarks](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/checkmarks-explained).

## Which checkmark should I get?

Blue for people and creators, Gold for businesses and brands, Black for government bodies only.

## Why can't I apply yet?

Blue needs an account at least 90 days old, 1,000 real followers, regular original posts and no recent violations. Tap **Request verification** and we'll notify you when you qualify. See [The Blue checkmark](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/blue-checkmark).

## How much does it cost?

In Nigeria Blue Verified is ₦2,500 a month on the web. See [Checkmark and plan prices by country](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/checkmark-prices).

## Why is it cheaper on the web?

Apple and Google take a fee on in-app purchases.

## Do you keep my selfie and ID?

No. We keep only the result, your ID type, country and the date. See [The selfie and ID check](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/identity-check).

## How long does verification take?

Usually a minute or two. If a person needs to review it, up to 3 days.

## What happens if I'm rejected?

You're refunded and can try again after 30 days. See [If your checkmark is rejected](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/checkmark-rejected).

## What are L0, L1 and L2?

Money verification levels: verified email, verified payout details, and the selfie and ID check. See [Verification levels](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/verification-levels).

## Do I need a checkmark to sell or get paid?

No. You need payout verification (L1) to withdraw, not a checkmark. Gold does unlock extras for verified stores. See [Verification levels](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/verification-levels).

## How do I cancel?

**Profile > More > Verification > Cancel subscription**, or in your app store if you paid there. See [Checkmark renewals and cancelling](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/checkmark-renewals-and-cancelling).

## Can I lose my checkmark?

Yes, if your plan ends or you break the rules, for example by impersonating someone. You'll be told why and how to appeal.
