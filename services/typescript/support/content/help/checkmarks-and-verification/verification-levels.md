Verification levels decide what you can do with money on Zudomart. They're separate from checkmarks, although the selfie and ID check is shared.

## L0: verified email

Everyone starts here once their email is confirmed.

- Browse, post, chat and follow.
- Buy with card or checkout, and buy coins and boosts by card.
- No wallet deposits and no withdrawals.

## L1: payout verified

You confirm who should receive money.

- **In Nigeria**: you consent to a BVN check and add a bank account in your own name. We check the account name with your bank.
- **Outside Nigeria**: you complete an identity check with our identity partner (government photo ID) and add a bank or mobile money account in your own name.

L1 unlocks your own wallet account (in Nigeria), deposits, withdrawals up to the daily cap, and receiving seller and creator earnings. Outside Nigeria, nobody can sell until they reach L1.

## L2: biometric

You take a selfie video and a photo of your ID, and our identity partner matches your face to the ID.

L2 unlocks higher withdrawal caps, the Blue or Gold checkmark and business plans.

## How to move up

1. Open **Wallet**. If you need to verify, a card explains why, with a button: **Get paid**, **Verify identity** or **Finish business verification**.
2. Follow the steps on **Get paid** (who you are, then where to send your money). It takes about 5 minutes.
3. For L2, tap **Verify identity** and complete the [identity check](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/identity-check).

## Good to know

- Your money is never lost while you verify. Earnings keep adding up; only withdrawing waits.
- Default daily withdrawal caps are ₦500,000 at L1 and ₦5,000,000 at L2. See [Withdrawal limits](/help/wallet-security/withdrawal-limits).
- Payouts only go to accounts in your own name. See [Why payout names must match](/help/wallet-security/payout-name-match).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Verify your payout details to continue. Nothing you have earned is lost while you verify."** You need L1. Tap **Get paid**.
- **"Finish the identity check (selfie and ID) to continue."** You need L2. Tap **Verify identity**.
- **"We couldn't confirm your verification. Try again in a moment."** A temporary problem. Try again shortly.

## Stay safe

Only enter your BVN, NIN or ID inside the Zudomart app or on app.zudomart.com. Zudomart staff will never ask for them in chat.
