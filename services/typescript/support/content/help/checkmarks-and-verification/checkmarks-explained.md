## Blue: people

For personal profiles, including creators. You may be suggested for Blue when you meet these rules:

- ID verified
- Account at least 90 days old
- At least 1,000 real followers
- No violations in the last 90 days

Paying never buys a badge on its own. Verification is always required.

## Gold: businesses and brands

For business and brand profiles after business verification. Gold comes with the seller Pro and Business plans once your business is verified, or with a Gold-only plan for brands that don't sell. Gold suggestions need an active store or brand and a success rate of at least 90%.

Gold is managed on the web at dashboard.zudomart.com, not in the app.

## Black: government

Only for official government bodies. It requires deep vetting and approval by two Zudomart admins, is re-verified every year, and reserves the handle. If payment lapses there's a 14-day grace period before the badge is removed.

## Losing a checkmark

Blue and Gold end when the subscription period ends without renewal. A checkmark can also be removed for breaking the rules. You'll get the reason and how to appeal.
