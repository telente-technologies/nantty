The Black checkmark marks official government and public institution accounts, so people can trust announcements and avoid fake pages.

## Who this is for

Government ministries, departments, agencies, state and local governments and public institutions, using a government profile. It isn't available to politicians' personal profiles, companies or fan pages; those use Blue or Gold.

## How to apply

1. Create or switch to your government profile. See [Government accounts](/help/business-and-brands/government-accounts).
2. Go to **Profile > More > Get verified**. Under **Black: government**, tap **Apply for government verification**.
3. The application continues on the web. Provide the official details and documents requested.
4. Our team vets the application with official sources. Two separate Zudomart staff must approve it.

## Price and billing

- ₦50,000 a month in Nigeria (other countries have local prices).
- Billed on the web, by application only. It can't be bought in the app.

## Good to know

- Black accounts are re-verified every year.
- The official handle is reserved, so look-alike names are blocked.
- If payment lapses there's a 14-day grace period before the badge is removed.
- The selfie and ID check used for Blue and Gold doesn't apply to Black.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Government profiles apply for the Black checkmark through official vetting."** Use **Apply for government verification** instead of the selfie check.
- **"This plan is available by invitation only."** Black can't be bought directly; apply first.

## Stay safe

Report accounts pretending to be a government body. See [Impersonation](/help/safety-and-reporting/impersonation).
