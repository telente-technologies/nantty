Prices are set for each country: Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, the United Kingdom, the European Union and the United States. Other countries see US prices in US dollars. Your country comes from your app store account, or from your currency on the web.

## Nigeria prices (monthly)

| Product | Web | In the app |
|---|---|---|
| Blue Essential (account protection, no badge) | ₦1,000 | ₦1,200 |
| Blue Verified (the blue checkmark) | ₦2,500 | ₦2,900 |
| Premium | ₦4,500 | ₦5,500 |
| Gold-only (brands that don't sell) | ₦3,000 | web only |
| Seller Pro (includes Gold after verification) | ₦5,000 | web only |
| Seller Business (includes Gold after verification) | ₦15,000 | web only |
| Black (government) | ₦50,000 | web only, invoiced |

- Paying yearly on the web saves 20%.
- If you have Blue, Gold-only for your businesses is 15% off (up to 3 businesses, never on Black). It doesn't stack with the yearly discount; you get whichever is bigger.
- In-app prices are higher because Apple and Google take a fee.

Prices can change. The price shown at checkout is the one you pay.
