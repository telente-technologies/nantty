Blue, Gold and Black plans are subscriptions. They renew each month or year until you cancel.

## How to cancel

**Paid on the web (card):**

1. Go to **Profile > More > Verification**.
2. Under **Your verification**, tap **Cancel subscription**.
3. Confirm **Cancel subscription** on **Cancel verification?**.

You'll see **Cancelled — your badge stays until** the date your period ends. You won't be charged again.

**Business and brand profiles:** tap **Manage on the web** on the Verification screen.

**Paid through the App Store or Google Play:** cancel in your phone's subscription settings. If you try in Zudomart you'll see **"This subscription is billed by the app store. Manage it in your store subscription settings."**

## Renewals

- The Verification screen shows **Renews** and the date.
- If a renewal payment fails, we retry 1, 3 and 5 days after the due date, then keep your plan for a short grace period. If it still isn't paid, the plan ends and the checkmark is removed.
- Government (Black) plans get a 14-day grace period.
- We tell you before a renewal price changes.

## Free trials

Blue Premium, Gold, Gold Pro and Gold Business can come with a 7-day free trial. Trials need a verified email, an account at least 7 days old, and are limited to one per person, card and device.

## Good to know

- Cancelling doesn't refund the current period; you keep the badge and perks until it ends.
- Changed your mind? Before the period ends you can resume instead of subscribing again.
- To pay for your plan with a different card, manage it on the web. See [Managing your subscriptions](/help/payments-and-wallet/manage-subscriptions).

## If something goes wrong

- **"You don't have an active verification subscription."** There's nothing to cancel.
- **"Only an active subscription can be cancelled."** It has already ended.
- **"Your subscription is still running; resume it instead of subscribing again."**
- **"This subscription has ended; subscribe again."**
- **"This card was already used for a free trial."**, **"A free trial was already used on this device."** or **"Verify your email to start a free trial."** You can still subscribe without a trial.
- **"Free trials open up once your account is a little older."**
