The Gold checkmark tells buyers they're dealing with a real, verified business. Verified stores also show a verified store label on their products.

## Who this is for

Business and brand profiles. Personal and creator profiles get Blue instead. To qualify you need an active store or brand page; stores usually need around 20 completed orders and an order success rate of at least 90%.

## Which plan

- **Gold Pro** (seller plan): Gold badge, seller dashboard access, verified store label, 3 staff seats, 1 store location and bulk catalogue import.
- **Gold Business** (seller plan): everything in Gold Pro plus a dedicated account manager, a lower escrow fee, 15 staff seats and 10 store locations.
- **Gold** (no selling): Gold badge and verified store label for brands that don't sell on Zudomart.

Gold plans are bought on the web. See [Checkmark and plan prices by country](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/checkmark-prices) and [Seller plans](/help/selling/seller-plans).

## How to get Gold

1. Switch to your business or brand profile (long-press the **Profile** tab).
2. Go to **Profile > More > Get verified**. Under **Gold: businesses and brands** you'll see **Gold after business verification**.
3. Tap **Manage on the web** to choose and pay for a Gold plan.
4. Upload your [business documents](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/business-documents) if you haven't already.
5. Tap **Verify your identity** and complete the selfie and ID check for the owner.
6. When everything is approved you'll see **Your business profile is verified**.

## Good to know

- If you have Blue on your personal profile, Gold for up to 3 businesses is 15% off.
- Free trials may be available on Gold plans for eligible accounts.
- Verified stores can offer buyers direct payment as well as escrow. See [Escrow or direct payment](/help/buying-and-orders/escrow-or-direct-payment).

## If something goes wrong

- **"This plan isn't available for this profile."** Switch to your business or brand profile first.
- **"Complete a few more orders to unlock a free trial."** Your store is too new for a trial; you can still subscribe.
- **"Finish business verification to withdraw your sales earnings."** Your documents are still missing or pending.
- Not approved? See [If your checkmark is rejected](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/checkmark-rejected).
