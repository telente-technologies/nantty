The Gold checkmark tells buyers they're dealing with a real, verified business. Verified stores also show a verified store label on their products.

## Who this is for

Gold is for eligible business, brand and store profiles. Personal and creator profiles use Blue. The business and representative must complete the evidence and authority checks requested by the current verification workflow; no paid plan bypasses those checks.

## Which plan

- **Gold Basic** and **Gold Premium** use the same Gold badge. Gold Premium is intended to include the existing Business seller workspace capabilities in one subscription; it must not require a second seller subscription.
- The legacy catalogue still uses Gold, Gold Pro and Gold Business codes. Effective operational limits are resolved server-side and may differ from old catalogue copy; check the signed-in plan screen for current entitlements.
- Gold badge approval remains separate from seller access, payment and store-risk controls.

Gold plan purchase channels depend on the specific legacy plan and configured native-store product. Follow the current checkout; code-level store price derivation alone does not confirm a purchasable store product. See [Checkmark and plan prices by country](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/checkmark-prices) and [Seller plans](/help/selling/seller-plans).

## How to get Gold

1. Switch to your business or brand profile (long-press the **Profile** tab).
2. Go to **Profile > More > Get verified**. Under **Gold: businesses and brands** you'll see **Gold after business verification**.
3. Tap **Manage on the web** to choose and pay for a Gold plan.
4. Upload your [business documents](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/business-documents) if you haven't already.
5. Tap **Verify your identity** and complete one of the methods currently offered for the owner in the issuing country.
6. When everything is approved you'll see **Your business profile is verified**.

## Multiple business profiles

Blue Premium can link up to five business profiles subject to ownership limits, but that allowance is not itself a promise that a multi-business Gold bundle or discount is active. The checkout must identify the covered business profiles and disclose any discount and renewal terms before purchase. See [Checkmark and plan prices by country](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/checkmark-prices).

## Good to know

- Any offers or trials are conditional and appear in checkout when available.
- Verified stores can offer buyers direct payment as well as escrow. See [Escrow or direct payment](/help/buying-and-orders/escrow-or-direct-payment).

## If something goes wrong

- **"This plan isn't available for this profile."** Switch to your business or brand profile first.
- **"Complete a few more orders to unlock a free trial."** Your store is too new for a trial; you can still subscribe.
- **"Finish business verification to withdraw your sales earnings."** Your documents are still missing or pending.
- Not approved? See [If your checkmark is rejected](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/checkmark-rejected).
