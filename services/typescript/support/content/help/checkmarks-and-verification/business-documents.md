Business documents let us confirm the company behind your store. Verified businesses get the Gold checkmark on a Gold plan, can offer buyers direct payment and lose the unverified sales cap.

## Who this is for

Sellers. The **Business documents** row only appears in Settings once you have a store. Registered businesses in Nigeria use their CAC details; sole traders selling as themselves are verified against their own identity during payout setup.

## How to upload

1. Go to **Settings > Business documents**.
2. Each document has a card:
   - **CAC certificate**
   - **ID** of the owner or a director
   - **Proof of address**
3. Tap **Add photo**, take or choose a clear photo, then tap **Send for review**.


## Statuses

- **Not uploaded**: nothing sent yet.
- **Pending review**: we're checking it.
- **Approved**: done.
- **Rejected**: the reason is shown in red. Fix the problem and tap **Send again**.

## Good to know

- You can open a store and sell before verification. Until then, Nigerian stores sell with escrow only, up to a total sales cap. See [Limits for unverified sellers](/help/selling/unverified-seller-limits).
- Your earnings are kept safe while you verify. Nothing is forfeited.
- Photos must be sharp, uncropped and show all four corners. Screenshots of documents are often rejected.
- The name on your CAC certificate should match the business name on your payout account.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Couldn't load your documents."** Pull down to retry.
- **"Finish business verification to withdraw your sales earnings."** Upload any missing documents, then wait for approval.
- **"This store has reached its sales limit while the seller's business verification is pending, so it can't take this order right now."** Your store hit the unverified cap. Finish verification to keep selling.

## Stay safe

Only upload documents in the Nantty app or on the seller dashboard. We never ask for documents by chat or email.
