You can open a store without documents. Uploading them lets us verify your business, which you need for the Gold checkmark and to offer direct payments.

## Upload

Settings > **Business documents**. Add:

- CAC certificate
- Government-issued ID of the owner or director
- Proof of address

Each document shows Not uploaded, Pending, Approved or Rejected. Rejections include the reason, so you can fix and upload again. We email you when documents are received, approved or need changes.
