Gifts are the main way to support a streamer. You pay for them with coins, and the streamer gets a share as earnings.

## How to send a gift

1. On a live, tap the gift button to open **Send a gift**.
2. Pick a gift. Gifts are grouped, for example **Popular**, **Premium** and **Legendary**. Bigger gifts get bigger animations.
3. Choose **Gifts per tap**: 1, 5, 10, 50 or 100.
4. Tap the gift to send it. Hold to keep sending and build a combo.

If you don't have enough coins, tap **Recharge coins** to buy more. See [Buying coins](/help/payments-and-wallet/buy-coins).

You can also tip the host directly with coins (**Tip the host with coins**).

## Gifting in a battle or to a guest

When several people are on stage, tap a streamer's tile to gift them. If a guest isn't in the Creator Program, the coins are paid to the host but still count toward the guest's battle score. See [Guests and battles](/help/live/live-guests-and-battles).

## What your gift does

- It plays on screen for everyone.
- It adds to the streamer's gift goal, if one is running. See [Gift goals](/help/live/live-goals).
- You earn 1 Nantty Point per coin, which ranks you on the live's **Top supporters**. See [Nantty Points](/help/live/zudopoints).
- The streamer earns a share of the coins. See [How creators earn](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-earnings).

## Good to know

- Gifts are final. Coins spent on gifts can't be refunded.
- Coins are not cash, and Nantty Points are never money.
- Streamers can turn gifts off for a live.
- You need to sign in to send gifts. Guests can watch without an account.

## If something goes wrong

- **"You don't have enough coins. Buy more coins and try again."** Recharge, then send again.
- **"You are sending gifts too fast."** Wait a moment.
- **"Gifts are turned off for this live."** The host disabled gifts.
- **"You can't send gifts to yourself."**
- **"That streamer is no longer on stage."** Pick someone who is on stage.

## Stay safe

Nobody from Nantty will ask you to send gifts to "verify" your account or win a prize. Report anyone who does.
