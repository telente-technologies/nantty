A gift goal tells viewers what you're working toward and shows progress as gifts come in.

## Who this is for

Hosts of a live. Viewers see the goal bar and can gift toward it.

## Set a goal

1. During your live, tap **Set a gift goal** (if no goal is running it says "No goal yet.").
2. Under **What's the goal for?** write a short title, up to 80 characters. For example "New ring light".
3. Enter a **Target (coins)** of at least 10 coins.
4. Optional: pick a specific gift, so only that gift counts toward the goal.
5. Tap **Save goal**.

A progress bar appears on the live for everyone.

## Change or replace a goal

- Tap **Edit goal** to change the title or target.
- Only one goal can run at a time. When a goal is complete, tap **Set a new goal** to start another.

## On a computer

If you stream with OBS, you can show your goal on stream by adding your Studio overlay as a browser source. See [Streaming with OBS](/help/live/streaming-with-obs).

## Good to know

- Goals count coins gifted on that live. They don't change how much you earn: you still get your normal share. See [How creators earn](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-earnings).
- Gifts are final, even if a goal isn't reached.
- Be honest about what a goal is for. Misleading fundraising breaks our [Community guidelines](/help/safety-and-reporting/community-guidelines).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Finish or edit your current goal first."** A goal is already running. Edit it instead.
- **"We couldn't save your goal. Try again."** Check your connection and try again.
- **"Only the host can do that."** Only the host can set goals.
- **"That gift is not available."** The gift you picked was removed. Choose another.
