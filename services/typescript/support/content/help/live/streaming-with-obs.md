## Set up

1. Sign in at studio.zudomart.com with your Zudomart account.
2. Create a live and open its control room.
3. Copy the **Server URL** and **Stream key**.
4. In OBS go to Settings > Stream, choose Custom, and paste them.
5. Click Start Streaming in OBS, then **Start** in the control room.

Keep your stream key secret. Anyone with it can stream to your live. You can get a new key from the control room.

## Overlays

Studio's overlay editor lets you place text, logos, shapes, countdowns and tickers. Add the overlay page as a **Browser Source** in OBS (1920 x 1080) and it appears on your stream.
