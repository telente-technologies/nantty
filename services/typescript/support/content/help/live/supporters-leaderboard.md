Each live has a supporters leaderboard that shows who is backing the streamer most on that live.

## See the leaderboard

- On a live, tap the top supporters chip near the header to open **Top supporters this live**.
- Hosts also see supporters in their gifts dashboard (**Gifts on this live**), with **Coins received** and **Your earnings**.
- After a live, the host's summary and **studio.nantty.com** show **Top gifters**.

If nobody has supported yet, the list says "Be the first to support" or "No points yet. Send a gift, like or share to top the ranking."

## How the ranking works

Viewers are ranked by the Nantty Points they earned on this live. If two people have the same points, the one who gifted more coins ranks higher.

You earn points by:

- **Gifting**: 1 point per coin
- **Watching**: 2 points a minute
- **Liking**: 1 point per like, for up to 10 likes a minute
- **Sharing**: 5 points, once per live
- **Following** the host: 10 points, once per live

Daily limits apply to non-gift actions. See [Nantty Points](/help/live/zudopoints) for the full rules.

Your own points on a live show in a small pill on screen.

## In battles

During a battle, each streamer's score is the coins gifted to them in that battle. The supporters list for a streamer counts the coins each viewer sent them. See [Guests and battles](/help/live/live-guests-and-battles).

## Good to know

- The leaderboard resets for every live. When a live ends, its final ranking is saved with the live.
- Points are for ranking only. Nantty Points are never money.
- Gifts are final, whatever your place on the list.
