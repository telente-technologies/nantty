## Guests

- The host can invite viewers or approve requests to join. Invites expire after 60 seconds.
- Up to 10 streamers can be on stage: the host plus 9 guests.
- The host can remove a guest, mute their mic or turn off their camera. Guests can mute themselves or leave.

## Battles

- The host picks 2 to 10 on-stage streamers and a length (1, 3 or 5 minutes).
- The score is the coins gifted to each streamer during the battle. Viewers tap a streamer's tile to gift them.
- **Default rule**: everyone keeps the coins they received.
- **Winner takes all**: everyone in the battle must accept first. The winner gets the coins gifted to the others.
- A tie means no winner and everyone keeps their own coins.
- Gifts are final. Viewers are never refunded.

Anyone invited can be a guest and battle, but only Creator Program members are paid for gifts. Gifts to a guest who isn't a member still count toward their score and are paid to the host.
