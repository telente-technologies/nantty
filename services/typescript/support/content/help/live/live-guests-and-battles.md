Lives can have up to 10 people on stage: the host plus 9 guests. Streamers on stage can then battle for gifts.

## Inviting guests

1. During your live, open **Guests**.
2. Tap **Invite** next to a viewer, or tap an **Empty seat** and pick someone. Use **Search viewers** to find people.
3. Viewers can also ask to join. Their request shows "Wants to join you on stage". Tap **Approve** or **Decline**.

Invites expire after 60 seconds ("Invite expired"). A guest who loses connection has a short window to reconnect.

## Controls on stage

- The host can mute a guest's mic, turn their camera off, or **Remove** them. A muted guest sees "The host muted you, so you cannot unmute."
- Guests can turn their own mic and camera on or off, or tap **Leave** to go back to watching.

## Battles

1. Open **Battle** and pick 2 to 10 streamers who are on stage.
2. Pick a length: **1 min**, **3 min** or **5 min**.
3. Choose whether to turn on **Winner takes all**, then start.

The score bar fills with the coins viewers gift each streamer during the battle. Viewers tap a streamer's tile to gift them.

- **Normal battle**: everyone keeps the coins they received.
- **Winner takes all**: every coin gifted to the other streamers during the battle goes to the winner. Every guest must accept first. If anyone declines, the host can **Run normal battle** or **Cancel battle**.
- **Draw**: no winner, and everyone keeps their own coins.
- **Leaving mid-battle** means you forfeit and can't win. You keep your coins unless it's winner takes all.
- The host can **End battle** early. Scores are final as they are at that moment.

## Who gets paid

Only Creator Program members are paid for gifts. Guests outside the program can battle, but coins gifted to them are paid to the host. Gifts still count toward the guest's score.

## Good to know

- Only one battle can run at a time.
- Gifts are final. Viewers are never refunded when a battle ends.

## If something goes wrong

- **"The stage is full. Up to 10 people can be on stage."** Remove a guest first.
- **"This invite has expired."** Ask the host to invite you again.
- **"A battle is already running."** Wait for it to end.
- **"That streamer is no longer on stage."** They left; pick someone else.
