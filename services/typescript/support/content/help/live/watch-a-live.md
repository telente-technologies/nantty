Anyone can watch public lives on Nantty, in the app or on the web at **app.nantty.com**.

## Find a live

- **Live rings**: a ring around a profile picture in the story tray, or on someone's profile, means they're live. Tap it to join.
- **Notifications**: tap the bell on a creator's profile after you follow them to hear when they go live.
- **Shared links**: friends can send you a live in chat or by link.
- **Promoted lives**: some lives appear between clips as a paid promotion and are labelled.

## What you can do

- **Chat**: type in **Say something...** and tap send. Type the at sign (@) then a username to mention someone. Comments can be up to 300 characters.
- **Like**: double tap the video to like the live.
- **Gift**: tap the gift button. See [Sending gifts on a live](/help/live/gifts-and-coins-on-live).
- **Shop**: on a store live, tap **Products in this live**.
- **Share**: open the live's options and choose **Send in chat**, **Share with people** or **Copy link**.
- **Join the stage**: if the host invites you, tap **Join stage**. See [Guests and battles](/help/live/live-guests-and-battles).

Watching, liking, sharing, following and gifting all earn you Nantty Points on that live. See [Nantty Points](/help/live/nanttypoints).

## Leave a live

Tap **Leave live**. When a live ends you'll see an ended screen with **View profile** and **Share this live**.

## Good to know

- Without an account you can watch. To comment, gift, share with people or report, you'll be asked to sign in, then returned to the live.
- Hosts can turn off comments, use slow mode or remove viewers. See [Live chat moderation](/help/live/live-chat-moderation).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Couldn't join this live. Try again."** Check your connection and try again.
- **"This live has ended."** or **"This live is not active."** The host has finished.
- **"You can't join this live."** The host or Nantty has restricted access.
- **"You were removed from this live."** The host or a moderator removed you.
- Video stuck? Leave and rejoin, or switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data.
