Only [Creator Program](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-program) members can host a live. Anyone can watch, and invited viewers can join as guests.

## From the app

1. Tap **+** and choose **Live**.
2. Add a title and pick a category: Just chatting, Gaming, Music, IRL, Education, or Shopping (only if you have a store).
3. Choose your source: **Camera**, **Screen**, or **External** (an encoder or app that streams to a server URL).
4. Optional: set up live ads in **Live monetization**, and add products if you sell.
5. Tap **Go live**.

Products are optional. A live without products is a normal live, and only sellers see product tools.

## During your live

Read and pin chat, invite guests, start battles, set gift goals and watch your viewer count. End the live when you're done to see a summary with top supporters.
