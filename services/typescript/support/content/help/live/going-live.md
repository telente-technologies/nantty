Only [Creator Program](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-program) members can host a live. Anyone can watch, and the host can invite viewers on stage as guests.

## How to go live from the app

1. Tap **Create** and switch to **Live**.
2. Add a title (tap the title area, which says **Title...**).
3. Pick a category: **Just chatting**, **Gaming**, **Music**, **IRL**, **Education** or **Shopping**. Shopping only appears if you have a store.
4. Choose your source:
   - **Camera**: your phone camera.
   - **Screen**: "Your screen goes live". Show a game or an app; the camera stays off.
   - **External**: stream from OBS or another encoder. See [Streaming with OBS](/help/live/streaming-with-obs).
5. Optional: tap **Live monetization** to allow ads. It shows **Ads off** until you opt in. See [Ads on your live](/help/live/live-ads).
6. Tap **Go live**.

With **External**, you get a **Server URL** and **Stream key** for that session, with a **Share server & key** button. The app says "Waiting for your encoder to connect" until your encoder starts. Tap **Open live room** to manage the live.

## During your live

- Read chat and pin a comment.
- Pin products if it's a store live (**Pin a product**).
- Invite guests and start battles. See [Guests and battles](/help/live/live-guests-and-battles).
- Set a gift goal. See [Gift goals](/help/live/live-goals).
- See your top supporters. See [Supporters leaderboard](/help/live/supporters-leaderboard).

## End your live

Tap **End live**, then confirm. Viewers are told the live has ended.

## Good to know

- You can only run one live at a time.
- Products are optional. A live without products is a normal live.
- Your rank ring appears on your avatar in the live header. See [Live rankings](/help/live/live-rankings).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Join the Creator Program to go live."** Apply first.
- **"Going live is paused on your account because of active strikes. See Settings > Account status."** You can go live again when strikes expire or an appeal succeeds.
- **"You are already live."** End your other live first.
- **"Only accounts with a store can host a shopping live."** Pick another category.
- **"Accept the live-ad terms to allow ads."** Agree to the terms in **Live monetization**, or leave ads off.
- **"The streaming service is unavailable. Try again shortly."** Wait a minute and try again.
