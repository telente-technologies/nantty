## Report a live

Open the live menu and choose **Report live**. Urgent reasons such as child safety, self-harm and violence are reviewed first.

## Automatic safety checks

Lives are checked automatically while they run. If something severe is detected with high confidence, viewers may see the stream blurred or paused and a moderator is alerted straight away. Less certain cases show the host a warning and go to an urgent review queue. A surge of reports also escalates the live.

A person always confirms before a live is ended. Automatic checks never ban anyone permanently on their own.

## If your live was paused

Wait for the review. If it was a mistake the stream resumes. If a moderator ends your live you'll see the reason, and you can appeal.
