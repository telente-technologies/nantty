Lives happen in real time, so they get extra safety checks. Here's how to report one and what happens if your own live is paused.

## Report a live

1. Open the live's options and tap **Report** followed by the host's username.
2. Choose a reason, for example **Child safety**, **Self-harm or suicide**, **Violence or dangerous acts**, **Nudity or sexual content**, **Harassment or bullying**, **Scam or fraud**, **Copyright or intellectual property** or **Something else**.
3. Add details if you want, then tap **Submit report**.

We attach the moment you reported and a still from the stream. Urgent reports go straight to our safety team, who review them first. If you send a lot of reports in a short time, you'll be asked to wait.

## Automatic safety checks

Lives are checked automatically while they run.

- If something severe is detected with high confidence, viewers see "This live is paused while we review it" and our safety team is alerted at once.
- Less certain cases show the host a warning ("Your live may break our community guidelines. Check what's on camera.") and go to an urgent review.
- A surge of reports also escalates a live.

A person always confirms before a live is ended. Automatic checks never ban anyone permanently on their own.

## If your live was paused

You'll see "Your live is paused for viewers" and "Our safety team is reviewing your live." Wait for the review. If it was a mistake, the stream resumes. If you think the pause is wrong, tap **Appeal this pause**, add context and tap **Send appeal**. A safety specialist looks at your live first.

If a moderator ends your live, you'll see the reason and can appeal. See [Suspensions and appeals](/help/safety-and-reporting/suspensions-and-appeals).

## Protect yourself as a host

- Turn off comments, use slow mode, block words and add moderators. See [Live chat moderation](/help/live/live-chat-moderation).
- Never share your stream key, address or banking details on stream.

## If you're in danger

If someone is at immediate risk, contact local emergency services first, then report the live.
