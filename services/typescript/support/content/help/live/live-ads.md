Ads on lives are off by default. If you turn them on, you earn a share of eligible ad revenue from your live. See [Ads revenue share](/help/creators-and-earnings/ads-revenue-share).

## Who this is for

Creator Program members hosting a live.

## Turn on live ads

1. When setting up your live, tap **Live monetization** (it shows **Ads off**).
2. Turn on **Allow Nantty ads**.
3. Read the **Live-ad terms** and tick **I agree to the live-ad terms**. Ads stay off until you agree.
4. Pick **Placements**: **Before viewers join**, **Natural breaks**, **Between segments**, **Overlay** or **At the end**.
5. Set **Max interruptions** (0 to 3) and a **Minimum interval** between ads: 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes.
6. Keep **Show ad indicator** on so viewers know when an ad is playing.
7. Optional: add **Your own sponsorship (optional)**.
8. Tap **Done**. The setup now shows **Ads on**.

## What the terms say

- Ads only appear in the places you choose.
- Ads never run more often than your limits.
- Revenue is shared under the monetisation policy that applies when the ad is delivered.
- You're responsible for your own sponsorship mentions. Nantty doesn't sell them or take a share of them.

## Good to know

- Your settings are saved with that live only. Set them again for the next one.
- When the terms change, you'll be asked to accept them again.
- Brands can also promote a live with a paid ad. That's separate from ads shown inside your live.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Accept the live-ad terms to allow ads."** Tick the agreement box.
- **"The live-ad terms have changed. Review and accept them again."** Open **Live monetization** and agree to the new version.
- Not earning from live ads? Check that the setup said **Ads on** before you went live, and that your monetisation isn't paused in **Settings > Account status**.
