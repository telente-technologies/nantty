---
title: Ads on your live
summary: Ads only run on your live if you turn them on and choose how often.
tags: live ads, monetization, creator
related: creator-earnings, going-live
---

Ads on lives are off by default. In **Live monetization** when you set up a live you can:

- Allow ads and choose placements.
- Set the maximum number of interruptions (0 to 3) and the minimum time between them.
- Show an ad indicator to viewers.
- Accept the live ads terms, saved with that live.
- Add your own sponsorship line.

You share eligible ads revenue from your lives. See [Creator earnings](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-earnings).
