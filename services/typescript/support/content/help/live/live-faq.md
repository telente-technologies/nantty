## Who can go live?

Only [Creator Program](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-program) members. Anyone can watch.

## Can I watch without an account?

Yes. You'll be asked to sign in to comment, gift or report. See [Watching a live](/help/live/watch-a-live).

## How do I go live from my computer?

Use **studio.zudomart.com** and OBS or any RTMP encoder. See [Streaming with OBS](/help/live/streaming-with-obs).

## How do gifts work?

You buy coins and send gifts. Gifts are final. See [Sending gifts on a live](/help/live/gifts-and-coins-on-live).

## Are ZudoPoints money?

No. ZudoPoints are never money. You can't buy, sell, withdraw or exchange them. See [ZudoPoints](/help/live/zudopoints).

## What is the ring around the host's picture?

It shows their global streamer rank this week. Tap it to see their category rank. See [Live rankings](/help/live/live-rankings).

## How do I get on the Top supporters list?

Earn ZudoPoints on that live by gifting, watching, liking, sharing and following. See [Top supporters](/help/live/supporters-leaderboard).

## How many people can be on stage?

Up to 10: the host and 9 guests. See [Guests and battles](/help/live/live-guests-and-battles).

## What is winner takes all?

A battle option where the winner gets every coin gifted to the other streamers during the battle. Everyone must agree first.

## Can I get my coins back after a battle?

No. Gifts are final, whoever wins.

## Will ads interrupt my live?

Only if you turn on live ads and choose where and how often. See [Ads on your live](/help/live/live-ads).

## How do I stop rude comments?

Use slow mode, blocked words and moderators, or turn comments off. See [Live chat moderation](/help/live/live-chat-moderation).

## Why was a live paused?

Automatic safety checks or urgent reports can pause a live while our safety team reviews it. See [Live safety](/help/live/live-safety).

## Why can't I go live?

You may not be in the Creator Program yet, or active strikes may have paused going live. Check **Settings > Account status**.

## How do I set a goal?

Tap **Set a gift goal** during your live. See [Gift goals](/help/live/live-goals).
