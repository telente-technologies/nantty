Nantty Points are how Nantty counts support on lives. They decide the [Top supporters](/help/live/supporters-leaderboard) on each live and streamers' [rank rings](/help/live/live-rankings).

## Nantty Points are not money

You can't buy, sell, withdraw, exchange or transfer Nantty Points. They have no cash value. They are different from coins, which you buy to send gifts. See [Buying coins](/help/payments-and-wallet/buy-coins).

## How viewers earn points

- **Send a gift**: 1 point per coin, no limit.
- **Watch**: 2 points for each minute you watch, for up to 600 minutes a day.
- **Like**: 1 point per like, for up to 10 likes a minute and 1,000 likes a day.
- **Share the live**: 5 points, once per live and up to 20 shares a day.
- **Follow the host**: 10 points, once per live and up to 50 follows a day.

Your points on a live show in a small pill on screen. Points only count while the live is running.

## How streamers earn points

Streamers earn 1 Nantty Point for every coin gifted to them on their lives. These points set their global and category rank for the last 7 days.

## Good to know

- Points are counted per live for the supporters list, and over the last 7 days for streamer rankings.
- Nantty can change point values and limits. The app always uses the current rules.
- Points earned through fake accounts, bots or self-gifting can be removed, and the accounts can get strikes. See [Community guidelines](/help/safety-and-reporting/community-guidelines).

## Stay safe

Anyone offering to sell you Nantty Points, or to "convert" them into cash, is running a scam. Report them. See [Report a problem](/help/safety-and-reporting/report-a-problem).
