Every paid placement on Zudomart is labelled, so you always know when someone paid for you to see something.

## Boosted

- Shows a small **Boosted** label with a rocket icon.
- It's a normal post that its author paid to show to more people with a [boost](/help/ads-and-boosts/boost-a-post).
- You can like, comment, share and follow just like any other post.

## Sponsored

- Shows a **Sponsored** label.
- It's an ad an advertiser created in [Ads Manager](/help/ads-and-boosts/ads-manager-campaigns). It might be a single image or video, a carousel, an app install ad or a promoted product, and usually has a button such as Shop now or Learn more.
- Sponsored ads can appear in the feed, between clips, in stories, in the Mart and in live discovery.

## Ads on lives

Ads only appear inside a live if the host turned on live ads. Hosts can show an ad indicator while an ad is playing. See [Ads on your live](/help/live/live-ads).

## Why the difference matters

- A Boosted post is someone sharing their own content more widely.
- A Sponsored ad is a business or creator advertising a product, service or app.

Both go through [ad review](/help/ads-and-boosts/ad-review-and-policies) before they run.

## What you can do

- Tap the menu on a boosted post or ad and choose **Not interested** to see fewer like it, or **Hide this post**.
- Report anything misleading or harmful with **Report**.
- Turn off **Personalized ads** in **Settings > Privacy & Data**. You'll still see ads, just less relevant ones. See [Why you see an ad](/help/ads-and-boosts/why-you-see-an-ad).

## Stay safe

Ads are reviewed, but always check a seller before paying. Never pay outside Zudomart checkout for something you saw in an ad. See [Stay safe when buying and selling](/help/safety-and-reporting/stay-safe-when-buying-and-selling).
