## What's the difference between a boost and an ad?

A boost promotes one of your posts, products, your store or your profile, and you can create and manage it entirely in the app or on the web from your [Ads hub](/help/ads-and-boosts/ads-in-the-app). Ads Manager is optional and adds full campaigns with several ads, saved audiences and reports. See [Ads Manager campaigns](/help/ads-and-boosts/ads-manager-campaigns).

## Do I have to use Ads Manager?

No. You can create ads, choose your audience, budget and schedule, pay, pause, resume, stop, add budget and see results without leaving the app.

## Do I need to be verified to advertise?

To create ads and pay by card or from your ad credit and balance, no. To use Ads Manager, pay from your earnings, top up or withdraw your ad balance, yes, you need payout verification. See [Who can advertise](/help/ads-and-boosts/who-can-advertise).

## How much does a boost cost?

Minimum budgets, package options and campaign durations are configured by region and can change. The Ads flow shows the applicable price and duration before you pay.

## Why can't I pay for a boost through Apple or Google?

Boosts in the app are paid by card or bank transfer in a secure checkout window, so there's no app store mark-up. See [Paying for a boost](/help/ads-and-boosts/pay-for-a-boost).

## Can I use my sales or creator earnings for ads?

Yes, if you're verified. Choose your earnings under **Pay with** on the web. See [Fund ads from your earnings](/help/ads-and-boosts/fund-ads-from-earnings).

## Do I get free boosts?

Do not assume a plan includes free boosts; any eligible promotion is displayed in the Ads flow and plan entitlements.

## Can I withdraw my ad balance or ad credit?

Your **ad balance** (real money you added) can be withdrawn to your Nantty wallet once it has been settled for 24 hours, if you're payout verified, minus the payment provider fee shown before you confirm. **Ad credit** is promotional and can never be withdrawn. See [Ad credit, ad balance and refunds](/help/ads-and-boosts/ad-credit-and-refunds).

## What is cashback?

Some campaigns may grant ad credit under their displayed terms. Credit is distinct from withdrawable funds; review the offer details before participating.

## What happens if my boost is rejected?

You see the reason and can tap **Edit and resubmit**. Money you paid by card stays in your ad balance, so you can use it for the new version or withdraw it. See [Ad review and policies](/help/ads-and-boosts/ad-review-and-policies).

## How long does review take?

Usually a few hours. Appeals are usually answered within 24 hours.

## What does Boosted mean? What about Sponsored?

Boosted (with a rocket) is someone's own post that they promoted. Sponsored is an ad from Ads Manager. See [Boosted vs Sponsored](/help/ads-and-boosts/boosted-vs-sponsored).

## How do I see my results?

Open the ad from your Ads hub, or **Reports** in Ads Manager. See [Understanding ad and boost results](/help/ads-and-boosts/ad-results).

## Why am I seeing an ad?

Ads are picked by an auction and the advertiser's audience. You can turn off **Personalized ads**. See [Why you see an ad](/help/ads-and-boosts/why-you-see-an-ad).

## Can I boost someone else's post?

No. You can only promote your own posts.

## Can I promote my profile or my store?

Yes. Choose **Promote profile** from your profile menu, or **Promote store** on your store page or in the seller area. The goals are **More profile visits / followers** and **More store visits**. Prices and minimums are the same as for posts. See [Boosting a post](/help/ads-and-boosts/boost-a-post).

In Ads Manager, set **Button opens** to **Your store** or **Your profile** and pick it from the list. See [Ads Manager campaigns](/help/ads-and-boosts/ads-manager-campaigns).

## Can I promote a store I work for?

Only if you're its owner or on its team as a **Manager**. See [Who can advertise](/help/ads-and-boosts/who-can-advertise).

## Are new followers counted in my results?

No. **Profile visits** and **Store visits** count taps on your ad. Follows gained aren't counted separately.

## My account is private. Can I still promote it?

Yes. The ad shows your profile card, and people who follow you from it send a follow request.

## What happens if my store is suspended?

Its ads and boosts pause automatically, and resume if the store is reinstated.

## Who won't see my ad?

Ad load depends on the active plan entitlement and configured platform settings. See [How many ads you see on each plan](/help/ads-and-boosts/ads-by-plan).

## How do creators earn from ads?

Approved creators get a share of eligible ads revenue on their content. See [Ads revenue share](/help/creators-and-earnings/ads-revenue-share).
