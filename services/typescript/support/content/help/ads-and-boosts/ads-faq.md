## What's the difference between a boost and an ad?

A boost promotes one of your posts from the app or web. Ads Manager runs full campaigns with several ads, audiences and reports. See [Boosting a post](/help/ads-and-boosts/boost-a-post) and [Ads Manager campaigns](/help/ads-and-boosts/ads-manager-campaigns).

## Do I need to be verified to advertise?

To boost by card, no. To use Ads Manager or pay from your Zudomart balance, yes, you need payout verification. See [Who can advertise](/help/ads-and-boosts/who-can-advertise).

## How much does a boost cost?

In Nigeria from ₦1,000 for 1 day. Packages are ₦1,000, ₦3,000, ₦7,000 and ₦15,000, or set your own daily budget for up to 14 days.

## Why can't I pay for a boost through Apple or Google?

Boosts in the app are paid by card or bank transfer in a secure checkout window, so there's no app store mark-up. See [Paying for a boost](/help/ads-and-boosts/pay-for-a-boost).

## Can I use my sales or creator earnings for ads?

Yes, if you're verified. Use **Pay from my Zudomart balance**. See [Fund ads from your earnings](/help/ads-and-boosts/fund-ads-from-earnings).

## Do I get free boosts?

Blue Premium members get one free boost a month, worth up to ₦1,000 in Nigeria. See [Your free boost with Premium](/help/ads-and-boosts/free-boost-with-premium).

## Can I withdraw ad credit?

No. Ad balance and ad credit can only be spent on ads and boosts. See [Ad credit and refunds](/help/ads-and-boosts/ad-credit-and-refunds).

## What happens if my boost is rejected?

You see the reason, and the full budget goes back to your ad balance as credit. See [Ad review and policies](/help/ads-and-boosts/ad-review-and-policies).

## How long does review take?

Usually a few hours. Appeals are usually answered within 24 hours.

## What does Boosted mean? What about Sponsored?

Boosted (with a rocket) is someone's own post that they promoted. Sponsored is an ad from Ads Manager. See [Boosted vs Sponsored](/help/ads-and-boosts/boosted-vs-sponsored).

## How do I see my results?

Open **Boost results** for a boost, or **Reports** in Ads Manager. See [Understanding ad and boost results](/help/ads-and-boosts/ad-results).

## Why am I seeing an ad?

Ads are picked by an auction and the advertiser's audience. You can turn off **Personalized ads**. See [Why you see an ad](/help/ads-and-boosts/why-you-see-an-ad).

## Can I boost someone else's post?

No. You can only promote your own posts.

## How do creators earn from ads?

Approved creators get a share of eligible ads revenue on their content. See [Ads revenue share](/help/creators-and-earnings/ads-revenue-share).
