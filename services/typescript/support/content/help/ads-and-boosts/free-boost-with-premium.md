If you have [Premium](/help/payments-and-wallet/premium), you get one free boost each calendar month.

## How much it covers

Your free boost covers a boost up to the minimum daily boost budget in your country, in total. In Nigeria that's ₦1,000, which is the 1-day package. In the app it shows as "Premium perk. Covers a boost up to ₦1,000 in total."

## How to use it

1. Open **Ads** from your profile menu and tap **Create ad**, or open your post's menu and choose **Promote post**.
2. Pick what to promote, your goal and audience.
3. Under **Pay with**, choose your free Premium boost and keep the budget within what it covers.
4. Check the preview and tap **Submit for review**.

Your boost goes through review like any other. Its results page says "Covered by your free Premium boost".

## When it resets

You get a new free boost on the 1st of each month (Lagos time, or your ad account's time zone). Unused free boosts don't roll over. After you've used it, the app tells you the date your next one arrives.

## Good to know

- You get one free boost a month, however many posts you have.
- If you choose a bigger budget, pay for the boost instead. See [Paying for a boost](/help/ads-and-boosts/pay-for-a-boost).
- Your free boost is ad credit: it can only be spent on that ad and can't be withdrawn.
- If your free boost is rejected or stopped before it delivers, the credit is removed and you get that month's free boost back. Once it has started delivering, it counts as used.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Free boosts come with Premium. You can still boost this post by card or bank transfer."** You don't have an active Premium plan.
- **"You've already used this month's free boost. You can boost by card or bank transfer instead."** Wait for next month or pay.
- **"This boost is bigger than your free boost covers. Pick a smaller package or pay by card."** Choose the smallest package.
