How you pay for a boost depends on where you are.

## Ways to pay

Under **Pay with** in **Create ad**, every option is listed. Ones you can't use are greyed out with the reason.

- **Card or bank transfer:** a secure checkout window from our payment partner opens. When you finish, you return to the ad's page.
- **Ad credit and balance:** spends your ad credit first, then your ad balance. No payout verification is needed. Your credit and balance must cover the whole amount.
- **Earnings:** money in **Available to withdraw**. Web only, and it needs payout verification. See [Fund ads from your earnings](/help/ads-and-boosts/fund-ads-from-earnings).
- **Free Premium boost**, if you have Premium and haven't used this month's.

Ads are not bought through the App Store or Google Play, so there's no app store mark-up.

## Adding days or budget later

From the ad's page, tap **Add days or budget**. You only pay the difference, by card or from your ad credit and balance.

## After you pay

The boost shows **Waiting for payment** until your payment is confirmed, then **In review**. Bank transfers can take a few minutes to confirm.

## Good to know

- Boosts are charged in the currency of your verified country.
- If a boost paid by card is rejected, the budget stays in your ad balance, not on your card. You can withdraw it or use it again. See [Ad credit, ad balance and refunds](/help/ads-and-boosts/ad-credit-and-refunds).
- Your receipt is in your ad account's billing history.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Pay from your earnings on zudomart.com."** Earnings payments only work on the web. In the app, pay by card or from your ad credit and balance.
- **"Your ad credit and balance don't cover this. Pay by card or add funds."** Top up, pick a smaller budget, or pay by card.
- **"Verify your payout details to use Zudomart Ads."** Paying from your balance needs payout verification.
- **"Amount is more than your available balance."** Pay by card, or choose a smaller budget.
- Stuck on **Waiting for payment**? If money left your account but the boost didn't start within an hour, [contact support](/help/contact-support/contact-support) with your payment receipt. Don't pay twice.

## Stay safe

Only pay inside the Zudomart checkout. Zudomart staff will never ask you to pay for a boost by transfer to a personal account, or ask for your card PIN or one-time codes.
