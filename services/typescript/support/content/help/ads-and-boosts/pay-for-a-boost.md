How you pay for a boost depends on where you are.

## In the iPhone and Android apps

1. On **Promote post**, choose your budget, then under **Pay with** choose card.
2. Tap **Boost post** with the total shown.
3. A secure checkout window from our payment partner opens inside the app. Pay by card or bank transfer and follow the on-screen checkout.
4. When you finish, you return to the boost's status page.

Boosts are not bought through the App Store or Google Play, so there's no app store mark-up. Paying from your Zudomart balance isn't available in the apps.

## On the web (app.zudomart.com)

Under **Pay with** you can choose:

- **Free boost**, if you have Premium
- card or bank transfer, through our payment partner's secure checkout
- **Pay from my Zudomart balance**, using money in **Available to withdraw**. This needs payout verification, and the balance must cover the whole boost. See [Fund ads from your earnings](/help/ads-and-boosts/fund-ads-from-earnings).

## After you pay

The boost shows **Waiting for payment** until your payment is confirmed, then **In review**. Bank transfers can take a few minutes to confirm.

## Good to know

- Boosts are charged in the currency of your verified country.
- If a boost is rejected, the full budget is returned to your ad balance as ad credit, not to your card. See [Ad credit and refunds](/help/ads-and-boosts/ad-credit-and-refunds).
- Your receipt is in your ad account's billing history.

## If something goes wrong

- **"That payment method isn't available in the app. Pay by card or bank transfer instead."** Balance payments only work on the web.
- **"Verify your payout details to use Zudomart Ads."** Paying from your balance needs payout verification.
- **"Amount is more than your available balance."** Pay by card, or choose a smaller budget.
- Stuck on **Waiting for payment**? If money left your account but the boost didn't start within an hour, [contact support](/help/contact-support/contact-support) with your payment receipt. Don't pay twice.

## Stay safe

Only pay inside the Zudomart checkout. Zudomart staff will never ask you to pay for a boost by transfer to a personal account, or ask for your card PIN or one-time codes.
