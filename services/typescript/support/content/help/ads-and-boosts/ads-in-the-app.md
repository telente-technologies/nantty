Everything you need to advertise is inside the Nantty app and on the web at **app.nantty.com**. [Ads Manager](/help/ads-and-boosts/ads-manager-campaigns) is optional, for people who want extra tools.

## Open your Ads hub

- **Your profile:** tap the menu on your own profile and choose **Ads**.
- **Seller area:** store owners and managers also find **Ads** in the seller area.

The hub lists your ads with their status, how much of the budget is spent, the main result and the end date. Use the filters to see **In review**, **Active**, **Paused**, **Completed** or **Rejected** ads. If you have none yet, tap **Create ad**.

## Create an ad

1. **What to promote:** one of your posts, a product from your store, your store, or your profile.
2. **Goal:** the choices depend on what you promote, for example **More views** for a post, **More product views** for a product, **More store visits** for a store, or **More profile visits** for your profile.
3. **Audience:** **Automatic**, or choose a country, states, an age range (18 to 65), gender and interests. You see an estimated reach before you pay.
4. **Budget and schedule:** a daily budget or a total budget, how many days (1 to 30), and an optional start date up to 60 days ahead. The minimum budget is shown in your currency.
5. **Pay with:** card or bank transfer, your ad credit and balance, your earnings (web only, payout verified), or your free Premium boost. If an option is greyed out, the reason is shown under it. See [Paying for a boost](/help/ads-and-boosts/pay-for-a-boost).
6. **Preview:** see how the ad will look in the feed.
7. Tap **Submit for review**.

Prices are always worked out by Nantty and shown before you pay.

## Manage an ad

Open an ad from the hub to:

- **Pause** it and **Resume** it later, before its end date.
- **Stop** it for good.
- **Add days or budget.** You only pay the difference, by card or from your ad credit and balance. A double tap never charges you twice.
- **Duplicate** it to start a similar ad.
- **Edit and resubmit** a rejected ad. The reason it was rejected is shown at the top.

## Results

Each ad shows impressions, people reached, clicks or visits, spend, cost per result and a simple daily chart. Profile, store and product visits count taps on the ad. Followers gained aren't tracked. See [Ad results](/help/ads-and-boosts/ad-results).

## Ad balance and ad credit

The **Ad balance** page shows two separate amounts:

- **Ad balance (withdrawable):** real money you added. You can top it up, and withdraw what you haven't spent to your Nantty wallet.
- **Ad credit (ads only):** promotional credit from Nantty. It can only be spent on ads and can't be withdrawn. If some credit expires, you see when.

See [Ad credit, ad balance and refunds](/help/ads-and-boosts/ad-credit-and-refunds).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Verify payouts to pay from your earnings."** Finish payout verification, or pay another way.
- **"Your ad credit and balance don't cover this. Pay by card or add funds."** Pick a smaller budget, top up, or pay by card.
- **"This ad's end date has passed. Extend it to run it again."** Add days, then resume.
- **"You can only add budget or days to an ad that is in review, running or paused."** Duplicate the ad instead.
