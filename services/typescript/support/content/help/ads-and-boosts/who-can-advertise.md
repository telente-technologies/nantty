Zudomart has two ways to advertise, with different requirements.

## Boosts: open to most people

You can [boost your own posts](/help/ads-and-boosts/boost-a-post) from the app or the web if you have a Zudomart account and are 18 or older.

- Paying by card or bank transfer: no extra verification.
- Your free Premium boost: needs an active Premium plan. See [Free boost with Premium](/help/ads-and-boosts/free-boost-with-premium).
- Paying from your Zudomart balance (web only): needs payout verification.

## Ads Manager: verified users only

To use Ads Manager at **ads.zudomart.com** you must be payout verified. That's needed to:

- create an ad account
- add funds or pay from your Zudomart balance
- create and submit campaigns for review

Payout verification means your identity and a payout account in your own name are confirmed. In Nigeria that's BVN consent and a bank account whose name matches yours. Outside Nigeria it's an identity check with our identity partner. See [Verification levels](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/verification-levels).

Verification keeps advertisers and creators safe, because every ad can be traced to a real, verified person or business.

## Who can't advertise

- Under-18 accounts can't run ads.
- Suspended advertisers can't run ads until the suspension ends.
- Ads for prohibited products are always rejected. See [Ad review and policies](/help/ads-and-boosts/ad-review-and-policies).

## Good to know

- Business ad accounts can link a Zudomart store to track purchases.
- You can invite teammates to an ad account. Only the owner can pay from their own Zudomart balance.
- Boosts and Ads Manager use the same ad account, so everything shows in one place.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Verify your payout details to use Zudomart Ads. Your verification keeps advertisers and creators safe."** Finish **Get paid** or **Verify identity**, then try again.
- **"We couldn't confirm your verification. Try again in a moment."** A temporary problem. Wait and retry.
- **"The advertiser is suspended."** [Contact support](/help/contact-support/contact-support).
