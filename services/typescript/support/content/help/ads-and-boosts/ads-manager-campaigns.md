Sign in at ads.zudomart.com with your Zudomart account.

## Build a campaign

1. Choose an objective.
2. Set your audience, budget and schedule. The minimum daily budget in Nigeria is ₦1,000.
3. Create ads in the formats you need: single image or video, carousel (2 to 10 cards), app install with store links, or a promoted post.
4. Pick a call to action such as Shop now, Learn more, Install or Sign up, and a destination: a website, a post, product, store, profile, messages or an app store.
5. Submit for review.

Ads take the shape of where they run: feed posts, clips (between clips only), Mart tiles and comment threads, always labelled. Ads appear in lives only when the creator allows it.

Reports show impressions, clicks and spend. Clicks go through signed links to protect against fraud.
