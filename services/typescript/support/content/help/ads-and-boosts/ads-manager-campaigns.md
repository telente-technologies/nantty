Ads Manager is where stores, brands and creators run full campaigns: several ad sets and ads, saved audiences, A/B tests, automated rules, team roles, billing and reports. Ads Manager is optional: you can create and manage ads for your posts, products, store or profile entirely from the [Ads hub](/help/ads-and-boosts/ads-in-the-app) in the app.

## Who this is for

Verified users only. You need payout verification to create an ad account, add funds or submit a campaign. See [Who can advertise](/help/ads-and-boosts/who-can-advertise).

## Get started

1. Go to **ads.nantty.com** and sign in with your Nantty account.
2. Create an ad account. Personal and business accounts are available; business accounts can link a Nantty store.
3. Open **Billing** and tap **Add funds**, or **Pay from my Nantty balance**. See [Fund ads from your earnings](/help/ads-and-boosts/fund-ads-from-earnings).

## Build a campaign

The campaign wizard has six steps. You can save a draft at any point.

1. **Objective**: Awareness & reach, Traffic & profile visits, Engagement, Messages, Followers, Store promotion or Sales.
2. **Campaign**: name and settings.
3. **Audience & placements**: who sees your ads, and where. Choose **Automatic (recommended)** or pick placements: Home feed, Reels, Stories, Marketplace grid & categories, Marketplace search or Live discovery. A live audience estimate updates beside you.
4. **Budget & schedule**: a **Daily budget** or **Lifetime budget**, start and end dates and a bid strategy. The minimum daily budget in Nigeria is ₦1,000.
5. **Ads**: single image or video, carousel or app install. Use an existing post, reel, product, live or store, or upload new media.
6. **Review**: check everything and submit for review.

## Promote a store or profile

1. Choose the **Store promotion** or **Followers** objective. The ad's button then opens your store or your profile.
2. In the **Ads** step, check **Button opens** is set to **Your store** or **Your profile**.
3. Pick the store or profile from the list. Only your own profile and stores you own or help run as a **Manager** are listed. A suspended store is greyed out with the reason.

The preview shows the card people see, with your logo or photo, name, checkmark and a **View store** or **View profile** button. Store and profile ads show in the home feed and in comment threads.

In the seller dashboard, open **Ads** and choose **Promote store** to start a campaign with **Store promotion** already picked.

## After you submit

Ads are checked against our policies, usually within a few hours. Statuses are Draft, In review, Active, Paused, Rejected and Completed. See [Ad review and policies](/help/ads-and-boosts/ad-review-and-policies).

Ads are always labelled **Sponsored**. They appear in lives only when the creator allows live ads.

## Good to know

- Ads run while there's budget and balance. Ad credit is used before your balance, soonest-expiring first.
- Ads for a store or profile pause automatically if it's suspended or deleted, and resume if it's reinstated.
- Clicks go through signed links to protect against fraud.
- Uploads must be JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, MP4 or MOV.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Verify your payout details to use Nantty Ads. Your verification keeps advertisers and creators safe."** Finish verification first.
- **"Add funds to your ad account to run this campaign."** Top up your balance.
- **"This ad account is not active."** Contact support.
- **"Upload a JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, MP4 or MOV file."** Change the file format.
- **"You can only promote content you own or manage."** Pick a store or profile from the list under **Button opens**.
- **"Top-ups are not available right now. Try again later."**
