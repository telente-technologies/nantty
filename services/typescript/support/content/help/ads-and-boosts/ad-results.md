Results show what you got for your money. Boosts have a simple results page; Ads Manager has full reports.

## Boost results (app and web)

Open your boost from its notification or the post, or go to **Boost results**. You'll see the status and:

- **Impressions**: how many times your post was shown
- **People reached**: how many different people saw it
- **Profile visits**: taps through to your profile
- **Messages**: chats started from the boost

Before you boost, **Estimated reach** and the estimated results (for example "About 300 to 900 views") are guesses based on similar boosts. Real results can be higher or lower.

## Ads Manager reports

Sign in at **ads.zudomart.com** and open **Reports** for your ad account. You can pick a **Date range**, **Compare with previous period**, use **Break down by** to split results, and choose a **Chart metric**. Metrics include:

- **Amount spent**
- **Impressions**, **Reach** and **Frequency** (average times each person saw your ad)
- **Clicks**, **Click-through rate**, **Cost per click** and **Cost per 1,000 impressions**
- **Purchases** and **Return on ad spend**, for business accounts linked to a Zudomart store

You can also schedule reports to be emailed daily, every Monday or on the first day of each month, to up to 10 addresses.

## Good to know

- Clicks and views marked as invalid (bots or fake clicks) are removed and not charged.
- Numbers can take a little time to update.
- Purchases are matched to ad clicks, so a sale only counts if the buyer clicked your ad first.
- Use tests (A/B experiments) in Ads Manager to compare two versions of an ad.

## Tips to improve results

- Use a clear photo or the first seconds of video to grab attention.
- Pick the goal that matches what you want: views, profile visits or messages.
- Narrow your audience to the states and interests that fit your product, but not so much that reach becomes tiny.
- Run for at least 3 days so the system can learn.

## If something goes wrong

If a boost shows **Running** but no impressions after a day, check that it was approved and that your audience isn't too narrow. Still stuck? [Contact support](/help/contact-support/contact-support).
