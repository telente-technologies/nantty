Every ad and every boost is checked against our advertising policies before it runs. The rules keep Zudomart safe for buyers and fair for sellers. The full policy is in Ads Manager at **ads.zudomart.com/policy**.

## How review works

- **Draft**: saved but not sent. Nothing is shown and nothing is charged.
- **In review**: checked against our policies, usually within a few hours.
- **Active** (boosts show **Running**): approved and delivering while there is budget and balance.
- **Paused**: stopped by you or an automated rule. Resume any time.
- **Rejected** (boosts show **Not approved**): the reason and the policy are shown on the ad.
- **Completed**: reached its end date or budget.

Automatic checks run first. If a critical check flags an ad, it can't be approved until a reviewer resolves it.

## Not allowed

Ads for these are always rejected:

- Illegal products and services, such as unlicensed drugs, stolen goods or fake documents
- Weapons and explosives
- Tobacco, vaping and recreational drugs
- Adult content and services
- Deceptive financial products, such as "double your money" offers, ponzi schemes or loan apps without a CBN licence
- Counterfeit goods, including "first copy" designer items
- Misleading claims, such as miracle cures, fake scarcity or fake reviews
- Gambling without written approval and a valid licence
- Discrimination and hate
- Text that implies you know someone's health, finances, religion or other private traits

## Allowed with limits

- **Alcohol**: 18+ only, where local law allows.
- **Health and weight-loss products**: 18+, NAFDAC registration where required, no unrealistic claims.
- **Political and social issues**: identity verification and a "Paid for by" disclaimer.
- **Financial services**: lenders, investment and payment apps must show their licence.

Ads must also be truthful, lawful and match the page they link to.

## If your ad is rejected

1. Open the ad to see the reason and policy.
2. Edit the ad and submit it again, or choose **Appeal** and explain why it follows the policy.

A person reviews every appeal, usually within 24 hours. You can appeal each decision once. After that, edit the ad and it will be reviewed again.

A rejected boost's budget goes back to your ad balance as ad credit. See [Ad credit and refunds](/help/ads-and-boosts/ad-credit-and-refunds).

## After approval

Ads that break the rules after approval can be paused. Repeated violations are recorded on your ad account and can lead to it being suspended.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Only rejected ads can be appealed."**
- **"This decision was already appealed."** Edit the ad instead.
- **"A critical automatic check flagged this ad."** Change the flagged content and resubmit.
- **"The advertiser is suspended."** [Contact support](/help/contact-support/contact-support).
