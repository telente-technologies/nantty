---
title: Ad review and policies
summary: Every ad is reviewed before it runs. Rejected ads get a reason so you can fix them.
tags: ad review, rejected, policy
related: ads-manager-campaigns, community-guidelines
---

- Ads and boosts are reviewed against our advertising policy before they run.
- Website links must be secure (https) and lead where the ad says.
- If an ad is rejected, the reason is shown. Edit and resubmit from the same screen.
- Ads that break the rules after approval can be paused.
