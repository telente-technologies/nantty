If you earn on Zudomart as a seller or creator, you can spend that money on ads directly.

## Who this is for

Payout-verified users with money in **Available to withdraw**. Only the owner of an ad account can pay from their own Zudomart balance; teammates can't.

## Top up your ad account (Ads Manager)

1. Sign in at **ads.zudomart.com** and open your ad account's **Billing** page.
2. The **Zudomart balance** card shows your money. Your sales, creator earnings, ad credit and coins are kept separately and never added together.
3. Tap **Pay from my Zudomart balance**.
4. Enter an **Amount** up to what you can withdraw, then tap **Pay**.

The money moves into your ad account's balance straight away and is used for your campaigns.

## Pay for a boost (web)

On the boost page at **app.zudomart.com**, under **Pay with**, choose **Pay from my Zudomart balance**. Your available balance must cover the whole boost. This option isn't offered in the phone apps.

## Good to know

- **It's a one-way move.** Ad balance can't be withdrawn again. See [Ad credit and refunds](/help/ads-and-boosts/ad-credit-and-refunds).
- **Same currency only.** If your Zudomart balance is in a different currency from your ad account, add funds by card instead.
- Only money in **Available to withdraw** can be used. Money that's pending in escrow, locked, or still in Creator earnings can't.
- Normal wallet safety rules still apply to the money you leave in your wallet. See [Your wallet explained](/help/payments-and-wallet/your-wallet-explained).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Amount is more than your available balance. Enter a smaller amount."**
- **"You have nothing available to withdraw yet. Add funds by card or bank transfer instead."**
- **"Only the owner of this ad account can pay from their Zudomart balance."**
- **"Verify your payout details to use Zudomart Ads. Your verification keeps advertisers and creators safe."** Finish verification.
- **"This payment was already started with a different amount. Check your balance, then try again."**
