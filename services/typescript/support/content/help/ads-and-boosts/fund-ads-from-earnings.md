If you earn on Nantty as a seller or creator, you can spend that money on ads directly.

## Who this is for

Payout-verified users with money in **Available to withdraw**. Only the owner of an ad account can pay from their own Nantty balance; teammates can't.

## Top up your ad account (Ads Manager)

1. Sign in at **ads.nantty.com** and open your ad account's **Billing** page.
2. The **Nantty balance** card shows your money. Your sales, creator earnings, ad credit and coins are kept separately and never added together.
3. Tap **Pay from my Nantty balance**.
4. Enter an **Amount** up to what you can withdraw, then tap **Pay**.

The money moves into your ad account's balance straight away and is used for your campaigns.

## Pay for an ad (web)

When you create an ad at **app.nantty.com**, choose your earnings under **Pay with**. Your available balance must cover the whole ad. In the phone apps this option is shown but greyed out with "Pay from your earnings on nantty.com"; you can still pay from your ad credit and balance or by card there.

## Good to know

- **You can move it back.** Unspent ad balance can be withdrawn to your wallet once it has been settled for 24 hours, minus the payment provider fee shown before you confirm. Ad credit can't be withdrawn. See [Ad credit, ad balance and refunds](/help/ads-and-boosts/ad-credit-and-refunds).
- **Same currency only.** If your Nantty balance is in a different currency from your ad account, add funds by card instead.
- Only money in **Available to withdraw** can be used. Money that's pending in escrow, locked, or still in Creator earnings can't.
- Normal wallet safety rules still apply to the money you leave in your wallet. See [Your wallet explained](/help/payments-and-wallet/your-wallet-explained).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Amount is more than your available balance. Enter a smaller amount."**
- **"You have nothing available to withdraw yet. Add funds by card or bank transfer instead."**
- **"Only the owner of this ad account can pay from their Nantty balance."**
- **"Verify your payout details to use Nantty Ads. Your verification keeps advertisers and creators safe."** Finish verification.
- **"This payment was already started with a different amount. Check your balance, then try again."**
