A boost shows one of your posts to more people, labelled **Boosted**. It's the quickest way to advertise on Zudomart. You can also promote a product, your store or your profile, and manage everything from your [Ads hub](/help/ads-and-boosts/ads-in-the-app). [Ads Manager](/help/ads-and-boosts/ads-manager-campaigns) is optional.

## Who this is for

Anyone with a Zudomart account can boost their own posts. Paying by card or from your ad credit and balance doesn't need payout verification. Paying from your earnings does. See [Who can advertise](/help/ads-and-boosts/who-can-advertise).

## How to boost a post

1. Open your post, tap the menu and choose **Promote post**.
2. Pick a **Goal**: **More views**, **More profile visits** or **More messages**.
3. Set the **Audience**: automatic, or choose a country, states and interests yourself. Boosts reach adults aged 18 to 65.
4. Set a daily or total budget and how many days (1 to 30), with an optional start date. In Nigeria the suggested packages are:
   - ₦1,000 for 1 day
   - ₦3,000 for 3 days
   - ₦7,000 for 1 week
   - ₦15,000 for 1 week, wider reach
5. Check the **Estimated reach** and estimated results.
6. Under **Pay with**, choose card or bank transfer, your ad credit and balance, your free Premium boost, or on the web your earnings. See [Paying for a boost](/help/ads-and-boosts/pay-for-a-boost).
7. Check the preview and tap **Submit for review**.

## Promote your profile or store

You can boost your profile or a store the same way. The ad shows your profile or store card, with a **View profile** or **View store** button.

1. Open **Ads** from your profile menu or the seller area, tap **Create ad**, and choose your profile, your store or one of your products.
2. The **Goal** is **More profile visits** for a profile, **More store visits** for a store, or **More product views** for a product.
3. Set the audience, budget and payment as above. Prices, packages and minimums are the same as for posts.

You can only promote your own profile, or a store you own or help run as a **Manager**. See [Who can advertise](/help/ads-and-boosts/who-can-advertise).

If your account is private, the ad still shows your profile card. People who tap **Follow** send you a follow request. See [Private accounts](/help/profiles-and-privacy/private-account).

## After you boost

Your boost goes to review first. Its status shows **Waiting for payment**, **In review**, **Active**, **Paused**, **Completed** or **Rejected**. It starts when it's approved (or on your chosen start date) and runs for the days you chose.

From the ad's page you can pause, resume, stop, add days or budget, duplicate it, and see results: impressions, people reached, visits or messages, spend and cost per result. Profile, store and product visits count taps on the ad. Followers gained aren't tracked. See [Ad results](/help/ads-and-boosts/ad-results).

## Good to know

- The minimum daily budget in Nigeria is ₦1,000. Other countries have their own minimum in their currency.
- Boosts are charged in the currency of your verified country.
- If a boost isn't approved, you can edit and resubmit it. Money you paid by card stays in your ad balance. See [Ad credit, ad balance and refunds](/help/ads-and-boosts/ad-credit-and-refunds).
- Everything you boost also appears in Ads Manager on the same account.
- If your profile or store is suspended or deleted, its boost pauses automatically. It picks up again if it's reinstated.
- Blue Premium and Gold Business members don't see ads, so they won't see your boost. See [How many ads you see on each plan](/help/ads-and-boosts/ads-by-plan).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Boosts are charged in the currency of your verified country."** Change the budget currency to match your country.
- **"Couldn't start your boost. Please try again."** Check your connection and try again. You won't be charged twice.
- **"This boost was already sent. Please try again."** Refresh to see your existing boost.
