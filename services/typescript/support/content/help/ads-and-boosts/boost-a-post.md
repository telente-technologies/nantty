A boost shows one of your posts to more people, labelled **Boosted**. It's the quickest way to advertise on Zudomart. For full campaigns use [Ads Manager](/help/ads-and-boosts/ads-manager-campaigns).

## Who this is for

Anyone with a Zudomart account can boost their own posts. Boosts paid by card don't need payout verification. Paying from your Zudomart balance does. See [Who can advertise](/help/ads-and-boosts/who-can-advertise).

## How to boost a post

1. Open your post, tap the menu and choose **Promote post**.
2. Pick a **Goal**: **More views**, **More profile visits** or **More messages**.
3. Set the **Audience**: automatic, or choose a country, states and interests yourself. Boosts reach adults aged 18 to 65.
4. Pick one of the **Packages**, or set your own **Daily budget** and **Duration** (1, 3, 7 or 14 days). In Nigeria the packages are:
   - ₦1,000 for 1 day
   - ₦3,000 for 3 days
   - ₦7,000 for 1 week
   - ₦15,000 for 1 week, wider reach
5. Check the **Estimated reach** and estimated results.
6. Under **Pay with**, choose your free Premium boost if you have one, or pay by card or bank transfer. See [Paying for a boost](/help/ads-and-boosts/pay-for-a-boost).
7. Tap **Boost post** (or **Use free boost**).

On the web at **app.zudomart.com**, the same steps are on the post's boost page, with an extra option to **Pay from my Zudomart balance**.

## After you boost

Your boost goes to review first. Its status shows **Waiting for payment**, **In review**, **Running**, **Completed** or **Not approved**. The boost starts when it's approved and runs for the days you chose.

Open **Boost results** to see impressions, people reached, profile visits and messages. See [Ad results](/help/ads-and-boosts/ad-results).

## Good to know

- The minimum daily budget in Nigeria is ₦1,000. Other countries have their own minimum in their currency.
- Boosts are charged in the currency of your verified country.
- If a boost isn't approved, its full budget goes back to your ad balance as ad credit. See [Ad credit and refunds](/help/ads-and-boosts/ad-credit-and-refunds).
- Everything you boost also appears in Ads Manager on the same account.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Boosts are charged in the currency of your verified country."** Change the budget currency to match your country.
- **"Couldn't start your boost. Please try again."** Check your connection and try again. You won't be charged twice.
- **"This boost was already sent. Please try again."** Refresh to see your existing boost.
