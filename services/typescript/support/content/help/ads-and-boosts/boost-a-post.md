---
title: Boosting a post
summary: Pay to show a post or product to more people for a few days.
tags: boost, promote, ads
related: ads-manager-campaigns, ad-review-and-policies
---

1. Open your post and tap **Promote**.
2. Pick a package, for example in Nigeria ₦1,000 for 1 day, ₦3,000 for 3 days, ₦7,000 for 7 days, or ₦15,000 for 7 days with wider reach.
3. Pay and track results in **Boost status**.

Boosted posts are labelled and go through ad review. In the apps boosts are paid through Apple or Google; prices there are slightly higher.
