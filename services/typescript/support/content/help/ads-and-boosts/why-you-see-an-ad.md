Ads help keep Nantty free and pay creators. Here's how we decide which ones you see, and how to control them.

## How ads are chosen

When there's a space for an ad, advertisers compete in an auction. The winner depends on:

- how much the advertiser is willing to pay
- how useful the ad is likely to be to you
- the audience the advertiser chose, for example country, state, age range or interests

There are limits on how often one person sees the same ad. Checkmark plans see fewer ads, and Blue Premium and Gold Business see none. See [How many ads you see on each plan](/help/ads-and-boosts/ads-by-plan).

## What can be used

If **Personalized ads** is on, ads can be based on your activity on Nantty, such as what you view, follow, search for and buy. Advertisers can also target broad groups such as location and age.

Advertisers can't target you by private traits. Ads that imply they know your health, finances, religion or similar are not allowed.

## Turn off personalised ads

1. Go to **Settings > Privacy & Data**.
2. Turn off **Personalized ads**.

You'll still see ads, just less relevant ones. Accounts under 18 are never shown personalised ads.

## Hide or report an ad

On any boosted post or ad, tap the menu:

- **Not interested**: see fewer ads like this
- **Hide this post**: remove it from your feed
- **Report**: tell us if it's misleading, a scam or offensive

## Where ads appear

Ads can appear in your feed, between clips, in stories, in the Mart and search, and in live discovery. They're always labelled **Boosted** or **Sponsored**. See [Boosted vs Sponsored](/help/ads-and-boosts/boosted-vs-sponsored). Ads only play inside a live if the host allowed them.

## Good to know

- Blue Premium and Gold Business members see no ads. Other checkmark plans see fewer. See [How many ads you see on each plan](/help/ads-and-boosts/ads-by-plan).
- Your privacy choices are explained in our Privacy Policy. See [Terms, privacy and cookies](/help/legal-and-data/terms-privacy-and-cookies).
