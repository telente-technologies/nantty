## Make a post

1. Tap **+** and choose **Post**.
2. Add up to 10 photos or videos. Each item gets its own dot under the post.
3. Write a caption. Type @ to mention people and # for hashtags.
4. Choose your **audience**: Everyone or Followers. Creators with fan subscriptions can make a post for subscribers only.
5. Choose **Who can reply**, see [Reply controls](/help/posts-and-feed/reply-controls).
6. If the content was made with AI, switch on **AI generated** so it's labelled.
7. Tap **Post**.

You can post straight away. Your media keeps uploading in the background and shows a preview until it's processed.

## Product posts

Sellers can tag their products. Only posts with tagged products show the shopping icon.

## Link-only posts

A post with only a link and no media shows a preview card of the page. Links to Zudomart content show a Zudomart card instead.

## Edit or delete

Open the post menu to edit your caption, change reply settings or delete the post.
