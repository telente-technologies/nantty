A post can be text on its own, or text with up to 10 photos and videos. Posts show in your followers' feeds, on your profile and in Explore.

## Make a post

1. Tap **+** and choose **POST** on the mode pill.
2. Write in the box that says "What's happening?". Type the at sign (@) to mention someone and # for a hashtag.
3. Add media with **Add photos or videos** or **Take a photo**. You can add up to 10 items. Each item gets a dot under the post.
4. If you run a business, brand or store profile, choose which profile you're posting as. See [Profile types](/help/profiles-and-privacy/profile-types).
5. Tap **Next** for the post settings:
   - **Who can see this post**: **Everyone** or **Followers**. Approved creators can also choose **Subscribers only**.
   - **Who can reply?**: see [Who can reply](/help/posts-and-feed/reply-controls).
   - **Tag people**: tag up to 20 people. See [Tagged people](/help/profiles-and-privacy/tagged-people).
   - **Mark as sensitive**: then pick what viewers could find sensitive. See [Sensitive content](/help/posts-and-feed/sensitive-content-and-age).
   - **AI generated**: "Turn on if this was made or heavily edited with AI. Viewers will see a label."
6. Tap **Post**.

You can post straight away. Your media keeps uploading in the background and shows a preview until it's ready.

## Drafts

If you close the composer partway, you can save your work. You'll see "Draft saved". Tap the drafts button at the top of the post composer to open **Drafts**: "Posts and clips you saved for later on this device." Drafts stay on your phone only, so they don't move to a new phone or the web.

## Product posts

Sellers can tag published products from their store. Only posts with tagged products show the shopping icon.

## Links in posts

Links become preview cards. A link to another Nantty post becomes a quote card. See [Link and quote cards](/help/posts-and-feed/link-cards).

## After posting

You can change reply settings, pin the post to your profile or promote it from the post menu. You can't edit the text or media. See [Editing or deleting](/help/posts-and-feed/edit-or-delete-a-post).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Write something or add media."** The post is empty.
- **"Some files weren't added"**: "We couldn't add one of the files." Try another file or a smaller video.
- **"One of the uploads failed. Remove it and try again."** Remove the item with the problem and post again.
- **"Only approved creators can post for subscribers only."** Join the [Creator Program](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-program) first.
- **"You must be 18 or older to post this kind of content."** Some sensitive labels are for adults only.
- **"You can't post as that profile."** You're not allowed to post for that business or brand profile.
