When you add a link to a post or comment, Zudomart shows a card instead of a bare web address, so people can see where it goes before they tap.

## The three kinds of card

**Quote cards (links to Zudomart posts and clips).** Paste the link of a post or clip and it shows as a quote card: the author's name, picture and the start of their post. Tapping it opens the original. If the link sits at the end of your text, the raw link is hidden and only the card shows. The original post counts it as a quote and its author is notified. See [Reposting and quoting](/help/posts-and-feed/repost-and-quote).

**Zudomart cards (products, stores, profiles and lives).** Links to other Zudomart pages show a small card labelled **Product**, **Store**, **Profile**, **Live**, **Post** or **Clip**. Tapping opens it in the app.

**Web previews (outside websites).** Links to other sites show the site name, page title, a short description and an image when the site provides one.

## How to add one

1. Copy the link. For a Zudomart post use the post menu > **Copy link**.
2. Paste it into your post or comment.
3. Post as usual. The card appears once the post is up.

Only the first link in your text becomes a card.

## Safety checks on outside links

Before an outside link opens, Zudomart checks it:

- **"Shortened link"**: "This link hides its real destination. Only open it if you trust the person who shared it."
- **"Suspicious link"**: the address "looks unusual and may be unsafe. Do not enter passwords or payment details."

You can tap **Open anyway** or **Cancel**. Zudomart will never ask for your password, card PIN or one-time codes through a link in a post.

## Good to know

- If the quoted post is deleted, made private or hidden from you, the card shows "This post is unavailable." Your own text stays.
- Stories and subscriber-only posts can't be quoted, so their links don't turn into quote cards.
- Links to a specific comment stay as normal links.
- Guests see Zudomart cards too and are asked to sign in when they try to act on them.
- Clicks on links in your posts show as **Link clicks** in [Post analytics](/help/posts-and-feed/post-analytics).
