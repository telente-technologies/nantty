Once a post is out, its text and media are fixed, the same way they are on X. This keeps quotes and replies honest, because nobody can change a post after people have reacted to it. Here is what you can still change, and how to remove things.

## What you can change on a post

Open the three-dot menu on your own post:

- **Edit reply settings**: change who can reply. The sheet is called "Who can reply?" and your current choice is marked "(current)". See [Who can reply](/help/posts-and-feed/reply-controls).
- **Pin to profile** or **Unpin from profile**. See [Pinning posts](/help/posts-and-feed/pin-a-post).
- **Turn off notifications for this post** if replies and likes are getting too much.
- **Promote post** to boost it.

You can't edit the caption, photos or video after posting. If you spot a typo, the usual fix is to reply to your own post with the correction, or post again.

## Delete a comment or reply

1. Press and hold your comment or reply.
2. Tap **Delete reply** (on posts) or **Delete comment** (on clips).
3. Confirm with **Delete**.

Deleting a comment also removes the replies under it. The app warns you first: "This removes your reply and the replies under it."

You can only delete your own comments. On your own post you can't delete other people's comments, but you can limit who replies, pin a better comment, block someone or report a comment.

## Delete a post

A delete button for whole posts isn't in the app or on the web yet. If you need one of your posts removed now, [contact support](/help/contact-support/contact-support) with a link to the post (post menu > **Copy link**) and we'll take it down for you. Only the author can ask for this.

When a post is deleted:

- it disappears from feeds, your profile and search,
- quotes of it show "This post is unavailable.",
- new comments on it fail with "This post was deleted, so your comment wasn't posted."

## If something goes wrong

- **"You can only delete your own comments"**: the comment isn't yours.
- **"Could not delete comment"**: check your connection and try again.
- **"Only the author can change who can reply."**: only the person who posted can change reply settings.
