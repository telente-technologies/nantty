Post analytics show how a post or clip is doing: how often it was seen, by how many people, and what they did next. They are private to you.

## Who this is for

Anyone, for their own posts and clips. If you post as a business or brand profile, the account that owns that profile can see its analytics. Nobody else can, and Zudomart never shows your numbers to other users apart from the public view, like and comment counts.

## How to open analytics

1. Find your post or clip.
2. Tap **Views** (the chart icon) in the row under the post, or on the right side of your clip.
3. The **Post analytics** screen opens. It notes: "Only you can see these numbers."

On someone else's post, tapping **Views** shows a short explainer instead. See [About views](/help/posts-and-feed/views-and-analytics).

## What the numbers mean

**Top numbers**

- **Impressions**: every time the post appeared on someone else's screen, including repeat views.
- **Unique viewers**: how many different people saw it.
- **Profile visits**: people who went to your profile from this post.
- **Engagements** and **Engagement rate**: likes, replies, reposts, saves and shares, and that total as a share of impressions.
- **New follows**: people who followed you after seeing the post.

**Video** (posts) or **Watching** (clips), for anything with a video:

- **Video views**, **Watch time**, **Average watch time** and **Completion rate** (how many people watched to the end).

**Impressions, last 7 days**: a bar for each day.

**Engagements**: **Likes**, **Replies** (or **Comments** on clips), **Reposts**, **Saves**, **Shares**, and **Link clicks** if your post has a link.

**Audience**: the split of who saw it, as percentages, when there's enough data.

Pull down to refresh.

## Good to know

- Your own views never count.
- Views are anonymous. You see totals, never a list of who viewed a post. Stories are different: you can see who viewed your story. See [Story viewers](/help/clips-and-stories/story-viewers-and-privacy).
- Zudomart doesn't pay for views. Creators earn from eligible monetisation instead. See [Creator earnings](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-earnings).

## If something goes wrong

- **"These analytics are private"**: "Only the author can see analytics for this post."
- **"This post isn't available"**: "It may have been deleted."
- **"Couldn't load analytics"**: "Check your connection and try again."
