Every post and clip has a three-dot menu (**More options**). The options change depending on whether the post is yours, and whether you are signed in.

## Options on your own posts

- **Promote post**: boost the post with Nantty ads. See [Boost a post](/help/ads-and-boosts/boost-a-post).
- **Copy link**: copies the post's link. You'll see "Link copied".
- **Download**: saves the photo or video to your phone, when the post has downloadable media.
- **Edit reply settings**: choose who can reply. See [Who can reply](/help/posts-and-feed/reply-controls).
- **Pin to profile** or **Unpin from profile**: keep up to 3 posts at the top of your profile. See [Pinning posts](/help/posts-and-feed/pin-a-post).
- **Turn off notifications for this post** (or **Turn on notifications for this post**).
- **Share via…**: opens your phone's share sheet.

## Options on other people's posts

- **Copy link** and **Share via…**
- **Download**, if the author allows downloads. Downloaded copies carry the Nantty logo and the author's username.
- **Turn off notifications for this post**: stops alerts about a post you replied to or were mentioned in. You'll see "You won't get notifications for this post". Tap again to see "Notifications for this post are back on".
- **Not interested**: "See fewer posts like this". The post disappears and your feed learns from it.
- **Hide this post** (or **Hide this clip**): removes it from your feed.
- **Report**: "Let us know what's wrong". See [Report a problem](/help/safety-and-reporting/report-a-problem).
- **Report copyright**: for music, video or images used without permission. See [Sounds and copyright](/help/posts-and-feed/sounds-and-copyright).

Guests can see **Copy link** and **Share via…**. Options like **Not interested** and **Report** ask you to sign in first.

## Buttons under the post

The row under each post has **Like**, **Reply**, **Repost**, **Views**, the save (bookmark) button and **Share**. Share offers **Send in chat** as well as your phone's share sheet. See [Share to chat](/help/chat/share-to-chat).

Tapping **Views** on your own post opens [Post analytics](/help/posts-and-feed/post-analytics). On anyone else's post it explains what views are. See [About views](/help/posts-and-feed/views-and-analytics).

## Good to know

- There is no option to edit a post's text or media. See [Editing or deleting](/help/posts-and-feed/edit-or-delete-a-post).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Couldn't update notifications. Try again."** The change didn't save. Check your connection.
- **"You've sent too many reports. Try again later."** You can send up to 5 reports an hour.
