## What you see

The For you feed starts with posts from people and stores you follow, then adds posts you may like based on what you watch, like, comment on, save and share. Guests see popular posts.

## How posts are chosen

1. **Candidates**: recent posts from accounts you follow, plus popular and related posts.
2. **Safety first**: removed, hidden and age-restricted posts are filtered out, along with anything from accounts you blocked.
3. **Ranking**: posts are ordered by how likely they are to interest you.
4. **Variety**: we avoid showing too many posts from the same account or topic in a row.

Verified and official accounts can get a small, capped boost. It never lets them jump over safety rules or flood your feed. Ads are labelled **Sponsored** or **Ad**.

## Shape your feed

- Follow, mute or block accounts.
- Turn personalisation off in Settings > Privacy & Data for a less tailored feed.
- Use "Not interested" on a post's menu.
