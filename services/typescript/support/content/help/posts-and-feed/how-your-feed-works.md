## Our promise

- Nobody gets a secret advantage. The only boosts are the ones on this page, and each one has a fixed upper limit.
- Nobody can pay for reach in the feed, apart from ads, which are always labelled **Promoted**, **Sponsored** or **Ad**.
- Zudomart staff cannot push a post or a trend. Staff can only demote or remove content that breaks our rules. Every removal needs a written policy reason and is logged.
- Good posts from free accounts can go viral. Quality and real engagement count for much more than any boost.

## How posts are chosen

1. **Candidates**: recent posts from accounts you follow, posts on topics you engage with, trending posts and fresh posts. Posts popular in your country are included when that helps.
2. **Safety and integrity**: we take out removed and hidden posts, sensitive or age-restricted posts you shouldn't see, posts from accounts you blocked or that are suspended, posts you marked "Not interested", and spam.
3. **Ranking**: we look at how fast a post gets real engagement compared with how many people saw it, whether people watch or read it to the end, and replies, reposts, saves and shares. Newer posts get a head start that fades over time. If personalisation is on, we also use what you like and who you follow.
4. **Capped boosts**: checkmark and Official accounts get a small boost (see below).
5. **Variety**: we don't show several posts in a row from one account, and we limit how much of one account or topic you see, so nobody can flood your feed.

## Boosts we disclose

Accounts with a checkmark get a small ranking boost, like the one X gives Premium subscribers. The boost multiplies a post's score and has a fixed upper limit:

| Account | Upper limit |
| --- | --- |
| Black checkmark (government) | up to 15% |
| Gold checkmark (business) | up to 12% |
| Premium | up to 10% |
| Blue checkmark (Verified) | up to 8% |
| Blue Essential | up to 5% |

The order is always Black ≥ Gold ≥ Premium ≥ Blue Verified ≥ Blue Essential.

**Official** accounts are accounts Zudomart created for public bodies, brands or our own teams and gave a checkmark. They show an **Official** label and get an extra editorial boost of up to 15%, so their important updates can trend. Together with the checkmark boost this can never go above 25%.

Admins can lower these numbers, but the limits are enforced by our servers and can't be raised from the admin console. Every change is recorded in our audit log.

Because a boost is a small multiplier, a post with much better engagement always ranks above a boosted post with weak engagement.

## Fake engagement doesn't count

We discount or ignore engagement that looks fake, including:

- bots, brand-new accounts and low-trust accounts,
- groups of accounts acting together, such as many accounts on the same network or device, or sudden bursts of likes,
- "like for like" pods where accounts only engage with each other,
- bought likes, follows or views,
- spammy hashtags such as follow-for-follow tags.

Posts and hashtags with manipulated engagement can't trend. We may send the accounts involved to our Trust & Safety team for review.

## Why am I seeing this?

Open the post menu and tap **Why am I seeing this?** You'll see the main reasons, for example:

- **You follow this account**
- **Trending: #topic**
- **Similar to posts you liked**
- **Popular near you**
- **Promoted** (an ad)
- **Checkmark account** or **Official account**, with the boost it received

## Trends and "What's happening"

Trends are hashtags and topics that are taking off right now, by country and by category (News, Sports, Entertainment, Music, Tech, Business, Fashion, Gaming, Comedy, Religion, Food, Politics, Lifestyle and more). A trend needs posts from several different accounts, so one account can't make something trend.

"What's happening" cards group related posts into a story with a short, neutral headline. They are labelled **Summarised from posts** and link to the posts they come from. We only use public posts, never sensitive or age-restricted ones, and we leave out manipulated posts. Political and religious stories use a plain headline such as "People are posting about #topic" instead of quoting anyone.

## Shape your feed

- Follow, block or unfollow accounts.
- Use **Not interested** on a post's menu.
- Turn personalisation off in Settings > Privacy & Data. Your feed then uses trends and popularity, not your activity.
- Switch to the **Following** tab to see only accounts you follow, newest first.
