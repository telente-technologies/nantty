## Comment and reply

Open a post to see the conversation. Write a comment, or tap **Reply** under any comment to answer it directly. Replies can go as deep as the conversation needs, and you can open a reply to see its own thread.

You can also reply to a comment with a short video.

## Quick actions on the feed

On a feed post, swipe right to like it and swipe left to open the comments. This doesn't work on posts with several media items, where swiping moves between photos. The buttons under each post always work too.

## Quotes

A quote is a repost with your own text. Under a post, **View quotes** lists everyone who quoted it, respecting their privacy settings.

## Promoted replies

Conversations can include replies marked **Promoted**. These are ads.
