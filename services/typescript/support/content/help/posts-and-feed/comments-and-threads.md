Every post and clip has a conversation under it. You can reply to the post itself or to anyone in the conversation.

## Comment on a post

1. Tap **Reply** under the post, or open the post.
2. Write your comment (1 to 1,500 characters). Type the at sign (@) to mention someone.
3. Send it. It shows "Posting" for a moment, then appears.

To answer a particular person, tap **Reply** under their comment. Your reply shows "Replying to" with their name.

## Comment on a clip

Tap **Comments** on the right side of the clip. The comments open in a panel over the video. Write in "Add comment..." and tap send. To reply to a comment, tap reply on it; tap **Cancel reply** to stop.

Clip authors can also tap **Reply with video** and pick a comment to answer it with a new clip.

## Follow a conversation

- Replies show under the comment they answer, up to three levels deep, so you can read a back-and-forth without tapping.
- Tap **View thread** or any reply to open the full thread on its own page.
- Sort the conversation with the dropdown: **Relevant**, **Latest** or **Most liked**.

Learn how the order works in [Comment order and the Author label](/help/posts-and-feed/comment-order-and-author-label).

## What you can do with a reply

Each reply has its own buttons to like, reply, repost, save and share. Press and hold your own reply to delete it. Press and hold a comment on your own post to pin it. On clips, press and hold to see **Report comment** too.

## Quick actions in the feed

Use the buttons under each post to like it or open the comments, or double-tap a photo to like it. Swiping left and right on the feed switches between **Feed** and **Mart**; on posts with several photos, swiping moves between the photos instead.

## Quotes

A quote is a repost with your own text. Under a post, **View quotes** lists everyone who quoted it. Pasting a post link into a comment also makes a quote. See [Reposting and quoting](/help/posts-and-feed/repost-and-quote).

## Good to know

- Deleting a comment also deletes the replies under it.
- Ads in a conversation are always labelled.
- The author can limit who replies. See [Who can reply](/help/posts-and-feed/reply-controls).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Comment not posted"**: "Please check your connection and try again."
- **"Comments need 1 to 1500 characters."**
- **"This post was deleted, so your comment wasn't posted."**
- **"Only the clip's author can reply with a video."**
- **"Couldn't load comments. Pull to try again."**
