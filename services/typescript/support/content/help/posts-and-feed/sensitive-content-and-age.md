## Sensitive content warnings

Posts that may be disturbing or graphic are covered with a warning. Tap **View** to see it. Your choice applies to that post only.

## Age rules

- If you are under 18, or we don't know your age, you never receive nudity or dangerous content. This is enforced by our servers in feeds, search and profiles, not just hidden in the app.
- Guests are treated as under 18.
- Adults can adjust sensitive content preferences in Settings > Content preferences.

## Labelling your own posts

Mark a post as sensitive in the composer if it may upset people. Content that breaks our [Community Guidelines](/help/safety-and-reporting/community-guidelines) is removed whether or not it is labelled.
