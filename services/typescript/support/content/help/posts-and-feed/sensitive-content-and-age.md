Some posts are fine for Zudomart but not for everyone. These are covered with a **Sensitive content** warning so you choose whether to see them.

## Sensitive content warnings

A covered post shows a blurred preview, the heading **Sensitive content**, what kind of content it is, and "You can choose whether to see it."

Tap **View** to show it. Your choice is remembered for that post only.

## The four labels

Posts can be marked for:

- **Nudity or sexual content** (adults only)
- **Graphic violence**
- **Dangerous acts** (adults only)
- **Self-harm**

## Age rules

- If you are under 18, or we don't know your age, you never see nudity or dangerous acts. Our servers enforce this in feeds, Explore, search and profiles, not just the app. Accounts under 18 always see warnings.
- If you haven't added your birthday, we treat your account as under 18 until you do.
- Guests are treated as under 18.

## Turn warnings off (adults)

1. Go to **Settings > Privacy > Content preferences**.
2. Turn on **Show sensitive content without warning**: "Posts marked as sensitive open straight away instead of showing a warning first. You can turn warnings back on at any time."

If this option is locked, the screen says: "Add your birthday under Settings, Account to manage this."

## Labelling your own posts

When you create a post or clip, turn on **Mark as sensitive** in the settings step and pick what viewers could find sensitive. Labels for adult content are only available if you are 18 or over.

Content that breaks our [Community Guidelines](/help/safety-and-reporting/community-guidelines) is removed whether or not it is labelled. If you see unlabelled sensitive content, [report it](/help/safety-and-reporting/report-a-problem).

## If something goes wrong

- **"You must be 18 or older to post this kind of content."** You chose an adults-only label but your account is under 18 or has no birthday.
- **"Only the author can change content warnings."** You can't change labels on someone else's post.
