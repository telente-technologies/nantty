Reply controls decide who can join the conversation under your post. They're useful when a post goes viral, when you want a conversation with friends only, or when you just want to share something without comments.

## The options

| Option | Who can reply |
| --- | --- |
| **Everyone** | Anyone signed in |
| **People you follow** | Only accounts you follow |
| **Followers only** | Only people who follow you |
| **Only people you mention** | Only accounts you mention in the post |
| **Off** | Nobody. Comments are turned off |

You can always reply to your own post, whatever you choose.

## Set it when you post

1. Create your post or clip.
2. Under **Who can reply?**, pick an option. The composer reminds you: "You can change this later from the post's menu."
3. Post as usual.

## Change it later

1. Tap the three-dot menu on your post.
2. Tap **Edit reply settings**.
3. In the **Who can reply?** sheet, pick a new option. Your current choice is marked "(current)".

The change applies straight away. Replies that are already there stay.

## What other people see

People who can't reply see why instead of the reply box:

- "The author turned off replies for this post."
- "Only people the author follows can reply."
- "Only the author's followers can reply."
- "Only people mentioned in this post can reply."
- "Only subscribers can reply to this post." (for subscriber-only posts from creators)
- "Sign in to reply." (for guests)

They can still like, repost, quote and share the post, unless you've blocked them.

## Good to know

- Reply controls only cover replies. To stop a person contacting you at all, [block them](/help/profiles-and-privacy/blocking-people).
- To decide who can mention you anywhere, see [Mentions](/help/posts-and-feed/mentions).
- To keep a good answer at the top, [pin a comment](/help/posts-and-feed/pin-a-comment).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Only the author can change who can reply."** You can only change reply settings on your own posts.
