## How many photos or videos can I add to a post?

Up to 10. Text-only posts are fine too. See [Creating posts](/help/posts-and-feed/create-a-post).

## Can I edit a post after posting?

No. The text and media are fixed once posted. You can still change who can reply, pin it or promote it. See [Editing or deleting](/help/posts-and-feed/edit-or-delete-a-post).

## How do I delete a post?

There isn't a delete button for whole posts yet. Contact support with the post's link and we'll remove it. You can delete your own comments and replies by pressing and holding them.

## What's the difference between a repost and a quote?

A repost (**Reshare**) shares the post as it is. A quote adds your own comment on top. Pasting a post link into a post or comment also makes a quote. See [Reposting and quoting](/help/posts-and-feed/repost-and-quote).

## How many posts can I pin?

Up to 3 on your profile. You can also pin one comment on each of your posts. See [Pinning posts](/help/posts-and-feed/pin-a-post) and [Pinning a comment](/help/posts-and-feed/pin-a-comment).

## Why is one comment always at the top?

The author pinned it, or it was written by the author. The author's comments and threads they joined come next. See [Comment order](/help/posts-and-feed/comment-order-and-author-label).

## How do I stop people replying to my post?

Post menu > **Edit reply settings** > **Off**. See [Who can reply](/help/posts-and-feed/reply-controls).

## A post I replied to keeps notifying me. How do I stop it?

Open its menu and tap **Turn off notifications for this post**.

## How are views counted? Can I see who viewed my post?

Each time a post appears on someone else's screen counts as a view, including repeats. You only see totals, never names. Tap **Views** on your own post for analytics. See [About views](/help/posts-and-feed/views-and-analytics).

## Does Nantty pay for views?

No. Creators earn a share of eligible monetisation revenue. See [Creator earnings](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-earnings).

## Why am I seeing this post?

Your feed mixes accounts you follow, topics you engage with and trends. In Explore, tap **Why am I seeing this?** under a post. See [How your feed works](/help/posts-and-feed/how-your-feed-works).

## How do I see fewer posts like this?

Post menu > **Not interested** or **Hide this post**.

## Where are my saved posts?

Profile > three-dot menu > **Saved**. See [Saving posts](/help/posts-and-feed/bookmarks-and-saved).

## Can someone pay to trend or go viral?

No. Ads are always labelled, and trends need posts from many different accounts. See [Explore and trends](/help/posts-and-feed/explore-and-trends).
