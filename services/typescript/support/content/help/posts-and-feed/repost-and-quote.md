A repost shares someone's post with your followers. A quote does the same but adds your own comment on top, like a quote on X.

## Who this is for

Anyone signed in. If you are browsing as a guest, Zudomart asks you to sign in first and then brings you back to the post.

## How to repost or quote

1. Tap the **Repost** button under the post (the two arrows).
2. Choose an option:
   - **Reshare** posts it to your followers straight away, with no text.
   - **Quote** opens a box that says "Add a comment". Write your text and tap **Repost**.
3. Your repost or quote appears on your profile under the **Reposts** tab and in your followers' feeds.

## Quote by pasting a link

You don't have to use the button. Copy a post's link (post menu > **Copy link**) and paste it into a new post or a comment. When the link points to a Zudomart post or clip, it becomes a quote card showing the original. If the link is at the end of your text, we hide the raw link and show only the card. See [Link and quote cards](/help/posts-and-feed/link-cards).

A link in a comment counts as a quote too: the original post's quote count goes up and its author is notified.

## See who quoted a post

When a post has quotes, a **View quotes** link with the number appears under it. Tap it to see every quote. Quotes from private accounts only show to people allowed to see them.

## Good to know

- Stories can't be reposted or quoted.
- Posts for subscribers only can't be reshared, except by their author.
- If the original post is deleted or becomes private, your quote stays but the card shows "This post is unavailable."
- Reposts and quotes count in the original post's analytics. See [Post analytics](/help/posts-and-feed/post-analytics).

## If something goes wrong

- **"We couldn't reshare that. Please try again."** The reshare didn't go through. Check your connection and try again.
- **"This post is unavailable, so it can't be quoted."** The post was removed, or you can no longer see it.
- **"Stories can't be reshared."** Stories are only for the 24 hours they're up and can't be reposted.
- **"Subscriber-only posts can't be reshared."** The creator made the post for paying subscribers only.
