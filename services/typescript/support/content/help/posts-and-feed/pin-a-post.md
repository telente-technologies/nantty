Pinned posts sit at the top of your profile with a **Pinned** badge, above everything else you've posted. Use them for your best work, a product launch or important news.

## Who this is for

Everyone with a profile. You can only pin your own posts, and business profiles can pin their own posts too.

## How to pin a post

1. Find the post in your feed, on your profile or on its own page.
2. Tap the three-dot menu on the post.
3. Tap **Pin to profile**.

From your own profile you can also tap the menu on a post and choose **Pin to profile** there. The same menu has **Promote this post** and **View post**.

## How to unpin

Open the same menu and tap **Unpin from profile**. The post goes back to its normal place on your profile, by date.

## Good to know

- You can pin up to 3 posts. To pin a fourth, unpin one first.
- Pinning doesn't change who can see the post. A followers-only post stays followers-only.
- Stories can't be pinned.
- Pinning doesn't notify your followers or push the post in anyone's feed.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Pin limit reached"**: "You can pin up to 3 posts on your profile. Unpin one to pin another."
- **"Couldn't update pin"**: "Please check your connection and try again."
- **"Stories can't be pinned to your profile."** Only posts and clips can be pinned.
- **"You can only pin your own posts."** You can't pin someone else's post. Save it instead from the bookmark button.
