Every post and clip shows a **Views** count. It tells you how many times the post was seen.

## What tapping Views does

- **On someone else's post or clip**, a short **Views** sheet explains: "Times this post was seen. A view counts each time it appears on someone else's screen, including repeat views. The author's own views don't count." Tap **Learn more** to open this page.
- **On your own post or clip**, tapping **Views** opens **Post analytics**, with impressions, unique viewers, engagement and more. See [Post and clip analytics](/help/posts-and-feed/post-analytics).

## What counts as a view

A view is counted each time a post or clip appears on someone's screen in Nantty: in a feed, on a profile, in search results, in a list of quotes, or when the post is opened from a link.

- Repeat views by the same person count.
- Views by people who aren't signed in count.
- Views from a promotion or boost count.
- Watching only part of a video still counts as a view. Analytics show separately how many people watched to the end.

## What doesn't count

- The author's own views never count.
- Traffic we detect as automated or fake is left out.
- Views while a post is hidden by moderation don't count.

## Who sees what

Everyone sees the total number of views on a post. Only the author can open post analytics. On a business or brand profile, the account that runs the profile can see them.

## Privacy

Views are counted anonymously. Authors never see who viewed a post or clip, only totals. Stories are the one exception: the author can see a list of who viewed each story. See [Story viewers and privacy](/help/clips-and-stories/story-viewers-and-privacy).

If you don't want your activity used to personalise your feed, turn off **Personalization** in **Settings > Privacy & Data**.

## Good to know

Nantty doesn't pay for views. Creators earn a share of eligible monetisation revenue instead. See [Creator earnings](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-earnings).
