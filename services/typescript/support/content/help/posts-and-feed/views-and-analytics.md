A view is counted each time a post or clip appears on someone's screen in Zudomart: in a feed, on a profile, in search results, in a list of quotes, or when the post is opened from a link.

## What counts as a view

Every appearance counts, including repeat views by the same person and views by people who aren't signed in. Views from a promotion count too. Watching only part of a video still counts.

## What doesn't count

The author's own views never count. We also leave out traffic we detect as automated, and views of posts while they are hidden by moderation.

## Who sees what

Everyone sees the total number of views on a post. Only the author can open post analytics, which show impressions by day, unique viewers, profile visits and engagement.

## Privacy

Views are counted anonymously. Authors never see who viewed their post, only totals. If you don't want your activity used to personalise your feed, turn off personalisation under Settings > Privacy & Data.
