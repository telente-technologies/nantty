When you open a post, the conversation under it is arranged so the most useful replies come first, much like on X. This page explains the order and the labels you'll see.

## The order of a conversation

With the default **Relevant** sort, top-level comments are shown in this order:

1. **The pinned comment**, marked **Pinned by author**. See [Pinning a comment](/help/posts-and-feed/pin-a-comment).
2. **Comments by the post's author.**
3. **Threads the author joined**, meaning comments the author replied to.
4. **Everything else**, ranked by likes and replies, with newer comments getting a head start that fades over time.

You can change the sort with the dropdown above the replies: **Relevant**, **Latest** or **Most liked**. The pinned comment always stays on top.

## The Author label

Any comment or reply written by the person who made the post shows a small **Author** label next to their name. It makes it easy to spot answers from the poster in a busy conversation.

## Threads shown inline

You don't have to tap into every comment to follow a back-and-forth. Replies under a comment are shown right there, up to three levels deep. When the author has replied in a thread, their reply is preferred in that preview. Each reply shows "Replying to" and the name it answers when that isn't obvious.

When a conversation goes deeper or has more replies, you'll see **View thread**. Tap it to open the full thread on its own page. Tap any reply to open its thread too.

## Quotes in comments

If you paste a link to a Zudomart post in a comment, it shows as a quote card inside your comment and counts as a quote of that post. See [Link and quote cards](/help/posts-and-feed/link-cards).

## Good to know

- Comments can be 1 to 1,500 characters.
- The order is saved while you scroll, so new comments don't make the list jump. Pull down to refresh.
- Ads that appear in a conversation always carry a label such as **Promoted** or **Sponsored**.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Comments need 1 to 1500 characters."** Your comment is empty or too long.
- **"This post was deleted, so your comment wasn't posted."** The post was removed while you were writing.
- **"Subscribe to this creator to see the conversation."** The post is for subscribers only.
