A story is a quick photo or video that stays up for 24 hours and then disappears. Use stories for everyday moments that don't need to stay on your profile.

## Post a story

Stories are created in the mobile app.

1. Tap **+** and choose **STORY** on the mode pill at the bottom.
2. Choose **Choose from library**, **Take story photo** or **Record a video**.
3. Add a sound or a colour look if you like.
4. Post it.

Each story is one photo or one video. To share several, post several stories.

On the web (app.zudomart.com) you can watch stories, but the page says "Story creation with camera upload is available in the mobile app."

## Watch stories

- Stories from people you follow show at the top of the home feed as rings around profile pictures.
- Tap a ring to start watching. Photos show for 5 seconds; videos play to the end.
- Tap the right side of the screen for the next story and the left side for the previous one. Drag down to close.
- When someone you follow is live, their ring shows a **LIVE** badge. A **LIVE · Watch** pill on their story opens the live.

## Reply to a story

Type in **Send a message...** at the bottom of someone's story. Your reply goes to your chat with them, end-to-end encrypted, with a small card of the story. See [Story replies](/help/clips-and-stories/story-replies-and-reactions).

## Good to know

- Stories are public: anyone who can see your profile can see them. See [Story viewers and privacy](/help/clips-and-stories/story-viewers-and-privacy).
- You can see who viewed your story from **Story analytics**.
- Stories can't be reposted, quoted, pinned or downloaded, and can't tag products or people.
- After 24 hours a story disappears from the tray and your profile.

## If something goes wrong

- **"A story needs exactly one photo or video."** Pick a single item.
- **"No stories right now"**: "Stories disappear after 24 hours." Nobody you follow has a story up.
- **"This story isn't available."** It has expired or was removed.
