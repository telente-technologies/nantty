---
title: Stories
summary: Stories are photos and short videos that disappear after 24 hours.
tags: stories, 24 hours
related: create-a-post, going-live
---

- Tap **+** and choose **Story** to share a photo or video.
- Stories show at the top of the home feed as rings around profile pictures.
- They disappear after 24 hours.
- When someone you follow is live, their story ring and profile picture show a **LIVE** badge. Tap it to join.
