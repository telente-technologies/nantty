## Watch clips

Open the Clips tab and swipe up for the next clip. Each swipe snaps to exactly one full-screen video. Landscape videos show a **View full screen** button.

## Make a clip

1. Tap **+** and choose **Clip**.
2. Pick exactly one video, or record one.
3. Add a sound, caption and audience, then post.

Clip comments open in a panel over the video. Ads in Clips only appear between clips, never on top of one.
