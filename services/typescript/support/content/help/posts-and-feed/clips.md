Clips are short videos that fill the screen, one per swipe. Each clip can have a sound, a caption and its own comments.

## Watch clips

1. Open the **Clips** tab.
2. Pick **For You** or **Following** at the top.
3. Swipe up for the next clip and down for the previous one.

The first time, a **How clips work** card shows the gestures. Tap **Got it** to close it. See [Clip player controls](/help/clips-and-stories/clip-player-controls) for tapping, double-tapping, the seek bar and 2× speed.

Landscape videos show a **View full screen** button. See [Landscape videos](/help/clips-and-stories/landscape-videos).

## What's on a clip

- The creator's name and a **Follow** button if you don't follow them yet.
- "Friends liked this" when people you know liked it.
- The caption, with the sound underneath. Tap the sound to see every clip using it.
- On the right: **Like**, **Comments**, **Share**, save, **Views**, **Tip** for creators, and **More options**.

## Make a clip

1. Tap **+** and choose **CLIP**.
2. Record or choose a video up to 3 minutes, or make a photo clip from up to 10 photos.
3. Trim it, add a caption and a sound, choose your settings and post.

Full steps: [Create a clip](/help/clips-and-stories/create-a-clip).

## Good to know

- Clip comments open in a panel over the video.
- Ads in Clips only appear between clips, never on top of one.
- Tapping **Views** on your own clip opens its analytics. See [Post analytics](/help/posts-and-feed/post-analytics).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Couldn't load clips."** Pull down or try again.
- **"A clip needs exactly one video."** Use one video, or make a photo clip instead.
