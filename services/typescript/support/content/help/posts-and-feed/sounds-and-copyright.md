---
title: Sounds and copyright
summary: Sounds belong to the person who uploaded them. Others can reuse them with credit, and copyright owners can claim their work.
tags: sound, music, copyright, claim
related: clips, report-a-problem
---

- When you post a video with original audio, you own that sound. Others can reuse it in their clips and your name is credited on the sound page.
- Tap a sound on any clip to see every clip that uses it.
- If your copyrighted work is used without permission, file a claim from the post menu > Report > Copyright. Claimed content can be limited while we review it, and the uploader can send a counter notice.
