Every clip has a sound: either a sound picked from Zudomart or the clip's own audio, shown as **Original sound**. Sounds can be reused, so a trend can spread while the creator stays credited.

## Use a sound

1. On a clip, tap the sound row under the caption.
2. The **Sound** page shows every clip using it.
3. Tap **Use this sound** to start a new clip with it, or **Save sound** to keep it for later.

You can also add a sound while making a clip. The picker, **Add sound**, has **Trending** and **Saved** tabs and a "Search sounds" box. See [Create a clip](/help/clips-and-stories/create-a-clip). Sounds can be used on clips and stories, not on regular posts.

## Your own sounds

When you post a clip with its own audio, that sound is yours and your name is credited on the sound page. Open your sound's page to:

- **Rename** it ("Name your sound").
- Turn **Allow others to use this sound** on or off.

If reuse is off, others get "This sound can't be used."

## Copyright

Only use music and audio you have the right to use.

If your copyrighted work is used without permission:

1. Open the post or clip menu (three dots).
2. Tap **Report copyright**.
3. Follow the steps to tell us what you own.

You can also tap **Report this sound** on a sound's page. While a claim is being reviewed, a clip can show "This sound is unavailable while under copyright review." The uploader can send a counter notice if they believe the claim is wrong. Repeated copyright problems affect account status. See [Copyright and account status](/help/safety-and-reporting/copyright-and-account-status).

## If something goes wrong

- **"This sound isn't available right now."** It was removed or is under review.
- **"Sound not found."** The sound was deleted.
- **"This sound can't be used."** The owner turned off reuse, or it's under review.
- **"Only clips and stories can use a sound."** Regular posts can't carry a picked sound.
