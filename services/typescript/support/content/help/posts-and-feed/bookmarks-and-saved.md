---
title: Saving posts, clips and products
summary: Save anything to find it later in Saved. Only you can see what you saved.
tags: save, bookmark, saved
related: create-a-post, clips
---

Tap the bookmark icon on a post, clip, video or product to save it. Tap again to unsave.

Find everything in **Saved** from your profile, filtered by type. Saves are private: people only see how many times a post was saved, never who saved it.
