Saving is a private way to keep something for later, whether it's a recipe clip, a product you might buy or a reply you want to come back to.

## What you can save

- Posts, including photos and videos
- Clips
- Replies in a conversation
- Products in the Mart

## How to save something

1. Tap the bookmark button. On a post it's in the row under the post. On a clip it's on the right side. On a reply it's in the reply's action row.
2. The bookmark fills in to show it's saved.
3. Tap it again to remove it from Saved.

If you're browsing as a guest, Nantty asks you to sign in first and then saves the item.

## Find your saved items

**On the app:** go to your profile, tap the three-dot menu (**More**) at the top and choose **Saved**.

**On the web (app.nantty.com):** open your profile and tap the **Saved items** icon.

The **Saved** screen has filters along the top: **All**, **Posts**, **Videos**, **Clips**, **Products** and **Replies**. Tap any item to open it. Pull down to refresh.

## Good to know

- Saves are private. People can see how many times their post was saved in their own analytics, but never who saved it.
- If a saved post is deleted or made private, it no longer opens for you.
- Saving a product isn't the same as adding it to your cart. Prices and stock can change, so check before you buy.
- Saving doesn't download anything. To keep a copy of a photo or video on your phone, use **Download** in the post menu, if the author allows it.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Couldn't load your saved items."** Tap retry or pull down to refresh.
- **"Nothing saved yet"** (or "No saved posts", "No saved clips" and so on). There's nothing saved under that filter. Try **All**.
