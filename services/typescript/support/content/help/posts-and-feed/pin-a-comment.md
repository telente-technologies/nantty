If you wrote the post or clip, you can pin one comment so it always shows first. It gets a **Pinned by author** badge so everyone knows you chose it. Use it to answer a common question, highlight a great reply or point people to a link.

## Who this is for

Only the author of the post or clip. You can pin anyone's comment, including your own.

## How to pin a comment

**On a post:**

1. Open the post to see the conversation.
2. Press and hold the comment.
3. Tap **Pin reply**.

**On a clip:**

1. Tap **Comments** on the clip.
2. Press and hold the comment.
3. Tap **Pin comment**.

To remove it, press and hold again and tap **Unpin reply** or **Unpin comment**.

## Good to know

- Only one comment can be pinned at a time. Pinning a new one unpins the old one automatically.
- Only top-level comments can be pinned, not replies inside a thread.
- If the pinned comment is deleted, nothing stays pinned until you pin another.
- The pinned comment shows first whatever sort order a viewer chooses. See [Comment order and the Author label](/help/posts-and-feed/comment-order-and-author-label).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Only the post author can pin a comment."** Only the person who posted can pin.
- **"Only top-level comments can be pinned."** Pin the comment that started the thread instead.
- **"Could not pin comment"** or **"Could not unpin comment"**: check your connection and try again.
