---
title: AI-generated content
summary: Switch on AI generated when your post was made with AI, so it carries a label.
tags: ai, label, generated
related: create-a-post, creator-earnings
---

If a photo, video or sound was created or heavily changed with AI, turn on **AI generated** in the composer. The post and clip then show an AI label.

Label honestly: misleading AI content can be removed under our Community Guidelines. Monetisation for AI-generated content uses a lower revenue share than original content.
