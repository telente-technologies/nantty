If a photo, video or sound was created or heavily changed with AI, Nantty asks you to say so. The post then carries an **AI generated** label so viewers know what they're looking at.

## How to label a post or clip

1. Create your post or clip as usual.
2. In the settings step, turn on **AI generated**: "Turn on if this was made or heavily edited with AI. Viewers will see a label."
3. Post it.

The **AI generated** label shows on the post in feeds and on the clip, above the caption.

## When to use it

Turn it on when AI made or heavily changed what people see or hear, for example:

- an image made with an AI image tool,
- a video where someone's face or voice was generated or swapped,
- a voice-over made with an AI voice.

You don't need it for normal edits like filters, cropping, trimming or brightness.

## Why it matters

- Label honestly. Misleading AI content, such as fake videos of real people or fake news, can be removed under our [Community Guidelines](/help/safety-and-reporting/community-guidelines), even if it is labelled.
- Monetisation for AI-generated content uses a lower revenue share than original content. See [Creator earnings](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-earnings).

## If you see unlabelled AI content

If you think a post is AI-generated and misleading, open the post menu and tap **Report**. See [Report a problem](/help/safety-and-reporting/report-a-problem).
