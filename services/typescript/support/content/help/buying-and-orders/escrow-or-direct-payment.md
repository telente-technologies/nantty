At checkout in Nigeria you may see two ways to pay a store. You choose for each seller in your cart.

## Who this is for

Buyers in Nigeria. Outside Nigeria there is no escrow; every order is a direct payment.

## The two options

**Escrow protection (recommended)**: "Money is held until you confirm delivery."
- Your payment stays locked in your own wallet with our payment partner.
- The seller is paid when you tap **Confirm receipt**, or 3 days after delivery if you raise no problem.
- You can open a dispute before release, and our team can refund you.

**Pay directly**: "The seller is paid right away."
- The money goes to the seller as soon as you pay.
- There is no escrow and no dispute button. For a problem, contact the seller or support, but we can't guarantee a refund.
- The order completes when you tap **Confirm receipt**, or automatically 3 days after delivery unless you ask for a return or refund first.

## How to choose

1. In checkout, find **How to pay each seller**.
2. For each verified store, tap **Escrow protection (recommended)** or **Pay directly**.
3. Stores that aren't verified show **Protected by Zudomart Escrow**. That's their only option.

The note at the bottom sums it up: "Escrow orders are held until you confirm delivery. Direct orders pay the seller right away."

## Good to know

- Only verified stores (Gold, "Verified business") can offer direct payment.
- Both options cost you the same. Buyers never pay the escrow fee.
- The store line shows its success rate and rating, or **New store**, to help you decide.
- If you're unsure, choose escrow. It's the only option where we can step in.

## If something goes wrong

- **"This store is not verified, so its orders can only be paid through escrow."** Choose escrow for that store.

## Stay safe

A seller who pushes you to pay directly, or to pay outside Zudomart altogether, may be trying to avoid escrow. Pick escrow, and report pressure like this. See [Report a scam](/help/safety-and-reporting/report-a-scam).
