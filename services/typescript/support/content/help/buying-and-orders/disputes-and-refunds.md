## Open a dispute

1. Open the order and tap **Open a dispute** before escrow releases.
2. Choose what went wrong and add photos and a description.
3. The seller has 48 hours to respond. If you can't agree, our team reviews it and decides.

The payment stays frozen in escrow while the dispute is open.

## Refunds

- Approved refunds go back to the account you paid from. They may be full or partial.
- Card refunds can take up to 10 business days to appear, depending on your bank.
- For some bank transfer payments we may ask you to enter your bank account in the app to receive the refund. We never ask for this by email.
- We notify you when a refund is sent.

If a refund hasn't arrived after 10 business days, [contact support](/help/contact-support/contact-support) with the order attached.
