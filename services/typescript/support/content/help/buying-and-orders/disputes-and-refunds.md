If something is wrong with an escrow order, open a dispute. While it is open, the seller can't receive the money.

## Who this is for

Buyers who paid with escrow in Nigeria. For direct payments and orders outside Nigeria, see the last section.

## How to open a dispute

1. Open your profile menu, tap **My orders**, open the order and tap **Open a dispute**. You can do this while the order is paid, shipped or delivered, before escrow releases.
2. Choose what went wrong: **I didn't receive my order**, **Item isn't as described**, **Item arrived damaged**, **I received the wrong item** or **Something else**.
3. Under **Describe the problem**, write at least 20 characters about what happened.
4. Add photos. They are required for damaged, wrong or not-as-described items. You can add up to 5.
5. Tap **Submit dispute**.

The app tells you: "The seller can't receive the money until the dispute is resolved. They have 48 hours to respond, then Nantty support steps in."

## What happens next

- Your dispute moves through **Opened**, **Waiting for seller**, **Under review**, then a result.
- You and the seller can message support from the dispute.
- Our team looks at the evidence from both sides and decides: a full refund, a partial refund, or release to the seller.
- You get a notification at each step.

## Refunds

- With escrow, a refund before release simply unlocks the money back to you.
- Where money has to go back to your card or bank, card refunds can take up to 10 business days to show, depending on your bank. See [Refund timelines](/help/buying-and-orders/refund-timelines).
- A seller can also refund you directly, in full or in part.
- We never ask for your bank details or PIN by email.

## If something goes wrong

- **"This order can no longer be disputed."** Escrow has already released (you confirmed, or 3 days passed after delivery). [Contact support](/help/contact-support/contact-support) with the order attached.
- **"This dispute is closed."** A decision has been made. Reply to support from the dispute if you have new evidence.

## Direct payments and orders outside Nigeria

Escrow and buyer protection are only in Nigeria. With **Pay directly**, and everywhere outside Nigeria, the seller is paid right away and you buy at your own risk. For a refund, contact the seller or support from the order. We can ask the seller to refund you, restrict or close their store, and pass the case to our payment partner, but we can't reverse a payment or guarantee a refund. See [Buying outside Nigeria](/help/buying-and-orders/buying-outside-nigeria) and our Terms on nantty.com/terms.

## Stay safe

Keep all chat and payment inside Nantty. Never agree to "cancel the dispute and I'll pay you back outside the app".
