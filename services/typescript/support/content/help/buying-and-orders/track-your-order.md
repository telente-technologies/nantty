title: Tracking your orders
summary: See every order's status, tracking and history in Orders.

Open **Orders** from your profile to see current and past orders.

Statuses: Placed, Paid, Accepted by seller, Shipped, Delivered, Completed, or Cancelled. Each order shows the shipping destination, the rule used and the fee, plus tracking when the seller adds it. You get notifications at each step.

Tap **Get help** on an order to contact support with the order already attached.
