Your saved addresses make checkout quick and help stores work out the right shipping fee.

## Who this is for

All buyers.

## How to add an address in the app

1. Go to **Settings > Shipping Address**.
2. Tap **Add new address**.
3. Fill in **Recipient name** and **Phone number**, then pick the country, state, local government area and city from the lists.
4. Add the **Street address**, and if you like a **Postal code (optional)** and **Delivery details (optional)**, such as a landmark or gate colour.
5. Turn on **Use as default address** if you want it picked first at checkout.
6. Tap **Save**.

To change or remove one, open it and edit, or tap **Delete address**.

## On the web

At app.nantty.com go to **Settings > Addresses**. Use **Add address**, **Edit**, **Delete** and **Make this my default address**. Your default shows a **Default** tag.

## Good to know

- You can save up to 20 addresses.
- Your first address becomes your default automatically.
- The phone number must include the country code, starting with a plus sign (for Nigeria, +234).
- The state and local government area decide which shipping rule a store uses, so choose them carefully.
- You can also pick or add an address during checkout.

## If something goes wrong

- **"You can save up to 20 addresses. Remove one to add another."** Delete one you no longer use.
- A phone number error: type it in international format, with the plus sign and country code.
- A postal code error: check the format for that country, or leave it empty if it's optional.

## Stay safe

Stores you buy from see your delivery address and phone number so they can deliver. Never post them in public comments or reviews.
