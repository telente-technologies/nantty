Some stores open before they finish business verification. You can still buy from them safely, because every order is protected by escrow.

## Who this is for

Buyers in Nigeria. Outside Nigeria, sellers can't sell at all until they are verified.

## How to tell if a store is verified

- Verified stores show the **Gold** checkmark, labelled **Verified business**.
- Stores that aren't verified have no badge. At checkout they show **Protected by Nantty Escrow**, and a brand-new store shows **New store** instead of a rating.

## What's different when you buy

- **Escrow only.** You can't pay these stores directly. The money stays locked in your own wallet until you confirm delivery.
- **Sales limit.** Until the seller finishes verification, the store can only take a limited total in sales. When it reaches that limit, it can't take new orders for a while.
- **Same protection.** You can open a dispute before release exactly as with any escrow order.

## Good to know

- An unverified store isn't a bad store. Many are new sellers who are still uploading documents.
- Check the success rate, rating and reviews on the store line at checkout.
- Seller earnings from these orders can't be withdrawn until they verify, so they have a reason to finish.

## If something goes wrong

- **"This store has reached its sales limit while the seller's business verification is pending. Remove its items or try again later."** Remove that store's items to check out the rest, or come back later.
- **"This store is not verified, so its orders can only be paid through escrow."** Choose escrow for that store.

## Stay safe

Never agree to pay an unverified store outside Nantty "to skip the limit". That removes your escrow protection. Report the store instead.
