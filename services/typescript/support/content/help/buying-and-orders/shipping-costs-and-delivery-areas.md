Nantty doesn't deliver orders itself. Each store chooses where it ships and how much it charges, and the fee is worked out from your address.

## Who this is for

All buyers.

## How shipping is worked out

1. You pick a delivery address with a country, state and local government area.
2. For each store in your cart, we find the store's rule that matches your address most closely: your local government area first, then your state, then your country.
3. The store charges that rule's fee once for everything you buy from it, or ships free.
4. If you spend enough to reach the store's free-shipping amount, shipping from that store is free.

## What you see

- "Enter your delivery address to calculate shipping" until you choose an address.
- "Add ₦X more for free shipping" when you're close to a store's free-shipping amount.
- "Free shipping on orders above ₦X" or **FREE** when it applies.
- "This seller does not currently ship to your selected location" if a store doesn't deliver to you.

## Good to know

- Shipping is charged per store, so buying several items from one store costs one fee.
- We recalculate shipping at checkout, so the price you pay matches the store's rules.
- Delivery time depends on the store and courier. When the seller ships, they add the courier, tracking number and sometimes an "Expected by" date.
- If none of the stores ship to your address, checkout stops with: "None of the sellers in your cart ship to that address."

## If something goes wrong

- A store you want doesn't ship to you: message the store to ask if they can add your area.
- Shipping looks wrong: check your address in [Saved addresses](/help/buying-and-orders/saved-addresses), especially the state and local government area.
