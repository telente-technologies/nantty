Confirming delivery tells us your order arrived safely. It releases the escrow payment to the seller.

## Who this is for

Buyers who paid with escrow in Nigeria. Direct payments have already gone to the seller, so there is nothing to confirm.

## How to confirm delivery

1. Open your profile menu, tap **My orders** and find the order under **Delivered — please confirm**.
2. Check every item before you do anything else.
3. Tap **Confirm receipt** (on the web, **Confirm delivery**).
4. Read the message: "Only confirm if everything arrived as described. The seller will be paid right away and this can't be undone."
5. Tap **Yes, release payment**, or **Not yet** to go back.

## Good to know

- You can only confirm once the order is marked delivered.
- If you neither confirm nor open a dispute, escrow releases automatically **3 days after delivery**.
- Once you confirm, the money leaves your wallet for the seller's and you can no longer open a dispute about the order.
- After confirming, you can rate your purchase. See [Leaving a review](/help/buying-and-orders/leave-a-review).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Only delivered orders can be confirmed."** The seller hasn't marked it delivered yet. Wait, or contact the seller from the order.
- The item is damaged, wrong or missing: don't confirm. Tap **Open a dispute** instead. See [Disputes and refunds](/help/buying-and-orders/disputes-and-refunds).

## Stay safe

Never confirm because a seller or delivery rider asks you to before you've checked the parcel. Zudomart will never ask you to confirm delivery to "unlock" anything.
