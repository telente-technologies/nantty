---
title: Confirming delivery
summary: Tap Confirm delivery when your order arrives in good condition so the seller gets paid.
tags: confirm delivery, received, escrow release
related: how-escrow-protects-your-order, disputes-and-refunds
---

Open the order and tap **Confirm delivery** after checking everything.

- Only confirm when you have the item and it's as described.
- Once confirmed, escrow releases the payment to the seller and you can't open a dispute about it.
- If you don't confirm, escrow releases automatically 3 days after delivery.
- You can leave a review for the product and store.
