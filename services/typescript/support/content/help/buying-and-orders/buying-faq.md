## Is it safe to buy on Zudomart?

In Nigeria, escrow keeps your money locked in your own wallet with our payment partner until you confirm delivery. See [How escrow protects your order](/help/buying-and-orders/how-escrow-protects-your-order).

## Does Zudomart hold my money?

No. Escrow money stays in your own wallet with our licensed payment partner until it is released. See [Who holds your money](/help/wallet-security/who-holds-your-money).

## Do I pay any fees as a buyer?

No checkout fee and no escrow fee. You pay the item price, any shipping and nothing else.

## Should I choose escrow or Pay directly?

Choose escrow unless you trust the store. Pay directly sends the money at once with no dispute option. See [Escrow or direct payment](/help/buying-and-orders/escrow-or-direct-payment).

## How do I pay?

Through the on-screen checkout from our payment partner, by card, bank transfer or USSD. See [Cart, shipping and checkout](/help/buying-and-orders/cart-and-checkout).

## Why is shipping charged for each store?

Each store ships its own items and sets its own fees. You pay one fee per store, never per item. See [Shipping costs and delivery areas](/help/buying-and-orders/shipping-costs-and-delivery-areas).

## How far is my order?

Open **My orders** and tap the order. The timeline shows each step, plus courier and tracking once shipped. See [Tracking your orders](/help/buying-and-orders/track-your-order).

## Can I cancel?

Only until the seller starts preparing it. See [Cancelling an order](/help/buying-and-orders/cancel-an-order).

## My order didn't arrive, or it's wrong. What do I do?

Don't confirm. Open a dispute before escrow releases, 3 days after delivery. See [Disputes and refunds](/help/buying-and-orders/disputes-and-refunds).

## What if I forget to confirm delivery?

Escrow releases to the seller automatically 3 days after delivery. See [Confirming delivery](/help/buying-and-orders/confirm-delivery).

## How long does a refund take?

Escrow money unlocks straight back to you. Card refunds can take up to 10 business days. See [How long refunds take](/help/buying-and-orders/refund-timelines).

## Can I buy from a store without a checkmark?

Yes, with escrow only. See [Buying from stores that aren't verified yet](/help/buying-and-orders/buying-from-unverified-stores).

## I live outside Nigeria. Am I protected?

There's no escrow outside Nigeria; you pay sellers directly at your own risk. See [Buying outside Nigeria](/help/buying-and-orders/buying-outside-nigeria).

## When can I leave a review?

Once your order is delivered. See [Leaving a review](/help/buying-and-orders/leave-a-review).

## A seller asked me to pay outside the app. Should I?

No. You lose escrow protection. Report them. See [Stay safe when buying and selling](/help/safety-and-reporting/stay-safe-when-buying-and-selling).
