Escrow means the seller doesn't get your money until you have your order. Nantty never holds your money: it stays locked in your own wallet with our licensed payment partner.

## Who this is for

Buyers in Nigeria. Escrow isn't available outside Nigeria. See [Buying outside Nigeria](/help/buying-and-orders/buying-outside-nigeria).

## How it works

1. You pay at checkout with **Escrow protection (recommended)**. You get "Payment confirmed" and your order shows **Paid into escrow**.
2. The seller prepares and ships your order.
3. When it arrives, check it and tap **Confirm receipt**. The money then moves to the seller.
4. If you do nothing, escrow releases automatically **3 days after delivery**.
5. If something is wrong, [open a dispute](/help/buying-and-orders/disputes-and-refunds) before then. The release is paused until the dispute is settled.

## Good to know

- Nantty's intended launch policy has no sales commission. Any applicable payment-provider charge is shown in the checkout quote before you confirm.
- If your order is cancelled or refunded before release, the money is unlocked back to you.
- Stores that aren't verified can only take escrow payments. Verified stores (Gold) can also offer **Pay directly**, which has no escrow protection. See [Escrow or direct payment](/help/buying-and-orders/escrow-or-direct-payment).
- After release, the seller's money sits in a short safety hold before they can withdraw it.

## Stay safe

Escrow only protects payments made through Nantty checkout. If a seller asks you to pay outside the app, you lose that protection. Never confirm delivery before you have checked the item.
