---
title: How escrow protects your order
summary: Your payment is held until you confirm delivery, or released automatically 3 days after delivery if you don't raise a problem.

When you pay with escrow, the seller doesn't get your money straight away.

1. You pay. The money is held in escrow.
2. The seller ships your order.
3. When it arrives, check it and tap **Confirm delivery**. The seller is then paid.
4. If you do nothing, escrow releases the payment automatically **3 days after delivery**.

If something is wrong, [open a dispute](/help/buying-and-orders/disputes-and-refunds) before the 3 days pass. A dispute pauses the timer until it's settled.

Stores that aren't verified can only take escrow payments. Verified stores (Gold checkmark) can also offer Pay directly, which doesn't have escrow protection.
