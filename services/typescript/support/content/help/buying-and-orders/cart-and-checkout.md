## Your cart

Items are grouped by store. Each store shows its items, discounts, shipping and total, and you see the grand total before you pay.

- Shipping is charged once per store, never per item.
- You may see "Add ₦X more for free shipping" when a store offers it.
- A store that doesn't ship to your address is left out of the total until you change the address.
- You only see products from stores that ship to your region. Prices show in your currency, marked approximate when converted from the seller's currency.

## Addresses

Addresses are picked from lists (country, state or region, local government area and city) so shipping is calculated correctly. Save several addresses in your address book.

## Checkout

1. Choose your address.
2. For verified stores you can choose **Escrow** or **Pay directly**. Other stores always use escrow.
3. Pay once for the whole cart by card, bank transfer or USSD.

There's no checkout fee and no escrow fee for buyers. Receipts go to your verified account email.
