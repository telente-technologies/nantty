Your cart can hold items from many stores. You check out once, but each store packs and ships its own part of the order.

## Who this is for

Buyers everywhere. Escrow protection is only for orders paid in Nigeria. Outside Nigeria you pay sellers directly. See [Buying outside Nigeria](/help/buying-and-orders/buying-outside-nigeria).

## Your cart

Items are grouped by store. Each store shows **Subtotal**, **Discounts**, **Shipping** and **Seller total**, and the bottom shows **Order total**, **Items**, any **Store credit** and **Total**.

- Shipping is charged once per store, never per item.
- You may see "Add ₦X more for free shipping" or "Free shipping on orders above ₦X" when a store offers it.
- If a store doesn't deliver to your address, the cart says "This seller does not currently ship to your selected location" and leaves it out of the total.
- Tap **Proceed to Checkout** when you're ready.

## How to check out

1. Choose a delivery address, or add one. See [Saved addresses](/help/buying-and-orders/saved-addresses).
2. Under **How to pay each seller**, pick for each verified store: **Escrow protection (recommended)** ("Money is held until you confirm delivery.") or **Pay directly** ("The seller is paid right away."). Stores that aren't verified show **Protected by Nantty Escrow**, which is the only option.
3. Tap **Place order and pay** (on the web, **Pay now**).
4. Pay with **Pay from wallet** when your balance covers the order, or follow the on-screen checkout from our payment partner: **Card**, **Bank transfer** or **USSD**. You can save a card on your device for next time.

## Good to know

- Buyers pay no checkout fee and no escrow fee.
- With escrow, your money stays locked in your own wallet with our payment partner until you confirm delivery. See [How escrow protects your order](/help/buying-and-orders/how-escrow-protects-your-order).
- Your receipt goes to your account email.
- Items from stores that sell in different currencies must be bought in separate checkouts.
- On the web you need a verified email before you can pay.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Payment didn't go through"** "No money was taken. You can try again or choose another method." Tap **Open checkout again**. If you already paid, tap **I've paid, check status**.
- **"None of the sellers in your cart ship to that address."** Choose another address or remove those items.
- **"There is not enough stock for one of the items."** Lower the quantity or remove the item.
- **"You cannot buy from your own store."**
- **"This store has reached its sales limit while the seller's business verification is pending. Remove its items or try again later."** See [Buying from unverified stores](/help/buying-and-orders/buying-from-unverified-stores).
- **"This seller must finish identity verification before they can sell in your country. Remove their items to continue."**

More fixes: [Checkout problems](/help/troubleshooting/checkout-problems).

## Stay safe

Always pay inside Nantty. A seller who asks you to pay by bank transfer to their personal account outside checkout is taking you out of escrow protection. Report them.
