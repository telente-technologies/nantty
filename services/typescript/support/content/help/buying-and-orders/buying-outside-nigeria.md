You can buy on Zudomart from Ghana, Kenya, South Africa and other countries. The way you pay is different from Nigeria.

## Who this is for

Buyers paying in any currency other than naira.

## How it works

1. Add items and go to checkout as usual. Prices show in your currency. Where a seller hasn't set a price for your country, you see an approximate local amount.
2. Pay through the on-screen checkout from our payment partner.
3. Your payment goes to the seller right away. This is called direct payment.
4. The seller ships and adds tracking. Follow it in **My orders**.

## What's different from Nigeria

- **No escrow.** Zudomart doesn't hold or lock your money, and there is no stored wallet balance.
- **No dispute button.** If something goes wrong, contact the seller from the order or tap **Get help with this order**.
- **Every seller is verified.** Outside Nigeria nobody can sell until they have verified their identity and a payout account in their own name with our identity partner.

## Good to know

- You buy at your own risk. We can ask the seller to refund you, restrict or close their store, and pass your case to our payment partner, but we can't reverse a payment or guarantee a refund.
- Items from stores that sell in different currencies must be bought separately.
- Buyers in Nigeria who buy from a Nigerian store still get escrow.

## If something goes wrong

- **"This seller must finish identity verification before they can sell in your country. Remove their items to continue."** Remove those items and check out the rest.
- **"Items from stores that sell in different currencies must be bought separately."** Check out each currency on its own.
- **"This product is not available in your region."** The seller doesn't sell or ship it to your country.

## Stay safe

Look at the store's rating, success rate and reviews before you buy. Never pay a seller outside Zudomart checkout; then we can't help at all. See [Payments outside Nigeria](/help/payments-and-wallet/payments-outside-nigeria).
