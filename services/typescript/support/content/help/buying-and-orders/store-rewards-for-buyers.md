Store rewards are credit a seller gives you back on an order, to spend at their store next time. Each store funds its own rewards.

## Who this is for

Buyers who shop at stores that have turned rewards on.

## How you earn it

- The store sets a percentage of your order (up to 20%), and may set a minimum order and a maximum per order.
- Credit is added after your order completes and escrow is released.
- Each balance has its own expiry date, set by the store (between 7 days and 2 years).

## Where to see it

- In the app: **Wallet > Store rewards**.
- On the web: the **Store rewards** page.

Each balance shows the store and "Expires" with a date. The page reminds you: "Store credit is funded by each seller. It can only be used at that store, as a discount on a future order. It is not cash and cannot be withdrawn."

## How to use it

You don't need a code. When you check out with items from that store, your credit is applied automatically and shows as a **Store credit** line in your cart. It can cover almost all of the store's subtotal, but not shipping.

## Good to know

- Credit only works at the store that gave it.
- If an order that earned credit is refunded, that credit is taken back.
- If you used credit on an order that is fully refunded, the credit you used is given back for 180 days.
- Nantty doesn't run a platform-wide cashback scheme. Rewards come from individual stores.

## If something goes wrong

- Credit not showing? It only arrives once the order is completed and escrow released. Check the order under **Completed**.
- Credit disappeared? It may have expired, or the order was refunded.
