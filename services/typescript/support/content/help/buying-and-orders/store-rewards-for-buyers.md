---
title: Using store rewards
summary: Some stores give you credit back on orders. Spend it as a discount at that store.
tags: store rewards, credit, discount
related: store-rewards, cart-and-checkout
---

When a store has rewards turned on, you earn store credit after an order's escrow is released.

- See your balances in **Store rewards** in your wallet or on the store page.
- Credit applies as a discount at checkout, only at that store.
- It may expire and it's removed if the order is refunded.
- Store credit is never cash and can't be withdrawn.
