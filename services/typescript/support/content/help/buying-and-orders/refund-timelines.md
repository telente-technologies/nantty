When an order is cancelled or a dispute goes your way, you get a refund. How fast it reaches you depends on how the money has to travel.

## Who this is for

Buyers expecting money back from an order.

## When you get a refund

- You cancel before the seller starts preparing.
- The seller rejects or cancels your order (always a full refund).
- The seller refunds you in full or in part.
- Our team decides a dispute in your favour, fully or partly.

## How long it takes

- **Escrow, before release:** the money was never paid out, so it's unlocked in your own Zudomart wallet straight away. You can spend it or withdraw it to your bank.
- **Back to a card:** "Card refunds can take up to 10 business days", depending on your bank.
- **Back to a bank account:** usually faster than cards, but your bank sets the timing.

You get a **Refund issued** or **Partial refund issued** notification when we send it. It says where the money went:

- "The money is back in your Zudomart wallet." This is the usual case for escrow orders.
- "Your refund is on its way back to the card or account you paid with." This happens when the order is refunded before your card payment settled, and for direct-payment orders.

## Good to know

- One order can only have one partial refund. After that, any further refund is for the rest of the order.
- If you used store credit on an order that is fully refunded, the credit you used comes back for 180 days.
- Store credit you earned from a refunded order is taken back.
- Direct-payment orders (and every order outside Nigeria) aren't refunded by escrow. The seller has to refund you. See [Buying outside Nigeria](/help/buying-and-orders/buying-outside-nigeria).
- Check your history in [Transactions and receipts](/help/payments-and-wallet/transactions-and-receipts).

## If something goes wrong

- No refund after 10 business days? Check with your bank first using the date and amount from the notification. Then tap **Get help with this order** so support can trace it with the order attached.
- We never ask you to send money, your PIN or a one-time code to "release" a refund.

## Stay safe

Anyone who says they can "speed up your refund" for a fee is a scammer. Report them. See [Payment scams](/help/wallet-security/payment-scams).
