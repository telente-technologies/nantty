Each order shows a status badge and a timeline. Here's what each one means.

## Who this is for

All buyers.

## Status badges

- **Awaiting payment**: you started checkout but the payment hasn't arrived yet.
- **Preparing**: you've paid and the seller is getting your order ready. Escrow orders also show **Paid into escrow**.
- **On the way**: the seller has shipped it. Courier and tracking appear on the order.
- **Delivered**: the seller marked it delivered. Check it and confirm, or open a dispute. You have 3 days.
- **Completed**: you confirmed delivery, or 3 days passed. Escrow has released to the seller.
- **Disputed**: you opened a dispute. Release is paused.
- **Cancelled**: the order was cancelled, rejected or refunded.

## Where orders appear in My orders

The list groups orders under **Delivered — please confirm**, **On the way**, **Seller preparing**, **Awaiting payment**, **In dispute**, **Completed** and **Cancelled & refunded**.

## The escrow timeline

For escrow orders in Nigeria, the order page shows these steps:

1. **Paid into escrow**
2. **Seller preparing**
3. **Shipped**
4. **Delivered**
5. **Buyer confirms or auto-release**: "Buyer has 3 days after delivery to confirm or report a problem"
6. **Released to seller**

If something goes wrong you may also see **Dispute open — release paused** or **Refunded to buyer**.

Direct-payment orders show **Paid to seller** instead of **Paid into escrow**.

## Good to know

- On the web (app.zudomart.com) the same statuses appear in **Your orders** with simpler filters: **Pending**, **Paid**, **Shipped**, **Delivered**, **Completed**, **Cancelled**.
- You can cancel only while an order is awaiting payment or just paid. See [Cancelling an order](/help/buying-and-orders/cancel-an-order).
