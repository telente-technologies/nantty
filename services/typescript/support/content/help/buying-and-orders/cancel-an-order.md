Changed your mind? You can cancel an order yourself, but only before the seller starts preparing it.

## Who this is for

Buyers. Cancelling yourself is done on the web for now.

## How to cancel

1. On the web (app.nantty.com), open **Your orders** and the order.
2. Tap **Cancel order**. The page explains: "You can cancel until the seller starts preparing your order. After that, ask the seller or open a dispute."
3. Confirm in **Cancel this order?**: "The seller won't ship it. Any payment held in escrow is returned to you."
4. Tap **Cancel order** again, or **Keep order** to go back.

You'll see "Your order is cancelled. Any payment held in escrow is returned to you."

In the mobile app, use **Contact seller** or **Get help with this order** on the order if you need to cancel.

## When the seller cancels

A seller can reject a new order or cancel one they can't fulfil, with a reason shown to you. You get an **Order cancelled** notification and a full refund.

## Good to know

- Cancelling is only possible while the order is awaiting payment or newly paid. Once it shows **Preparing**, you can't cancel yourself.
- A cancelled escrow order unlocks the money back to you. See [Refund timelines](/help/buying-and-orders/refund-timelines).
- If payment arrives after an order is cancelled, a refund is requested automatically.

## If something goes wrong

- **"That order can no longer be cancelled."** The seller has started on it. Ask the seller, or wait for delivery and [open a dispute](/help/buying-and-orders/disputes-and-refunds) if there's a problem.
- **"This order was cancelled, so there is nothing to pay. Start a new checkout if you still want it."** Add the items again and check out.
