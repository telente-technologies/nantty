Your review helps other buyers decide and helps the seller improve. Only people who actually bought and received the product can review it.

## Who this is for

Buyers whose order is delivered or completed.

## How to leave a review

1. Open the order in **My orders**. After delivery you'll see **Rate your purchase**: "Your review helps other buyers and the seller." You can also start from the **Order completed** notification.
2. Under **How would you rate it?** tap a star: Poor, Fair, Good, Very good or Excellent.
3. Add a **Title (optional)** (up to 120 characters) and **Your review** (up to 2,000 characters).
4. Tap **Post review**. You'll see "Thanks for your review".

## Good to know

- Every review shows **Verified purchase**.
- You can review each product once. Reviews can't be edited, but you can delete yours with **Delete my review** and write a new one.
- Sellers can post a public reply, shown as **Reply from** the seller.
- Sellers can report reviews that break the rules, for example fake, abusive or off-topic ones. Honest negative reviews stay up.
- Reviews are text only, no photos.
- You can't review your own store.

## If something goes wrong

- **"You can review this once your order for it is delivered."** Wait until the order shows **Delivered**.
- **"Tap a star to rate this product."** Choose a rating first.
- **"Write a few words about the product."** Add some text to **Your review**.
- **"You've already reviewed this product."** Delete your old review if you want to write a new one.
- **"Couldn't post your review. Check your connection and try again."**

## Stay safe

Keep reviews about the product and the order. Don't share phone numbers, addresses or other private details, and never accept payment for a review. See [Community guidelines](/help/safety-and-reporting/community-guidelines).
