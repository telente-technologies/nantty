Stores can go live to show off their products. While you watch, you can see what's for sale and buy it without leaving Zudomart.

## Who this is for

Anyone watching a shopping live from a store. Buying follows the same rules as any order, including escrow in Nigeria.

## How to buy during a live

1. Join the live. Shopping lives show the store's products.
2. In the app, tap the shopping button in the comment bar (**Products in this live**). The **In this live** sheet lists each product with its name and price.
3. Tap a product to open its product page. You can go back to the live afterwards.
4. Choose options such as size or colour, add it to your cart and check out as usual. See [Cart, shipping and checkout](/help/buying-and-orders/cart-and-checkout).

On the web (app.zudomart.com), the products appear in a strip under the video.

## Good to know

- Only products the store has published can appear in a live, and only from that live's store.
- Prices, shipping and stock are the same as on the store page. There is no separate live price unless the store has a sale running.
- Escrow protection works exactly as for any other order. Gifts and coins sent during a live are separate from buying and are not refundable.

## Stay safe

Only buy through the product page and checkout. If a host or someone in the chat asks you to send money by transfer, or to message a phone number to buy, don't. That is outside escrow protection. Report it from the live. See [Live safety](/help/live/live-safety).
