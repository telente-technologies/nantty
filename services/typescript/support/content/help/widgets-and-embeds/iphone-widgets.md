You can put Zudomart widgets on your iPhone home screen or in Today View (swipe right on your first home screen).

## Who this is for

iPhone users with the Zudomart app installed from the App Store and signed in.

## How to add a widget

1. Open the Zudomart app once and make sure you're signed in.
2. Go to your home screen and touch and hold an empty area until the apps jiggle.
3. Tap **Edit** (or the **+** button) in the top corner, then **Add Widget**.
4. Search for **Zudomart** and tap it.
5. Swipe to choose the widget (for example order tracking or live now) and the size you want.
6. Tap **Add Widget**, place it where you like and tap **Done**.

## Change or remove a widget

- **Change options**: touch and hold the widget and tap **Edit Widget**, if that widget has options.
- **Remove it**: touch and hold the widget and tap **Remove Widget**.
- You can also change widget settings in the Zudomart app under **Settings > Widgets**.

## Good to know

- Widgets need the Zudomart app from the App Store. They don't work in Expo Go or other developer preview builds.
- iOS decides how often widgets refresh to save battery. Opening the Zudomart app refreshes them.
- If Low Power Mode is on, widgets may update less often.
- Widgets are read-only. Tapping one opens the matching screen in the app.

## If something goes wrong

- **Zudomart isn't in the widget list**: update the app from the App Store, open it once, then restart your iPhone and try again. See [Update the app](/help/troubleshooting/update-the-app).
- **The widget is blank or asks you to sign in**: open the Zudomart app and sign in. See [Widget troubleshooting](/help/widgets-and-embeds/widget-troubleshooting).
