Nantty widgets put the things you check most on your home screen. Tap a widget to jump straight to that part of the app.

## Who this is for

Anyone with the Nantty app on an iPhone or Android phone. Some widgets are for buyers, some for sellers and some for creators. Widgets need the Nantty app installed from the App Store or Google Play.

## The widgets

### Order tracking (buyers)

Shows up to 3 of your active orders, newest first, with the item, the store, a photo and the latest status: **Order confirmed**, **Being prepared**, **Ready to ship**, **On the way**, **Out for delivery**, **Delivery attempt failed** or **Delivered**. Tap an order to open it.

### Seller sales today (sellers)

Shows your store name, sales so far today, the number of orders today, how many orders are waiting to ship and your available balance. "Today" starts at midnight in your store's time zone (WAT for most Nigerian stores). You can choose to hide the money amounts so they aren't visible on your home screen; the order counts still show. Tap it to open your seller dashboard.

### Live now

Shows up to 8 accounts you follow who are live right now, with the stream title and viewer count. Tap one to join.

### Creator stats (creators)

A quick look at how your content and earnings are doing today. Tap it to open your creator tools.

### New stories

Shows people you follow who have posted new stories. Tap to start watching.

## How to add a widget

- On iPhone, see [Add widgets on iPhone](/help/widgets-and-embeds/iphone-widgets).
- On Android, see [Add widgets on Android](/help/widgets-and-embeds/android-widgets).

You can manage widget options in the app under **Settings > Widgets**.

## Good to know

- Widgets are read-only. You can't buy, send money or change anything from a widget; tapping opens the app.
- Widget information can be up to about a minute old, and your phone decides how often to refresh widgets, so they may lag a little behind the app.
- Widgets use a separate, limited sign-in that lasts 24 hours and renews when you open the app. If you sign out, or end that device's session in [Active sessions](/help/account-and-login/active-sessions), widgets stop showing your details.
- Anyone who can see your home screen can see what your widgets show. Use the hide-amounts option on the sales widget if you share your phone.
