Nantty widgets work on most Android phones, including Samsung, Tecno, Infinix, itel, Xiaomi and Pixel.

## Who this is for

Android users with the Nantty app installed from Google Play and signed in.

## How to add a widget

1. Open the Nantty app once and make sure you're signed in.
2. Touch and hold an empty area of your home screen.
3. Tap **Widgets**.
4. Scroll or search for **Nantty** and tap it to see the widgets.
5. Touch and hold the widget you want and drag it onto your home screen.
6. To resize it, touch and hold the widget, then drag the handles.

The exact words differ a little between phone makers.

## Remove a widget

Touch and hold the widget and drag it to **Remove**, or tap **Remove** in the menu that appears.

You can change widget settings in the Nantty app under **Settings > Widgets**.

## Good to know

- Widgets need the Nantty app from Google Play. They don't work in Expo Go or other test builds.
- Android and your phone maker decide how often widgets refresh. Opening the Nantty app refreshes them.
- Some phones stop apps in the background to save battery, which can freeze widgets. If yours stop updating, allow Nantty to run in the background: open your phone's **Settings > Apps > Nantty > Battery** and choose **Unrestricted** or turn off battery optimisation. The wording depends on your phone.
- Widgets are read-only. Tapping one opens the matching screen in the app.

## If something goes wrong

- **Nantty isn't in the widget list**: update the app from Google Play, open it once and try again. Restarting your phone helps on some models. See [Update the app](/help/troubleshooting/update-the-app).
- **The widget is blank or out of date**: see [Widget troubleshooting](/help/widgets-and-embeds/widget-troubleshooting).
