If you have your own website or blog, you can show your Nantty store there. Visitors see your products and can buy them through Nantty, so every order keeps Nantty's checkout and buyer protection.

## Who this is for

Sellers with a Nantty store, and creators who sell products. The website embed is coming soon and isn't in the seller dashboard yet. This page explains how it will work, and we'll add the exact button names when it launches.

## What you can embed

- **Your store**: a block showing your store name and a selection of your products.
- **Products**: one product or a few chosen products, with photo, name and price.
- **Buy button**: a single button for one product that opens Nantty checkout.

## How adding an embed will work

1. Open your seller dashboard on **dashboard.nantty.com** once the embed option is live.
2. Choose what to embed: store, products or buy button.
3. Copy the embed code.
4. Paste it into your website where you want it to appear. Most website builders, including WordPress, have an "HTML" or "Embed" block for this.
5. Publish your page and check it on a phone and a computer.

## Good to know

- Prices, stock and product photos come from Nantty, so they stay up to date when you change them in your store.
- Buyers pay through Nantty checkout, never on your website. In Nigeria, escrow protects buyers as usual.
- If you hide or delete a product, it disappears from the embed.
- Only embed your own store. Embedding someone else's store to look like their official site counts as impersonation.

## If something goes wrong

- **Nothing shows on my site**: check you pasted the whole code into an HTML or embed block, not a normal text block.
- **Old prices show**: your website may be caching the page. Clear its cache.
- **The buy button doesn't open checkout**: make sure the product is published and in stock, and that your store is approved to sell.

Need help? Go to **Settings > Support > Contact support** and choose **Selling**, or email sellers@nantty.com.
