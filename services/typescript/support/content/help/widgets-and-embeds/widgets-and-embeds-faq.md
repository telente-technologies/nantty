## Which widgets does Nantty have?

Order tracking, seller sales today, live now, creator stats and new stories. See [Nantty home-screen widgets](/help/widgets-and-embeds/home-screen-widgets).

## Do widgets work on both iPhone and Android?

Yes. See [Add widgets on iPhone](/help/widgets-and-embeds/iphone-widgets) and [Add widgets on Android](/help/widgets-and-embeds/android-widgets).

## Why can't I find Nantty in my widget list?

Widgets need the Nantty app installed from the App Store or Google Play, updated and opened at least once. They don't work in Expo Go or developer preview builds. See [Widget troubleshooting](/help/widgets-and-embeds/widget-troubleshooting).

## Can I pay or send money from a widget?

No. Widgets only show information. Tapping one opens the app, where everything works as usual.

## How up to date are widgets?

Usually within about a minute, but your phone decides how often widgets refresh. Opening the app refreshes them.

## Can other people see my sales on my home screen?

Anyone who sees your screen can. Turn on the option to hide amounts on the seller sales widget to show order counts only.

## What time does "today" start on the sales widget?

Midnight in your store's time zone, which is WAT for most Nigerian stores.

## Do widgets stay signed in forever?

No. They use a limited sign-in that lasts 24 hours and renews when you open the app. Signing out or ending the session in [Active sessions](/help/account-and-login/active-sessions) stops them.

## Do widgets use a lot of data or battery?

No. They download small updates, and your phone limits how often they refresh.

## Can I put my Nantty store on my own website?

Yes, sellers and creators can embed their store, products or a buy button. See [Embed your store on your website](/help/widgets-and-embeds/website-embed).

## Do buyers pay on my website?

No. The buy button opens Nantty checkout, so buyers keep Nantty's protection.

## Is there a widget for my computer?

Not yet. Widgets are for phones. On a computer, use **app.nantty.com**.
