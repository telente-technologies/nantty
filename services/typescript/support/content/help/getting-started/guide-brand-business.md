Businesses and brands get their own profiles on Nantty, separate from your personal one but run from the same account.

## Step 1: Choose the right profile type

- **Business profile**: for companies and shops, especially if you'll sell.
- **Brand profile**: for brands that want a presence, with or without selling.
- **Government profile**: for official public bodies only.

See [Profile types](/help/profiles-and-privacy/profile-types).

## Step 2: Create it

Go to **Settings > Create business profile** or **Settings > Create a brand profile** and follow the steps. See [Business profiles](/help/business-and-brands/business-profiles) and [Brand pages](/help/business-and-brands/brand-pages).

## Step 3: Switch between profiles

Long-press the **Profile** tab to switch. Posts, likes and messages are sent as the profile you're using. See [Switching profiles](/help/business-and-brands/switch-profiles).

## Step 4: Verify and get Gold

Upload your [business documents](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/business-documents) and choose a Gold plan. See [The Gold checkmark](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/gold-checkmark).

## Step 5: Sell (optional)

Open a store and follow the [seller's guide](/help/getting-started/guide-seller). Seller plans add Gold and more tools: see [Seller plans](/help/selling/seller-plans).

## Step 6: Add your team

Give staff access to the seller dashboard without sharing your password. See [Your seller team](/help/selling/seller-team).

## Step 7: Advertise

Boost posts or run campaigns. See the [advertiser's guide](/help/getting-started/guide-advertiser).

## Step 8: Reach your customers

Send marketing emails to customers who opted in. See [Marketing emails](/help/business-and-brands/marketing-emails).

## Government bodies

Use a government profile and apply for the [Black checkmark](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/black-checkmark). See [Government accounts](/help/business-and-brands/government-accounts).

## Stay safe

Never share your password with staff; add them as team members instead. Report anyone pretending to be your brand: see [Impersonation](/help/safety-and-reporting/impersonation).
