Zudomart brings social media and shopping together. You can follow people and stores, post photos, videos and clips, chat with friends, watch and host live streams, and buy products with escrow protection, without leaving the app.

## What you can do

- **Share**: post photos and videos, short clips and stories. See [Posts, clips and stories](/help/posts-and-feed).
- **Talk**: message people one to one or in groups. See [Chat and messages](/help/chat).
- **Watch and go live**: join live streams, send gifts and support creators. Creators in the Creator Program can go live themselves.
- **Shop**: browse Mart, add items from several sellers to one cart and pay once. Your payment is held in escrow until you confirm delivery.
- **Sell**: open a store from the app, then run it from the free seller dashboard at dashboard.zudomart.com.
- **Earn as a creator**: gifts, fan subscriptions and a share of eligible ads revenue.

## Where Zudomart works

Zudomart launches in Nigeria first. Prices are shown in your local currency where it is supported, and you always see the exact price before you pay.
