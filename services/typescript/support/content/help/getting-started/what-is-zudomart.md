Zudomart brings social media and shopping together in one app. You can follow people and stores, post photos, clips and stories, chat with friends, watch live streams and buy from real sellers without leaving the app.

## What you can do

- **Share**: post photos, videos, short clips and stories. See [Posts and feed](/help/posts-and-feed).
- **Talk**: message people one to one or in groups. Chats are end-to-end encrypted. See [Chat](/help/chat).
- **Watch and go live**: join live streams and send gifts with coins. Creators in the Creator Program can go live themselves. See [Live](/help/live).
- **Shop**: browse **Mart**, add items from several sellers to one cart and pay once. In Nigeria your payment stays locked in escrow until you confirm delivery. See [How escrow protects your order](/help/buying-and-orders/how-escrow-protects-your-order).
- **Sell**: open a store from the app for free, then run it from the free seller dashboard at **dashboard.zudomart.com**. There's no commission on product sales. See [Start selling](/help/selling/start-selling).
- **Earn as a creator**: gifts, fan subscriptions and a share of eligible ads revenue. See [The Creator Program](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-program).
- **Advertise**: boost a post or run campaigns in Ads Manager. See [Ads and boosts](/help/ads-and-boosts).

## Your money

Zudomart never holds your money itself. In Nigeria each verified user gets their own wallet held by our licensed payment partner, and money only leaves to a bank account in your own name. See [Who holds your money](/help/wallet-security/who-holds-your-money).

## Where Zudomart works

Zudomart is built in and for Nigeria first, and you can also use it in Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, the UK, the eurozone, the US and beyond. Escrow and wallets are Nigeria-only for now. See [Countries and availability](/help/getting-started/countries-and-availability).
