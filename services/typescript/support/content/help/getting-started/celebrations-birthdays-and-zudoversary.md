Nantty celebrates two days with you: your birthday and the day you joined.

## Your birthday

On your birthday, balloons appear on your profile. You always see them yourself. Visitors see them too unless you turn this off. Your age and date of birth are never shown to others. Nantty doesn't send a birthday email.

**To turn off balloons for visitors:** go to **Settings > Privacy & Data** and switch off **Birthday balloons** ("Show balloons on your profile to visitors on your birthday").

## Your Nantty anniversary

Each year on the day you joined, you see a **Happy Nantty anniversary!** celebration, for example "Thanks for being part of Nantty for 1 year". It includes a few highlights from your year when you have them, such as posts, followers, orders and lives. You also get an anniversary email.

## Choose how you're told

- **App**: **Settings > Notification**, then **Milestones: push** ("Birthdays and Zudoversaries, like your account anniversary") and **Milestones: email** ("A celebration email on your Zudoversary").
- **Web**: **app.nantty.com > Settings > Milestones**, with **Push notifications** and **Email**.

## Good to know

- Celebrations follow your own time zone, so they start at midnight where you are.
- Each celebration shows once per day in the app and once on your profile, so it never gets in the way.
- Your birthday comes from sign-up. If it's wrong, contact support under **My account**.
