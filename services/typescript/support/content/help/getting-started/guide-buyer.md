Here's how buying works on Nantty, from browsing to a finished order.

## Step 1: Find what you want

Open **Home > Mart**, browse categories or search. Check the seller's store, reviews and whether they're verified. See [Buying from unverified stores](/help/buying-and-orders/buying-from-unverified-stores).

## Step 2: Save your delivery address

Add it in **Settings > Shipping Address** so checkout is quick. See [Saved addresses](/help/buying-and-orders/saved-addresses).

## Step 3: Add to cart and check out

You can buy from several sellers in one cart and pay once. Check the delivery cost before you pay. See [Cart and checkout](/help/buying-and-orders/cart-and-checkout) and [Shipping costs and delivery areas](/help/buying-and-orders/shipping-costs-and-delivery-areas).

## Step 4: Choose how to pay

- **In Nigeria**: pay with escrow. Your money stays locked in your own wallet with our payment partner until you confirm delivery. Verified stores may also offer direct payment. See [Escrow or direct payment](/help/buying-and-orders/escrow-or-direct-payment).
- **Outside Nigeria**: you pay the seller directly through checkout. See [Buying outside Nigeria](/help/buying-and-orders/buying-outside-nigeria).

See also [Payment methods](/help/payments-and-wallet/payment-methods).

## Step 5: Track your order

Follow it in **Profile > More > My orders**. See [Tracking your order](/help/buying-and-orders/track-your-order) and [Order statuses](/help/buying-and-orders/order-statuses).

## Step 6: Confirm delivery

Check the item, then confirm. If you don't confirm or dispute, escrow releases automatically 3 days after delivery. See [Confirming delivery](/help/buying-and-orders/confirm-delivery).

## Step 7: If something is wrong

Open a dispute before the money is released. See [Disputes and refunds](/help/buying-and-orders/disputes-and-refunds) and [Refund timelines](/help/buying-and-orders/refund-timelines).

## Step 8: Leave a review

Help other buyers. See [Leaving a review](/help/buying-and-orders/leave-a-review).

## Stay safe

Always pay inside Nantty. Never send money to a seller's personal account or pay a "delivery fee" by transfer outside checkout. See [Staying safe when buying and selling](/help/safety-and-reporting/stay-safe-when-buying-and-selling).
