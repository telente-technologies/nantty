Welcome. Follow these steps to get set up in about ten minutes.

## Step 1: Create your account

Sign up with your email, Google or Apple. We email you a 6-digit code to confirm your address. See [Creating your account](/help/account-and-login/create-an-account).

## Step 2: Make your profile yours

Add a photo, name and bio in **Edit profile**, and check your username. See [Editing your profile](/help/profiles-and-privacy/edit-your-profile) and [Usernames](/help/account-and-login/usernames).

## Step 3: Choose who sees your posts

Your profile is public by default. To approve followers yourself, switch to **Private** in **Settings > Privacy**. See [Private accounts](/help/profiles-and-privacy/private-account).

## Step 4: Learn the tabs

**Home**, **Clips**, **Create**, **Chats** and **Profile**. See [Finding your way around the app](/help/getting-started/find-your-way-around).

## Step 5: Follow people and stores

Search for friends, creators and shops, and follow them. Your **Following** feed fills up with their posts. Tap the bell on a profile to hear when they post. See [Followers and following](/help/profiles-and-privacy/followers-and-following).

## Step 6: Post something

Tap **Create** to share a post, clip or story. See [Creating a post](/help/posts-and-feed/create-a-post).

## Step 7: Set your notifications and look

Pick what reaches your phone in [Notification settings](/help/notifications-and-settings/notification-settings), and try [dark mode](/help/notifications-and-settings/dark-mode-and-appearance).

## Step 8: Keep your account safe

Use a strong password and never share codes. See [If your account was hacked](/help/account-and-login/hacked-account) and [What support will never ask for](/help/contact-support/what-support-never-asks).

## What next?

- Want to shop? Read the [buyer's guide](/help/getting-started/guide-buyer).
- Want to sell? Read the [seller's guide](/help/getting-started/guide-seller).
- Want to earn as a creator? Read the [creator's guide](/help/getting-started/guide-creator).
- New words? Check the [glossary](/help/getting-started/glossary).
