New words on Nantty, explained simply. Follow the links for the details.

## Money

- **Wallet**: your own account held by our licensed payment partner (Nigeria only). Nantty tracks it but never holds your money. See [Your wallet explained](/help/payments-and-wallet/your-wallet-explained).
- **Escrow**: in Nigeria, when you pay with escrow, the money stays locked in your own wallet until you confirm delivery, then moves to the seller. See [How escrow protects your order](/help/buying-and-orders/how-escrow-protects-your-order).
- **Direct payment**: you pay the seller straight away through checkout, without escrow. Used by verified stores and everywhere outside Nigeria. See [Escrow or direct payment](/help/buying-and-orders/escrow-or-direct-payment).
- **Pending (escrow)**: order money waiting for the buyer to confirm delivery.
- **Locked**: released order money in a short safety hold (24 hours) before you can withdraw it.
- **Available to withdraw**: money you can send to your bank now.
- **Owed to Nantty**: refunds or chargebacks taken from your next earnings. See [Owed to Nantty](/help/payments-and-wallet/owed-to-nantty).
- **Ad credit**: prepaid credit for ads and boosts. It can't be withdrawn.
- **Payout account**: a bank or mobile money account in your own name that receives withdrawals.
- **Step-up code**: a quick "Confirm it's you" check with your password or an emailed code before sensitive actions like withdrawing. See [Step-up codes](/help/wallet-security/step-up-codes).

## Verification

- **L0**: verified email. You can browse and buy.
- **L1 (payout verified)**: your payout details are confirmed. You can receive money and withdraw.
- **L2 (identity verified)**: the approved identity check for the available country-specific method is complete. Higher limits and checkmarks.

See [Verification levels](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/verification-levels).

- **Checkmark tiers**: **Blue** for people, **Gold** for businesses and brands, **Black** for government. See [Blue, Gold and Black checkmarks](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/checkmarks-explained).

## Coins, points and rewards

- **Coins**: bought with money to send gifts on lives. Coins aren't cash. See [Buying coins](/help/payments-and-wallet/buy-coins).
- **Nantty Points**: points that rank supporters in a live. They're never money. See [Nantty Points](/help/live/nantty-points).
- **Store rewards**: store credit a seller gives you on completed orders, to spend only at that store. See [Using store rewards](/help/buying-and-orders/store-rewards-for-buyers).

## Creators and promotion

- **Creator Program**: the programme that unlocks going live, gifts, fan subscriptions and earnings. See [The Creator Program](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-program).
- **Fan subscription**: a monthly payment fans make to support a creator.
- **Boost**: paying to show one of your posts to more people. It shows a **Boosted** label.
- **Sponsored**: an ad from Ads Manager, labelled **Sponsored**. See [Boosted vs sponsored](/help/ads-and-boosts/boosted-vs-sponsored).

## Profiles

- **Personal, business, brand and government profiles**: different profile types under one account. See [Profile types](/help/profiles-and-privacy/profile-types).
- **Nantty anniversary**: the anniversary of the day you joined.
