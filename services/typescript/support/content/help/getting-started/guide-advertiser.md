Nantty has two ways to reach more people: a quick boost on one post, or a full campaign in Ads Manager.

## Step 1: Check you can advertise

Anyone can boost a post. Ads Manager is for verified users. See [Who can advertise](/help/ads-and-boosts/who-can-advertise).

## Step 2: Pick boost or ad

- **Boost**: push one of your existing posts to more people. Boosted posts show a **Boosted** label.
- **Ad**: a campaign with your own audience, budget and schedule in Ads Manager. Ads show a **Sponsored** label.

See [Boosted vs sponsored](/help/ads-and-boosts/boosted-vs-sponsored).

## Step 3: Boost a post

Open your post's menu and tap **Promote post**. Choose a goal and budget, then pay on the secure checkout page. Blue Premium members get one free boost a month. See [Boosting a post](/help/ads-and-boosts/boost-a-post), [Paying for a boost](/help/ads-and-boosts/pay-for-a-boost) and [Your free boost with Premium](/help/ads-and-boosts/free-boost-with-premium).

## Step 4: Run a campaign

Sign in at **ads.nantty.com**, create a campaign, choose your audience and budget, and submit it for review. See [Ads Manager campaigns](/help/ads-and-boosts/ads-manager-campaigns) and [Ad review and policies](/help/ads-and-boosts/ad-review-and-policies).

## Step 5: Pay

Pay by card at checkout, use prepaid ad credit, or, if you're a verified seller, fund ads from your earnings. Ad credit can't be withdrawn. See [Funding ads from earnings](/help/ads-and-boosts/fund-ads-from-earnings) and [Ad credit and refunds](/help/ads-and-boosts/ad-credit-and-refunds).

## Step 6: Read your results

Check reach, clicks and spend, then adjust. See [Ad results](/help/ads-and-boosts/ad-results).

## Good to know

- Ads also run during lives. See [Live ads](/help/live/live-ads).
- Viewers can see why they were shown an ad. See [Why you see an ad](/help/ads-and-boosts/why-you-see-an-ad).
