The Zudomart app has five tabs along the bottom. Here's what each one does and where things live.

## Home

The top of **Home** has two sections: **Feed** and **Mart**. Tap them, or swipe left and right, to switch. The page follows your finger, so you can peek and let go to stay where you are. This works the same on the app and on the web.

- **Feed**: posts from people and stores. Use the menu at the top of the feed to choose **For you** or **Following**, and filter by topic.
- **Mart**: shopping, with **Product categories**, **Sales of the Day**, **New Arrivals** and **Featured Brands**.
- **Search** (magnifying glass): search people, posts and products. On **Mart** it searches the mart.
- **Notifications** (bell): your activity, with **All** and **Mentions** filters and **Mark all as read**.

## Clips

Full-screen short videos. Switch between **For You** and **Following** at the top. See [Clips for you and following](/help/clips-and-stories/clips-for-you-and-following).

## Create

The button in the middle. Choose what to make with the switcher at the bottom: **STORY**, **POST** or **CLIP**. Sellers also see **PRODUCT**, and approved creators see **LIVE**. See [Creating a post](/help/posts-and-feed/create-a-post).

## Chats

Your private, end-to-end encrypted messages and group chats. Start a **New message** or search your chats. See [Chat](/help/chat).

## Profile

Your profile, posts, reposts and clips (and your store if you sell).

- Tap **More** (top right) for your menu: **Seller dashboard** (sellers), **Creator tools** or **Switch to creator account**, **Follow requests** (private accounts), **Saved**, **My orders**, **Wallet**, **Get verified** or **Verification**, and **Settings**.
- Long-press the **Profile** tab to switch between your personal, business and brand profiles. See [Switching profiles](/help/business-and-brands/switch-profiles).
- Use **Edit profile** and **Share profile** under your name.

## Where's the Help Centre?

**Profile > More > Settings > Support > Help centre**. From there you can also **Contact support** and see **My support requests**.

## Good to know

- Guests can browse every tab, but Create, Chats and notifications ask you to sign in.
- Settings has its own tour: [A tour of Settings](/help/notifications-and-settings/settings-overview).
