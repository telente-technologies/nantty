Anyone can open Zudomart and look around before creating an account.

## How to browse as a guest

On the welcome screen, tap **Browse first** instead of signing up. On the web, just open **app.zudomart.com**.

## What guests can do

- Scroll the feed, clips, **Mart**, categories and search.
- Open profiles, stores, products, reviews, posts and their comments.
- Watch live streams and read live chat.
- Read this Help Centre.

## What needs an account

Guests are view-only. Liking, commenting, following, saving, sharing to chat, sending gifts, joining a live, adding to cart, buying, messaging, posting and seeing notifications all need an account.

When you try one of these, Zudomart asks you to sign in or sign up. Tap **Not now** to keep browsing. After you sign in you come straight back to where you were, and the action finishes when possible, for example the like you tapped is applied.

## Your privacy as a guest

Guests only send essential, anonymous usage data. Personalisation and advertising tracking stay off until you sign in and choose your settings in **Settings > Privacy & Data**.

## Good to know

- Your feed as a guest isn't personalised. Signing up and picking topics gives you a feed that fits you.
- Signing up only needs an email address and takes about two minutes. See [Creating your account](/help/account-and-login/create-an-account).
