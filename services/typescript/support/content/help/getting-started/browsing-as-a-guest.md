Anyone can open Zudomart and look around without creating an account.

## What guests can do

- Scroll the For you feed, clips, Mart, categories and search.
- Open profiles, stores, products, reviews, posts and their comments.
- Watch live streams and read live chat.
- Read this Help Centre.

## What needs an account

Guests are view-only. Liking, commenting, following, saving, sharing to chat, sending gifts, joining a live as a guest, adding to cart, buying, messaging and posting all need an account.

When you try one of these, Zudomart asks you to sign in or sign up. After you sign in you come straight back to where you were, and the action finishes when possible, for example the like you tapped is applied.

## Your privacy as a guest

Guests only send essential, anonymous usage data. Personalisation and advertising tracking stay off until you sign in and choose your settings.
