Nantty doesn't pay for views; it shares eligible monetisation revenue with creators. Here's how to get set up.

## Step 1: Build your profile

Post regularly with original content: posts, clips and stories. See [Creating a post](/help/posts-and-feed/create-a-post) and [Creating a clip](/help/clips-and-stories/create-a-clip).

## Step 2: Join the Creator Program

Open **Profile > More > Switch to creator account** and apply. Track it under **Creator application status**. Once approved, the menu shows **Creator tools**. See [The Creator Program](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-program).

## Step 3: Get verified (optional)

The Blue checkmark helps fans trust it's really you. See [The Blue checkmark](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/blue-checkmark).

## Step 4: Go live

Approved creators get **LIVE** in the **Create** tab, or can stream from a computer with **studio.nantty.com**. See [Going live](/help/live/going-live) and [Streaming with OBS](/help/live/streaming-with-obs).

## Step 5: Earn

- **Gifts**: fans send gifts with coins during lives. See [Gifts and coins on live](/help/live/gifts-and-coins-on-live).
- **Fan subscriptions**: set a monthly price for subscriber perks. You keep 80% of the net. See [Fan subscriptions](/help/creators-and-earnings/fan-subscriptions).
- **Ads revenue share**: see [Ads revenue share](/help/creators-and-earnings/ads-revenue-share).
- **Nantty Points** rank your supporters but are never money. See [Nantty Points](/help/live/nanttypoints).

## Step 6: Set up payouts

Tap **Get paid** to confirm your payout details. Your earnings keep adding up while you verify. See [Setting up payouts](/help/creators-and-earnings/payout-setup).

## Step 7: Understand holds and withdraw

Subscription money is held for 7 days before it moves to your available balance. See [Creator earnings holds](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-earnings-holds) and [Withdrawals](/help/creators-and-earnings/withdrawals).

## Step 8: Track what works

Use [creator analytics](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-analytics) and [post analytics](/help/posts-and-feed/post-analytics) to see what your audience likes.

## Stay safe

Keep your lives safe with moderation tools. See [Live safety](/help/live/live-safety) and [Live chat moderation](/help/live/live-chat-moderation).
