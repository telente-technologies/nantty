You can sign up and use Nantty's social features from anywhere. Payments, selling and verification work a little differently depending on where you are.

## Local prices

Prices are set locally in:

- Nigeria (naira)
- Ghana (cedi)
- Kenya (shilling)
- South Africa (rand)
- United Kingdom (pound)
- Eurozone countries (euro)
- United States (dollar)

Everywhere else sees prices in US dollars. Change your country in **Settings > App Preference > Country & Currency**. See [Country, currency and language](/help/notifications-and-settings/language-and-currency).

## What works where

| Feature | Nigeria | Outside Nigeria |
|---|---|---|
| Posts, clips, chat, live | Yes | Yes |
| Buying | Escrow or direct payment | Direct payment only |
| Wallet with its own account number | Yes | Not yet |
| Selling | Yes; unverified stores have a cap | Only after identity verification |
| Withdrawals | To a Nigerian bank account in your name | Earnings to a bank or mobile money account in your name |

## Buying outside Nigeria

There's no escrow and no stored balance yet. You pay the seller directly through checkout, protected by our dispute and refund policy and the seller's verification. See [Buying outside Nigeria](/help/buying-and-orders/buying-outside-nigeria).

## Selling outside Nigeria

You must complete identity verification (L1) before you can sell. If you try earlier, checkout is blocked with a verification message. See [Selling outside Nigeria](/help/selling/selling-outside-nigeria).

## Identity checks by country

Accepted identity methods and ID types differ by country and by the products enabled for Nantty. The verification screen lists the options currently available to you; no document type is guaranteed to be accepted in every country. Nigerian options may include NIN number checks when enabled. See [How identity checks work](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/identity-check).

## Good to know

- The app is in English.
- In-app plan and coin prices follow your App Store or Google Play country.
