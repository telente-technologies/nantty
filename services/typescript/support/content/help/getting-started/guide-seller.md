Selling on Zudomart is free to start, with no commission on product sales. Here's the path from opening a store to money in your bank.

## Step 1: Apply to sell

Go to **Settings > Start selling on Zudomart** ("Free to apply"). See [Start selling](/help/selling/start-selling).

## Step 2: Learn the seller dashboard

Once approved, **Seller dashboard** appears in your **Profile > More** menu. For the full toolset, sign in at **dashboard.zudomart.com**. See [The seller dashboard](/help/selling/seller-dashboard-overview).

## Step 3: Set up payouts and verify

Tap **Get paid** in your Wallet to confirm your payout details (BVN and a bank account in your name in Nigeria). Upload [business documents](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/business-documents) to verify your store.

- In Nigeria you can sell before verification, with escrow only and up to a sales cap. See [Limits for unverified sellers](/help/selling/unverified-seller-limits).
- Outside Nigeria you must be identity-verified before you can sell. See [Selling outside Nigeria](/help/selling/selling-outside-nigeria).

## Step 4: Set delivery

Choose where you deliver and what it costs. See [Shipping settings](/help/selling/shipping-settings).

## Step 5: Add products

Use **PRODUCT** in the **Create** tab, or the dashboard. Add photos, variants, stock and prices. See [Products and inventory](/help/selling/products-and-inventory) and [Prohibited items](/help/selling/prohibited-items).

## Step 6: Handle orders

Prepare, ship and update each order. See [Orders and shipping](/help/selling/seller-orders-and-shipping). If a buyer disputes, respond quickly: see [Responding to a dispute](/help/selling/respond-to-a-dispute).

## Step 7: Get paid

When the buyer confirms (or 3 days after delivery), escrow money moves to your wallet, stays **Locked** for 24 hours, then becomes **Available to withdraw**. See [Seller payouts](/help/selling/seller-payouts) and [Fees and the escrow fee](/help/selling/fees-and-escrow-fee).

## Step 8: Grow

- Run discounts and [store rewards](/help/selling/store-rewards).
- Sell on live: see [Selling on live](/help/selling/sell-on-live).
- Upgrade to a plan for Gold and team seats: see [Seller plans](/help/selling/seller-plans).
- Promote products: see the [advertiser's guide](/help/getting-started/guide-advertiser).

## Stay safe

Never ship before an order is paid in Zudomart, and never take payment outside the app. See [Staying safe when buying and selling](/help/safety-and-reporting/stay-safe-when-buying-and-selling).
