Your one Zudomart account works everywhere.

## The main places

- **Mobile app** (iPhone and Android): everything, including posting, chat, live, shopping and your wallet.
- **Web app** at app.zudomart.com: browse, shop, chat and manage your account from a computer.
- **Seller dashboard** at dashboard.zudomart.com: free for every seller. Run products, inventory, orders, delivery, payouts and reports. See [The seller dashboard](/help/selling/seller-dashboard-overview).
- **Studio** at studio.zudomart.com: for creators who stream from a computer with software such as OBS, and to design overlays. See [Going live](/help/live/going-live).
- **Ads Manager** at ads.zudomart.com: create and manage ad campaigns. See [Ads Manager campaigns](/help/ads-and-boosts/ads-manager-campaigns).

You sign in to each of these with your Zudomart account. There are no separate logins.

## Links you share

Profile links look like app.zudomart.com/yourusername. Product, store, post, clip and live links open in the app if you have it, or in the browser.
