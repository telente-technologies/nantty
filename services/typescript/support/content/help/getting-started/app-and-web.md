Your one Zudomart account works everywhere. Sign in once on each device; there are no separate logins.

## The main places

- **Mobile app** (iPhone and Android): everything, including posting, clips, stories, chat, live, shopping, selling basics and your wallet. Download it from the App Store or Google Play.
- **Web app** at **app.zudomart.com**: browse, shop, chat, check orders, manage verification, settings and the Help Centre from a computer.
- **Seller dashboard** at **dashboard.zudomart.com**: free for every seller. Run products, inventory, orders, delivery, payouts, staff and reports. See [The seller dashboard](/help/selling/seller-dashboard-overview).
- **Studio** at **studio.zudomart.com**: for creators who stream from a computer with software such as OBS, and to design overlays. See [Streaming with OBS](/help/live/streaming-with-obs).
- **Ads Manager** at **ads.zudomart.com**: create and manage ad campaigns. See [Ads Manager campaigns](/help/ads-and-boosts/ads-manager-campaigns).

## What's different on the web

- Gold, seller and government plans are bought on the web.
- Many plans and coins cost less on the web, because app stores charge a fee for in-app purchases. See [App Store and Google Play purchases](/help/payments-and-wallet/app-store-purchases).
- Some steps open a secure web page from the app, for example paying for a boost or a checkmark. You come back to the app when you finish.

## Links you share

Profile links look like **app.zudomart.com/yourusername**. Product, store, post, clip and live links open in the app if you have it installed, or in your browser if not.

## Good to know

- Your theme choice syncs between the app and the web.
- Keep the app updated to get fixes and new features. See [Updating the app](/help/troubleshooting/update-the-app).
- Home-screen widgets need the app installed from the App Store or Google Play. See [Widgets and embeds](/help/widgets-and-embeds).
