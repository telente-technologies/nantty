## What is Zudomart?

A social marketplace: posts, clips, chat and live, plus shopping from real sellers. See [What is Zudomart?](/help/getting-started/what-is-zudomart)

## Is it free?

Yes. Signing up, posting, chatting, buying and opening a store are free. Optional extras include checkmarks, Premium, seller plans, coins and ads. See [Checkmark and plan prices by country](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/checkmark-prices).

## Do I need an account to look around?

No. Tap **Browse first**. You need an account to like, follow, buy or post. See [Browsing without an account](/help/getting-started/browsing-as-a-guest).

## Can I sign up with my phone number?

No, Zudomart uses email (or Google or Apple). See [Creating your account](/help/account-and-login/create-an-account).

## Which countries does Zudomart work in?

Everywhere, with local prices in seven regions. Escrow and wallets are Nigeria-only for now. See [Countries and availability](/help/getting-started/countries-and-availability).

## Is my money safe when I buy?

In Nigeria, escrow keeps your money locked until you confirm delivery. Zudomart never holds your money itself. See [How escrow protects your order](/help/buying-and-orders/how-escrow-protects-your-order).

## How do I start selling?

**Settings > Start selling on Zudomart**. See the [seller's guide](/help/getting-started/guide-seller).

## Does Zudomart take a cut of my sales?

There's no commission on product sales. Sellers pay a small escrow fee on escrow orders plus payment processing. See [Fees and the escrow fee](/help/selling/fees-and-escrow-fee).

## How do creators earn?

Gifts, fan subscriptions and a share of eligible ads revenue. Zudomart doesn't pay for views. See the [creator's guide](/help/getting-started/guide-creator).

## Can I use Zudomart on my computer?

Yes, at **app.zudomart.com**. See [Using Zudomart on the app and on the web](/help/getting-started/app-and-web).

## Where are my settings and wallet?

Tap **Profile**, then **More** at the top right. See [Finding your way around the app](/help/getting-started/find-your-way-around).

## What do words like escrow, L1 or ZudoPoints mean?

See the [glossary](/help/getting-started/glossary).

## How do I contact support?

**Settings > Support > Contact support**. See [Contacting support](/help/contact-support/contact-support).
