## Delete

1. Consider [downloading your data](/help/legal-and-data/download-your-data) first.
2. Go to **Settings > Privacy & Data > Delete account** and confirm.

You'll see: "Your account will be permanently deleted in 30 days. Log in any time before then to cancel."

## Changing your mind

Just log in within 30 days. Logging in cancels the deletion and your account carries on as normal.

## After 30 days

Your profile, posts, media and messages are permanently deleted. Records we must keep by law, such as orders, payments and tax records, are anonymised and kept for the required period. Your username is held for 120 days before anyone else can take it.

Sellers and creators should withdraw any available balance and finish open orders and disputes first.
