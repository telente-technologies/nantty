Read our policies on the website: Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy, Community Guidelines, Seller Terms, Creator Terms, Ads Policy and the Refund and Escrow Policy. Each shows its version, the date it takes effect and earlier versions.

## When policies change

- We email everyone and show a notice in the app with a summary of what changed.
- For material changes you'll be asked to accept the new version after it takes effect. Until you do, some actions are paused, but downloading your data and deleting your account always stay available.
- Your acceptance is recorded with the policy version.

## Cookies

On the web we use essential cookies to keep you signed in and secure. Optional analytics and advertising cookies only run with your consent.
