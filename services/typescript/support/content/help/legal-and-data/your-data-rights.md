You can use these rights for free under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and similar laws where you live.

## Do it yourself in the app

- **Download your data**: Settings > Privacy & Data > Download your data. See [Downloading your data](/help/legal-and-data/download-your-data).
- **Delete your account**: Settings > Privacy & Data > Delete account. See [Deleting your account](/help/legal-and-data/delete-your-account).
- **Correct your data**: edit your profile, addresses and settings.
- **Change consent**: cookie settings on our websites, or Settings > Privacy & Data in the app.

## Other requests

To ask for a copy of specific data, to object to or restrict a use, or for a person to review an automated decision, email our privacy team or Data Protection Officer, or open a ticket and choose **Privacy**. The full process is on the Data rights requests page on our website.

## Checking it's you

If you ask from the app while signed in, that's usually enough. Otherwise we may ask you to confirm from your account's email address. We only ask for ID if there's no other way, and we delete it afterwards.

## How long it takes

We answer within the time your law sets. For example: within 30 days in Nigeria and South Africa, within one month in the UK and EU, and within 7 days for access requests in Kenya.

## What we may not be able to do

We must keep some records by law, such as orders, payments and verification records. Chats are end-to-end encrypted, so we don't have your messages; they're on your devices. If we refuse part of a request, we tell you why and how to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or your local regulator.
