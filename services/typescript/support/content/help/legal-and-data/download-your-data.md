---
title: Downloading your data
summary: Request a copy of your Zudomart data from Settings > Privacy & Data.
tags: data export, download data, gdpr, privacy
related: delete-your-account, privacy-settings
---

1. Go to **Settings > Privacy & Data > Download your data**.
2. Request an export.
3. We notify and email you when it's ready, with a download link that expires after a while.

The export includes your profile, posts, comments, messages you can access, orders and settings. You can request a new export later.
