## The app won't load. What should I try first?

Tap **Try again**, check your connection, close and reopen the app, then update it. See [App not loading or showing errors](/help/troubleshooting/app-not-loading).

## What does "Something went wrong." mean?

A temporary problem. Tap **Try again**. If it keeps happening on the same screen, report a bug. See [Common error messages](/help/troubleshooting/common-error-messages).

## I forgot my password.

Tap **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen and we'll email you a 6-digit code. See [Can't sign in](/help/troubleshooting/cant-sign-in).

## My code never arrived.

Check spam and every inbox tab for **noreply@mail.zudomart.com**, then ask for a new code after 60 seconds. See [Not getting emails or codes](/help/troubleshooting/not-getting-emails).

## Can I get my code by SMS instead?

No. Zudomart only sends codes by email.

## My payment failed but money left my account.

Don't pay again. On the payment screen tap **I've paid, check status**, then **Get help with this payment**. See [Checkout and payment problems](/help/troubleshooting/checkout-problems).

## My video won't upload.

Check the size and length limits and use Wi-Fi for big files. See [Upload failed](/help/troubleshooting/upload-failed).

## Videos won't play or have no sound.

Tap **Unmute video**, check your volume and connection. See [Videos or lives won't play](/help/troubleshooting/video-wont-play).

## I'm not getting notifications.

Check **Settings > Notification > Push notifications** and **System notification settings**. See [Notifications not arriving](/help/troubleshooting/notifications-not-arriving).

## How do I know which version of the app I have?

Go to **Settings > About** and look at **Version**. See [Update the Zudomart app](/help/troubleshooting/update-the-app).

## Will reinstalling the app delete my account?

No. Your account, orders and wallet are on our servers. Chat keys are on your device, so read [Chat devices and keys](/help/chat/chat-devices-and-keys) first.

## My widget is blank.

Open the app and sign in. See [Widget troubleshooting](/help/widgets-and-embeds/widget-troubleshooting).

## How do I report a bug?

Go to **Settings > Support > Report a bug**, and include your phone model, app version, what you did and a screenshot. See [Report a bug](/help/contact-support/report-a-bug).

## None of this helped. How do I reach a person?

Go to **Settings > Support > Contact support** and choose the topic that fits. See [Contact support](/help/contact-support/contact-support).
