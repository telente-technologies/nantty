Uploads usually fail because of the connection, the file size or length, or the file type. The app keeps your upload in a queue so you can retry without starting again.

## Who this is for

Anyone posting photos or videos, or adding product photos, story media, profile pictures or chat attachments.

## Quick fixes

1. **Stay connected.** If you lose signal you'll see "You're offline. The upload will continue when you're back online." Keep the app open until it finishes.
2. **Tap to retry.** If you see **"Upload failed. Tap to retry."**, tap it.
3. **Use Wi-Fi** for big videos.
4. **Keep the app open** while large videos upload.
5. **Check the limits** below and trim or compress your file if needed.

## Size and length limits

| Where | Photos | Videos |
|---|---|---|
| Posts | up to 20 MB | up to 500 MB and 10 minutes |
| Clips | not supported | up to 500 MB and 3 minutes |
| Stories | up to 20 MB | up to 100 MB and 60 seconds |
| Profile picture | up to 5 MB | not supported |
| Profile banner | up to 10 MB | not supported |
| Products | up to 10 MB | up to 200 MB and 2 minutes |
| Chats | up to 20 MB | up to 100 MB and 5 minutes |

Chats also accept PDF documents up to 25 MB, and voice notes up to 10 minutes.

## Supported file types

- **Photos**: JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC and AVIF.
- **Videos**: MP4, MOV and WebM.

## What the messages mean

- **"This video is larger than the 500 MB limit for clip uploads."** (or similar) The file is too big for that place. Trim it or export at a lower quality.
- A video that's too long is rejected after upload. Trim it to the length limit above.
- **"HEIC photos must be converted to JPEG before upload."** or **"Convert the photo to JPEG and upload it again."** On iPhone, go to **Settings > Camera > Formats** and choose **Most Compatible**, or share the photo as JPEG.
- **"Finish or cancel your pending uploads first."** You have too many uploads waiting. Let them finish or cancel some.
- **"This upload can no longer be completed."** or **"This upload was removed."** Start the upload again.
- **"This file is still being processed."** The upload worked; we're preparing it. Wait a few minutes.
- **"Couldn't upload your photo. Try again."** Check your connection and retry.

## Good to know

- Every upload is checked for viruses and against our Community Guidelines. Files that fail are rejected.
- Chat media is end-to-end encrypted and deleted after 30 days.

## If it still doesn't work

Go to **Settings > Support > Report a bug** and tell us the file type, size and where you were posting.
