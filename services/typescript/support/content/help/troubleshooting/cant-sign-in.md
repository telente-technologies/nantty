You sign in to Zudomart with your email or username and your password, or with **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Apple** if you set your account up that way. Phone numbers can't be used to sign in, and we never text you sign-in codes.

## Who this is for

Anyone who can't get into their Zudomart account.

## Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot password?**
2. Enter the email on your account. We email you a 6-digit code.
3. Enter the code on **Enter 6-digit code**, then choose a new password.

Codes expire after 5 minutes. You can ask for a new one after 60 seconds. Can't find the email? See [Not getting emails](/help/troubleshooting/not-getting-emails).

## What the messages mean

- **"The email, username or password is incorrect."** Check for typos, extra spaces and Caps Lock. If you signed up with Google or Apple, use that button instead. Still stuck? Reset your password.
- **"Couldn't log in. Check your connection and try again."** Your phone couldn't reach us. Check your internet.
- **"The code is invalid or has expired."** or **"That code didn't work. Try again."** Use the newest code we sent, within 5 minutes.
- **"Too many incorrect attempts. Request a new code."** Ask for a new code and type it carefully.
- **"Too many requests. Try again shortly."** Wait a few minutes before trying again.
- **"Confirm you're a person to continue."** Complete the quick check that appears. See [Security checks](/help/account-and-login/security-checks).
- **"Your session has ended. Sign in again."** You were signed out, for example after a long time away or after signing out of all devices. Sign in again.
- **"This sign-in method is not available right now."** Google or Apple sign-in is briefly unavailable. Try again later or use your password.
- **"The provider has not verified your email address."** Verify your email with Google or Apple first, or sign in with your password.
- **"We could not verify your sign-in. Try again."** The Google or Apple step didn't finish. Try again.
- **"This account has been suspended."** or **"This account has been banned."** See [Account suspensions and appeals](/help/safety-and-reporting/suspensions-and-appeals).
- **"This account was deleted. Create a new account to come back."** The 30-day window to cancel deletion has passed. See [Delete your account](/help/legal-and-data/delete-your-account).

## Two-factor authentication

If you turned on two-factor authentication, you'll be asked for a second step after your password. See [Two-factor authentication](/help/account-and-login/two-factor-authentication).

## Good to know

- You stay signed in on a device for up to 30 days of not using it. After that, sign in again.
- Signing in within 30 days of asking to delete your account cancels the deletion.

## If you think someone else got in

If your password stopped working and you didn't change it, or you got a sign-in alert you don't recognise, see [Hacked account](/help/account-and-login/hacked-account) straight away.

## Stay safe

Zudomart will never ask for your password or codes by phone, chat or email. Never share them.
