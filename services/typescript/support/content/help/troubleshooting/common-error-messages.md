## Connection and general

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "You're offline" / "Check your connection, then try again." | Turn on Wi-Fi or mobile data, then tap **Try again**. |
| "Something went wrong." | A temporary problem. Tap **Try again**. |
| "Couldn't load your orders." (or wallet, cart, profile, reports...) | That section didn't load. Pull down to refresh or tap **Try again**. |
| "The service is unavailable. Try again." | Wait a few minutes. |
| "Too many requests. Try again shortly." | Wait a minute before trying again. |
| "Sign in to continue." | Sign in, and you'll return to where you were. |
| "Accept the updated policies to continue." | Open the app and accept the updated terms. |
| "Confirm you're a person to continue." | Complete the quick check. See [Security checks](/help/account-and-login/security-checks). |
| "You do not have permission to do that." | Your account or store role can't do this action. |

## Sign-in and account

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "The email, username or password is incorrect." | Check for typos or reset your password. See [Can't sign in](/help/troubleshooting/cant-sign-in). |
| "Your session has ended. Sign in again." | Sign in again. |
| "An account with this email already exists." | Sign in instead, or reset your password. |
| "That username is taken." | Choose another username. |
| "This account has been suspended." | See [Account suspensions and appeals](/help/safety-and-reporting/suspensions-and-appeals). |
| "This account was deleted. Create a new account to come back." | The deletion is final. Create a new account. |
| "Your current password is incorrect." | Re-enter your current password. |

## Codes and security checks

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "The code is invalid or has expired." | Use the newest code within 5 minutes. |
| "Too many incorrect attempts. Request a new code." | Ask for a new code. |
| "Verify your email address first." | Verify your email. See [Not getting emails or codes](/help/troubleshooting/not-getting-emails). |
| "Confirm your identity again to continue." | Enter your password or the code we email you. See [Step-up codes](/help/wallet-security/step-up-codes). |
| "We could not confirm it's you. Try again." | The check failed. Try again carefully. |

## Uploads

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Upload failed. Tap to retry." | Tap it to retry on a good connection. |
| "You're offline. The upload will continue when you're back online." | Reconnect and keep the app open. |
| "Finish or cancel your pending uploads first." | Let current uploads finish. |
| "HEIC photos must be converted to JPEG before upload." | Save the photo as JPEG and try again. |
| "This file is still being processed." | Wait a few minutes. |

See [Upload failed](/help/troubleshooting/upload-failed) for size limits.

## Checkout and orders

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Payment didn't go through" | No money was taken. Try again or use another method. See [Checkout and payment problems](/help/troubleshooting/checkout-problems). |
| "There is not enough stock for one of the items." | Lower the quantity or remove the item. |
| "None of the sellers in your cart ship to that address." | Choose another address. |
| "That checkout is closed. Start a new checkout." | Go back to your cart. |
| "Our payment partner is unavailable. Try again in a few minutes." | Wait and try again. |
| "That order can no longer be cancelled." | It's already being prepared or shipped. Contact the seller or open a dispute after delivery. |
| "Only delivered orders can be confirmed." | Wait until the order is delivered. |

## Escrow and disputes

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "This order can no longer be disputed." | The money was already released. Contact support. |
| "This dispute is closed." | The dispute was decided. |
| "This order is under dispute; its funds stay frozen until the dispute is resolved." | Wait for the dispute outcome. |

## Wallet and withdrawals

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Verify your payout details to continue. Nothing you have earned is lost while you verify." | Finish payout verification. |
| "Finish the identity check (selfie and ID) to continue." | Complete the selfie and ID check. |
| "Amount is more than your available balance." | Withdraw less. |
| "This withdrawal is too small to cover the bank transfer fee." | Withdraw a larger amount. |
| "You have reached today's number of withdrawals." | Try again tomorrow. |
| "Wait a few minutes before another withdrawal." | Wait 10 minutes. |
| "New payout accounts can receive withdrawals 48 hours after they are added." | Wait, or use an older account. |
| "Withdrawals go only to a bank account in your own verified name." | Add an account in your own name. |
| "Your wallet is on hold while we check it. Contact support if this lasts." | See [Wallet on hold](/help/wallet-security/wallet-on-hold). |

See [Withdrawal limits](/help/wallet-security/withdrawal-limits) for the full list.

## Safety and widgets

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "You have sent a lot of reports. Try again in a little while." | Wait before reporting more. |
| "Decisions can be appealed for 30 days." | The appeal window has closed. |
| "Going live is paused on your account because of active strikes. See Settings > Account status." | See [Account health and strikes](/help/safety-and-reporting/account-health-and-strikes). |
| "Widgets can't refresh right now. Try again later." | Wait and open the app. |

Didn't find your message? Go to **Settings > Support > Contact support** and include a screenshot.
