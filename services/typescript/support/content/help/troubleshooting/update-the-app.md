New versions fix bugs, add features and keep your account secure. If something isn't working, updating is one of the first things to try.

## Who this is for

Anyone using the Zudomart app on iPhone or Android.

## Check your version

In the app, go to **Settings > About**. Your version is shown next to **Version**. Support may ask for it.

## Update on iPhone

1. Open the **App Store**.
2. Tap your profile picture at the top right.
3. Scroll to see pending updates and tap **Update** next to Zudomart.

To update automatically, go to **Settings > App Store** on your iPhone and turn on **App Updates**.

## Update on Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store**.
2. Search for **Zudomart**.
3. Tap **Update**.

To update automatically, open the Zudomart page in Play Store, tap the three dots and turn on **Enable auto update**.

## On the web

**app.zudomart.com** always runs the latest version. If something looks out of date, refresh the page.

## Good to know

- Only install Zudomart from the App Store or Google Play. Copies from other websites or APK files shared in chats can be fake and steal your account.
- Some features, like home-screen widgets and in-app subscriptions, only work in the app from the App Store or Google Play, not in test builds such as Expo Go.
- If there's no **Update** button, you already have the latest version available for your phone.
- Very old phones may not be able to run the newest version. You can still use **app.zudomart.com** in your browser.

## If something goes wrong

- **The update won't download**: check you have enough free space and a stable connection, then try again.
- **The app crashes after updating**: restart your phone. If it still crashes, uninstall and reinstall the app, then sign in again. Your account, orders and wallet are safe on our servers. Chats are end-to-end encrypted with keys on your device, so read [Chat devices and keys](/help/chat/chat-devices-and-keys) before you uninstall.
- Report problems through **Settings > Support > Report a bug**.
