Video needs a steady connection. Most playback problems are fixed by checking your network and sound settings.

## Who this is for

Anyone watching posts, clips, stories or lives in the app or on **app.zudomart.com**.

## No sound

- Tap **Unmute video** on the video. Feed videos often start muted.
- Turn up your phone's media volume, not just the ringer.
- On iPhone, check the silent switch or Action button.
- Check that your earphones or Bluetooth speaker are connected, or disconnect them.

## Video won't start or keeps buffering

1. Check your connection. Move closer to your Wi-Fi router or switch to mobile data.
2. Pause the video for a few seconds to let it load, then play.
3. Close and reopen the app.
4. If you've turned on any data saving setting, video may load at lower quality or more slowly. See [Data and storage](/help/notifications-and-settings/data-and-storage).
5. Update the app. See [Update the app](/help/troubleshooting/update-the-app).
6. Restart your phone.

## Black screen or blurred video

- A blurred video with a warning is sensitive content. Tap to view it if you choose to. Under-18s can't see it. See [Sensitive content and age](/help/posts-and-feed/sensitive-content-and-age).
- If only the sound is missing and the post says the audio was muted, a copyright claim removed the soundtrack. See [Copyright and account status](/help/safety-and-reporting/copyright-and-account-status).
- If a video you just posted shows **"This file is still being processed."**, wait a few minutes. Longer videos take longer.

## Lives

- **"Couldn't load this live."** Tap **Try again**. The live may also have just ended.
- **"This live is paused while we review it"** Our safety checks paused it. It resumes if the review finds no problem. See [Live safety](/help/live/live-safety).
- Lives need a stronger connection than recorded videos. If a live keeps freezing, try Wi-Fi.

## Clips

- **"Couldn't load clips."** Pull down or tap **Try again**.
- Swipe up for the next clip. Tap to pause, double-tap the left or right side to skip 10 seconds. See [Clip player controls](/help/clips-and-stories/clip-player-controls).

## On the web

- Use an up-to-date Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox.
- Allow sound for **app.zudomart.com** if your browser blocked autoplay sound, then click the video.
- Turn off extensions that block media.

## If it still doesn't work

Go to **Settings > Support > Report a bug** with a link to the video and your phone model.
