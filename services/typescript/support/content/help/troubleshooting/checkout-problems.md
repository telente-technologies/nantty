If checkout isn't working, the message on screen usually tells you why. Here is what each one means and what to do.

## Who this is for

Buyers paying for orders in the app or on **app.nantty.com**.

## If your payment didn't go through

The payment screen says **"Payment didn't go through"** and "No money was taken. You can try again or choose another method."

1. Tap **Open checkout again** and follow the on-screen checkout.
2. Try another method: debit or credit card, bank transfer or USSD.
3. If you're sure you paid (for example by bank transfer), tap **I've paid, check status**. We confirm every payment on our servers before your order is placed, so a transfer can take a few minutes to show.
4. Still stuck? Tap **Get help with this payment**. It opens a support ticket linked to this payment.

If money left your account but there's no order, don't pay again. Tap **I've paid, check status**, then contact us. See [Payment failed](/help/payments-and-wallet/payment-failed).

## Cart and order messages

- **"There is not enough stock for one of the items."** Lower the quantity or remove the item.
- **"That product is not available right now."** The seller paused or removed it.
- **"None of the sellers in your cart ship to that address."** Choose another delivery address or seller.
- **"Items from stores that sell in different currencies must be bought separately."** Check out each store on its own.
- **"Checkout is not available in this store's currency yet."** You can't buy from this store yet.
- **"You cannot buy from your own store."**
- **"Your cart is empty."** Add items again; they may have sold out.
- **"That checkout is closed. Start a new checkout."** The checkout timed out. Go back to your cart and start again.
- **"This order was cancelled, so there is nothing to pay. Start a new checkout if you still want it."**

## Store and verification messages

- **"This store has reached its sales limit while the seller's business verification is pending, so it can't take this order right now."** Try again later or buy elsewhere. See [Buying from unverified stores](/help/buying-and-orders/buying-from-unverified-stores).
- **"This store is not verified, so its orders can only be paid through escrow."** Choose escrow at checkout.
- **"This store cannot take payments yet."** The seller hasn't finished setting up.
- **"Verify your email to continue."** Verify your email first.

## Service messages

- **"Our payment partner is unavailable. Try again in a few minutes."**
- **"The payment provider could not complete the request. Try again shortly."**
- **"We could not prepare the payment. Try again in a moment."**

These are temporary. Wait a few minutes and try again. You won't be charged twice for the same order.

## Stay safe

Only pay through Nantty checkout. Never pay a seller directly by bank transfer outside the app, even if checkout seems slow. Nantty will never ask for your card PIN or one-time codes in chat.
