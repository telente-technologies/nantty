Most loading problems come from the connection or an old version of the app. Work through these steps in order.

## Who this is for

Anyone using the Zudomart app on iPhone or Android, or **app.zudomart.com** in a browser.

## Quick fixes

1. **Tap Try again** on the error screen. Many errors clear on a second try.
2. **Check your connection.** Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data, or turn Airplane mode on and off. If your data bundle has run out, pages won't load.
3. **Pull down to refresh** the screen you're on.
4. **Close the app completely** and open it again.
5. **Update the app.** See [Update the app](/help/troubleshooting/update-the-app).
6. **Restart your phone.**
7. **Free up space.** Phones that are almost full can struggle to load photos and videos.

## On Android: clear the cache

Go to your phone's **Settings > Apps > Zudomart > Storage** and tap **Clear cache**. Don't tap **Clear data** unless you're happy to sign in again.

## On the web

- Refresh the page. If that doesn't work, do a hard refresh (Ctrl and Shift and R on Windows, Cmd and Shift and R on Mac).
- Use an up-to-date version of Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox.
- Turn off ad blockers or extensions for **app.zudomart.com**, then try again.

## What the messages mean

- **"You're offline"** with "Check your connection, then try again." Your phone has no internet. Zudomart may show what it saved earlier ("You're offline — showing saved content") until you're back online.
- **"Something went wrong."** A temporary problem. Tap **Try again**.
- **"Couldn't load your orders."** (or wallet, cart, profile and so on) That one section didn't load. Tap **Try again** or pull down to refresh.
- **"The service is unavailable. Try again."** One of our services is briefly down. Wait a few minutes.
- **"Too many requests. Try again shortly."** You (or your network) sent a lot of requests quickly. Wait a minute.
- **"Your session has ended. Sign in again."** Sign in again. See [Can't sign in](/help/troubleshooting/cant-sign-in).
- **"Accept the updated policies to continue."** We updated our terms. Open the app and accept them to carry on.

For more, see [Common error messages](/help/troubleshooting/common-error-messages).

## If it still doesn't work

Go to **Settings > Support > Report a bug** and tell us your phone model, app version (in **Settings > About > Version**), what you were doing and what you saw. A screenshot helps. See [Report a bug](/help/contact-support/report-a-bug).
