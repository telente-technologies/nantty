Push notifications need to be on in both Nantty and your phone. Work through these checks.

## Who this is for

Anyone missing push notifications on iPhone or Android.

## Check Nantty's setting

1. Go to **Settings > Notification**.
2. Make sure **Push notifications** shows **ON**. Tap it to switch.

If you see "Couldn't load your notification settings.", tap **Try again**.

## Check your phone's setting

1. In **Settings > Notification**, tap **System notification settings**. This opens your phone's settings for Nantty.
2. Allow notifications, and turn on sounds, banners and lock screen alerts.

## Other things that block notifications

- **Do Not Disturb or Focus mode** silences notifications. Check your phone's quick settings.
- **Battery saving (Android)**: some phones stop apps in the background. Go to your phone's **Settings > Apps > Nantty > Battery** and choose **Unrestricted**, or turn off battery optimisation for Nantty.
- **Low Power Mode (iPhone)** can delay some notifications.
- **Signed out**: notifications only go to devices where you're signed in. Open the app to check.
- **Old app version**: update the app. See [Update the app](/help/troubleshooting/update-the-app).

## Missing notifications from one account or post

- **Posts and lives from someone you follow**: tap the bell on their profile to get notified. See [Post notifications bell](/help/notifications-and-settings/post-notifications-bell).
- **A post you muted**: if you turned off notifications for a post from its menu, turn them back on there. See [Mute a post](/help/notifications-and-settings/mute-a-post).
- **Chats**: check you haven't muted the conversation, and check **Requests** and **Spam** in your inbox for chats from people you don't follow.

## Still not working

1. Turn push notifications **OFF** in **Settings > Notification**, wait a few seconds, then turn them **ON**.
2. Sign out and sign back in.
3. Restart your phone.
4. If nothing works, uninstall and reinstall the app from the App Store or Google Play. Read [Chat devices and keys](/help/chat/chat-devices-and-keys) first, because chat keys live on your device.

Then go to **Settings > Support > Report a bug** and tell us your phone model and which notifications you're missing.

## Good to know

Important account and money messages, such as sign-in alerts, new payout accounts and receipts, are also sent by email. See [Not getting emails or codes](/help/troubleshooting/not-getting-emails).
