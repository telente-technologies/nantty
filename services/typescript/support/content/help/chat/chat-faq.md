## Can Zudomart read my messages?

No. Message text, photos, videos, voice notes and files are end-to-end encrypted. Locations and polls are not, and our servers know who you chat with and when. See [End-to-end encryption](/help/chat/end-to-end-encryption).

## How do I check a chat is really encrypted?

Open **Conversation info** > **Security code** and compare the 60-digit code with the other person in person.

## I got a new phone. Where are my old messages?

Old messages don't move to a new device, because only your old phone had the keys. They show "Sent before this device joined secure chat." See [Chat devices and keys](/help/chat/chat-devices-and-keys).

## How many devices can I use chat on?

Up to 5, including the web app.

## Someone says they messaged me but I can't see it.

Check the **Requests** and **Spam** folders using the dropdown on the inbox title. See [Your inbox folders](/help/chat/chat-folders).

## How do I stop strangers messaging me?

**Chat settings > Who can send you message requests** > **People I follow** or **Nobody**. See [Message requests](/help/chat/chat-requests-and-spam).

## Why is a chat in Spam?

Because of how the sender behaves, such as messaging many new people at once. We never read messages to decide. Tap **Not spam** to move it to Requests.

## Why did my photos disappear?

Photos, videos, voice notes and documents are deleted 30 days after they're sent. Text stays. See [Media expiry](/help/chat/chat-media-and-encryption).

## Can I edit or unsend a message?

Not at the moment. You can turn on disappearing messages (**24 hours** or **7 days**) in **Conversation info**.

## How do I share a post or product in a chat?

Tap **Share** > **Send in chat**, pick up to 20 chats and add a note if you like. See [Share to chat](/help/chat/share-to-chat).

## How big can a group be?

Up to 256 members. See [Group chats](/help/chat/group-chats).

## I forgot my chat passcode.

Tap **Forgot passcode?** > **Log out**, then sign in again and set a new one. See [Chat passcode](/help/chat/chat-passcode-and-notifications).

## How do I mute one chat?

Open the chat menu > **Notifications for this chat** > **Mute for 8 hours**, **Mute for 1 week** or **Mute always**.

## How do I restore a lost streak?

Either of you can restore it within about 48 hours, 3 times a month or 6 with Premium. See [Chat streaks](/help/chat/streaks).

## How do I report someone in chat?

Chat menu > **Report user** or **Report and block**. You choose which messages to send. See [Reporting chats](/help/chat/reporting-chats).
