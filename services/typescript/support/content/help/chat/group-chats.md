Group chats let you talk with friends, family, customers or a community in one place. Like all chats, they're end-to-end encrypted.

## Create a group

1. Open **Messages** and tap the menu.
2. Tap **New group**.
3. Pick the people to add. The button shows how many you've chosen, for example **Create group (5)**.
4. Tap it to create the group, then give it a name.

You can add up to 100 people at a time, and a group can have up to 256 members.

The same menu also has **New channel**, for broadcasting to subscribers, where only admins post.

## Roles

- **Owner**: the person who created the group.
- **Admins**: can rename the group, **Add members**, **Make group admin**, **Remove from group**, change disappearing messages and control @all. Only the owner can remove an admin.
- **Members**: can send messages, react and mention others.

Manage all of this from the group's **Conversation info**.

## Mention everyone

Type the at sign (@) and a name to mention one person, or **@all** to notify everyone. Admins can turn on **Only admins can @all**: "When on, only admins can notify every member with @all."

## Leave a group

In **Conversation info**, tap **Leave group**: "You'll stop getting messages from this group. Someone will need to add you back." For channels, tap **Unsubscribe** or **Leave channel**.

## Report a group or member

From the group menu, choose **Report group** or **Report a member**. See [Reporting chats](/help/chat/reporting-chats).

## Good to know

- Streaks are for one-to-one chats only, not groups.
- Mute a busy group with **Mute for 8 hours**, **Mute for 1 week** or **Mute always**.
- If someone in the group adds a new device, you may see "A member's security code changed." That's normal.

## If something goes wrong

- **"A group can have up to 256 members."** The group is full.
- **"Only admins can post in this channel. Long press a post to react."**
- **"This group has too many devices for one encrypted message."** Try again later.
