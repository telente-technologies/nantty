---
title: Group chats
summary: Create groups, add members, make admins and mention everyone with @all.
tags: groups, group chat, admin
related: mentions, reporting-chats
---

- Start a group from **New message > New group** and add members.
- Group admins can add or remove members, change admins and edit group info.
- Mention members with @, or everyone with **@all**. Admins can turn on "Only admins can @all".
- Leave a group from Group info. Report a group or a member from the same screen.
