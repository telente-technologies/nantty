Streaks are for one-to-one chats.

## How a streak grows

The streak goes up by one each day you **both** send a message on your own local day. Each person's day follows their own time zone.

## Hourglass

An hourglass appears when there are 4 hours left in either person's day and the streak still needs a message.

## Broken streaks

If a day is missed the streak breaks. Either of you can restore it for both within about 48 hours. You get 3 restores a month, or 6 with [Premium](/help/payments-and-wallet/premium). Restores reset on the 1st of each month and don't carry over.

## Milestones

Every 100 days you both get a badge and a celebration.
