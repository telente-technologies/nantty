A streak counts how many days in a row you and a friend have both messaged each other. It's a fun way to keep in touch.

## Who this is for

Streaks are for one-to-one chats only. Groups and channels don't have streaks.

## How a streak grows

The streak goes up by one each day you **both** send a message on your own local day. Each person's day follows their own time zone, so a friend abroad isn't disadvantaged.

## The hourglass

An hourglass appears when there are 4 hours left in either person's day and the streak still needs a message. Turn on **Streak reminders** in **Chat settings > Push notifications** to get "A nudge 4 hours before your day ends if a streak is at risk".

## Broken streaks

If a day is missed, the streak breaks. Either of you can restore it for both of you within about 48 hours.

- You get 3 restores a month, or 6 with [Premium](/help/payments-and-wallet/premium).
- The chat shows how many you have left, for example "2 of 3 restores left this month. Premium members get 6 a month."
- Restores reset on the 1st of each month and don't carry over.

## Milestones

Every 100 days (100, 200, 300 and so on) you both get a badge and a celebration.

## If something goes wrong

- **"You have no streak restores left this month."** Wait until the 1st, or get [Premium](/help/payments-and-wallet/premium) for 6 a month.
- The restore option has gone: the 48-hour window has passed, so the streak starts again from your next shared day.
