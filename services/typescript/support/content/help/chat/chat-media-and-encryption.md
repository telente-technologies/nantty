Chats are end-to-end encrypted. See [End-to-end encryption](/help/chat/end-to-end-encryption) for exactly what that covers. This page explains how long things last in a chat and what you can send.

## Media expiry (30 days)

Chat settings explain: "Photos, videos and voice notes in chats are deleted automatically 30 days after they are sent. Your text messages are kept."

Documents expire after 30 days too. After that:

- photos, videos and voice notes show **Media expired**,
- documents show **File expired**,
- the text of your messages stays.

Save anything you want to keep to your phone before then.

## Disappearing messages

Open the chat menu > **Conversation info** to set disappearing messages to **Off**, **24 hours** or **7 days**. In groups, admins choose this setting.

## What you can send

- **Text, photos and videos.**
- **Voice notes** from 1 second to 5 minutes.
- **Documents** up to 100 MB: PDF, Office files, text, zip or images.
- **Location**: tap **Share location**, then **Send current location**, or share your live location for **15 minutes**, **1 hour** or **8 hours**. "Your location is shared only with this chat." Locations are not end-to-end encrypted, so Zudomart's servers can see them. Only share with people you trust.
- **Polls**: **Create poll** with 2 to 6 options, and turn on **Allow multiple answers** if you like, then **Send poll**. Polls are not end-to-end encrypted either.
- **Reactions**: press and hold a message to open **React to message** and pick an emoji.
- **Posts, products, stores, profiles and lives** using **Send in chat**. See [Share to chat](/help/chat/share-to-chat).

## Link previews

Previews of outside links are created on the sender's phone and travel encrypted with the message. Shortened or unusual links show a warning before they open. Links to Zudomart content show a card that respects the content's privacy.

## Good to know

- You can't edit or unsend a message once it's sent, so check before you tap send.
- A new phone doesn't get your old messages. See [Chat devices and keys](/help/chat/chat-devices-and-keys).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Files can be up to 100 MB."** Send a smaller file or a link to it.
- **"Live location can be shared for up to 8 hours."**
- **"A poll needs a question and 2 to 6 options."**
