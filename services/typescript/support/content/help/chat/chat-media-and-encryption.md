## End-to-end encryption

Messages are end-to-end encrypted, so only the people in the chat can read them. Zudomart can't read your messages. Each device has its own keys.

## Media expiry

Photos, videos, voice notes and documents sent in chats are deleted 30 days after they are sent. After that the message shows "Media expired". Text messages stay. Save anything you want to keep.

## Link previews

Previews of outside links are created on the sender's phone and travel encrypted with the message. Shortened or unusual links show a warning before they open. Links to Zudomart content show a card that respects the content's privacy.

## Other things you can send

Locations (current or live for 15 minutes, 1 hour or 8 hours, shared only with that chat), documents up to 100 MB, polls with 2 to 6 options, and posts, products, stores, profiles or lives using **Send in chat**.
