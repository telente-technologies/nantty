Your inbox has three folders. Switch between them with the dropdown at the top: **Chats**, **Requests** and **Spam**.

## Requests

When someone you haven't chatted with messages you, it lands in Requests. You can **Accept**, **Delete**, **Block** or **Report**. They can't see read receipts until you accept.

Choose who can send you requests in Chat settings: Everyone, People I follow, Followers, or Nobody. This is enforced by our servers, so people outside your choice can't start a chat with you.

## Spam

Messages are sent to Spam based on how the sender behaves, such as messaging lots of strangers at once. Zudomart never reads your message content to decide. Tap **Not spam** to move a chat to Requests.
