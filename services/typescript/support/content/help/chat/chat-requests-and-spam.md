Your inbox has three folders: **Chats**, **Requests** and **Spam**. Switch between them with the dropdown on the inbox title. See [Your inbox folders](/help/chat/chat-folders).

## Who can send you message requests

1. Open **Messages**, tap the menu and choose **Chat settings**.
2. Under **Who can send you message requests**, choose:
   - **Everyone**
   - **People I follow**
   - **Followers**
   - **Nobody**

This is enforced by our servers, so people outside your choice can't start a chat with you. They see "This account isn't accepting message requests from you."

People you follow can always message you, and their messages go straight to **Chats**.

## Requests

When someone you haven't chatted with messages you, it lands in **Requests**. "They won't see that you've read this until you accept."

You can:

- **Accept**: moves the chat to **Chats** and lets them see read receipts.
- **Delete**: removes the request.
- **Block**: "They won't be able to message you. You can unblock them in chat settings."
- **Report**, or **Report and block**. See [Reporting chats](/help/chat/reporting-chats).

## Spam

Chats go to Spam based on how the sender behaves, never on what they write. Zudomart can't read your messages. Reasons include:

- an account that's sending to many new people in a short time,
- a new account messaging lots of people,
- our behaviour model flagging the account.

A spam chat shows "Flagged by Zudomart based on this account's activity." Tap **Not spam** to move it to **Requests**, then accept it if you want.

## Stay safe

Many scams start with a message from a stranger. Don't pay anyone outside Zudomart checkout, don't share one-time codes, and be careful with unusual links. Zudomart will never ask for your password, card PIN or codes in a chat. See [Stay safe when buying and selling](/help/safety-and-reporting/stay-safe-when-buying-and-selling).

## If something goes wrong

- **"You can't message this account."** One of you has blocked the other, or the account isn't available.
- **"You can't message yourself."**
