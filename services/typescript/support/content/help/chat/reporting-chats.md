If someone in a chat is harassing you, scamming you or breaking our rules, report them. Our team reviews every report.

## How to report

**In a one-to-one chat**, open the chat menu and choose:

- **Report user**,
- **Report and block**,
- **Report conversation**.

**In a group**, open the group menu and choose **Report group** or **Report a member**.

**From Requests and Spam**, tap **Report** or **Report and block** before accepting.

## Pick a reason

- **Child safety** (marked Urgent and reviewed first)
- **Self-harm or suicide** (we also show support resources)
- **Violence or threats**
- **Harassment or bullying**
- **Hate**
- **Sexual content**
- **Spam or scam**
- **Fraud or payment scam**
- **Impersonation**
- **Selling prohibited items**
- **Other** (you must add details, up to 500 characters)

## Choose the evidence

Chats are end-to-end encrypted, so Nantty can't see them. The report screen says: "Chats are end-to-end encrypted, so Nantty can't read them. Only the messages you choose below leave your device."

Choose either:

- **Last messages**: the last 10, 20 or 50, or
- **Pick messages**: select up to 100 yourself.

"These messages will be sent to Nantty for review. The other person won't be told who reported them."

## After you report

You'll see "Thanks, we'll review this", with quick options to **Block**, **Mute** or **Restrict** ("Their messages go to Requests"). Follow your reports in **Settings > Support > My reports**.

## In danger?

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first. In Nigeria, call 112.

## Stay safe

Nantty staff will never message you asking for your password, card PIN or one-time codes. Report anyone who does.

## If something goes wrong

- **"You've sent too many reports. Try again later."** You can send up to 5 reports an hour.
