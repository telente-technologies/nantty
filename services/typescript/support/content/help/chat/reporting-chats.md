## How to report

- **A person**: open the chat menu and choose Report user, or Report and block.
- **A message**: press and hold the message and choose Report message.
- **A conversation or group**: chat menu > Report conversation, or Group info > Report group or Report member.
- From Requests and Spam you can also Report or Report and block.

## What gets sent

Chats are end-to-end encrypted, so Zudomart can't see them. When you report, your device attaches only the messages you select, with your consent. The person you report is never told who reported them.

## Reasons

Child safety (reviewed urgently), self-harm (we also show support resources), violence or threats, harassment, hate, sexual content, spam or scams, fraud, impersonation, prohibited items, or something else.

You can follow your reports in Settings > Support > My reports.
