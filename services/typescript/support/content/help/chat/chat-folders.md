Your Zudomart inbox keeps conversations with people you know apart from messages from strangers and likely spam.

## Switch folders

1. Open **Messages**.
2. Tap the title at the top. A dropdown opens.
3. Choose **Chats**, **Requests** or **Spam**.

## Chats

Conversations you've started, accepted, or that come from people you follow. Chats has two tabs: **All Messages** and **Direct Messages** (one-to-one chats only, without groups and channels). The menu on this screen also has **New group**, **New channel** and **Chat settings**.

## Requests

Messages from people you haven't chatted with and don't follow. A note reminds you: "They won't see that you've read this until you accept."

For each request you can **Accept** (it moves to Chats), **Delete**, **Block**, **Report** or **Report and block**. If it's empty: "No message requests."

You control who can send you requests in **Chat settings > Who can send you message requests**. See [Message requests and spam](/help/chat/chat-requests-and-spam).

## Spam

Conversations Zudomart flagged because of how the sender is behaving, for example a new account messaging lots of people, or someone sending to many new people in an hour. A note says "Flagged by Zudomart based on this account's activity." We never read your messages to decide.

For each one you can tap **Not spam**, **Delete**, **Block**, **Report** or **Report and block**. **Not spam** moves the conversation to **Requests**, where you can then accept it. If it's empty: "No chats flagged as spam."

## Good to know

- If you follow someone, their messages always go straight to **Chats**, never to Requests or Spam.
- Story replies arrive in the same way as messages. See [Replying to stories](/help/clips-and-stories/story-replies-and-reactions).
- Can't find a message? Check **Requests** and **Spam** before assuming it never arrived.

## Stay safe

Scammers often message strangers. Be careful with requests asking you to pay outside Zudomart, share codes or click unusual links. Zudomart will never ask for your password, card PIN or one-time codes in a chat.
