**Send in chat** lets you share something you found on Zudomart straight into your conversations, so friends see a proper card instead of a bare link.

## What you can share

- posts and clips,
- products and stores,
- profiles,
- lives (the card shows **Live now** while the live is on).

## How to share

1. Tap **Share** on a post or clip, or open the menu on a product, store or profile.
2. In the **Share** sheet, tap **Send in chat**. (You can also choose **Copy link** or **Share link** to use other apps.)
3. Tick one or more chats from the list.
4. Optionally write in "Add a note (optional)", up to 1,000 characters.
5. Tap **Send**, or **Send to** followed by the number of chats.

You'll see "Sent" or "Shared in" followed by the number of chats. On the web you'll see "Sent to" the number of chats, with **Open messages**.

On a profile, choose **Send in chat** from **More options**. See [Sharing your profile](/help/profiles-and-privacy/share-your-profile).

## Good to know

- You can send to up to 20 chats at once.
- Channels where you're only a subscriber don't appear in the list, because you can't post in them.
- Your note is end-to-end encrypted like any message. Zudomart can see which item you shared, so it can show the card. See [End-to-end encryption](/help/chat/end-to-end-encryption).
- The card respects privacy. If the item is deleted, made private or hidden from the person, the card shows "This is no longer available."
- Sharing doesn't repost anything to your profile. To share with all your followers, use [Repost or quote](/help/posts-and-feed/repost-and-quote).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Couldn't send"**: check your connection and try again.
- **"You can't message this account."** One of you has blocked the other.
- **"Only admins can post in this channel. Long press a post to react."**
