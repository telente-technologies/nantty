## Chat passcode

Chat settings > Passcode sets a 4 to 6 digit code, with Face ID or fingerprint if your phone supports it, and an auto-lock delay. The passcode stays on your device and is never sent to Zudomart. If you forget it, sign out and sign in again to reset it.

## Mute a chat

Open the chat menu and choose **Mute** for 8 hours, 1 week or always.

## Notifications

In Chat settings > Notifications choose alerts for messages, requests, groups and streak reminders, and whether notifications show a message preview.
