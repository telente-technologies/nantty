Chat settings let you add an extra lock to your messages and control how loudly chats reach you. Open **Messages**, tap the menu and choose **Chat settings**.

## Chat passcode

1. In **Chat settings**, tap **Chat passcode**.
2. Set a 4 to 6 digit passcode.
3. Turn on **Unlock with biometrics** to use Face ID or your fingerprint, if your phone supports it.
4. Choose **Auto-lock**: **Immediately**, **After 1 min**, **After 5 min**, **After 15 min** or **After 1 hour**.

"The passcode locks your chats on this device only. It is never sent to Zudomart. If you forget it, you'll need to log in again to reset it."

When your chats are locked you'll see **Chats are locked**. Enter your passcode or tap **Use biometrics**. Use **Change passcode** or **Remove passcode** to update it later.

### Forgot your passcode?

Tap **Forgot passcode?** and then **Log out**. Sign back in and set a new passcode. Your account is safe, but remember that a fresh sign-in on a device may not bring back older chat messages. See [Chat devices and keys](/help/chat/chat-devices-and-keys).

## Mute a chat

1. Open the chat and its menu.
2. Open **Notifications for this chat**.
3. Choose **Mute for 8 hours**, **Mute for 1 week** or **Mute always**.

Tap **Unmute** to turn alerts back on. The other person isn't told.

## Chat notifications

In **Chat settings > Push notifications**, turn each type on or off:

- **Messages**
- **Message requests**
- **Groups and channels**
- **Streak reminders**: "A nudge 4 hours before your day ends if a streak is at risk"
- **Show message preview**: turn off to hide message text in notifications

For all other notifications, see [Notification settings](/help/notifications-and-settings/notification-settings).

## Good to know

- The passcode is per device. Set it on each phone you use.
- The web app doesn't have a chat passcode. Lock your computer when you step away.
- **Blocked accounts** in Chat settings lists everyone you've blocked.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Wrong passcode, try again"**: check the digits. If you can't remember it, use **Forgot passcode?**.
