Every order arrives in the app and in **Orders** on **dashboard.zudomart.com**. These are marketplace orders from Zudomart buyers.

## Who this is for

Sellers and team members with order access.

## How to handle an order

1. A new order shows **New order — accept or reject**. Tap **Accept order**, or **Reject** if you can't fulfil it.
2. Pack the items.
3. Ship it. Enter **Ships from**, **Courier**, **Tracking number** (4 to 40 characters) and an optional **Tracking link**. In the app use **Mark as shipped** and pick a courier from the list. If the buyer collects, use **Mark ready for pickup**.
4. When it arrives, tap **Mark as delivered**.
5. The buyer confirms receipt, or escrow releases to your wallet 3 days after delivery unless they report a problem.

## Rejecting or cancelling

Choose **Reject and refund** (new orders) or **Cancel and refund** (later), and write a **Reason shown to the buyer** (5 to 500 characters). The buyer always gets a full refund.

## Shipping costs

You set where you deliver and what it costs in **Delivery > Shipping rules**. Buyers pay one fee per store order, never per item. See [Shipping settings](/help/selling/shipping-settings).

## Good to know

- Escrow orders show the money as **Pending (escrow)** until release. See [Seller payouts](/help/selling/seller-payouts).
- Direct-payment orders pay you right away, with only the processing fee.
- Buyers can cancel by themselves only before you start preparing. Accept quickly so they aren't left waiting.
- A dispute pauses release. See [Responding to a dispute](/help/selling/respond-to-a-dispute).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Only new orders can be accepted or rejected."** The order has moved on or was cancelled. Refresh it.
- **"This order is under dispute; its funds stay frozen until the dispute is resolved."**

## Stay safe

Only mark an order delivered when it has really arrived. Never ask buyers to confirm receipt before they have the parcel, or to pay you outside Zudomart.
