## Handling orders

1. A new order arrives in the app and the dashboard.
2. **Accept** or reject it. Rejected orders are refunded to the buyer.
3. Pack and ship, then mark it **Shipped** with tracking if you have it.
4. Mark it **Delivered** when it arrives. The buyer confirms delivery or escrow releases automatically 3 days later.

## Shipping rules

Zudomart doesn't deliver for you. In Delivery > Shipping rules you choose destinations by country, state and local government area, and set a flat fee or free shipping for each group, plus an optional free-shipping threshold.

The most specific rule wins: area, then state, then country. Buyers see the shipping cost before they pay, and it's charged once per store per order, never per item. We recalculate it at checkout so prices can't be changed.

If you don't ship to a buyer's address, your items are left out of their checkout.
