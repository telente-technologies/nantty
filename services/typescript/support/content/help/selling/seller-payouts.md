## Your balance

The dashboard's Payments and finance section shows money **in escrow**, **locked** (released in the last 24 hours), **available** and **payouts in progress**, plus fees, refunds and statements.

## Timing

1. Buyer pays: money is held in escrow.
2. Buyer confirms delivery, or 3 days pass after delivery with no dispute: escrow is released, minus the escrow and processing fees.
3. 24 hours later the money is available.
4. The daily payout run sends it to your bank.

Direct payments for verified stores settle to your bank on the next working day instead.

A dispute pauses the release until it's resolved. Set up your bank first, see [Setting up payouts](/help/creators-and-earnings/payout-setup).
