Your sales money lives in your own wallet, held by our licensed payment partner. Zudomart never holds it. This is how an order turns into money you can withdraw.

## Who this is for

Sellers. Escrow and the wallet balance are for Nigeria. Outside Nigeria, buyers pay you directly and the money settles straight to your payout account, so the escrow steps below don't apply.

## How order money reaches you (Nigeria)

1. The buyer pays with escrow. The money stays locked in the buyer's own wallet and shows on your side as **Pending (escrow)**.
2. You ship. The buyer taps **Confirm delivery**, or 3 days pass after delivery without a dispute.
3. The money moves to your wallet, minus the escrow fee and processing fee, and sits in **Locked** for 24 hours.
4. After 24 hours it becomes **Available to withdraw**.
5. You tap **Withdraw** and choose a payout account.

A dispute pauses step 2 until it is resolved.

## The lines on your wallet

Open **Wallet** in the app to see seven lines:

- **Available to withdraw**: "Sales and creator earnings you can withdraw now, after anything you owe."
- **Pending (escrow)**: "Order money held until the buyer confirms delivery."
- **Locked**: "Released order money in its short safety hold before it becomes available."
- **Creator earnings**: gift, subscription and ad-share earnings on their way to your available balance.
- **Ad credit**: prepaid credit for ads and boosts. It can't be withdrawn.
- **Coins**: coins are not cash.
- **Owed to Zudomart**: "Refunds or chargebacks taken from your next earnings."

The dashboard on **dashboard.zudomart.com** shows the same money plus the fee breakdown and statements.

## Withdrawing

- You need payout verification (level 1): in Nigeria, your BVN consent and a bank account in your own name. See [Verification levels](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/verification-levels).
- Sellers must also finish business verification before withdrawing sales money. See [Verify your store](/help/selling/verify-your-store).
- Withdrawals need a fresh security check, go only to an account in your own name, and have daily limits. See [Withdrawal limits](/help/wallet-security/withdrawal-limits).
- A flat payout fee per withdrawal is shown before you confirm.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Finish business verification to withdraw your sales earnings."** Your earnings are safe and keep building. Finish verification, then withdraw.
- **"Verify your payout details to continue. Nothing you have earned is lost while you verify."** Complete payout verification first.
- **"Withdrawals are locked"** on the **Withdraw** screen means payout setup isn't finished. Tap **Finish payout setup**.
- **"Amount is more than your available balance."** Money still in **Pending (escrow)** or **Locked** can't be withdrawn yet.

## Stay safe

Zudomart will never ask for your password, card PIN or security codes, and will never ask you to "release" an order by sending money. Withdrawals only go to a bank account in your own name.
