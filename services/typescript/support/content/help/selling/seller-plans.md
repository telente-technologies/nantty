**Free** includes orders, products, stock levels and alerts, customers, messages, delivery, reviews, storefront, discount codes, payouts and core sales and inventory reports.

**Pro** adds advanced inventory, bulk tools, advanced analytics and reports, customer segments, team, flash sales, bundles, Mart featuring, ad results, multiple warehouses, automated alerts, and API keys and webhooks.

**Business** adds multiple stores, per-member permissions and accounting integrations.

## Buying a plan

Plans are bought on the web in the dashboard under Settings > Billing. Eligible stores can start a free trial with a saved card; a small verification charge is refunded straight away, and we remind you before the trial ends. Yearly billing saves 20%.
