You can run a store from Ghana, Kenya, South Africa and other countries. The rules are stricter than in Nigeria because there is no escrow.

## Who this is for

Sellers whose store sells in a currency other than naira.

## Before you can sell

"Outside Nigeria, sellers must verify their identity and payout account before selling." That means:

1. Complete the identity check with our identity partner (a government ID).
2. Add a payout bank or mobile-money account in your own name.

Until both are done, you can't publish products and buyers can't check out your items. See [Verification levels](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/verification-levels).

## How you get paid

- Buyers pay you directly through checkout. There is no escrow and no stored Zudomart balance.
- The money settles to your payout account.
- You pay only the payment processing fee. No escrow fee and no commission.

## Good to know

- Set local prices for each country with **Prices by country**. See [Prices per country](/help/selling/prices-per-country).
- Buyers buy at their own risk, so your verification, rating and reviews matter a lot. Answer messages quickly and ship with tracking.
- Refunds are up to you: use the refund button on the order. See [Refunding a buyer](/help/selling/seller-refunds).
- Paid plans are priced in your local currency. See [Seller plans](/help/selling/seller-plans).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Verify your identity and add a payout account in your own name before selling."** Finish verification, then publish again.
- Buyers may see **"This seller must finish identity verification before they can sell in your country. Remove their items to continue."** This means your verification isn't complete.

## Stay safe

Only take payment through Zudomart checkout. Payments outside the app aren't covered by any of our protection and can get your store closed.
