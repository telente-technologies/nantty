Some things can't be sold on Nantty at all, and some only by verified sellers who meet extra rules. These lists come from our Community Guidelines on nantty.com.

## Who this is for

All sellers, including anyone selling on live or promoting products in posts and ads.

## Never allowed

You may not list, sell, promote or arrange the sale of:

- Firearms, ammunition, gun parts, explosives, fireworks without a licence, and weapons designed to hurt people.
- Illegal drugs, drug paraphernalia and controlled substances.
- Prescription-only medicines, unregistered drugs, injectables and medical devices without NAFDAC registration where required, and skin creams with mercury or excessive hydroquinone.
- Tobacco, cigarettes, vapes, e-liquids and shisha.
- Counterfeit, replica or "first copy" goods, and anything that infringes copyright, trademarks or designs.
- Stolen goods, and goods whose ownership you can't prove.
- Identity documents, certificates, registered SIM cards, bank accounts, cards, account logins and personal data.
- Counterfeit money, and unlicensed loans, investments, crypto investment schemes and foreign exchange services.
- Unlicensed gambling, betting and lotteries.
- Adult sexual services and sexual content.
- People, organs and body parts.
- Endangered wildlife and products from them, such as ivory and pangolin scales.
- Police, military, customs and other official uniforms and insignia.
- Hazardous chemicals, banned pesticides and recalled products.
- Antiquities that can't be lawfully sold.
- Exam questions, answers and cheating services.
- Hacking tools, malware and services to get around security or verification.
- Goods on the Nigeria Customs Service import prohibition list, or banned in the buyer's country.

## Restricted: verified sellers only, with extra rules

- **Alcohol**: licensed sellers only, buyers 18 or over, where the law allows.
- **Food, cosmetics, supplements and herbal products**: show the NAFDAC number where required, and make no medical claims.
- **Electronics**: must meet Standards Organisation of Nigeria requirements, and refurbished items can't be sold as new.
- **Drones and regulated equipment**: only where the buyer may own them.
- **Knives and tools**: for lawful household or work use, never to under-18s.

## What happens if you break the rules

Listings can be hidden or removed. Repeated or serious breaches lead to warnings, strikes, selling restrictions, a lower sales limit, suspension or a permanent ban. Money that belongs to you isn't forfeited. See [Account health and strikes](/help/safety-and-reporting/account-health-and-strikes).

## Good to know

- Tag brands only on genuine goods. Brands can see listings that use their name and report counterfeits. See [Brand pages](/help/business-and-brands/brand-pages).
- Not sure about an item? Ask before you list. [Contact support](/help/contact-support/contact-support).
