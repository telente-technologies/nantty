Buyers see prices in their own currency. You can let Zudomart convert your price, or set exact prices for each country.

## Who this is for

Sellers who ship to more than one country.

## How to set country prices

1. At **dashboard.zudomart.com**, open **Products** and the product.
2. Scroll to **Prices by country**.
3. Each country has its own box: Nigeria (NGN), Ghana (GHS), Kenya (KES), South Africa (ZAR), United Kingdom (GBP), Eurozone (EUR) and United States (USD). An empty box shows **Default**.
4. Enter a price for the countries you want and save.

## How it works

"Leave a country empty and buyers there pay your price, shown to them as an approximate local amount. A country price is charged in that country's currency."

- If one variant has no price for a country, buyers there pay your default price for the whole order from you.
- A compare-at (was) price must be higher than the price.
- Buyers only see products in countries you ship to. See [Shipping settings](/help/selling/shipping-settings).

## Good to know

- Converted prices are marked as approximate because exchange rates move.
- A buyer can't mix stores that sell in different currencies in one checkout.
- Selling outside Nigeria needs identity verification first. See [Selling outside Nigeria](/help/selling/selling-outside-nigeria).

## If something goes wrong

- **"must be more than zero"** Enter a real price, or leave the box empty.
- **"is set twice"** You've entered the same currency twice. Remove one.
- **"must be higher than the price"** Fix the compare-at price.
- Buyers see **"This product is not available in your region."** You don't sell or ship there.
