A shopping live lets you show products on camera while viewers ask questions and buy. It's one of the fastest ways to sell.

## Who this is for

Sellers with a store. "Only accounts with a store can host a shopping live." There's no extra plan needed.

## How to sell on live

1. Make sure the products you want to show are published. See [Products, prices and inventory](/help/selling/products-and-inventory).
2. Switch to the profile that owns the store and go live. See [Going live](/help/live/going-live).
3. During the live, open **Pin a product** and tap **Pin** next to a product to show it. It shows **Pinned** while it's on screen.
4. Viewers tap **Products in this live** to see the **In this live** list, open a product and check out as usual.

## Good to know

- You can only show products from this live's store, and only products that are published.
- Orders from a live are normal orders. In Nigeria they're protected by escrow, and the usual fees apply.
- Plan your stock: have enough on hand for the items you'll feature.
- Run a discount code or flash sale during the live to give viewers a reason to buy now. See [Promotions and discounts](/help/selling/promotions-and-discounts).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Only accounts with a store can host a shopping live."** Switch to your store's profile, or open a store first.
- **"Publish the product before showing it on screen."**
- **"You can only show products from this live's store."**
- **"You can only go live for a store you belong to."**
- **"We couldn't pin that product. Try again."** Check your connection.

## Stay safe

Never ask viewers to pay you by transfer or to message a phone number to buy. Sales outside checkout have no protection and break our rules. See [Live safety](/help/live/live-safety).
