Nantty's intended launch policy takes **no commission on sales**. Actual payment-processing and withdrawal fees charged by the payment provider are passed through at cost and must be shown before you confirm.

## Who this is for

Sellers in every country. Escrow is available only where the product offers it. Any provider fee for collecting or transferring money is displayed in checkout or on the withdrawal confirmation screen.

## The fees

- **Sales commission**: 0% under the intended launch policy.
- **Payment processing**: the actual card or transfer fee the payment provider charges to collect the buyer's payment; it varies by payment method and country.
- **Withdrawals**: any actual provider transfer fee, passed through at cost and shown on the **Withdraw** screen before you confirm.

Any provider charge is itemized so you can see exactly what was charged. If checkout shows a Nantty percentage commission, do not rely on this policy text: contact support, because the environment's saved fee configuration must be reconciled before launch.

## Where to see them

In the seller dashboard, review the order/withdrawal breakdown and the provider charges shown for that transaction. Dashboard labels must match the effective fee configuration.

## Example

The actual provider processing charge is shown in checkout. There is no fixed Nantty percentage example because the amount varies by payment method and transaction.

## Good to know

- Nantty does not add sales commission or an escrow surcharge under the intended launch policy. Any actual provider charge that applies to a buyer's checkout is shown in the checkout total before confirmation.
- Any provider fees and refund treatment are shown in the applicable checkout/refund terms; no unearned platform commission is added under the intended policy.
- Plan charges and ads you pay from your wallet show in the same breakdown, but they are not order fees.
