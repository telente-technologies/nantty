Sometimes a seller ends up owing Nantty money, for example after a refund once the order money had already reached them, or after a chargeback. Small amounts are repaid automatically from your next earnings. If the amount gets too large, we pause new sales until it's back under your limit.

## Who this is for

Sellers. See [Owed to Nantty](/help/payments-and-wallet/owed-to-nantty) for how amounts owed come about and how they're repaid.

## Your limit

Your limit depends on how far you've verified:

| You are | You can owe up to (Nigeria) |
|---|---|
| Not yet verified (email only) | ₦10,000 |
| Payout-verified (L1) | ₦50,000 |
| Identity-verified (L2), or a trusted seller | ₦200,000 |

A **trusted seller** has sold on Nantty for at least 90 days with no disputes decided against them and no lost chargebacks in the last 90 days.

**Outside Nigeria**, your limit is half the Nigerian amount for your level, in your local currency, at our current exchange rates.

## What pauses, and what doesn't

When you owe more than your limit:

- **Paused:** publishing new or edited listings, and buyers checking out from your store.
- **Not affected:** orders you already have. You can still ship, confirm delivery and reply to disputes, and buyers can still confirm receipt.

## How to start selling again

New earnings repay what you owe first. As soon as the amount drops to your limit or below, selling resumes on its own. Verifying further also raises your limit. See [Verify your store](/help/selling/verify-your-store).

## If something goes wrong

- **"You owe more than your selling limit allows. Clear what you owe to list items and take new orders again."** Check **Owed to Nantty** in your wallet to see the amount and where it came from. If you think a refund or chargeback was wrong, contact support from **Help** with the order number.

## Good to know

Nantty may change these limits. The figures above are the current ones.
