Store rewards turn one-time buyers into regulars. After an order, the buyer gets credit they can only spend at your store.

## Who this is for

All sellers, on any plan.

## How to set up store rewards

1. In **dashboard.zudomart.com** go to **Marketing > Store rewards**.
2. Set **Credit back (% of order value)**, from 0 to 20%.
3. Optionally set a **Minimum order to earn** and a **Maximum credit per order**.
4. Set **Credit expires after (days)**, from 7 to 730 days.
5. Turn on the **Store rewards** switch. You can switch it off any time. You'll see "Store rewards saved."

## How it works

- "Credit is added after the order completes and escrow is released, and is taken back if the order is refunded."
- The buyer's credit is applied automatically at checkout as a **Store credit** line when they buy from you again.
- **Outstanding credit** shows "What you owe customers as discounts".

## Good to know

- You fund these rewards. When a buyer spends credit, it works like a discount on that order, so you receive less for it.
- Buyers can "only spend it as a discount on a future order at your store. It is never cash and cannot be withdrawn."
- "You fund it, Zudomart never holds money for it." Zudomart doesn't run a platform-wide cashback scheme.

## If something goes wrong

- A buyer says their credit is missing: it only appears after the order completes and escrow releases, and it disappears if the order is refunded or the credit expired.
