title: Store rewards
summary: Give buyers store credit as a percentage of their order, to spend only at your store.
tags: rewards, store credit, loyalty
related: promotions-and-discounts, store-rewards-for-buyers
---

Turn on **Store rewards** in Marketing:

- Choose the percentage back as store credit.
- Set a minimum order, a maximum per order and an expiry.
- Switch rewards on or off any time.

Credit is earned when an order's escrow is released and reversed if the order is refunded. Buyers can only spend it at your store as a discount, never as cash. The dashboard shows how much credit is outstanding. You fund rewards yourself; Zudomart doesn't run a platform-wide cashback scheme.
