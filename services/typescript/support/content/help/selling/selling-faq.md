## How do I start selling?

Tap **Start selling on Nantty** in **Settings** or on your profile and apply. See [Opening a store](/help/selling/start-selling).

## Does Nantty take commission?

No. Nantty's intended launch policy has no commission on sales; actual payment-provider processing fees are passed through at cost and disclosed before checkout. See [Seller fees](/help/selling/fees-and-escrow-fee).

## Do I need a paid plan?

No. Free covers everyday selling with up to 100 active products. Paid seller plan availability and prices are shown in the signed-in checkout; fresh-install Nigeria source defaults currently include ₦5,000 for Seller Pro and ₦30,000 for Seller Business. See [Seller plans](/help/selling/seller-plans).

## Can I sell before my business is verified?

In Nigeria, yes: escrow only, up to ₦200,000 in total sales, and you can't withdraw sales money until you verify. See [Selling before verification](/help/selling/unverified-seller-limits).

## How do I verify my business?

Upload your CAC certificate, a government ID and proof of address in the app. See [Verify your store](/help/selling/verify-your-store).

## Can I sell from outside Nigeria?

Yes, after you verify your identity and a payout account in your own name. Buyers pay you directly. See [Selling outside Nigeria](/help/selling/selling-outside-nigeria).

## When do I get paid?

In Nigeria, after the buyer confirms or 3 days after delivery, then a 24-hour hold. See [Seller payouts](/help/selling/seller-payouts).

## Does Nantty hold my money?

No. Your money is in your own wallet with our licensed payment partner. See [Who holds your money](/help/wallet-security/who-holds-your-money).

## Does Nantty deliver for me?

No. You choose couriers and set shipping rules. See [Shipping settings](/help/selling/shipping-settings).

## A buyer opened a dispute. What now?

Send your side with evidence within 48 hours. See [Responding to a dispute](/help/selling/respond-to-a-dispute).

## How do I refund a buyer?

Tap **Refund** on the order in the dashboard. See [Refunding a buyer](/help/selling/seller-refunds).

## Can I reply to reviews?

Yes, publicly, and you can report reviews that break the rules. See [Reviews and ratings for sellers](/help/selling/reviews-and-ratings-for-sellers).

## What can't I sell?

See [Prohibited and restricted items](/help/selling/prohibited-items).

## How do I get the Gold checkmark?

Get your business documents approved and be on Pro, Business or the Gold-only plan. See [The Gold checkmark](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/gold-checkmark).

## Can staff help run my store?

Yes, on Pro or Business. See [Adding staff to your store](/help/selling/seller-team).
