The app is for selling from anywhere. The dashboard is for running your business. It's free for every Zudomart seller at **dashboard.zudomart.com**. Sign in with your Zudomart account.

## Who this is for

Store owners and their team members.

## What's in it

- **Overview**: today's sales, orders to handle and alerts.
- **Sell**: **Orders**, **Delivery** (**Shipments**, **Areas & fees**, **Shipping rules**), **Messages**, **Customers** and **Reviews**.
- **Catalog**: **Products** (including **Prices by country**, brand tagging and **Import & export**), **Inventory** and **Storefront**.
- **Grow**: **Analytics**, **Marketing** (discounts, flash sales, bundles, featured products and store rewards), **Ad results** and **Affiliates & creators**.
- **Money**: **Payments & finance** (balance, escrow, fees, withdrawals) and **Reports**.
- **Manage**: **Team**, **Brand** and **Settings** (including **Billing** and **Verification**).

## Good to know

- Orders in the dashboard are the same marketplace orders customers place in the Zudomart app and on the web. There's no in-store till or cash register.
- Features from a higher plan stay visible with an upgrade notice, so you can see what you'd get.
- Team members only see the sections their role allows. See [Adding staff to your store](/help/selling/seller-team).
- In the app, open your profile menu and tap **Seller dashboard** for orders and products on the go.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Upgrade to the ... plan to use this feature."** That tool needs Pro or Business. See [Seller plans](/help/selling/seller-plans).
- Can't see a section? Your role may not include it. Ask the store owner.

## Stay safe

Only sign in at dashboard.zudomart.com. Never share your login with staff; add them to your team instead.
