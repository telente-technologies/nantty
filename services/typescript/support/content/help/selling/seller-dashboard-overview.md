**The app is for selling from anywhere. The dashboard is for running your business.** It's free for every Zudomart seller at dashboard.zudomart.com. Sign in with your Zudomart account.

## Sections

Overview, Products, Inventory, Orders, Customers, Sales and analytics, Marketing, Ads, Payments and finance, Delivery, Messages, Storefront, Reviews, Team, Reports and Settings.

Orders in the dashboard are the same marketplace orders customers place in the Zudomart app. There's no in-store till or cash register.

Features from a higher plan stay visible with an upgrade link, so you can see what you'd get.
