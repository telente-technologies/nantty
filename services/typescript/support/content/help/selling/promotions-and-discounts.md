Promotions help you win new buyers and bring old ones back. You'll find them under **Marketing** in **dashboard.zudomart.com**.

## Who this is for

All sellers. Discount codes, product discounts and scheduled sales are "Included on every plan." Flash sales, bundles and featured products need Pro or Business.

## Create a discount

1. Go to **Marketing** and choose **Discount code**, **Product discount** or **Scheduled sale**.
2. Give it a **Name**.
3. For a code, enter the **Coupon code**, such as WELCOME10 (3 to 24 letters, numbers, dashes or underscores). "Customers enter the code at checkout in the Zudomart app." A product discount applies automatically, with no code.
4. Pick the **Discount type**: **Percent off** or **Amount off**.
5. Under **Applies to**, choose **Everything**, **Categories** or **Chosen products**.
6. Optionally set **Minimum spend**, **Total uses** and **Uses per customer**.
7. Set **Starts** and, if you want, **Ends**. A scheduled sale needs an end date.

## Pro and Business promotions

- **Flash sale**: a short sale of up to 7 days on 1 to 20 products, each with a **Flash price** and an optional **Stock cap**.
- **Bundle**: 1 to 10 products sold together for less. The bundle price must be lower than buying them separately.
- **Featured products**: "Promote up to 4 products in Zudomart Mart."

## Other ways to promote

- **Store rewards**: give buyers store credit to spend with you next time. See [Store rewards](/help/selling/store-rewards).
- **Boosts and ads**: put a post or product in front of more people. See [Boosting a post](/help/ads-and-boosts/boost-a-post) and [Ads Manager campaigns](/help/ads-and-boosts/ads-manager-campaigns).
- **Shopping lives**: show products live. See [Selling on live](/help/selling/sell-on-live).

## Good to know

- Discounts come out of your price. Zudomart still takes no commission.
- Discounts show as their own line in the buyer's cart.
- Results show in **Reports** and **Analytics**.

## If something goes wrong

- **"That code is already used in this store."** Pick a different code.
- **"End the flash sale before deleting it."**
- **"This flash sale has ended and can no longer be edited."** Create a new one.
- **"Upgrade to the Pro plan to use this feature."** Flash sales, bundles and featured products need Pro.

Buyers may see **"That code does not exist."** or **"This code cannot be used on this order."** if the code is mistyped, expired, used up or the order doesn't meet the minimum spend.
