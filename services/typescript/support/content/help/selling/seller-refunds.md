Sometimes the right thing is to give a buyer their money back. You can do it yourself from the order.

## Who this is for

Sellers, for paid orders that aren't in dispute.

## How to refund

1. At **dashboard.nantty.com** open **Orders** and the order.
2. Tap **Refund**, then pick **Full refund** or **Partial refund**.
3. For a partial refund, enter the **Amount to refund**. The dialog shows how much can still be refunded.
4. Write a **Reason shown to the buyer** (at least 5 characters).
5. Confirm. "The money goes to the buyer's Nantty wallet, or back to their card if the payment hasn't settled yet."

## When you can refund

- From a new order through to delivered or completed, once it's paid.
- **Before escrow releases**: full or partial refunds.
- **After escrow releases**: full refunds only.
- Only one refund per order, so get the amount right.

## Rejecting or cancelling

**Reject and refund** or **Cancel and refund** always gives the buyer a full refund. See [Orders and shipping for sellers](/help/selling/seller-orders-and-shipping).

## Good to know

- No escrow fee is charged when you refund before release. After release, only your seller portion is recovered; Nantty's escrow and processing fees are not refunded.
- Refunding after release takes the money back from your earnings. If you've already withdrawn it, it shows as **Owed to Nantty**: "Refunds or chargebacks taken from your next earnings." See [Owed to Nantty](/help/payments-and-wallet/owed-to-nantty).
- Store credit the buyer earned on the order is taken back.
- For direct-payment orders, buyers rely on you for refunds.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Nantty decides refunds while a dispute is open."** Respond to the dispute instead. See [Responding to a dispute](/help/selling/respond-to-a-dispute).
- **"This order was already paid out to you, so it can only be refunded in full."**
- **"This order has already been refunded or a refund is on its way."**
- **"Enter a refund amount above zero."**
- **"You cannot refund more than the buyer paid."**

## Stay safe

Only refund through Nantty. Never send a buyer money by transfer to "refund" them; it won't be recorded and it's a common scam.
