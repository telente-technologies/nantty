Anyone with a Zudomart account can apply to open a store. It's free to apply and free to sell on the Free plan.

## Who this is for

New sellers. In Nigeria you can start selling with escrow before your business is verified. Outside Nigeria you must verify your identity and payout account before you can list or sell anything. See [Selling outside Nigeria](/help/selling/selling-outside-nigeria).

## How to apply

1. Tap **Start selling on Zudomart** in **Settings**, on your profile, or on the home banner.
2. **Tell us about your store**: name and what you sell.
3. **How can buyers and couriers reach you?**: contact details and address.
4. **Verify your identity**: choose **NIN slip or card**, **International passport**, **Driver's licence** or **Voter's card (PVC)**, enter the **Document number** and add a **Photo of your document**.
5. **Where should we pay you?**: enter your **Account number**. "We'll confirm the account name with your bank." It must be in your own name.
6. **Review and submit**, then tap **Submit application**.

## After you apply

You'll see **Application under review**: "We're checking your details and ID. This usually takes up to 2 working days — we'll notify you as soon as it's done."

When approved: **You're approved!** "Your store is live. Add your first products and start selling with escrow protection."

## What's free

- Selling, orders, products, delivery, payouts and the seller dashboard on **dashboard.zudomart.com** are free on every plan.
- Zudomart takes **0% commission**. Escrow orders carry a small escrow fee. See [Seller fees](/help/selling/fees-and-escrow-fee).
- Paid plans add capacity and tools. See [Seller plans](/help/selling/seller-plans).

## Payment rules for new stores

Until your business is verified, every order is paid through escrow and your store has a sales limit. Verified stores (Gold) can also let buyers pay directly. See [Selling before verification](/help/selling/unverified-seller-limits) and [Verify your store](/help/selling/verify-your-store).

## Stay safe

Zudomart will never ask you to pay a fee to get approved, or for your password or one-time codes. Applications are only made in the app.
