## Apply to sell

From your profile choose **Start selling** and fill in your store details. Once approved you can list products from the app and from the seller dashboard.

## What's free

Every approved seller can use the seller dashboard for free. Basic selling is never locked behind a paid plan. See [Seller plans](/help/selling/seller-plans).

## Payment rules for new stores

Until your business is verified, every order is paid through escrow. Verified stores (Gold) can also let buyers pay directly.
