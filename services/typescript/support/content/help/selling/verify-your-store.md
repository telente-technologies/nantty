Business verification proves your store is a real business. "Selling never waits for this": you can keep selling while we review.

## Who this is for

Sellers in Nigeria who want to remove the sales limit, withdraw sales earnings, offer direct payment or get Gold. Sellers outside Nigeria must also verify their identity before selling at all. See [Selling outside Nigeria](/help/selling/selling-outside-nigeria).

## What you need

- **CAC certificate**
- **Government ID**
- **Proof of address**

Each file can be a PDF, JPEG, PNG or WebP of up to 10 MB.

## How to verify

Documents are uploaded in the app:
1. Go to **Settings > Business documents**, or tap **Verify your business** on the prompt in your seller dashboard.
2. For each document tap **Add photo**.
3. Tap **Send for review**.

On the web, **Settings > Verification** at **dashboard.zudomart.com** shows your **Gold badge** and **Documents** status. To change documents, use the app.

Each document shows **Not uploaded**, **Pending review**, **Approved** or **Rejected**. If one is rejected, fix it and tap **Send again**.

## What changes when you're approved

- The unverified sales limit is removed.
- You can withdraw your sales earnings (you also need payout verification).
- You can let buyers choose **Pay directly** as well as escrow.
- On Pro, Business or the Gold-only plan, your store shows the **Gold** checkmark: "Your store shows the gold badge."

## Good to know

- The dashboard shows your status: "Your store is not verified yet", "We are reviewing your store" or "Your store is verified".
- Gold needs both approved documents and a paid plan. See [Seller plans](/help/selling/seller-plans).
- Payout verification (BVN consent and a bank account in your own name) is separate. See [Verification levels](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/verification-levels).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Business documents must be approved before a Gold checkmark can be applied for."** Upload and wait for approval first.
- **"Finish business verification to withdraw your sales earnings."** Your money is safe and still yours. Finish this to withdraw.
- A document was rejected: make sure it's clear, in date and the name matches your store or ID.

## Stay safe

Only upload documents inside the Zudomart app or dashboard. We never ask for documents or fees by chat or email.
