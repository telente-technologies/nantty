Zudomart doesn't deliver for you. You decide where you ship and how much it costs, and we show buyers the right fee at checkout.

## Who this is for

All sellers. Shipping rules are "Included on every plan, including Free."

## How to add a shipping rule

1. At **dashboard.zudomart.com** go to **Delivery > Shipping rules**.
2. Tap **Add rule**.
3. Enter a **Destination group name**, such as South West.
4. Choose the **Rule**: **Flat fee per order** (then enter the **Fee per order**) or **Free shipping**.
5. Pick destinations from the tree: whole countries, states or local government areas.
6. Make sure **Rule is active** is on, and save.

"One price for the whole order group going to these destinations, never per item. The most specific destination wins: local government area, then state, then country."

## Free shipping above a total

Under the free-shipping panel, turn on **Offer free shipping above a total**, enter **Free shipping from** and tap **Save threshold**. "Orders at or above this total ship free to any destination you serve." Buyers see "Add ₦X more for free shipping" when they're close.

## Check your rules

Use the live preview: pick a **State**, **Local government area** and **Order total** to see exactly what a buyer would pay, or whether they "cannot check out: you do not ship there yet".

## Good to know

- With no rules, "customers cannot check out". Add at least one rule before you start selling.
- Your products only show to buyers in places you ship to.
- If two rules match equally, free shipping wins, then the cheaper fee.
- You can have up to 50 shipping rules and 50 delivery areas.
- **Areas & fees** lets you add delivery areas with **Min days** and **Max days** estimates.
- Removing a rule means customers there "will use the next most specific rule, or cannot check out."

## If something goes wrong

- **"This store reached the shipping rule limit."** Combine destinations into fewer rules.
- **"That destination is not a known country, region or area."** Pick it from the list instead of typing.
- **"Enter an order total above zero."** Free shipping needs a real amount.
