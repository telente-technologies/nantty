---
title: Adding staff to your store
summary: Add existing Zudomart users by username and give them a role. They sign in with their own accounts.
tags: team, staff, roles, permissions
related: seller-dashboard-overview, seller-plans
---

Team seats come with Pro (5) and Business (25).

1. Dashboard > Team > Add member.
2. Search for their Zudomart username.
3. Pick a role: Manager, Inventory staff, Sales staff or Accountant.
4. They accept in the app and sign in to the dashboard with their own Zudomart login.

There are no separate staff accounts or email invites. Business stores can fine-tune each member's permissions. Remove a member any time from Team.
