Your team signs in with their own Zudomart accounts. There are no shared logins or separate staff accounts.

## Who this is for

Store owners on Pro (5 seats) or Business (25 seats). "On Free, only the owner signs in."

## How to add a team member

1. Sign in at **dashboard.zudomart.com** and go to **Team**.
2. Tap **Add team member**.
3. Under **Find a Zudomart user**, search for their username.
4. Pick a **Role**: **Manager**, **Inventory staff**, **Sales staff** or **Accountant**. Choose which locations they can work in.
5. Tap **Send team request**.
6. They accept in the app. Until then they show as **Waiting to accept**.

## Managing your team

- **Team members** lists everyone with their role, locations and **Last active**.
- **Permission matrix** shows what each role can do. On Business you can turn individual permissions on or off per member.
- **Staff activity** shows who did what and when.
- **Suspend** a member to block access for now, or **Reactivate** them later. You can also remove them.

## Good to know

- Each person "sees only what their role allows".
- The seat meter shows **Team seats in use**.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Every team seat on your plan is in use"** or **"Your plan has no free team seats. Upgrade or remove someone first."** Remove someone or upgrade.
- Can't find someone? They need their own Zudomart account first.

## Stay safe

Never share your own password with staff. Remove or suspend people as soon as they leave your business.
