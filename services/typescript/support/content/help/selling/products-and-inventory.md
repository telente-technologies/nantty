Your products are what buyers see in your store, in search and in the Mart. Add them from the app or the dashboard.

## Who this is for

Approved sellers. Outside Nigeria you must be identity-verified before you can publish. See [Selling outside Nigeria](/help/selling/selling-outside-nigeria).

## How to add a product

In the app: open your profile menu, tap **Seller dashboard**, then **Add product**.
On the web: in **dashboard.nantty.com** go to **Products** and choose **New product**.

1. Add a name (2 to 120 characters) and a description (up to 5,000 characters).
2. Add **Photos**: "Up to 10 JPEG, PNG, WebP or AVIF photos, 10 MB each." You can also add up to 3 videos.
3. Choose **Categories** and fill in any **Attributes**.
4. Under **Options**, add up to 3 options such as size or colour. You can have up to 100 variants, each with its own price and stock.
5. Optionally tag a **Brand** and set **Prices by country**.
6. Choose a status: **Draft — not for sale yet**, **Active — ready to sell** or **Archived — kept for records**. You can also schedule it with **Publish automatically on**, up to 12 months ahead.

## Where products appear

Products only show to buyers in places you ship to. Set your destinations in **Delivery > Shipping rules**. See [Shipping settings](/help/selling/shipping-settings).

## Inventory

**Inventory** tracks stock per location with low-stock alerts on every plan. Pro adds stock movements, transfers, suppliers and reorder levels, and bulk import and export. Locations: 1 on Free, 3 on Pro, 10 on Business.

## Good to know

- Active products: 100 on Free, 2,000 on Pro, unlimited on Business.
- Up to 20 tags per product.
- Check our [Prohibited and restricted items](/help/selling/prohibited-items) before you list.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Add at least one image before publishing."**
- **"Only active products can be published."** Change the status to Active first.
- **"Your plan has reached its active product limit."** Archive products or upgrade. See [Seller plans](/help/selling/seller-plans).
- **"Verify your identity and add a payout account in your own name before selling."** Outside Nigeria, verify first.
