## Add a product

From the app (**+ > Product**) or the dashboard (Products > New): add photos, title, description, category, brand, variants such as size or colour, price and stock.

## Prices per country

Your default price is shown in each buyer's currency, marked as approximate when it's converted. You can set your own price for specific countries if you prefer.

## Where products appear

Products only show to buyers in places you ship to. Set up your destinations in delivery settings, see [Shipping](/help/selling/seller-orders-and-shipping).

## Inventory

Track stock per location, make adjustments, and get low-stock alerts. Pro adds movements, transfers between locations, reorder points and suppliers.
