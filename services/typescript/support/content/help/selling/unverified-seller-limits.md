You don't have to wait for business verification to start selling in Nigeria. Until you're verified, a few limits keep buyers safe.

## Who this is for

Sellers in Nigeria whose business verification isn't approved yet. Outside Nigeria you can't sell at all until you're verified. See [Selling outside Nigeria](/help/selling/selling-outside-nigeria).

## The limits

- **Escrow only.** Buyers can't pay you directly. Every order is protected by escrow.
- **Sales limit.** Your store can take up to **₦200,000** in total sales while verification is pending. The total counts paid orders plus checkouts still in progress, and it doesn't reset each month.
- **No withdrawals of sales money.** Your earnings move through escrow into your wallet as normal, but you can't withdraw them until you're verified.

Nothing is forfeited. The money stays yours and waits for you.

## How to lift the limits

Finish business verification. See [Verify your store](/help/selling/verify-your-store). Once approved, the limit goes away, you can offer direct payment and you can withdraw.

## Good to know

- The limit is set by Nantty and may change. The figure above is the current default.
- Buyers see your store without a checkmark and with **Protected by Nantty Escrow** at checkout.
- You still pay only the escrow fee and processing fee on each order.

## If something goes wrong

- **"This store has reached its sales limit while the seller's business verification is pending, so it can't take this order right now."** You've hit the limit. Buyers see a message asking them to remove your items. Finish verification to take orders again.
- **"Finish business verification to withdraw your sales earnings."** Verify your business, then withdraw.
- **"Verify your payout details to continue. Nothing you have earned is lost while you verify."** Finish payout verification too.

## Stay safe

Never ask buyers to pay you outside Nantty to get round the limit. It removes their protection and can get your store closed.
