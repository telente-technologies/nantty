Reports give you and your accountant a clear record of your business. You'll find them under **Reports** at **dashboard.zudomart.com**.

## Who this is for

Store owners, and team members whose role includes reports (such as **Accountant**).

## How to download a report

"Pick a report, set the dates, then download it as CSV, Excel or PDF for your accountant."

1. Open **Reports**.
2. Pick a report.
3. Set the date range.
4. Download it as CSV, or Excel or PDF on Pro and Business.

## Reports on every plan

- Sales by day
- Sales by product
- Sales by category
- Sales by location
- Tax report
- Inventory valuation
- Low and out of stock

## Advanced reports (Pro and Business)

- Orders by status
- Fulfilment time
- New vs returning customers
- Top customers
- Best sellers and slow sellers
- Settlements, fees, refunds and payouts
- Ad campaigns

On Free you'll see a notice that the report "is part of advanced reports. Upgrade to Pro to see it and export every report to CSV, Excel and PDF."

## Good to know

- The **Fees** report shows the escrow fee and payment processing fee as separate lines. See [Seller fees](/help/selling/fees-and-escrow-fee).
- How far back you can look in **Analytics** depends on your plan: 30 days on Free, 365 on Pro, 730 on Business.
- Your wallet history and receipts are in **Payments & finance**.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Upgrade to the Pro plan to use this feature."** That report or export format needs Pro.
- A report looks empty? Check the date range and that orders in it are paid.
