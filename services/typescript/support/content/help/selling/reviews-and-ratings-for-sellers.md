Reviews build trust. Every review on Zudomart comes from a buyer who actually received the order, and shows **Verified purchase**.

## Who this is for

Sellers and team members with access to **Reviews**.

## Where to find reviews

At **dashboard.zudomart.com** open **Reviews**: "What buyers say about your products and your store in the Zudomart app. Reply publicly, and report reviews that break the rules."

You'll see **Reputation**, **Rating distribution** and **Rating trend**, and you can filter by **Needs a reply** or **Most helpful**.

## How to reply

1. Open a review and write in **Public reply**: "Everyone who sees the review sees your reply. Be kind and specific."
2. Tap **Post reply** (2 to 1,000 characters). "Reply posted. The buyer gets a notification in the app."
3. Use **Edit reply** or **Remove reply** later if you need to.

## How to report a review

1. Open the review and choose **Report this review**.
2. Pick a reason: **Fake — this person did not buy**, **Abusive or offensive**, **Spam or advertising**, **Shares private information**, **Not about the product or order** or **Something else**.
3. Send it. "Thanks. Zudomart will review it within 2 working days. The review stays up until then."

## How your rating works

- Buyers rate from 1 to 5 stars once their order is delivered or completed.
- Your rating is the simple average of all ratings.
- Buyers can delete their own review but can't edit it.
- You can't review your own store.

## Good to know

- Honest negative reviews aren't removed. A calm, helpful reply often matters more to future buyers than the review itself.
- Ratings and your order success rate show to buyers at checkout.

## If something goes wrong

- **"That review was already reported."** Our team is already looking at it.

## Stay safe

Never offer money or discounts in exchange for good reviews or for removing bad ones, and never buy fake reviews. Both break our rules. See [Community guidelines](/help/safety-and-reporting/community-guidelines).
