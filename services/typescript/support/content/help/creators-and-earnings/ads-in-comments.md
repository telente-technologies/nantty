As you scroll through the replies on a post, you may see an ad labelled **Promoted**.

## Where they appear

- Only in comment sections in the feed. The first ad shows after a few replies, with plenty of replies between ads.
- Never on posts marked sensitive or made for children, and never on a deleted post.
- Not at all for Blue Premium and Gold Business members. See [How many ads you see on each plan](/help/ads-and-boosts/ads-by-plan).

## Who gets paid

If the post was made by an approved [Creator Program](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-program) member, they get the same share as ads on their content (40% by default, lower for AI-generated posts). See [Ads revenue share](/help/creators-and-earnings/ads-revenue-share).

If the post's author isn't an approved creator, the ad still shows, but the money isn't shared. Creators don't earn from ads they see on their own posts. Invalid or automated views aren't paid.

## For creators

Comment-section ads are on automatically. You don't need to opt in. Your earnings from them are included under **Ads** in Creator Studio earnings. If your monetisation is paused by strikes, comment ads on your posts still show, but your share goes to the platform until monetisation is restored.
