You set your subscription price by picking a tier, not by typing any amount. Tiers keep prices simple for fans and let Apple and Google sell the same plan in the apps.

## Price tiers in Nigeria

Monthly tiers are ₦500, ₦1,000, ₦1,500, ₦2,000, ₦3,000, ₦4,000 and ₦5,000. ₦1,000 is selected by default.

Yearly plans cost 10 times the monthly price, so fans get 2 months free. For example, the ₦1,000 tier is ₦10,000 a year.

Other countries have their own tiers in their own currency (for example Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, the UK, the eurozone and the US). The tiers you see match your currency.

## Web price and app price

The tier you pick is the **web price**: what fans pay at **app.nantty.com**. In the iPhone and Android apps, Apple and Google take a fee, so we add it to the fan's price instead of taking it from you. When you choose a tier, the form shows exactly what fans will pay in the iPhone app and on Android.

Apple's fee is usually higher than Google's, so the iPhone price is usually the highest. The web is always the cheapest place for fans to subscribe.

## What you earn

You keep 80% of each payment after the payment or app store fee. Because the store fee is added on top in the apps, you get about the same amount whichever way a fan pays.

Example: a fan pays the ₦1,000 tier on the web. After the card processing fee, you keep 80% of the rest.

## How to change a price

Prices are fixed per plan. To charge a different price, create a new plan with the new tier and tap **Pause new sign-ups** on the old one. Existing subscribers keep their current price.

## Good to know

- A plan can list up to 20 benefits.
- Pick a price that matches what you deliver every month. Fans can cancel any time.
- Subscription earnings show in Creator Studio and move to your wallet like other creator earnings. See [When creator earnings become available](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-earnings-holds).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Pick one of the subscription price tiers."** Choose one of the prices shown.
- **"Choose a price inside the regional subscription price range."** The price must be one of your country's tiers.
- **"Subscription plans can't be priced in this currency."** Your currency isn't supported yet. Contact support.
- **"Subscription plans are available to approved creators."** Join the [Creator Program](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-program) first.
