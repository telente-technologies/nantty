Zudomart doesn't pay per view. Instead, when an advertiser pays for an ad shown next to your content, part of that money goes to you.

## Who this is for

Approved [Creator Program](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-program) members whose monetisation isn't paused by strikes.

## How it works

- **Ads on your content**: you get a share of what advertisers pay for eligible ads shown with your posts and clips. The default share is 40%. Your current rate is listed in Creator Studio under **How you earn**.
- **Ads on your lives**: only if you turn them on in **Live monetization**. See [Ads on your live](/help/live/live-ads).
- **Premium pool**: part of what Premium members pay goes into a monthly pool. It's shared among creators by how much time Premium members spend watching their content.

## What changes your share

- **AI-generated content** earns a lower share than original content. Label AI content honestly. See [AI-generated label](/help/posts-and-feed/ai-generated-label).
- **Invalid traffic** (fake or automated views and clicks) is filtered out. Advertisers aren't charged for it and you aren't paid for it.
- **Strikes** can pause monetisation. While it's paused, no ads share or pool money is credited. It comes back when strikes expire or an appeal succeeds.

## Getting paid

Ads earnings are added to your **Creator earnings** in batches as ads are settled. They then move to **Available to withdraw** in the daily payout run. See [When creator earnings become available](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-earnings-holds).

## Good to know

- Views alone don't earn money. Revenue depends on ads actually delivered and paid for.
- Your own sponsorships are your business. Zudomart doesn't take a share of deals you make yourself.
- Rates are set by Zudomart and can change. Changes apply from when an ad is delivered.

## If something goes wrong

If your ads earnings look wrong, check **Settings > Account status** for active strikes, then [contact support](/help/contact-support/contact-support) with the dates you're asking about.
