## Before you can withdraw

- Finish [payout setup](/help/creators-and-earnings/payout-setup) (payout verification, level 1).
- Sellers also need business verification to withdraw sales money.
- Have a payout account in your own name that's at least 48 hours old. See [New payout account wait](/help/wallet-security/new-payout-account-cooldown).

## When money becomes available

- **Seller escrow money** is released when the buyer confirms delivery, or 3 days after delivery if there's no dispute. It then sits in **Locked** for 24 hours before it's **Available to withdraw**.
- **Creator earnings** (gifts, subscriptions and ad share) show under **Creator earnings** until they move to your available balance. Convert earned coins to cash in Creator Studio.
- **Money you added** to your wallet can be withdrawn after its [deposit hold](/help/wallet-security/deposit-holds).

## How to withdraw

1. Open **Wallet** and tap **Withdraw**. On the web (**app.zudomart.com**), open **Wallet** > **Payout accounts & withdraw**.
2. Enter the amount. You'll see your **Available** balance at the top.
3. Under **Send to**, choose a payout account.
4. Check the payout fee, then tap **Withdraw**.
5. [Confirm it's you](/help/wallet-security/step-up-codes) with your password or an emailed code.

## Fees and timing

- A **flat payout fee** applies to each withdrawal and is added on top of the amount. You see it before you confirm.
- Money usually arrives **the same day**.
- Your first payout can take a few extra minutes while it's checked.

## Limits

Up to 3 withdrawals a day, at least 10 minutes apart, one at a time, and a daily amount cap based on your verification level. See [Withdrawal limits](/help/wallet-security/withdrawal-limits).

## Common messages

- **"That's more than your available balance."** The amount plus the fee is more than you can withdraw. Money in **Pending (escrow)**, **Locked** or a deposit hold can't be withdrawn yet.
- **"Withdrawals are locked"**: tap **Finish payout setup**.
- **"Finish business verification to withdraw your sales earnings."** Your earnings are safe and keep building. Finish verification, then withdraw.
- **"This withdrawal is too small to cover the bank transfer fee."** Withdraw a larger amount.
- **"Clear the amount you owe before withdrawing."** Money owed to Zudomart from a refund or chargeback is repaid first.

## If a withdrawal fails

If the bank rejects it, the money goes back to **Available to withdraw** once we've confirmed the failure. Check that the payout account is still open and in your name, then try again. We never retry a failed withdrawal automatically, so the same money can't be sent twice.

## Outside Nigeria

There's no stored wallet balance. Your share of each sale is paid straight to your payout account once the payment settles.
