## When money is available

- Seller escrow money is released when the buyer confirms delivery, or automatically 3 days after delivery.
- Released money is locked for 24 hours, then becomes available.
- Creators convert earned coins to cash in Creator Studio.

## Payouts

Available money is sent to your bank in a daily payout run. You'll get a notification and email when a payout completes, or if it fails with the reason.

## Fees

The daily automatic payout is free. Extra instant withdrawals may carry the bank transfer fee, shown before you confirm.

## If a payout fails

The money returns to your balance. Check your bank details in your payout account and try again.
