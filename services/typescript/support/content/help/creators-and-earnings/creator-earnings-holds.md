Money you earn as a creator doesn't go straight to **Available to withdraw**. It first collects in the **Creator earnings** line of your wallet, then moves across automatically.

## The journey of your money

1. **You earn it.** Gifts and tips arrive as earned coins. Subscription payments, ads share and Premium pool money arrive as cash.
2. **Convert coins.** Earned coins only become money when you [convert them](/help/creators-and-earnings/convert-earned-coins).
3. **Creator earnings.** Cash waits here. The wallet describes it as "Gift, subscription and ad-share earnings waiting to move to your available balance."
4. **Daily move.** Once a day, Nantty moves your whole Creator earnings balance to **Available to withdraw** if all the checks below pass.
5. **Withdraw.** From Available to withdraw you can [withdraw to your bank](/help/creators-and-earnings/withdrawals).

## Why your earnings might not move yet

Your earnings stay in Creator earnings, safe and still yours, until:

- **You're payout verified.** You need verified payout details and a payout account in your own name. See [Setting up payouts](/help/creators-and-earnings/payout-setup).
- **You've reached the minimum.** In Nigeria the balance must be at least ₦100 before it moves.
- **Monetisation isn't paused.** Active strikes can pause monetisation. It resumes when strikes expire or an appeal succeeds. Check **Settings > Account status**.

## Affiliate commissions

Affiliate commissions show as **Pending** until the order completes, for escrow and direct payments alike, so a refunded or cancelled order doesn't pay out. Once paid, a commission refunded later is taken back from your wallet, and the line in your history names the store and order.

## Refunds and chargebacks

If a fan's payment is refunded or charged back after you were paid, your share is taken back. If your balance can't cover it, the amount shows under **Owed to Nantty** and comes out of your next earnings.

## Good to know

- Nothing you earn is lost while you verify. It keeps waiting for you.
- Once money is in Available to withdraw, normal [withdrawal limits](/help/wallet-security/withdrawal-limits) apply.
- Creator Studio shows estimates; your wallet is the final record.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Verify your payout details to continue. Nothing you have earned is lost while you verify."** Finish **Get paid**.
- If your earnings haven't moved for more than 2 days and every check above passes, [contact support](/help/contact-support/contact-support).
