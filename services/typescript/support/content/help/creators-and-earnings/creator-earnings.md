Zudomart doesn't pay for views. It shares eligible monetisation revenue with creators.

## Gifts

Viewers buy coins and send gifts on your lives. Your share of each gift depends on your creator tier and goes up to 70%. Gifts arrive as earned coins, which you convert to cash in Creator Studio.

## Fan subscriptions

You keep 80% of each subscription after the app store or payment fee. See [Fan subscriptions](/help/creators-and-earnings/fan-subscriptions).

## Ads revenue

Eligible ads shown on your content and your lives (if you turn live ads on) earn you a share of the revenue. Views are filtered for fraud before revenue is shared. AI-generated content earns a lower share than original content. Premium subscriptions feed a separate pool shared with creators by how much Premium members watch.

## See your earnings

Creator Studio shows coins, earned coins, gifts, subscribers, top supporters and estimated earnings for each period.
