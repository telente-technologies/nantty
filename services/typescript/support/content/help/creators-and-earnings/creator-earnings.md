Zudomart doesn't pay for views. It shares eligible monetisation revenue with creators in the [Creator Program](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-program). Your current rates are always listed in Creator Studio under **How you earn**.

## Gifts and tips

Viewers buy coins and send gifts on your lives, or tap **Tip the host with coins**. You keep up to 70% of the coins. Your share depends on your creator tier: Starter 50%, Partner 60% and Top creator 70%, and it can rise with your monthly gift volume. Gifts arrive as **earned coins**, which you [convert to cash](/help/creators-and-earnings/convert-earned-coins) in Creator Studio.

## Fan subscriptions

You keep 80% of each subscription payment after the payment or app store fee. In the apps the store fee is added to the fan's price, so you get about the same whether a fan pays in the app or on the web. See [Fan subscriptions](/help/creators-and-earnings/fan-subscriptions).

## Ads revenue share

Eligible ads shown on your content, and on your lives if you allow live ads, earn you a share of the revenue (40% by default). AI-generated content earns a lower share. Premium subscriptions also feed a separate pool shared with creators by how much Premium members watch. See [Ads revenue share](/help/creators-and-earnings/ads-revenue-share).

## Affiliate commissions

Promote sellers' products and earn a commission on each completed sale. In Creator Studio open **Affiliate** to find products open to affiliates, copy your link for each one and see clicks, sales and earnings. Tagging an approved product in a post, clip or live counts too.

A commission is paid only when the order completes: the buyer confirms receipt, or 3 days pass after delivery with no dispute, return or refund request. Nothing is paid while the order is paid, shipped or delivered, and a sale refunded, cancelled or returned before completion pays nothing. Your own purchases never earn a commission.

## See your earnings

Open your profile menu and tap **Creator tools** to open **Creator Studio**. Pick **This week**, **This month** or **All time** to see **Earnings this period**, split into Gifts, Affiliate commissions and Bonuses, plus new followers, your **Available** balance and your top lives and posts. See [Creator analytics](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-analytics).

On a computer, **studio.zudomart.com** shows earnings per live, top gifters and battle results.

## Getting paid

1. Set up payouts once with **Get paid**. See [Setting up payouts](/help/creators-and-earnings/payout-setup).
2. Earnings move into your wallet's **Available to withdraw** line. See [When creator earnings become available](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-earnings-holds).
3. Tap **Withdraw earnings** in Creator Studio to withdraw to a bank account in your own name. See [Withdrawals](/help/creators-and-earnings/withdrawals).

## Good to know

- Coins are not cash. Only coins you earn as a creator can be converted.
- Sending coins to another person carries a 25% platform fee.
- Withdrawals pass on the bank transfer fee. The exact fee is shown before you confirm.
- Earnings are never lost while you verify your payout details.
