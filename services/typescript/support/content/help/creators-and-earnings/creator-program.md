The Creator Program lets you earn on Zudomart. Accepted creators can go live, receive gifts, offer fan subscriptions, earn affiliate commissions on sellers' products and share in eligible ads revenue. You don't need a store to be a creator.

## Who this is for

People with a personal profile who make original content. Business, brand and government profiles can't join. You must be 18 or older.

## Check your eligibility

1. Open your profile menu and tap **Switch to creator account**. This opens the **Creator Program** screen.
2. Under **Eligibility** you'll see a live checklist with your progress against each target. The default targets are:
   - At least 1,000 real followers
   - Account at least 30 days old
   - 10 original posts in the last 60 days
   - No violations in the last 90 days
3. You also need a verified email address.

The exact targets are set by Zudomart, shown in the app and can change. The list updates automatically as you grow.

## Apply

1. When you're eligible, tap **Apply now**.
2. On **Apply as a creator**, choose up to 3 categories under **What do you create?** (for example Fashion, Comedy, Music or Education).
3. Optionally add your Instagram, TikTok or YouTube and some sample screenshots. These help us review faster.
4. Under **Where should earnings go?** choose a payout account, or tap **Add a payout account**. See [Setting up payouts](/help/creators-and-earnings/payout-setup).
5. Accept the **Creator Terms** and tap **Submit application**.

## Decisions

The checklist shows one of three labels:

- **Instant approval**: you meet every requirement, so you're approved straight away.
- **Manual review**: you're close (for example at least 500 real followers) and a member of our team checks your application, usually within a few working days.
- **Not eligible yet**: keep going. The button reads **Apply when you're eligible**.

Track progress any time from your profile menu, which shows **Creator application status**. Once approved, the same menu item becomes **Creator tools** and opens Creator Studio.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Your creator application is already pending or approved."** You already have an application open. Check its status from your profile menu.
- **"You can apply again after ..."** A rejected application has a 30-day wait before you can reapply. The date is shown on the status screen.
- **"Pick at least one content category"**, **"Accept the Creator terms to continue"** or **"Choose a payout account"**: complete the missing part of the form.
- **"Join the Creator Program to go live."** Going live is only for approved creators.

## Good to know

- Active strikes can pause going live and monetisation until they expire or an appeal succeeds. See [Suspensions and appeals](/help/safety-and-reporting/suspensions-and-appeals).
