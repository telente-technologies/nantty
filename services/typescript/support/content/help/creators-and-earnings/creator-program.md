## Apply

Go to Settings > **Switch to creator account**. You'll see a checklist with your progress, for example:

- Real followers (shown as your count against the target)
- Account age of at least 30 days
- A verified phone number
- Enough original posts in the last 60 days
- No violations in the last 90 days

The exact targets are shown in the app and can change.

## Decisions

- **Approved instantly** when you meet every requirement and nothing needs a closer look.
- **Manual review** when a person needs to check, usually within a few days.
- **Not eligible yet**, with the requirements you still need and when you can reapply.

We email and notify you about each step.

## What you get

Creator Studio, going live, gifts, fan subscriptions and a share of eligible ads revenue. You don't need a store to be a creator.
