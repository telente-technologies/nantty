The Creator Program lets you earn on Nantty. Accepted creators can go live, receive gifts, offer fan subscriptions, earn affiliate commissions on sellers' products and share in eligible ads revenue. You don't need a store to be a creator.

## Who this is for

People with a personal profile who make original content. Business, brand and government profiles can't join. You must be 18 or older.

## Check your eligibility

1. Open your profile menu and tap **Creator Program**.
2. Under **Eligibility**, review your current value, target, remaining amount, data freshness and decision state for each requirement. The current minimums are:
   - 100,000 qualifying impressions in the previous 30 days
   - 10,000 real followers, including at least 500 verified followers
   - An account at least 30 days old
   - 10 original posts in the last 60 days
   - A verified email address
   - No violations in the last 90 days for automatic approval
3. You must be at least 18. Nantty checks age on the server; a missing or unverifiable date of birth does not qualify.

“Real followers” means current, unique, eligible follows; self-follows, blocked or inactive accounts, and accounts identified as bots or fraudulent are excluded. “Verified followers” are qualifying real followers who have completed Nantty identity verification. A “qualifying impression” is a unique, attributable content impression from a verified Nantty user, deduplicated and filtered for self-views, bots, replayed events and invalid traffic. These values come from trusted server-side records, not profile claims. If a trusted data snapshot is missing or stale, the app shows the requirement as unavailable and prevents application submission. It does not create a rejection or start a reapplication cooldown because of missing data.

## Apply

1. When you're eligible, tap **Apply now**.
2. On **Apply as a creator**, choose up to 3 categories under **What do you create?** (for example Fashion, Comedy, Music or Education).
3. Optionally add your social links or content samples. They are not eligibility requirements.
4. Under **Where should earnings go?** choose a payout account, or tap **Add a payout account**. See [Setting up payouts](/help/creators-and-earnings/payout-setup).
5. Accept the **Creator Terms** and tap **Submit application**.

## Decisions

The checklist shows one of four labels:

- **Auto-approval eligible**: all measured thresholds are met and server-side policy, identity and risk checks allow automatic approval. This is not a promise of approval until the server confirms it.
- **Manual review**: required numeric thresholds are met, but an authorized reviewer must assess a policy, standing or risk flag. Manual review cannot waive age, email, account-age, follower, verified-follower, impression or original-content minimums.
- **Not eligible yet**: keep going. The button reads **Apply when you're eligible**.
- **Eligibility data unavailable**: trusted Analytics or Moderation has not supplied a current snapshot. You cannot submit until both required sources are available; missing data is not treated as zero, rejection, or a reapplication cooldown.

Track progress any time from your profile menu, which shows **Creator application status**. Once approved, the same menu item becomes **Creator tools** and opens Creator Studio.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Your creator application is already pending or approved."** You already have an application open. Check its status from your profile menu.
- **"You can apply again after ..."** A rejected application has a 30-day wait before you can reapply. The date is shown on the status screen.
- **"Pick at least one content category"**, **"Accept the Creator terms to continue"** or **"Choose a payout account"**: complete the missing part of the form.
- **"Join the Creator Program to go live."** Going live is only for approved creators.
