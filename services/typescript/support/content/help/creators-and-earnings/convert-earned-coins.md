When viewers send you gifts or tips, your share arrives as **earned coins**. Earned coins are kept apart from coins you bought. Only earned coins can be converted to cash.

## Who this is for

Approved creators who have verified their payout details. See [Setting up payouts](/help/creators-and-earnings/payout-setup).

## How to convert

1. Open your profile menu and tap **Creator tools** to open Creator Studio.
2. In the **Coins** card you'll see your **Balance** (bought coins) and **Earned** coins.
3. Tap **Convert earned coins**.
4. Type the number of coins under **Earned coins to convert**. You can convert any amount up to your earned coins.
5. Tap **Convert**. The receipt says how much cash you received and how many earned coins remain.

Tap **Coin activity** to see every coin coming in and going out.

## How much you get

Each earned coin converts at the rate set for your country. In Nigeria the default is ₦10 per coin. Your share of each gift (50% to 70% by tier) is already taken into account in the number of earned coins you receive, so there is no second cut when you convert.

Example: a viewer sends a 1,000-coin gift. At the 60% Partner share you get 600 earned coins, which convert to ₦6,000.

## What happens next

Converted money joins your **Creator earnings** line in your wallet and moves to **Available to withdraw** in the next daily payout run. See [When creator earnings become available](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-earnings-holds).

## Good to know

- Earned coins convert into the currency of the country you verified in.
- Creator Studio's **Pending** figure shows the cash value of earned coins you haven't converted yet.
- Conversions can't be undone.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Enter an amount up to your earned coins."** You typed more than you have.
- **"You do not have enough earned coins to convert."** Your earned balance changed. Refresh and try a smaller amount.
- **"Earned coins convert into the currency of your verified country."** Conversion is only offered in your verified country's currency.
- **"Earned coins cannot be converted to this currency yet."** Your currency isn't supported for conversion yet. Contact support.
- **"Verify your payout details to continue. Nothing you have earned is lost while you verify."** Finish **Get paid** first. Your coins wait for you.

## Stay safe

Nantty will never ask you to send coins or money to "unlock" a conversion. Nobody from Nantty will ask for your password or one-time codes.
