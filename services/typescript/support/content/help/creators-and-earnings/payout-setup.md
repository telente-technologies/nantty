Sellers and creators use one **Get paid** flow. If you're both, you only set it up once: "One payout account for everything you earn."

Open it from the **Get paid** or **Set up payouts** card on your **Wallet**, from **Finish payout setup** on the **Withdraw** screen, or from Creator Studio.

## Nigeria: the steps

1. **Who you are**: your legal name as it appears on your ID, date of birth, home address, and your BVN or NIN (11 digits). Afterwards we only show the last 4 digits of your ID number. "Nigerian banks and regulators require us to confirm who receives money before we can send it."
2. **Your business** (only if you sell as a registered business): your CAC registration number (it starts with RC or BN) and business type.
3. **Where to send your money**: choose your bank and enter your 10-digit account number. We check the account name with your bank, and it must match your legal name. Tap **Save account** and [confirm it's you](/help/wallet-security/step-up-codes) with your password or an emailed code.
4. **Verify your ID**: only if we ask. You add a photo of your national ID card, international passport, driver's licence or voter's card on a secure verification page.

Each step saves straight away. When the steps are done, your wallet is opened in your verified name. That runs in the background: "You can leave this screen and come back, and we'll let you know when it's ready." If something can't be finished yet you'll see **Try again**, and your details are kept, so there's nothing to type again.

## Outside Nigeria

Payout verification is an identity check with a government ID, plus a payout account in your own name. Outside Nigeria you can't sell until this is done. See [Verification levels](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/verification-levels).

## What level 1 unlocks

- Withdrawals to a bank account in your own name, within [daily limits](/help/wallet-security/withdrawal-limits)
- Converting earned coins to cash
- Ads Manager
- Adding money to your wallet (Nigeria)

The identity check (selfie and ID, level 2) raises your withdrawal limit and is needed for checkmarks.

## Before setup is finished

- You can still sell with escrow (Nigeria) and earn as a creator. Your earnings are recorded and wait safely for you. Nothing is lost while you verify.
- **Withdraw** shows **"Withdrawals are locked"** with a **Finish payout setup** button.
- Sellers must also finish business verification before withdrawing sales money. See [Verify your store](/help/selling/verify-your-store).

## If the name doesn't match

You'll see "This doesn't match" your legal name. Use an account in your own name, or go back and correct your legal name. Payouts never go to someone else's account, including a friend's or a family member's.

## Changing your bank account later

Add another account in **Wallet** > **Payout accounts**. New accounts can receive withdrawals 48 hours after you add them. See [New payout account wait](/help/wallet-security/new-payout-account-cooldown).

You never need a separate login with a payment company. Everything is managed inside Nantty.
