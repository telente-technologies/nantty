Sellers and creators use one **Get paid** flow. If you're both, you set it up once.

## Steps

1. **Who you are**: legal name, date of birth, address and BVN or NIN. Only the last 4 digits of your ID number are kept.
2. **Business** (registered sellers only): CAC number and business type.
3. **Bank account**: choose your bank and enter your 10-digit account number. We look up the account name, and it must match your legal name.
4. **Verify your ID**: only if we ask, by uploading a document.

Each step saves straight away. If something can't be sent yet you'll see **Needs attention** and a **Try again** button. A temporary problem on our side never loses your details.

## Before setup is finished

You can still sell with escrow and earn as a creator. Released money waits as "pending transfer" until your payout account is ready.

You never need a separate payment provider login. Everything is managed inside Zudomart.
