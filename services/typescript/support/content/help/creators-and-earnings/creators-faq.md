## How do I become a creator?

Open your profile menu and tap **Switch to creator account**. If your checklist is all green, tap **Apply now**. See [The Creator Program](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-program).

## What are the requirements?

By default: 1,000 real followers, an account at least 30 days old, 10 original posts in the last 60 days, no violations in the last 90 days, and a verified email. You may get a manual review with fewer followers.

## Do I need a store?

No. You need a verified email and a payout account in your own name. A store is optional.

## My application was rejected. When can I try again?

After 30 days. The date is shown on the **Creator application** screen.

## Does Zudomart pay for views?

No. You earn from gifts, tips, fan subscriptions, affiliate commissions and a share of eligible ads revenue. See [How creators earn](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-earnings).

## How much of a gift do I keep?

50% to 70% of the coins, depending on your tier and monthly gift volume. Your share arrives as earned coins.

## How do I turn coins into money?

In Creator Studio tap **Convert earned coins**. In Nigeria each earned coin is worth ₦10 by default. See [Converting earned coins](/help/creators-and-earnings/convert-earned-coins).

## Can I convert coins I bought?

No. Bought coins are for sending gifts. Coins are not cash.

## How much should I charge for a subscription?

Pick a tier from ₦500 to ₦5,000 a month in Nigeria. See [Pricing your fan subscription](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-subscription-pricing).

## Why do fans pay more in the app?

Apple and Google charge a fee, which is added to the fan's price so you earn about the same. The web is cheaper for fans.

## Why is my money still under Creator earnings?

It moves to **Available to withdraw** once a day when you're payout verified, it's at least ₦100 and monetisation isn't paused. See [When creator earnings become available](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-earnings-holds).

## How do I withdraw?

Tap **Withdraw earnings** in Creator Studio, or **Withdraw** in your wallet. See [Withdrawals](/help/creators-and-earnings/withdrawals).

## Do I earn from ads on my lives?

Only if you turn on live ads. See [Ads on your live](/help/live/live-ads).

## Can I use my earnings to run ads?

Yes. Verified users can pay for ads from money they can withdraw. See [Fund ads from your earnings](/help/ads-and-boosts/fund-ads-from-earnings).

## Will strikes affect my earnings?

Active strikes can pause monetisation and going live. Earnings already made stay yours. See [Suspensions and appeals](/help/safety-and-reporting/suspensions-and-appeals).
