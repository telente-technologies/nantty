Creator Studio is your home as a creator. It shows how much you're earning and which content is working.

## Who this is for

Approved [Creator Program](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-program) members.

## Open Creator Studio

- **In the app**: open your profile menu and tap **Creator tools**.
- **On a computer**: sign in at **studio.zudomart.com** with your Zudomart account.

## What you see in the app

Pick a period at the top: **This week**, **This month** or **All time**.

- **Get paid** card, until your payout details are set up.
- **Coins**: your bought coin **Balance**, your **Earned** coins, **Convert earned coins** and **Coin activity**.
- **Wallet & earnings**: **Earnings this period** with lines for Gifts, Affiliate commissions and Bonuses, new followers, your **Available** balance and **Pending** (the cash value of earned coins not yet converted). Tap **Withdraw earnings** to go to your wallet.
- **Content**: **Create post or clip**.
- **Top lives & posts**: your best content with views and earnings. Tap a post to open its full **Post analytics**.
- **Subscription plans**, your affiliations and **How you earn** (your current rates).

## Per-post analytics

Tap **Views** on your own post, or a post in **Top lives & posts**, to open **Post analytics**. It shows daily impressions for the last seven days and, for videos, watching stats. See [Post analytics](/help/posts-and-feed/post-analytics).

## Per-live stats on the web

At **studio.zudomart.com**, open a live to see **Earnings and audience**: peak viewers, people watching now, gifts received, coins earned, new subscribers, **Top gifters** and **Battle results**.

## Good to know

- Earnings figures are estimates until money moves into your wallet. The wallet is the final record.
- Zudomart doesn't pay for views. Views help you see what works; earnings come from gifts, subscriptions, commissions and ads share.
- Creator Studio numbers refresh when you pull down to refresh.

## If something goes wrong

If Creator Studio shows an error with **See the Creator Program**, your creator status may have changed. Open the button to check. If numbers look wrong, [contact support](/help/contact-support/contact-support).
