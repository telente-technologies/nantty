## For creators

1. Open Creator Studio > **Subscription plans**.
2. Create a plan: name, price, monthly or yearly, and the benefits fans get.
3. Pause new sign-ups or resume a plan any time.

Suggested price bands start at about ₦500 a month. You keep 80% of what fans pay after the app store or payment fee.

Make a post or clip for subscribers only and non-subscribers see a locked preview.

## For fans

Tap **Subscribe** next to a creator's name, pick a plan and pay. Manage or cancel in Settings > Payment methods > Subscriptions. When you cancel, access continues until the end of the period you paid for.

In the mobile apps subscriptions are paid through Apple or Google. On the web they're paid by card or bank.
