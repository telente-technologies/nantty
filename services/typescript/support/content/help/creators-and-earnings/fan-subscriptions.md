Fan subscriptions let your most loyal followers support you every month or year in return for perks you choose.

## Who this is for

Approved [Creator Program](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-program) members can create plans. Anyone with a Nantty account can subscribe.

## For creators: create a plan

1. Open **Creator Studio** from your profile menu (**Creator tools**).
2. In **Subscription plans**, tap **Create a plan**.
3. Give it a **Plan name** (up to 40 characters).
4. Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**, then pick a **Price** from the tiers shown. In Nigeria monthly tiers run from ₦500 to ₦5,000. See [Pricing your subscription](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-subscription-pricing).
5. Add **Benefits (one per line)**, for example subscriber-only lives or a monthly discount code.
6. Tap **Create plan**.

Each plan shows its price, subscriber count and **Active** or **Paused**. Tap **Pause new sign-ups** to stop new subscribers (existing ones keep access), or **Resume plan** to reopen it. **Recent subscribers** lists who joined.

To lock content, turn on **Subscribers only** in the audience settings when you create a post or clip. Non-subscribers see a locked preview.

## For fans: subscribe

1. Open the creator's profile and tap **Subscribe**.
2. Pick a plan and tap **Subscribe for** the price shown.
3. Pay in the secure payment window (web) or through the App Store or Google Play (apps).

The button then reads **Subscribed**. Tap it to see your subscriptions.

## Cancel or manage

Go to **Settings > Payment Methods > Subscriptions**.

- Subscriptions paid on the web: tap **Cancel subscription**. Your access continues until the end of the period you paid for, and you can **Resume subscription** before then.
- Subscriptions billed by Apple or Google: tap **Manage in the App Store** or **Manage in Google Play** and cancel there.
- New phone? Tap **Restore purchases**.

## Good to know

- The creator keeps 80% of what's left after the payment or app store fee.
- Prices in the apps are a little higher because the store fee is added to the fan's price. Subscribing on the web is cheaper.
- If a renewal payment fails, we retry daily for up to 3 days before the subscription lapses.

## If something goes wrong

- **"You are already subscribed to this creator."** Check **Subscriptions** in settings.
- **"Your subscription is still running; resume it instead of subscribing again."** Tap **Resume subscription**.
- **"This creator is not accepting subscriptions right now."** The creator paused or left the program.
- **"This subscription is billed by the app store. Manage it in your store subscription settings."** Cancel with Apple or Google.
- **"You've reached the in-app limit for subscriptions at this price. Subscribe on the web instead."** Subscribe at **app.nantty.com**.
