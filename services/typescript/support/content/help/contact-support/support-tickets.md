Open **Settings > Support > My support requests**. A badge shows tickets with replies you haven't read.

## Statuses

- **Open**: we've received it and will reply soon.
- **Needs your reply**: we've answered and may need more from you.
- **Waiting for support**: you've replied and it's back with us.
- **Resolved**: we think it's sorted. Reply within 7 days to reopen it.
- **Closed**: no more replies. Open a new ticket if you need more help.

## Rate us

When a ticket is resolved you can rate the help you got from 1 to 5. It helps us improve.

## Questions that help others

When we solve a common question, we may turn it into a Help Centre topic marked **From a resolved question**. We remove every personal detail first, such as names, contact details, order numbers, addresses and amounts, and we never use safety tickets.
