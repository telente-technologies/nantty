Every message you send to support becomes a request you can follow from start to finish.

## How to see your requests

Open **Settings > Support > My support requests**, or **My requests** at the top of the **Help centre**. A count such as "2 new" shows replies you haven't read. Tap a request to read the conversation and reply in the box at the bottom (**Message Zudomart Support…**).

On the web: **app.zudomart.com > Help Centre > My support requests**.

## Statuses

- **Open**: we've received it and will reply soon.
- **Needs your reply**: we've answered and may need more from you.
- **Waiting for support**: you've replied and it's back with us.
- **Resolved**: we think it's sorted.
- **Closed**: no more replies on this request.

## Resolved and closed

When we mark a request **Resolved**, you have 7 days to reply if it isn't actually fixed; your reply reopens it. After 7 days it closes automatically. A closed request shows "This request is closed. Contact support again if you still need help." Just open a new one and we'll pick it up.

## Rate us

When a request is resolved you'll see **How happy are you with the help you got?** Pick a score from 1 to 5 and tap **Send rating**. You can rate each request once.

## Questions that help others

When we solve a common question, we may turn it into a Help Centre topic marked **From a resolved question**. We remove every personal detail first, such as names, contact details, order numbers, addresses and amounts, and we never use safety requests.

## If something goes wrong

- **"This ticket was resolved more than 7 days ago. Open a new ticket and we will pick it up."**
- **"This ticket is closed. Open a new ticket and we will pick it up."**
- **"You can rate support once your ticket is resolved."**
- **"Couldn't load your requests."** Pull down to retry.
