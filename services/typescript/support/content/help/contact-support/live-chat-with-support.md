---
title: Live chat with support
summary: Chat with a support agent in real time. Support chats are recorded and are not end-to-end encrypted.
tags: live chat, agent, recorded
related: contact-support, support-tickets
---

Every ticket is a conversation you can continue in real time. When an agent is online you'll see their replies appear as they send them.

Support chats are **recorded for quality and safety and are not end-to-end encrypted**, unlike your private chats. Don't share passwords, PINs or one-time codes.

When the chat ends, the ticket is marked resolved and you can rate it.
