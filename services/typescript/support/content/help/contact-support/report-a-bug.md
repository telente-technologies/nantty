Bugs are things that don't work the way they should: a button that does nothing, a screen that crashes or a wrong number. Reporting them helps us fix them for everyone.

## How to report a bug in the app

1. Go to **Settings > Support > Report a bug**.
2. Your email app opens a message to support@zudomart.com with the subject "Bug report".
3. Tell us:
   - what you were trying to do
   - what you expected to happen
   - what happened instead, with the exact error message
   - your phone model and the app version (from **Settings > About > Version**)
4. Attach screenshots or a short screen recording, then send.

If you'd rather stay in the app, open **Settings > Support > Contact support**, choose **Something else** and describe the bug there. You can add screenshots and follow our replies in **My support requests**.

## Before you report

- Update the app. Many bugs are already fixed. See [Updating the app](/help/troubleshooting/update-the-app).
- Close and reopen the app, and check your connection.
- Look up the message in [Common error messages](/help/troubleshooting/common-error-messages).

## Security problems

If you find a security weakness, such as a way to see someone else's data, email **security@zudomart.com** instead. Please don't share it publicly or test it on other people's accounts.

## Good to know

- We read every bug report, but we may not reply to each one. If the bug stops you buying, selling or getting paid, use **Contact support** so we can follow up.
- Never include passwords, card numbers or codes in a report.
