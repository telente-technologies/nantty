Order and payment problems are the most common reasons people contact us. Picking the right route gets them solved fastest.

## Which route to use

- **The order is late**: check **My orders** first. See [Tracking your order](/help/buying-and-orders/track-your-order). Message the seller from the order if it's past the expected date.
- **The item is wrong, damaged, not as described or never arrived**: open a dispute before the escrow money is released. See [Disputes and refunds](/help/buying-and-orders/disputes-and-refunds).
- **You paid but the order shows unpaid, or you were charged twice**: use **Get help with this payment**.
- **Anything else, or the dispute isn't moving**: use **Get help with this order**.

## How to get help with an order

1. Go to **Profile > More > My orders** and open the order.
2. If you can, tap **Open a dispute**. The seller can't receive the money while a dispute is open.
3. Otherwise tap **Get help with this order**. The support form opens with the topic **An order** and the order already linked.
4. Describe what happened, add photos of the item or packaging, and tap **Send**.

## How to get help with a payment

1. On the payment screen, try **I've paid, check status** first. Bank transfers can take a few minutes to confirm.
2. If it still doesn't confirm, tap **Get help with this payment**. The form opens under **Payments & wallet** with the payment linked.
3. Add a screenshot of your bank alert or debit, then tap **Send**.

## Good to know

- Payment requests are high priority. We aim to reply within about 4 hours.
- With escrow in Nigeria, your money stays locked until you confirm delivery or the dispute is decided, so it's safe while we look.
- Outside Nigeria, direct payments go straight to the seller. Contact support quickly if something goes wrong. See [Escrow or direct payment](/help/buying-and-orders/escrow-or-direct-payment).

## If something goes wrong

- **"This order can no longer be disputed."** The escrow money was already released. Contact support with **Get help with this order**.
- **"This dispute is closed."** Read the outcome on the order, then contact support if you disagree.

## Stay safe

Never pay a seller outside Zudomart or send money to "unlock" an order. Support will never ask you to pay into a personal account. See [Payment scams](/help/wallet-security/payment-scams).
