## How do I contact Zudomart?

In the app: **Settings > Support > Contact support**. See [Contacting support](/help/contact-support/contact-support).

## Is there a phone number?

No. We help through the app and by email, so there's always a written record and nobody can impersonate us on a call.

## How long until you reply?

Payment and safety requests first, usually within about 4 hours. Most others within about 8 hours, and general questions within a day.

## Where do I see your reply?

In **Settings > Support > My support requests**. We also send a notification and an email. See [Your support requests](/help/contact-support/support-tickets).

## Can I chat with a person?

Yes. Your request works like a chat, and you see replies in real time when an agent is online. See [Live chat with support](/help/contact-support/live-chat-with-support).

## Are support chats private?

They're recorded for quality and safety and aren't end-to-end encrypted, unlike your chats with other people.

## My request was resolved but the problem is back.

Reply within 7 days to reopen it. After that, open a new request.

## I have a problem with an order.

Use **Get help with this order** on the order, or open a dispute. See [Getting help with an order or payment](/help/contact-support/get-help-with-an-order).

## I can't sign in. How do I reach you?

Email **support@zudomart.com** from the address on your account. See [I can't sign in](/help/troubleshooting/cant-sign-in).

## How do I report a bug?

**Settings > Support > Report a bug**. See [Reporting a bug](/help/contact-support/report-a-bug).

## How do I report a person or post?

Use **Report** on the post, profile or chat. Track it in **Settings > Support > My reports**. See [Reporting a problem](/help/safety-and-reporting/report-a-problem).

## Someone says they're Zudomart support. Are they?

Only if the message is in **My support requests** or from an email ending in zudomart.com. See [What Zudomart support will never ask for](/help/contact-support/what-support-never-asks).

## Can support see my private messages?

No. Your chats are end-to-end encrypted. If you report a chat, you choose which messages to send.
