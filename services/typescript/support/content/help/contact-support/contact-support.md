Most answers are here in the Help Centre. If you still need help, our support team is one tap away.

## How to contact support

1. Open **Settings > Support > Contact support**, or tap **Contact support** in the **Help centre**. From an order, tap **Get help with this order**; from a payment, **Get help with this payment**. These attach the order or payment for you.
2. Under **What do you need help with?**, pick a topic: **An order**, **Payments & wallet**, **My account**, **Selling**, **Checkmarks**, **Creator and earnings**, **Live**, **Ads and boosts**, **Safety** or **Something else**.
3. Write a short **Subject**, for example "My order hasn't arrived".
4. Under **Tell us more**, include order numbers and what you've already tried.
5. Optionally, **Link an order or payment** and **Add screenshot** (up to 5 per message).
6. Tap **Send**. You'll get a reference like SUP-10421.

On the web, go to **app.zudomart.com > Help Centre > Contact support**.

## When we'll reply

- **Payments & wallet** and **Safety** requests go to the front of the queue. We aim to reply within about 4 hours.
- Most other topics: within about 8 hours.
- **Ads and boosts** and **Something else**: within a day.

You'll get a push notification, a badge on **My support requests** and an email when we reply.

## Email us

If you can't open the app, write to the right team. Every address ends in **zudomart.com**.

- Orders, payments and your account: **support@zudomart.com**
- Seller stores and payouts: **sellers@zudomart.com**
- Creator Program: **creators@zudomart.com**
- Your personal data: **privacy@zudomart.com**
- Copyright notices: **copyright@zudomart.com**
- Security problems you've found: **security@zudomart.com**

**Contact Us** in **Settings > Support** opens an email to support@zudomart.com. Emails we send automatically come from **mail.zudomart.com**, such as noreply@mail.zudomart.com. Replies to those go to support@zudomart.com.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Attachments are not available right now. Send your message without them."** Send the text now and add screenshots later in the same request.

## Stay safe

Never include your password, card PIN or one-time codes. Zudomart will never ask for them. See [What support will never ask for](/help/contact-support/what-support-never-asks).
