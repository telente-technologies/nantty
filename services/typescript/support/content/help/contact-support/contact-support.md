Most answers are here in the Help Centre. If you still need help:

1. Open **Help Centre > Contact support** (or **Get help** on an order, payment or live, which attaches it for you).
2. Choose a topic, write a short subject and describe what happened. Add screenshots if they help.
3. Send it. You'll get a reference number like SUP-10421.

## When we'll reply

We aim to reply within a few hours. Urgent safety and payment issues are handled first. You'll get a push notification, an in-app badge and an email when we reply.

## Keep yourself safe

Never include your password, card PIN or one-time codes. Zudomart will never ask for them.

## Email us

If you can't open the app, write to the right team. Every address ends in @zudomart.com and we never email from any other domain.

- Orders, payments and your account: **support@zudomart.com**
- Seller stores and payouts: **sellers@zudomart.com**
- Creator Program: **creators@zudomart.com**
- Your personal data, or our Data Protection Officer: **privacy@zudomart.com**
- Copyright notices: **copyright@zudomart.com**
- Security problems you've found: **security@zudomart.com**

Emails we send automatically come from **@mail.zudomart.com**, such as noreply@mail.zudomart.com and receipts@mail.zudomart.com. Replies to those go to support@zudomart.com.
