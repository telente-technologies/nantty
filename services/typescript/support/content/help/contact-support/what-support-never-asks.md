Scammers often pretend to be "Nantty customer care" to steal accounts and money. Knowing what we'd never do keeps you safe.

## Nantty support will never

- ask for your password, card PIN, CVV or the codes we email you
- ask you to send money, pay a "release fee" or transfer funds to "unlock" a withdrawal, refund or order
- ask you to pay into a personal bank account or any account that isn't the official checkout
- ask for your BVN or ID in a chat or by email (you only enter these in the app's own verification screens)
- message you first on WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram or by phone call
- ask you to install remote-control or screen-sharing apps
- send you links to sign in on a website that isn't nantty.com

## Where real support lives

- Inside the app: **Settings > Support > My support requests**, and replies from Nantty Support in that thread.
- Email from addresses ending in **nantty.com** or **mail.nantty.com**.
- On the web: **app.nantty.com > Help Centre**.

If someone contacts you any other way claiming to be Nantty, it's not us.

## If you're not sure

1. Stop. Don't share anything or pay anything.
2. Open the Nantty app yourself and check **My support requests** for a real message.
3. If it's not there, it's fake.

## If you've already shared something

- **Password or code**: reset your password straight away. See [If your account was hacked](/help/account-and-login/hacked-account).
- **Card details or PIN**: call your bank and block the card.
- **Sent money**: contact your bank, then [report the scam](/help/safety-and-reporting/report-a-scam) and contact support under **Safety**.

## Report fake support accounts

Report the profile or chat from its menu. See [Impersonation](/help/safety-and-reporting/impersonation).
