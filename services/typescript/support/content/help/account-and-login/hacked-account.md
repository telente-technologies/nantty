If you see a sign-in you don't recognise, posts or messages you didn't send, or a password change you didn't make, act quickly.

## What to do now

1. **Reset your password.** On the log-in screen tap **Forgot password?**, enter your email and follow the code. A reset signs out every device, including the attacker's.
2. **Secure your email account.** Your Nantty password can be reset through your email, so change your email password too and turn on its own 2FA.
3. **Check your sessions.** Sellers can review **Settings > Security** on **dashboard.nantty.com** and tap **Sign out all others**. See [Devices and active sessions](/help/account-and-login/active-sessions).
4. **Check your wallet.** Open **Wallet > Payout accounts** and **All transactions**. Report any payout account you didn't add.
5. **Tell us.** In the app go to **Settings > Support > Contact support**, choose **My account**, and say what happened. If you can't sign in, email **support@nantty.com** from the address on your account.

## How your money is protected

- Withdrawals only go to a bank account in your own name.
- Adding a payout account needs a fresh **Confirm it's you** check and sends you an email and push notice.
- A new payout account can't receive withdrawals for 48 hours, which gives you time to stop it.
- Withdrawals and new payout accounts always need your password or an emailed code.

See [Wallet safety basics](/help/wallet-security/wallet-safety-basics).

## Signs to watch for

- A "New sign-in" alert for a device you don't own. See [Login alerts](/help/account-and-login/login-alerts).
- A "Your password was changed" email you didn't expect.
- A code email you didn't ask for.
- "We signed you out for your safety". We detected a reused sign-in token and ended the affected sessions.

## If something goes wrong

- **"The email, username or password is incorrect."** The password may have been changed. Use **Forgot password?**.
- **"This account has been suspended."** Hacked accounts are sometimes restricted after spam. Contact support and we'll review it.

## Stay safe

Most takeovers start with a fake message asking for a code or a "verification" link. Nantty will never ask for your password, card PIN or codes. See [Payment scams](/help/wallet-security/payment-scams).
