## Can I sign up or sign in with my phone number?

No. Zudomart uses email only, plus Google or Apple sign-in. Your phone number is just a contact detail you can add in **Settings > Account**. See [Creating your account](/help/account-and-login/create-an-account).

## Can I sign in with my username?

Yes. On the log-in screen you can type your email or your username with your password. See [Signing in, passwords and resets](/help/account-and-login/sign-in-and-passwords).

## I didn't get my code. What now?

Wait a minute, check spam, then tap resend. Codes last 5 minutes. See [Not getting our emails](/help/troubleshooting/not-getting-emails).

## How old do I need to be?

At least 13. Accounts under 18 always see warnings on sensitive content and never see adult content.

## I forgot my password.

Tap **Forgot password?** on the log-in screen and follow the emailed code. See [Signing in, passwords and resets](/help/account-and-login/sign-in-and-passwords).

## I joined with Google. Can I add a password?

Yes. Use **Forgot password?** with the same email to set one. You can keep using Google too.

## Can I turn on two-factor authentication?

Not yet. The setting is in **Settings > Security & Password** but sign-in 2FA isn't live. See [Two-factor authentication](/help/account-and-login/two-factor-authentication).

## How do I change my email?

It can't be changed in the app yet. See [Changing your email address](/help/account-and-login/change-your-email).

## How often can I change my username?

Once every 30 days, up to 3 times a year. See [Usernames: rules and changes](/help/account-and-login/usernames).

## How do I sign out of other phones?

Change your password, or, if you sell, use **Sign out all others** in the seller dashboard. See [Devices and active sessions](/help/account-and-login/active-sessions).

## I got a "New sign-in" alert I don't recognise.

Reset your password now. See [If your account was hacked](/help/account-and-login/hacked-account).

## Why am I asked to "Confirm you're a person"?

It's a quick security check after unusual activity. See [Security checks](/help/account-and-login/security-checks).

## What's the difference between deactivating and deleting?

**Deactivate account** (in **Settings > Account**) hides your profile and store until you log in again. **Delete my account** (in **Settings > Privacy & Data**) starts a 30-day countdown to permanent deletion. See [Deleting your account](/help/legal-and-data/delete-your-account).

## Can I have more than one account?

One email gives one account, but you can add business and brand profiles to it and switch between them. See [Switching profiles](/help/business-and-brands/switch-profiles).
