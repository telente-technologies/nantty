## Why you see a security check

We show a short check, run by Cloudflare Turnstile, when:

- your account tried to open things that belong to someone else, such as another person's orders, messages, payouts or staff-only pages, many times in a short period;
- your account or device behaves like automated software, for example perfectly regular request timing, a browser controlled by a script, or follows and likes far faster than a person could manage.

Most checks finish on their own in a second or two. Sometimes you are asked to tick a box.

## What happens next

- **After unusual activity:** pass the check once and you can carry on. We stop asking for a while.
- **If your account is marked as likely automated:** you are asked again at the start of each session and before sensitive actions such as posting, following, messaging, liking many posts quickly, checking out and withdrawing money. A Trust & Safety reviewer can remove this if it is a mistake.
- **If checks keep failing**, or the unusual activity carries on after several checks in a day, your account is **suspended temporarily**. You'll see the reason "Security: repeated unauthorised access attempts" and can appeal from the suspension notice.

Automated systems never ban or delete accounts. A person on our team reviews the evidence before any ban or deletion, and you can still appeal.

## Tips

- Turn off browser extensions that block scripts from challenges.cloudflare.com, then try again.
- Make sure your device's date and time are set automatically.
- In the app, the check opens in a secure in-app browser sheet and brings you straight back.

## Privacy

Cloudflare receives technical signals from your browser or device to run the check and tells us only whether it passed. We log refused requests as page types and counts, never the content you sent. See the Privacy Policy, "Security" and "Who we share data with".
