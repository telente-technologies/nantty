You need a Zudomart account to like, follow, comment, message, post, buy or sell. Signing up takes about two minutes and only needs an email address.

## Who this is for

Anyone aged 13 or older, in any country. Your phone number is not used to sign up or sign in. You can add one later as a contact detail in **Settings > Account > Phone number**.

## How to sign up with your email

1. Open the app and tap **Email** on the welcome screen. On the web, go to **app.zudomart.com** and choose **Sign up**.
2. Under **What's your email?**, enter your email address and tap **Send Code**.
3. Check your inbox for a 6-digit code and type it on the **Enter 6-digit code** screen.
4. Under **When's your birthday?**, enter your date of birth as dd/mm/yyyy. It is never shown publicly.
5. Under **Create password**, choose a password with 8 to 20 characters, at least 1 letter and 1 number, and 1 special character such as # $ % or !.
6. Under **Create username**, keep the username we suggest or type your own. You can change it later.
7. Pick three or more topics you like so your first feed isn't empty.

By continuing you agree to our Terms of Service and confirm you have read the Privacy Policy and Cookie Use.

## Sign up with Google or Apple

Tap **Continue with Google**, or **Continue with Apple** on an iPhone, and follow the prompts. Your email address comes from Google or Apple, so you don't need a code. If you later want to sign in with a password too, use **Forgot password?** to set one.

## Good to know

- Codes expire after 5 minutes. You can ask for a new one after 1 minute.
- After 5 wrong tries you need to request a new code.
- One email address can only belong to one account.
- Before you sign up you can look around as a guest. See [Browsing without an account](/help/getting-started/browsing-as-a-guest).

## If something goes wrong

- **"An account with this email already exists."** Sign in instead, or use **Forgot password?**.
- **"You must be at least 13 years old to use Zudomart."** You can't create an account yet.
- **"Enter a real date, like 27/10/2000."** Check the birthday format: day, month, then year.
- **"That code didn't work. Try again."** or **"The code is invalid or has expired."** Request a new code and use the latest email.
- **"Your verification expired. Go back and request a new code."** You took longer than 15 minutes after confirming the code. Start again.
- **"The provider has not verified your email address."** Confirm your email with Google or Apple first, or sign up with **Email**.
- **"We cannot create an account with these details."** These details are blocked from signing up. [Contact support](/help/contact-support/contact-support) if you think this is a mistake.
- No code? See [Not getting our emails](/help/troubleshooting/not-getting-emails).

## Stay safe

Zudomart will never ask for your password or the codes we email you. Anyone who asks is trying to take over your account.
