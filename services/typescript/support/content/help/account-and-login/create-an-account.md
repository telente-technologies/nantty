## Sign up

1. Open the app (or app.zudomart.com) and choose **Create account**.
2. Enter your details or continue with a supported social sign-in.
3. Enter the one-time code we send to confirm your email or phone.
4. Accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, then pick your interests to shape your first feed.

Zudomart gives you a username automatically. You can change it later, see [Usernames](/help/account-and-login/usernames).

## Keep your account safe

- Never share your password, card PIN or one-time codes. Zudomart staff will never ask for them.
- Turn on two-factor authentication in Settings > Security.
