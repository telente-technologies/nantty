Two-factor authentication (2FA) asks for a second code after your password.

## Turn it on

Go to Settings > Security > Two-factor authentication and follow the steps. We email you when 2FA is turned on.

## Tips

- Keep your recovery options up to date.
- If you get a code you didn't ask for, someone may know your password. Change it now.
