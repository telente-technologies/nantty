Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a second step after your password, so a stolen password alone can't get someone into your account.

## Where things stand

You'll find **Two-factor authentication** in **Settings > Security & Password**, showing **OFF**. Tapping **Turn on 2FA** describes how it will work: a one-time code, sent to your email, every time you log in on a new device.

Two-factor sign-in is not available yet. If you try to turn it on today you'll see **"Two-factor sign-in is not available yet."** Nothing is wrong with your account. We'll announce it in the app when it goes live, and we'll send you a "Two-factor sign-in turned on" notice when you switch it on.

## What protects you today

- **Strong password rules**: 8 to 20 characters with a letter, a number and a special character. See [Signing in, passwords and resets](/help/account-and-login/sign-in-and-passwords).
- **Confirm it's you**: before sensitive actions like withdrawing money or adding a payout account, we ask for your password or a code we email you. See [Step-up codes](/help/wallet-security/step-up-codes).
- **New sign-in alerts**: when your account signs in on a device we haven't seen, we send a push notification and an email. See [Login alerts](/help/account-and-login/login-alerts).
- **Automatic sign-out**: if we detect a stolen session being reused, we sign the affected sessions out and email you.
- **Security checks**: unusual activity triggers a quick human check. See [Security checks](/help/account-and-login/security-checks).

## Good to know

- All codes go to your email. Zudomart doesn't send login codes by SMS.
- Keep your email account itself secure, with its own strong password and 2FA, because it is the key to resetting your Zudomart password.
- Sellers see a **Sign-in protection** card on **dashboard.zudomart.com** under **Settings > Security**. It points back to this same setting in the app.

## Stay safe

If you receive a Zudomart code you didn't request, someone may know your email or password. Change your password now and read [If your account was hacked](/help/account-and-login/hacked-account). Never share a code with anyone, including people who say they work for Zudomart.
