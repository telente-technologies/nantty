Your email address is how you sign in, how we send codes and receipts, and where payment updates go. A verified email is the first verification level (L0).

## Who this is for

Everyone. If you signed up with **Email**, the code you entered during sign-up already verified it. If you joined with Google or Apple, the address they shared is already confirmed by them.

## How to verify your email on the web

If checkout or a payment screen asks you to verify:

1. Go to **app.nantty.com**, open **Settings** and choose **Email** ("Verify the email used for receipts and payments").
2. Tap **Send code**. We email a 6-digit code to the address on your account.
3. Type the code under **Verification code** and tap **Verify email**.
4. You'll see **Email verified**, then **Your email is verified**. Tap **Continue** to go back to what you were doing.

## Why it matters

- Checkout needs a verified email, because payments and receipts use it.
- Blue and Gold checkmarks need a verified email before you can apply.
- Free trials of paid plans need a verified email.
- Codes to **Confirm it's you** before withdrawals go to this address.

## Good to know

- Codes expire after 5 minutes. **Resend code** unlocks after a short countdown.
- Check your spam or promotions folder. Our emails come from addresses ending in **mail.nantty.com**.
- You can't change your email in the app yet. See [Changing your email address](/help/account-and-login/change-your-email).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Your email address is already verified."** Nothing more to do.
- **"Request a verification code first."** Tap **Send code** before entering a code.
- **"The code is invalid or has expired."** Send a new code and use the newest email.
- **"Too many incorrect attempts. Request a new code."** Ask for a fresh code.
- **"Verify your email address first."** An action needs a verified email. Follow the steps above.
- Still no email? See [Not getting our emails](/help/troubleshooting/not-getting-emails).

## Stay safe

Never share your email code. Nantty staff will never ask for it.
