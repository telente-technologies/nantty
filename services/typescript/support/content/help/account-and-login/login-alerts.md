Security alerts tell you about important changes to your account so you can react quickly if something isn't right.

## Alerts we send

- **New sign-in**: when your account signs in on a device we haven't seen before. In the app it reads "New sign-in to your account" with the device and location. The push says "Was this you? Review your account activity." We also email you.
- **Your password was changed**: after any password change or reset, in the app and by email.
- **You were signed out everywhere**: when all your sessions are ended at once.
- **We signed you out for your safety**: when we detect a stolen sign-in token being reused. Sign in again and change your password.
- **Account restricted, warning, suspended or banned**: with the reason and how to appeal.

## Good to know

- Security alerts belong to a protected category. You can't switch them off, so you always hear about changes to your account.
- Your very first sign-in on a new account doesn't trigger an alert.
- Locations are estimates based on your network, so a nearby city is normal.
- You'll find in-app alerts on the **Notifications** screen (the bell on **Home**).

## What to do with an alert

- **It was you**: nothing to do.
- **It wasn't you**: reset your password with **Forgot password?**, then follow [If your account was hacked](/help/account-and-login/hacked-account).

## Stay safe

Our security emails come from addresses ending in **mail.zudomart.com** and never ask you to reply with a code or password. If an "alert" asks you to log in through a link to another website, it's fake. Open the Zudomart app directly instead.
