Nantty sign-in is email based. There is no phone number or SMS login, and every code we send goes to your email.

## How to sign in

1. On the welcome screen tap **Email**, then **Log in** if you're on the sign-up view.
2. Enter your email address or your username, and your password.
3. Tap **Log in**.

If you joined with Google or Apple, tap **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Apple** instead. On the web, go to **app.nantty.com** and choose **Log in**.

## Forgot your password

1. On the log-in screen tap **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter the email on your account and tap **Send Code**.
3. Enter the 6-digit code from your email.
4. On **Create new password**, type a new password twice and tap **Save password**. It must be different from your old one.

Resetting your password signs you out on every device. Sign in again with the new password.

## Change your password

1. Go to **Profile > More (top right) > Settings > Security & Password > Password**.
2. Enter your current password, then your new password twice.
3. Tap **Save**.

Other devices are signed out; the device you're using stays signed in. We send you a "Your password was changed" notice and an email. If it wasn't you, reset your password straight away and read [If your account was hacked](/help/account-and-login/hacked-account).

## Password rules

- 8 to 20 characters
- at least 1 letter and 1 number
- at least 1 special character, for example # $ % ! or the at sign (@)

## Good to know

- Codes expire after 5 minutes, and you can request a new one after 1 minute.
- Sessions stay signed in for up to 30 days of inactivity. After that, sign in again.
- When you do something sensitive, such as withdrawing money, we may ask you to **Confirm it's you** with your password or an emailed code. See [Step-up codes](/help/wallet-security/step-up-codes).

## If something goes wrong

- **"The email, username or password is incorrect."** Check for typos, or use **Forgot password?**.
- **"Your current password is incorrect."** When changing your password, re-enter the one you use today.
- **"Passwords don't match"** The two new passwords must be the same.
- **"Your reset code expired. Request a new one."** Go back and send a new code.
- **"Too many incorrect attempts. Request a new code."** Ask for a fresh code.
- **"Your session has ended. Sign in again."** You were signed out, often after a password change. Sign in again.
- **"This account has been suspended."** See [Suspensions and appeals](/help/safety-and-reporting/suspensions-and-appeals).
- **"This account was deleted. Create a new account to come back."** The 30-day window to cancel deletion has passed.
- More fixes: [I can't sign in](/help/troubleshooting/cant-sign-in).

## Stay safe

Nantty will never ask for your password or your email codes by chat, phone or email. Never type them anywhere except the Nantty app or app.nantty.com.
