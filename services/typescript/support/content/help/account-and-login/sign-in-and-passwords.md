## Sign in

Enter your email or phone and your password, or use the social sign-in you signed up with.

## Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in screen tap **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your email or phone. We send a one-time code.
3. Enter the code and choose a new password.

Codes expire after a short time. If yours expired, request a new one.

## Change your password

Go to Settings > Security > Change password. We email you when your password changes. If that wasn't you, reset your password straight away and contact support.

## Not receiving codes

- Check the email spam folder, or that your phone has signal.
- Wait a minute before requesting another code. Requests are limited to protect your account.
- If you changed your phone number and can't receive codes, [contact support](/help/contact-support/contact-support).
