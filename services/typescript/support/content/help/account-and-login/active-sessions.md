A session is one device or browser where your account is signed in. Keeping an eye on them helps you spot someone else using your account.

## Who this is for

Everyone can sign out of the device they're holding. The full list of sessions is currently shown in the seller dashboard, so sellers and their teams can see and end sessions there. Everyone else can end all other sessions by changing their password.

## Sign out of this device

In the app go to **Settings** and tap **Log out** at the bottom, then confirm **Log out**.

## See and end sessions (sellers)

1. Sign in at **dashboard.nantty.com**.
2. Go to **Settings > Security**.
3. **Where you are signed in** lists your dashboard and Nantty app sessions with device, location and when each was last active. Your current one is marked **This device**.
4. Tap **Sign out** next to a session, or **Sign out all others** to end every session except this one.

The same page shows **Recent sign-ins**, including blocked attempts and wrong passwords.

## Sign out everywhere (everyone)

Change your password in **Settings > Security & Password > Password**. Every other device is signed out at once. Resetting your password with **Forgot password?** signs out every device, including the one you're using.

## Good to know

- A session stays signed in for up to 30 days without use, then asks you to sign in again.
- When a new device signs in, we send a "New sign-in" alert. See [Login alerts](/help/account-and-login/login-alerts).
- If we detect a stolen sign-in token being reused, we sign the affected sessions out and tell you "We signed you out for your safety".

## If something goes wrong

- **"Use sign out to end the session you are using."** You tried to end your current session from the list. Use log out instead.
- **"That session was not found."** It has already ended. Refresh the list.
- **"Your session has ended. Sign in again."** This device was signed out. Sign in again.

## Stay safe

If you see a session you don't recognise, sign it out, change your password and check [If your account was hacked](/help/account-and-login/hacked-account).
