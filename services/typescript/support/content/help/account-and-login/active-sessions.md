## See your devices

Settings > Security > Active sessions lists every device signed in to your account.

## Sign out somewhere else

Tap a session and choose **Sign out**. That device has to sign in again.

## New sign-in alerts

When your account is used on a new device we send you an alert. If you don't recognise it, sign that session out and change your password. If Zudomart detects a stolen sign-in token being reused, we sign the affected sessions out for your safety and email you.
