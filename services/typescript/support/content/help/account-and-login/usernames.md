Your username is your @handle and your profile link (app.zudomart.com/yourusername).

## Rules

- Some usernames are reserved for Zudomart, brands or public bodies, and some look-alike names are blocked. If a name isn't allowed, the app tells you why.
- Changes are limited so people can keep finding you. The app shows when you can next change it.

## Changing your username

Go to Edit profile > Username, check it's available and save. Your old links stop pointing to you.

## When you give up a username

A username you stop using is held for 120 days before anyone else can take it. Only Zudomart staff can reassign a username earlier, for example to resolve an impersonation or trademark case.
