One Zudomart account can hold your personal profile and up to 10 business or brand profiles. You sign in once and switch between them.

## Who this is for

Anyone who runs a business, brand or organisation profile next to their personal one.

## How to switch profiles

1. Long-press the **Me** tab at the bottom of the app. You can also tap the profile switcher on your own profile.
2. The **Switch profile** sheet opens: "Posts, messages and store actions happen as the profile you choose."
3. Each profile shows its handle and whether it is **Personal** or **Business**. Tap the one you want.

When you write a post, the composer shows a **Posting as** row with the current profile. Tap it to change who you post as before you publish.

## Add a new profile

From the **Switch profile** sheet tap **Create business profile** or **Create a brand profile**. After you create it, the app switches you to the new profile. See [Business profiles](/help/business-and-brands/business-profiles) and [Brand pages](/help/business-and-brands/brand-pages).

## Good to know

- Posts, comments, likes, messages and store actions all come from the profile you are switched to. Check before you post.
- Your login, password and two-factor settings belong to your account, so you sign in once for every profile.
- Switching profiles is done in the mobile app. Store tools are also on the web at **dashboard.zudomart.com**.
- Staff who help with your store should use their own Zudomart accounts. See [Adding staff to your store](/help/selling/seller-team).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Couldn't load your profiles."** Check your connection, close the sheet and try again.
- **"That profile was not found."** The profile may have been removed. Close the sheet, open it again and pick another.

## Stay safe

Never share your password so someone else can post for your business. Anyone with your login can reach every profile on your account.
