Your personal account can own at most two linked profiles: **one Store or one Business page** (never both), plus **one Brand**.

## What you can have

| You own | Allowed? |
|---|---|
| Nothing (just your personal profile) | Yes |
| 1 Store | Yes |
| 1 Business page | Yes |
| 1 Brand | Yes |
| 1 Store and 1 Brand | Yes |
| 1 Business page and 1 Brand | Yes |
| A Store and a Business page | No, choose one |
| 2 or more Stores, Business pages or Brands | No |

## Store or Business page

- A **Store** sells in Mart. Its profile shows the Mart icon and a products tab.
- A **Business page** is for organisations that don't sell, such as NGOs, media houses and venues. It never shows the Mart icon or a products tab.

Go to **Settings › Store, Business page and Brand** to pick one. Once you have one, the other option is hidden and you'll see "You already have a Store" or "You already have a Business page".

## Brands

1. Tap **Create a brand**. The option only appears if you don't have a Brand yet.
2. A new Brand starts as a showcase: posts, clips, lives and brand protection, with no products.
3. To sell, open brand settings and turn on **Sell products with this brand**. You'll confirm the change and confirm it's you.

A brand storefront is a sales channel of your Brand. It doesn't use your Store slot, so you can still have one Store or one Business page as well.

Brand selling follows the same money rules as stores: your Zudomart wallet, escrow in Nigeria, identity verification before selling outside Nigeria, and seller debt limits.

## Turning brand selling off

- Your brand products and storefront are hidden straight away.
- You can't add new listings.
- Open orders and payouts carry on until they're complete.

## If your account has more than the rule allows

Accounts created before this rule may have extra profiles. Nothing is deleted. The extras can't post or add listings until you choose which to keep:

1. Go to **Settings › Choose which profiles to keep**.
2. Pick one Store or Business page, and one Brand.
3. Tap **Keep selected profiles** and confirm it's you.

Profiles you don't keep stay visible, and their open orders and payouts carry on. Support can also help you choose.

## If something goes wrong

- **"You can have one Store or one Business page, not both."** You already have one of them. Manage it from your profiles.
- **"You can have one Brand."** Your account already has a Brand.
- **"Choose which profiles to keep before using this one."** Your account has extra profiles on hold. Choose which to keep first.
- **"Turn on selling for this brand before adding listings."** Turn on **Sell products with this brand** in brand settings.
