## Do I need a separate account for my business?

No. One account holds your personal profile, plus one Store or one Business page and one Brand. See [Stores, business pages and brands](/help/business-and-brands/stores-business-pages-and-brands). You sign in once and switch between them. See [Switching between your profiles](/help/business-and-brands/switch-profiles).

## Do I need a store to have a business profile?

No. A business profile works for any organisation, including NGOs, media and venues. You can open a store later. See [Business profiles](/help/business-and-brands/business-profiles).

## What is the difference between a business profile and a brand page?

A business profile is your organisation's public profile. A brand page represents a product brand: it shows the brand's Official Store and other sellers who stock it. See [Brand pages and Official Stores](/help/business-and-brands/brand-pages).

## Can my business get the Blue checkmark?

No. Business and brand profiles can only carry Gold. Blue is for personal profiles, including creators. See [The Gold checkmark](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/gold-checkmark).

## How does my business get Gold?

Upload your CAC certificate, a government ID and proof of address, then be on a paid seller plan or the Gold-only plan. Gold only appears after your documents are approved. See [Business documents](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/business-documents).

## How much is Gold for a business that doesn't sell?

The Gold-only plan is ₦3,000 a month in Nigeria, paid on the web. It is "For brands and businesses that do not sell on Nantty." Yearly billing is 20% cheaper. See [Checkmark prices](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/checkmark-prices).

## What is an Official Store?

The brand's own store, approved by our brand team. Only registered brands selling through a Gold-verified store can become one. Buyers see an **Official Store** badge.

## Someone is selling fakes of my brand. What do I do?

Report the listing from its menu, or file a claim from the brand tools in the seller dashboard on **dashboard.nantty.com**. See [Report a scam](/help/safety-and-reporting/report-a-scam).

## Who can have a government account?

Only official public bodies. They go through deep vetting, two separate approvals and yearly re-verification for the Black checkmark, which costs ₦50,000 a month. See [Government accounts](/help/business-and-brands/government-accounts).

## Can I post as my business from the web?

Switching profiles is done in the mobile app. Long-press the **Me** tab, pick the business profile, then post.

## Can I email my store's customers from Nantty?

No. Sellers can't send marketing emails through Nantty. Use discount codes, flash sales, store rewards and ads instead. See [Promotions and discounts](/help/selling/promotions-and-discounts).

## How do I stop Nantty marketing emails?

Tap **Unsubscribe** in any marketing email, or turn off **Marketing messages** in **Settings > Privacy & Data**. See [Marketing emails](/help/business-and-brands/marketing-emails).

## Can my staff manage my business?

Yes, on a paid seller plan. Add them by username in the dashboard team page. They use their own logins. See [Adding staff to your store](/help/selling/seller-team).
