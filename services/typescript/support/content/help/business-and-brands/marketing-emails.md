Marketing emails are the optional emails we send about things you might like. You decide whether you get them and how often.

## What counts as marketing

- **Picked for you**: products we think you'll like.
- **Price drop on saved item**.
- **Back in stock**.
- **Abandoned cart**: a reminder about items left in your cart.
- **Weekly digest**.

These only feature verified sellers and brands. Order updates, receipts, security alerts and legal notices are not marketing, so they always arrive even if you turn marketing off.

## How to choose what you get

In the app:

1. Open **Settings > Privacy & Data**.
2. Under **Your choices**, turn **Marketing messages** on or off: "Personalised picks from verified sellers, price drops, back in stock, weekly digest."
3. Under **How often should we email you?** pick **Weekly**, **Only important** or **Off**.

On the web (app.nantty.com), the same **Marketing messages** choice is in your privacy settings.

## Unsubscribe from an email

Every marketing email has **Unsubscribe** and **Email preferences** links at the bottom, with a line explaining why you got it. One tap on **Unsubscribe** turns marketing emails off. Many email apps also show their own unsubscribe button at the top, which does the same thing.

## Good to know

- We send at most 3 marketing emails in 7 days, or 1 a week if you chose **Weekly**.
- **Only important** and **Off** stop marketing emails completely.
- If you mark one of our emails as spam, we stop sending you marketing emails.
- Sellers on Nantty can't email you marketing from their stores. They reach buyers through discounts, flash sales and ads inside Nantty instead. See [Promotions and discounts](/help/selling/promotions-and-discounts).

## If something goes wrong

- **"This unsubscribe link is not valid."** The link is old or was cut off. Turn off **Marketing messages** in the app instead.
- Still getting emails after you unsubscribed? Order and security emails continue by design. If you still get marketing emails a few days later, [contact support](/help/contact-support/contact-support).

## Stay safe

Our emails only come from nantty.com or mail.nantty.com addresses. We never ask for your password, card PIN or codes by email. If an email looks like us but asks for those, it isn't us. See [Report a scam](/help/safety-and-reporting/report-a-scam).
