A brand page is the home of a brand on Zudomart. Buyers use it to find the brand's official store and to see which other sellers stock its products.

## Who this is for

- **Brand owners** who make or own a product line.
- **Sellers** who stock branded goods.
- **Buyers** who want the genuine item.

## How to create a brand

1. Long-press the **Me** tab to open **Switch profile**.
2. Tap **Create a brand profile**.
3. Fill in **Brand name**, **Handle**, **Category**, **Description** (at least 20 characters) and **Website (optional)**.
4. Tap **Create brand**.

The app explains it this way: "A brand profile represents your business or organisation. Verify it with Gold to open an Official Store."

## What buyers see on a brand page

- **Follow** to see the brand's posts.
- **Official store**: the brand's own verified store, marked **Official Store**, with a **Visit** button. If there isn't one yet, the page says the brand "hasn't opened a verified official store yet."
- **Also sold by other sellers**: "Independent sellers — not the official store."
- **Posts** from the brand.

## Official Store status

Only registered brands selling through a Gold-verified store can become an Official Store. Our brand team checks the trademark registration, that the trademark owner matches the CAC company, that the contact email is on the brand's own website domain, and that at least one linked store is Gold verified. When approved, the brand's verified stores show the **Official Store** badge. Brand owners manage this on the web in the seller dashboard on **dashboard.zudomart.com** (the app shows **Manage on the web**).

## Tagging a brand on your products (sellers)

On a product, the **Brand** section lets you tag the brand "so buyers can find it and brands can protect their name." Under how you sell it, pick **Our own brand**, **Authorised reseller** or **Genuine resale**. Only registered brands, plus your own, can be tagged. "Only tag another brand for genuine goods. Official brands can see your listing and report counterfeits."

## If something goes wrong

- **"That brand handle is already taken."** Choose a different handle.
- You think a listing is fake: report the product from its menu. See [Report a scam](/help/safety-and-reporting/report-a-scam).

## Stay safe

Selling fake goods under a real brand name breaks our rules and can close your store. When in doubt, buy from the store marked **Official Store**.
