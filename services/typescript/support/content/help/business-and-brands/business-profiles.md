A business profile is a public profile for an organisation: a shop, a brand, an NGO, a media house, a venue or anything else that isn't you as a person. It sits in the same Zudomart account as your personal profile, so you don't need a second login.

## Who this is for

Anyone running an organisation, in any country. You don't need a store to have a business profile, and you don't need a business profile to buy.

## How to create a business profile

1. Long-press the **Me** tab, or tap the profile switcher on your own profile.
2. Tap **Create business profile**.
3. Fill in **Business name** (at least 2 characters; the app allows up to 50), **Handle** (up to 20 characters) and **About (optional)** (up to 300 characters).
4. Under **Categories (add as many as fit)** pick 1 to 5 categories. If nothing fits, tap **Something else? Add your own**.
5. Tap **Create business profile**. The app switches you to the new profile straight away.

## What you can change later

From the business profile you can edit the display name, description, website and whether the profile is private. Custom categories are "reviewed by ZudoMart before they become public".

## Good to know

- One account holds one personal profile and up to **10** business profiles.
- Posts, messages and store actions happen as the profile you have switched to. See [Switching profiles](/help/business-and-brands/switch-profiles).
- A business profile can open a store later. See [Opening a store](/help/selling/start-selling).
- Business and brand profiles can only carry the **Gold** checkmark, never Blue. Gold comes with a paid seller plan or the Gold-only plan after business verification. See [The Gold checkmark](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/gold-checkmark).
- A website link must be a real web address.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Add your business name (2-80 characters) and a description of at most 300."** Shorten or fill in the name or About text.
- **"Pick between 1 and 5 categories."** Choose at least one category and no more than five.
- **"Custom categories are 2-60 characters."** Make your own category shorter or longer.
- **"That handle isn't available."** Someone already uses that handle. Try another.
- **"You have reached the limit of business profiles."** You already run 10. Remove one you don't use first.
- **"Enter a valid website link."** Check the address, for example yourshop.com.

## Stay safe

Only the account owner signs in. If staff need to help with your store, add them through the seller dashboard team page instead of sharing your password. See [Adding staff to your store](/help/selling/seller-team).
