## Where is Settings?

**Profile** tab, then **More** at the top right, then **Settings**. See [A tour of Settings](/help/notifications-and-settings/settings-overview).

## How do I turn off all push notifications?

**Settings > Notification > Push notifications**, set it to **OFF**. See [Notification settings](/help/notifications-and-settings/notification-settings).

## Can I choose which kinds of notifications I get?

You can control pushes overall, milestone alerts, the bell for each account you follow, individual posts and chats. Finer controls per category are coming.

## Why do I still get some emails after turning things off?

Security, safety, privacy and receipt emails always go out because they protect your account and money.

## How do I stop marketing emails?

Use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, or turn off **Marketing messages** in **Settings > Privacy & Data**.

## How do I get notified when someone posts?

Follow them, then tap the bell on their profile. See [Post notifications from people you follow](/help/notifications-and-settings/post-notifications-bell).

## A post keeps notifying me. Can I stop it?

Yes. Open the post menu and tap **Turn off notifications for this post**. See [Muting notifications for one post](/help/notifications-and-settings/mute-a-post).

## I'm not getting any notifications.

Check **Push notifications** is **ON**, then your phone's settings. See [Notifications not arriving](/help/troubleshooting/notifications-not-arriving).

## How do I turn on dark mode?

**Settings > App Preference > Theme > Dark Mode**. See [Dark mode and appearance](/help/notifications-and-settings/dark-mode-and-appearance).

## Can I change the language?

Not yet. Nantty is in English for now.

## Prices are in the wrong currency.

Change your country in **Settings > App Preference > Country & Currency**. See [Country, currency and language](/help/notifications-and-settings/language-and-currency).

## How do I see sensitive posts without a warning?

If you're 18 or over, switch on **Show sensitive content without warning** in **Settings > Privacy > Content preferences**. See [Content preferences and personalisation](/help/notifications-and-settings/content-preferences).

## How do I stop birthday balloons on my profile?

Switch off **Birthday balloons** in **Settings > Privacy & Data**. See [Birthday balloons and your Nantty anniversary](/help/getting-started/celebrations-birthdays-and-zudoversary).
