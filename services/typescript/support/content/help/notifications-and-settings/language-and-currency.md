title: Region and currency
summary: Prices show in your local currency where supported. Unsupported currencies fall back to US dollars.

Zudomart works out your country from your profile, then your connection, and shows prices in your currency: Nigerian naira, Ghanaian cedi, Kenyan shilling, South African rand, British pound, euro or US dollar. Other currencies fall back to US dollars.

Change your region in **Settings > App preferences > Region**. Converted prices are marked approximate, and you always see the exact amount before paying.
