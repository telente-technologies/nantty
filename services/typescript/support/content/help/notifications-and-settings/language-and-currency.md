Nantty shows prices in your local currency and uses your country to decide which payment options, plans and prices apply to you.

## Supported countries and currencies

Nigeria (naira), Ghana (cedi), Kenya (shilling), South Africa (rand), the United Kingdom (pound), eurozone countries (euro) and the United States (dollar). Everywhere else sees prices in US dollars.

## How we choose your country

We use the country on your profile first, then your connection. You can change it any time.

## How to change your country and currency

1. Go to **Profile > More > Settings > App Preference**.
2. Tap **Country & Currency**. It shows your current currency, for example "Nigerian Naira (₦)".
3. Pick your country from the list. Prices reload in the new currency.

## Language

**Language** shows **English**. Nantty is in English only for now.

## Good to know

- Converted prices are marked approximate. You always see the exact amount and currency before you pay.
- Changing your country doesn't move your wallet. Wallets, escrow and withdrawals are only in Nigeria for now. See [Payments outside Nigeria](/help/payments-and-wallet/payments-outside-nigeria).
- In-app plan prices come from your App Store or Google Play country, which can differ from the country you pick here.
- Sellers set their own prices per country. See [Prices per country](/help/selling/prices-per-country).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Subscription plans can't be priced in this currency."** Pick a supported country, or pay in US dollars.
- **Wrong currency after travelling**: set your country again in **Country & Currency**.
