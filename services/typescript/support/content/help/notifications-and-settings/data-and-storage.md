Zudomart is full of photos and video, so it helps to know where your data and storage go.

## Who this is for

Anyone on a limited data bundle or a phone that's running out of space. There's no separate Data and storage screen in Settings yet; these tips use your phone's own settings and Zudomart features.

## Mobile data

- Videos in your feed start playing when most of the video is on screen. Scroll past quickly to avoid loading them.
- Clips and lives use the most data. Watch them on Wi-Fi when you can.
- Uploading clips and long videos uses data too. Large uploads continue in the background, so you can post on Wi-Fi and keep using the app.
- To stop Zudomart using mobile data entirely, turn off mobile data for the app in your phone's settings.

## Offline use

When your connection drops you'll see "You're offline — showing saved content". You can still scroll what has already loaded. Likes, follows, unfollows and chat messages you send while offline are queued and sync when you're back online; the banner shows how many pending actions will sync.

## Storage on your phone

- Zudomart keeps a cache of recent images and videos so things load faster.
- Chat photos and videos are removed after 30 days; text messages stay. See [Chat media and encryption](/help/chat/chat-media-and-encryption).
- Media you save with **Download** goes to your phone's gallery, not the app.
- To free space, clear the app's cache in your phone's settings (Android), or offload and reinstall the app (iPhone). Your account and posts are not deleted, but chats are end-to-end encrypted and stored on your devices, so read [Chat devices and keys](/help/chat/chat-devices-and-keys) before reinstalling.

## A copy of your data

To get a copy of the data we hold about you, go to **Settings > Privacy & Data** and request your data export, or **Settings > Security & Password > Data > Request my data**. See [Downloading your data](/help/legal-and-data/download-your-data).

## If something goes wrong

- **Videos won't play on mobile data**: see [Videos that won't play](/help/troubleshooting/video-wont-play).
- **Upload stuck**: see [Upload failed](/help/troubleshooting/upload-failed).
