When a post takes off, or you're tagged in a busy thread, the notifications can pile up. You can silence just that post.

## How to mute a post

1. Find the post or clip.
2. Tap the **...** menu on the post (on clips, the options button).
3. Tap **Turn off notifications for this post**.
4. You'll see "You won't get notifications for this post".

## How to turn them back on

Open the same menu and tap **Turn on notifications for this post**. You'll see "Notifications for this post are back on".

## What it stops

For that one post, you stop getting notified about:

- comments and replies
- mentions of you
- likes on the post, and likes on your comments there
- your comment being pinned
- reposts and quotes of your post
- people tagging you in it
- video replies to a clip

## Good to know

- It works on your own posts and on other people's.
- You need to be signed in. Sponsored posts don't have this option.
- Muting a post doesn't hide it, unfollow anyone or tell the author.
- To hear less from a whole account, see [Muting accounts](/help/profiles-and-privacy/mute-accounts).
- To stop all pushes, use **Settings > Notification**. See [Notification settings](/help/notifications-and-settings/notification-settings).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Couldn't update notifications. Try again."** Your change didn't save. Check your connection and try again.
