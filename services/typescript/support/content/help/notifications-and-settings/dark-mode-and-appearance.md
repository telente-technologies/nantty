Nantty comes in a light and a dark look. You can pick one or let your phone decide.

## How to change the theme in the app

1. Go to **Profile > More (top right) > Settings > App Preference**.
2. Tap **Theme**. The row shows your current choice.
3. Choose one:
   - **Light Mode**: the default Nantty look.
   - **Dark Mode**: easier on the eyes at night.
   - **System**: matches your phone's appearance setting, switching automatically when your phone does.

The change applies straight away.

## On the web

1. Open **Settings** on **app.nantty.com**.
2. Under **Appearance**, use **Theme** to choose light, dark or match your device.
3. The line underneath confirms your choice, for example "Following your device (dark right now)" or "Always dark".

## Good to know

- Your choice syncs to your account, so the app and the web match after you sign in on another device.
- **System** is handy if your phone switches to dark mode at sunset.
- Dark mode can save battery on phones with OLED screens.

## Related settings

- Bigger text, bold text and other display options come from your phone. In **App Preference**, **Accessibility** opens your phone settings. See [Text size and display](/help/accessibility/text-size-and-display).
- Less animation: see [Reduce motion](/help/accessibility/reduce-motion).

## If something goes wrong

- **The theme doesn't change**: close and reopen the app. If you chose **System**, check your phone's own light or dark setting.
- **The web looks different from the app**: refresh the page after changing the theme in the app.
