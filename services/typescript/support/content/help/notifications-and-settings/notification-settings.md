You control which notifications reach your phone and inbox. Here's what each setting does.

## How to change notifications in the app

1. Go to **Profile > More (top right) > Settings > Notification**.
2. Tap **Push notifications** to switch it **ON** or **OFF** for this account.
3. Tap **System notification settings** to open your phone's settings for Nantty. There you can turn off sounds, banners or lock-screen previews.
4. Under **Milestones: push** and **Milestones: email**, choose whether to hear about birthdays and Nanttyversaries.

## On the web

Open **Settings** on **app.nantty.com**. Under **Milestones**, switch **Push notifications** and **Email** on or off.

## Fine-tuning by person, post and chat

- **One account's posts**: use the bell on their profile. See [Post notifications from people you follow](/help/notifications-and-settings/post-notifications-bell).
- **One post**: turn notifications off from the post's menu. See [Muting notifications for one post](/help/notifications-and-settings/mute-a-post).
- **Chats**: mute a conversation from the chat. See [Chat passcode and notifications](/help/chat/chat-passcode-and-notifications).
- **Marketing**: in **Settings > Privacy & Data**, switch **Marketing messages** off, or set it to **Weekly** or **Only important**.

## Messages you always get

Some notices can't be turned off because they protect your account or money, or the law requires them:

- sign-in and password alerts
- safety notices such as warnings, restrictions and appeal results
- privacy notices such as data export and account deletion updates
- receipts and payment confirmations

## Good to know

- Marketing emails need your consent and every one has an unsubscribe link.
- If you've already seen something in the app, we may skip the email.
- Turning off **Push notifications** doesn't stop in-app notifications. They still appear on the **Notifications** screen (the bell on **Home**).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Couldn't load your notification settings."** Pull down to refresh.
- Not getting any pushes? See [Notifications not arriving](/help/troubleshooting/notifications-not-arriving).
