Go to **Settings > Notifications**.

For each category (orders, escrow, payments, selling, creator, checkmarks, social, live, safety, privacy, support and marketing) choose push and email.

- Some emails always go out because they're about your account or the law, such as security alerts, receipts and policy changes.
- Marketing emails need your consent and always include an unsubscribe link.
- If you've already seen something in the app, we may skip the email.
