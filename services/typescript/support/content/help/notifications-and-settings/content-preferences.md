You can shape what you see on Zudomart: how sensitive posts are shown, whether your feed and ads are tailored to you, and what you'd like to see less of.

## Sensitive content warnings

1. Go to **Settings > Privacy > Content preferences**.
2. Switch **Show sensitive content without warning** on or off.

When it's off (the default), posts marked as sensitive show a warning first and you tap to view. When it's on, they open straight away. You can turn warnings back on at any time.

Sensitive labels come from the person who posted, from our moderators and from automated checks that our team reviews.

**Under 18s**: you'll see **Sensitive content warnings stay on**. Accounts under 18 always see warnings, and adult content such as nudity or dangerous acts is never shown. If we don't have your birthday, we treat your account as under 18 until you add it.

## Personalised feeds and ads

Go to **Settings > Privacy & Data**. Under **Your choices**:

- **Personalization**: use what you view, search and buy to tailor your feed and recommendations.
- **Personalized ads**: show ads based on your activity. Turning this off still shows ads, just less relevant ones.
- **Analytics**: share anonymous usage data so we can fix problems.
- **Marketing messages**: personalised picks from verified sellers, price drops, back in stock and a weekly digest.

## See less of something

On any post or clip, open the menu and tap **Not interested** ("See fewer posts like this"). We'll show fewer similar posts.

On **Home**, use the feed menu to switch between **For you** and **Following**, and pick a topic to filter your feed.

## Good to know

- Turning **Personalization** off gives you a more general feed; you'll still see posts from people you follow.
- Your topics from sign-up are a starting point. What you watch and like matters more over time. See [How your feed works](/help/posts-and-feed/how-your-feed-works).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Couldn't save that choice. Try again."** Your change didn't save. Try again.
- **"Couldn't load your privacy choices."** Tap retry.
