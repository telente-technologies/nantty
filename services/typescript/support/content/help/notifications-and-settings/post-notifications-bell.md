Following someone puts their posts in your Following feed. The bell goes further and sends you a notification each time they post.

## Who this is for

Signed-in users, on profiles you already follow. If the account is private, your follow request must be accepted first.

## How to turn on post notifications

1. Open the person's profile.
2. Tap the **Post notifications** button (the bell next to **Message**).
3. On **Get notified when they post**, choose:
   - **All posts**: posts and clips.
   - **Lives too**: also notify me when they go live.
   - **Off**: no post notifications.

The bell icon changes to show your choice: a plain bell for **All posts**, a ringing bell for **Lives too**.

## What you're notified about

- New public posts and clips.
- With **Lives too**, when they start a live, except subscriber-only lives.

You won't be notified about replies, video replies, stories or posts from their business or brand profiles.

## Good to know

- The bell only appears once you follow the account. If you unfollow, the bell disappears.
- Post notifications are push and in-app notifications. They only reach your phone if **Push notifications** is **ON** in **Settings > Notification**.
- To stop alerts about one post (for example a busy comment thread), see [Muting notifications for one post](/help/notifications-and-settings/mute-a-post).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Couldn't update post notifications. Try again."** Check your connection and try again.
- **No bell on a profile**: follow the account first, or wait for them to accept your request.
- **Not getting the alerts**: see [Notifications not arriving](/help/troubleshooting/notifications-not-arriving).
