Settings is where you manage your account, privacy, notifications, payments and help.

## How to open Settings

In the app tap the **Profile** tab, then **More** at the top right, then **Settings**. Use **Search settings** (the magnifying glass at the top) to jump to something quickly. Tapping your name card at the top opens **Edit profile**.

## What's in Settings (app)

**Account and safety**

- **Account**: your **Username**, **Email**, **Phone number** and **Deactivate account**.
- **Account status**: your standing, copyright notices, strikes and warnings. See [Copyright and account status](/help/safety-and-reporting/copyright-and-account-status).
- **Security & Password**: **Password**, **Blocked users**, **Two-factor authentication** and **Data** (request a copy of your data).
- **Privacy**: profile visibility (**Public** or **Private**), who can mention you, **Allow downloads** and **Content preferences**.
- **Privacy & Data**: your choices on **Personalization**, **Personalized ads**, **Analytics** and **Marketing messages**, **Birthday balloons**, your data export, policies and **Delete my account**.
- **Notification**: push notifications and milestone alerts. See [Notification settings](/help/notifications-and-settings/notification-settings).

**App and payments**

- **App Preference**: **Theme**, **Language**, **Country & Currency** and **Accessibility**.
- **Payment Methods**: saved cards. See [Saved cards](/help/payments-and-wallet/saved-cards).
- **Shipping Address**: delivery addresses. See [Saved addresses](/help/buying-and-orders/saved-addresses).

**Selling and profiles**

- **Start selling on Nantty** (or **Business documents** once you sell).
- **Create business profile** and **Create a brand profile**.

**Help**

- **Support**: **Help centre**, **Contact support**, **My support requests**, **My reports**, **Contact Us** and **Report a bug**.
- **About**: Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and the app version.

At the bottom, **Log out** signs this device out.

Home-screen widgets and website embeds have their own guide: see [Widgets and embeds](/help/widgets-and-embeds).

## On the web

**app.nantty.com > Settings** has **Appearance**, **Milestones** notifications, and links to **Privacy & data**, **Verification**, **Addresses**, **Email**, **Profile**, **Saved items**, **Account status**, **Your reports**, **Orders** and the **Help Centre**.

## Good to know

- Wallet, payout accounts and orders live in the **Profile > More** menu, not in Settings.
- Some options only appear when they apply to you, such as **Business documents** for sellers.
