If movement on screen makes you dizzy or distracts you, turn on reduced motion in your device settings. Nantty checks this setting and calms things down.

## Who this is for

Anyone with motion sensitivity, vestibular conditions or who just prefers fewer animations.

## How to turn it on

- **iPhone**: **Settings > Accessibility > Motion > Reduce Motion**.
- **Android**: **Settings > Accessibility > Remove animations** (on some phones it's under **Visibility enhancements** or **Display**).
- **Windows**: **Settings > Accessibility > Visual effects > Animation effects**, turned off.
- **Mac**: **System Settings > Accessibility > Display > Reduce motion**.

Nantty picks up the change straight away; you don't need to restart.

## What changes in the app

- Bottom sheets, dialogs and full-screen pop-ups appear without sliding.
- The highlights banner on Home stops scrolling by itself.
- Loading placeholders stop shimmering.
- Like bursts, streak milestone celebrations, battle confetti, floating balloons and flying gift animations on lives are turned off or simplified.
- Image fade-ins are switched off.

## What doesn't change

- Videos, clips and lives still play, because that is the content itself. Tap to pause any video.
- Swiping between clips still moves the screen, because you control it.

## On the web

**app.nantty.com** follows your system's reduced motion setting and turns off non-essential animations.

## If something goes wrong

If something still moves a lot with reduced motion on, tell us which screen through **Settings > Support > Report a bug**.
