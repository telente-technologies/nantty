Nantty works with VoiceOver on iPhone, TalkBack on Android and screen readers on the web. Most buttons, tabs, menus and media controls have spoken names and hints.

## Who this is for

Blind and low-vision users, and anyone who uses a screen reader.

## Turn on your screen reader

- **iPhone**: **Settings > Accessibility > VoiceOver**.
- **Android**: **Settings > Accessibility > TalkBack** (the path differs slightly by phone).
- **Computer**: use your usual screen reader with **app.nantty.com**.

## Posts and comments

- Each post reads its author, text and controls. Photos read as "Open image 1 full screen" and so on, and videos have **Play video full screen**, **Mute video** and **Unmute video** controls.
- **Actions**: on a post in your feed, use the actions menu (VoiceOver rotor or TalkBack actions) to choose **Like post** or **Open comments** without hunting for the buttons.
- **Comments**: actions include **Report comment**, **Reply with video**, and **Pin comment** or **Unpin comment** on your own posts. A long press opens comment options.
- **Report sheets** read reasons as a list of choices with the selected one marked.

## Videos and clips

- The video progress bar is adjustable: swipe up or down to skip 10 seconds forward or back.
- Clips announce "Tap to pause" and that you can hold to play at 2x speed.
- The playback speed button reads its current speed, such as "Playback speed 1.5x".
- The first time you open clips, a gestures guide explains the swipes and taps. Tap to dismiss it.

## Home and shopping

- The highlights banner on Home stops scrolling by itself while a screen reader is on, so you can move through it at your own pace.
- Error messages and important updates, such as "Appeal sent", are announced when they appear.

## On the web

- Press Tab at the top of any page to reach **Skip to content**.
- Focused items get a clear outline, including in high-contrast (forced colours) mode.
- Videos support keyboard shortcuts: Space or K to play and pause, J and L to go back or forward 10 seconds, and the left and right arrow keys for 5 seconds.

## Good to know

Photos and videos posted by other people don't yet have written descriptions, so a screen reader can't describe what's in them. See [Captions and image descriptions](/help/accessibility/captions-and-alt-text).

## If something goes wrong

If a control isn't announced or can't be reached, go to **Settings > Support > Report a bug** and tell us the screen and what you expected to hear.
