## Does Nantty work with VoiceOver and TalkBack?

Yes. Buttons, tabs and media controls have spoken names, and posts and comments have quick actions. See [Using Nantty with a screen reader](/help/accessibility/screen-readers).

## Where are the accessibility settings in the app?

Go to **Settings > App Preference > Accessibility**. It opens your phone's settings, because Nantty follows your device's accessibility options.

## How do I make text bigger?

Increase your phone's text size. Nantty follows it. See [Text size and display](/help/accessibility/text-size-and-display).

## Why doesn't all text get as big as my phone setting?

Most text grows up to about 1.3 times so the feed and clips keep their layout. Text in sheets, dialogs and menus can grow more. Use your phone's zoom if you need it bigger.

## Is there a dark mode?

Yes. **Settings > App Preference > Theme**, then **System**, **Light Mode** or **Dark Mode**.

## Can I stop animations?

Turn on reduced motion in your device settings. See [Reduce motion](/help/accessibility/reduce-motion).

## Do videos have captions?

Not yet. You can mute, seek, skip 10 seconds and change playback speed. See [Captions and image descriptions](/help/accessibility/captions-and-alt-text).

## Can I add alt text to my photos?

Not yet. Describe your photo in the post text for now.

## Can I use Nantty with a keyboard?

On **app.nantty.com**, yes. Press Tab to move, use **Skip to content**, and use Space or K, J, L and the arrow keys to control videos.

## Can I slow down or speed up clips?

Yes. Tap the speed button on a clip to cycle through 1x, 1.5x, 2x and 0.5x, or press and hold to play at 2x.

## Does the Home banner keep moving?

It stops scrolling by itself when reduced motion or a screen reader is on.

## Something is hard to use. Who do I tell?

Go to **Settings > Support > Report a bug**, or [contact support](/help/contact-support/contact-support). Tell us your device, your assistive technology and the screen.
