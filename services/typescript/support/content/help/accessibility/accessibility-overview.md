We want everyone to be able to shop, watch, chat and sell on Nantty. The app and **app.nantty.com** follow your device's accessibility settings, so there is little to set up inside Nantty itself.

## Who this is for

Anyone who uses a screen reader, larger text, high contrast, reduced motion, a keyboard or other assistive technology, and anyone helping them.

## What Nantty supports

- **Screen readers**: VoiceOver on iPhone, TalkBack on Android, and screen readers on the web. Buttons, tabs and controls have spoken names. See [Using Nantty with a screen reader](/help/accessibility/screen-readers).
- **Larger text**: text grows with your phone's text size setting. See [Text size and display](/help/accessibility/text-size-and-display).
- **Dark mode**: choose **System**, **Light Mode** or **Dark Mode**. See [Dark mode and appearance](/help/notifications-and-settings/dark-mode-and-appearance).
- **Reduced motion**: when it's on in your phone or computer settings, Nantty turns off sliding sheets, auto-scrolling banners and most celebration animations. See [Reduce motion](/help/accessibility/reduce-motion).
- **Keyboard on the web**: a **Skip to content** link, visible focus outlines and video shortcuts.
- **Video controls**: pause, seek with a bar, skip 10 seconds, mute and change speed.

## How to open your phone's accessibility settings

1. In the app, go to **Settings > App Preference**.
2. Tap **Accessibility**. This opens your phone's settings for Nantty, where you can reach the accessibility options.

## What we are still working on

- Closed captions on videos and lives.
- Letting you write image descriptions (alt text) for your photos.

See [Captions and image descriptions](/help/accessibility/captions-and-alt-text).

## Tell us what's hard to use

If a button has no spoken name, text gets cut off, or something can't be reached, please tell us:

- In the app: **Settings > Support > Report a bug**.
- Or **Settings > Support > Contact support**, then **Something else**.

Tell us your device, your assistive technology and the screen you were on. It helps us fix it quickly.
