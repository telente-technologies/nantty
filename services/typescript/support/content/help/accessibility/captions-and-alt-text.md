Captions and image descriptions help people who can't hear the sound or see the picture. Here is what Nantty offers today and how you can make your own posts more accessible.

## Who this is for

Viewers who are deaf, hard of hearing, blind or low vision, and creators and sellers who want everyone to enjoy their posts.

## What's available today

- **Mute and unmute** any video, and watch with the sound off.
- **Seek bar and skip**: drag the bar or skip 10 seconds to rewatch a part.
- **Playback speed**: on clips, tap the speed button to switch between 1x, 1.5x, 2x and 0.5x.
- **Screen reader labels** on photos and video controls. Photos are announced, for example, as "Open image 1 full screen".

## What's not available yet

- **Closed captions** on videos, clips and lives. There are no automatic or uploaded subtitles yet.
- **Alt text**: you can't yet add a written description to your photos, so screen readers can't describe what's in them.

We'll update this article when these arrive.

## Tips for creators and sellers

Until captions and alt text arrive, you can help everyone by:

- **Putting key words in the post text**: describe what's in the photo or video, for example "Red ankara dress, sizes 8 to 16, shown front and back".
- **Adding on-screen text** to your video when you edit it before uploading, for anything you say that matters.
- **Saying prices and sizes out loud and in writing** in product videos and lives.
- **Avoiding text only inside images**, such as a price written on a flyer with no caption.
- **Describing your live** in its title so people know what it's about before they join.

## For sellers

Write full product titles and descriptions: colour, size, material and condition. Buyers using screen readers rely on this text. See [Products and inventory](/help/selling/products-and-inventory).

Captions and descriptions are high on our list. Tell us what matters most to you through **Settings > Support > Contact support**, choosing **Something else**.
