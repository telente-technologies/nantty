Nantty uses your device's text and display settings, so you set them once and they work across the app.

## Who this is for

Anyone who finds text small or hard to read, or prefers a darker or higher-contrast screen.

## Make text bigger in the app

Nantty follows your phone's text size:

- **iPhone**: **Settings > Display & Brightness > Text Size**, or **Settings > Accessibility > Display & Text Size > Larger Text** for bigger sizes.
- **Android**: **Settings > Display > Font size** (some phones call it **Font size and style**).

You can also get there from Nantty: **Settings > App Preference > Accessibility** opens your phone's settings.

Most text in the app grows up to about 1.3 times its normal size, so screens such as the feed and clips keep their layout. Text in pop-up sheets, dialogs and menus can grow more, up to about 1.8 times. If you need text even larger, use your phone's zoom or magnifier.

## Dark mode

1. Go to **Settings > App Preference > Theme**.
2. Choose **System** (follows your phone), **Light Mode** or **Dark Mode**.

See [Dark mode and appearance](/help/notifications-and-settings/dark-mode-and-appearance).

## Zoom and contrast

- **Phone zoom**: iPhone **Settings > Accessibility > Zoom**; Android **Settings > Accessibility > Magnification**.
- **Bold text and higher contrast** settings on your phone also apply to Nantty where your phone supports them.

## On the web

- Use your browser zoom (Ctrl and + on Windows, Cmd and + on Mac). Pages reflow to fit.
- Nantty on the web follows your system's light or dark setting, or the theme you choose.
- In Windows high-contrast (forced colours) mode, focus outlines use your system colours so you can always see where you are.

## Good to know

- Photo and video sizes don't change with text size. Pinch to zoom photos when you open them full screen.
- Very large text can make long buttons wrap onto two lines. That's expected.

## If something goes wrong

If text gets cut off or overlaps at a large size, take a screenshot and send it through **Settings > Support > Report a bug**.
