Every Nantty profile has its own link, so you can put it in your WhatsApp status, your Instagram bio or a flyer and bring people straight to you.

## Share your own profile

1. Go to your profile.
2. Tap **Share profile** under your name.
3. Your phone's share sheet opens with a ready message: "Follow" your name "on Nantty" plus your link.
4. Pick WhatsApp, Instagram, SMS, email, or **Copy** to paste it anywhere.

## Share someone else's profile

1. Open their profile and tap **More options** (three dots).
2. Choose:
   - **Send in chat** to send the profile as a card to one or more of your Nantty chats, with an optional note. See [Share to chat](/help/chat/share-to-chat).
   - **Share or copy profile link** to use your phone's share sheet.

On the web (app.nantty.com), the profile's **More options** menu has **Send in chat** and **Copy link**. You'll see "Link copied".

## What the link looks like

Profile links use your username, in the form **app.nantty.com/** followed by your username. If you change your username, your link changes too, so update it anywhere you've shared it. See [Usernames](/help/account-and-login/usernames).

## What people see when they open it

- The link opens your profile on Nantty. Guests can look around public content, and are asked to sign in to follow, like or message.
- **Inside Nantty posts and chats**, profile links show as a Nantty card labelled **Profile**.

If your account is private, people who open the link see your name and picture, but only approved followers see your posts. See [Private accounts](/help/profiles-and-privacy/private-account).

## Good to know

- There is no QR code for profiles yet. Share the link instead.
- Links to stores show as a **Store** card inside Nantty posts and chats.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Couldn't copy the link"**: try again, or use the share sheet.
- **"Couldn't send"**: the chat share didn't go through. Check your connection and try again.
