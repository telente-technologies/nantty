---
title: Followers and following lists
summary: Tap your follower or following count to see and search the list.
tags: followers, following, follow
related: private-account, blocking-people
---

Tap the **Followers** or **Following** count on any profile to open the list. You can search by name or username.

- Lists respect privacy: a private account's lists are only visible to its approved followers.
- To remove someone from your followers, open the list and choose Remove.
- To stop seeing someone without unfollowing, mute them from their profile menu.
