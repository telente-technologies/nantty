Every profile shows how many followers it has and how many accounts it follows. Tap either number to open the **Connections** screen.

## Open the lists

1. Go to any profile, including your own.
2. Tap **Followers** or **Following**.
3. Switch between tabs at the top. Use **Search people** to find someone by name or username.

Tap a person to open their profile. Use the sort button to open **Sort and filter**, and **Reset** to go back to the default order.

## Extra tabs on your own profile

Only you can see these:

- **Shopped**: people connected to you through shopping.
- **Suggested**: people you might want to follow.
- **Blocked**: everyone you've blocked, with an **Unblock** button.

If someone else tries to open them, they see "Only you can see this list."

## Options for a person in the list

Tap the options next to someone to:

- **View profile**,
- **Send message**,
- **Block** them. See [Blocking](/help/profiles-and-privacy/blocking-people).

## Privacy

- A private account's followers and following lists are only visible to its approved followers. Others see "This account is private. Follow them to see their followers and following."

## Removing a follower

There isn't a **Remove follower** button yet. To stop someone following you:

- **Block** them. This removes the follow both ways. You can unblock them straight after if you only wanted to remove the follow, and they won't be told.
- Or make your account private so future followers need your approval. See [Private accounts](/help/profiles-and-privacy/private-account).

## Seeing less of someone you follow

Unfollow them, or use **Not interested** on their posts. See [Muting accounts and quieter alternatives](/help/profiles-and-privacy/mute-accounts).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Couldn't load this list."** Pull down to retry.
- **"Nobody here yet"**: "Try a different name or username."
