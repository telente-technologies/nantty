A private account lets you choose who sees what you post. Only people you approve can follow you and see your posts.

## Make your account private

1. Go to **Settings > Privacy**.
2. Under **Profile visibility**, tap **Private**.

The screen explains: "Only approved followers can see your posts, followers and following. New followers need your approval."

You can also get there from **Settings > Privacy & Data > Private profile** ("Choose who can see your profile").

To go back, tap **Public**: "Anyone can see your posts and follow you."

## What changes

- New followers need your approval. Their button shows **Requested** until you decide.
- People who don't follow you see "This account is private" and "Follow this account to see their posts."
- Your stories are only shown to approved followers.
- Your followers and following lists are hidden from non-followers.
- People who already follow you keep following you.

## Handle follow requests

1. Go to your profile and tap **More** (three dots).
2. Tap **Follow requests**. A badge shows how many are waiting.
3. Tap **Approve** or **Decline** for each person.

People you decline aren't told. They can send a new request later.

## Good to know

- Private accounts can still be found in search, and people can see your name, photo and bio.
- Anything you send in a chat, comment on a public post, or share elsewhere can still be seen by the people there.
- To remove someone who already follows you, see [Followers and following](/help/profiles-and-privacy/followers-and-following).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Couldn't load follow requests."** Pull down to retry.
- **"This request was already handled."** You or another device already approved or declined it.
- **"Request not found"**: the person cancelled their request.
