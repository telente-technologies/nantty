## Make your account private

Settings > Privacy > Account privacy > **Private**.

## What changes

- New followers need your approval. Their button shows **Requested** until you decide.
- People who don't follow you see "This account is private" instead of your posts.
- Your followers and following lists are hidden from non-followers.

## Handle requests

Open **Follow requests** from your profile shortcuts or from Settings > Privacy. Approve or decline each one. Declined people are not told.

Switching back to public approves pending requests automatically.
