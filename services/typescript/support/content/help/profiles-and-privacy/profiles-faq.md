## How do I change my name, photo or bio?

Profile > **Edit profile**. Names can be up to 50 characters and bios up to 160. See [Editing your profile](/help/profiles-and-privacy/edit-your-profile).

## How do I change my username?

**Settings > Account > Username**. There's a limit on how often you can change it each year. See [Usernames](/help/account-and-login/usernames).

## How do I make my account private?

**Settings > Privacy > Profile visibility > Private**. See [Private accounts](/help/profiles-and-privacy/private-account).

## Where are my follow requests?

Profile > **More** (three dots) > **Follow requests**.

## Will someone know if I unfollow or block them?

No. Neither unfollowing nor blocking sends a notification. Unfollowing always asks you to confirm first. See [Unfollow and the follow bell](/help/profiles-and-privacy/unfollow-and-follow-bell).

## How do I get notified when someone posts?

Follow them, then tap the bell on their profile and choose **All posts** or **Lives too**.

## Can I mute someone?

There's no account mute yet. Unfollow, use **Not interested**, turn their bell off or mute your chat with them. See [Muting accounts](/help/profiles-and-privacy/mute-accounts).

## How do I remove a follower?

There's no remove button yet. Block and then unblock them, which removes the follow without telling them. See [Followers and following](/help/profiles-and-privacy/followers-and-following).

## Where is my blocked list?

**Settings > Security & Password > Blocked users**, or the **Blocked** tab in your Connections. See [Blocking](/help/profiles-and-privacy/blocking-people).

## Someone tagged me in a post I don't like. What can I do?

Ask them to remove it, block them, or report the post if it's abusive. See [Tagged people](/help/profiles-and-privacy/tagged-people).

## How do I share my profile?

Profile > **Share profile**. To share someone else's, use **More options** > **Send in chat** or **Share or copy profile link**. See [Sharing your profile](/help/profiles-and-privacy/share-your-profile).

## What's in the three-dot menu on my profile?

Shortcuts to Saved, orders, wallet, verification and settings, plus creator and seller tools. See [Profile menu shortcuts](/help/profiles-and-privacy/profile-menu-shortcuts).

## Can I have a personal and a business profile?

Yes. One account can run your personal profile, plus one Store or one Business page and one Brand. See [Profile types](/help/profiles-and-privacy/profile-types).

## Can people download my photos and clips?

Only if **Allow downloads** is on in **Settings > Privacy**. Downloads carry the Zudomart logo and your username. Stories and private or subscriber-only posts are never downloadable.
