Tags show who is in a post or clip, or who it's for. Tagged people get a notification and their names show under the post.

## Tag people

1. Create a post or clip as usual.
2. In the settings step, open **Tag people**.
3. Use "Search people" to find someone and add them. The counter shows how many you've tagged, for example "3 of 20 tagged".
4. Post as usual.

The sheet reminds you: "Tag up to 20 people. Tags apply to the whole post. Tagged people are notified and can remove themselves."

## See who's tagged

Under a tagged post you'll see a line like "Ada and 2 others". Tap it to open **Tagged in this post** (or **Tagged in this clip**), with a follow button next to each person. Tap **Show more** for long lists.

## Who can't be tagged

Some people are left out automatically, without an error:

- yourself,
- accounts that are deactivated or suspended,
- anyone who has blocked you, or whom you've blocked.

Stories can't have tags.

## A tag you don't want

The tagging sheet says tagged people can remove themselves. The **Remove tag** button isn't in the app yet. Until it arrives:

- ask the person who tagged you to remove it,
- block them, which stops them tagging you again,
- if the post is abusive or shares your private information, open the post menu and tap **Report**. See [Report a problem](/help/safety-and-reporting/report-a-problem).

## Tags and mentions

A tag is attached to the whole post. A mention is a name typed in the text with the at sign (@). Mention settings are separate. See [Mentions](/help/posts-and-feed/mentions).

## If something goes wrong

- **"You've tagged 20 people, the most for one post."** Remove someone to add another.
- **"You can tag up to 20 people."** The same limit, checked when you post.
- **"Only the author can edit tags."** You can't change tags on someone else's post.
- **"No people found."** Check the spelling or try their username.
- **"Search isn't available right now. Try again."**
