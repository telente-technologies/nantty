Privacy settings live in **Settings > Privacy** and **Settings > Privacy & Data**.

## Who can contact you

- **Message requests**: Everyone, People I follow, Followers or Nobody. See [Message requests](/help/chat/chat-requests-and-spam).
- **Mentions**: Everyone, People you follow, or No one. See [Mentions](/help/posts-and-feed/mentions).

## How your data is used

Each choice is stored on its own and you can change it any time:

- **Personalisation**: use your activity to shape your feed and recommendations. Off means you still get a feed, just less tailored.
- **Usage analytics**: help us improve the app.
- **Advertising**: use your activity to choose ads. Off still shows ads, just less relevant ones.
- **Marketing emails**: personalised picks from verified sellers, price drops, back in stock and a weekly digest. Pick Weekly, Only important or Off.

We record which version of the Privacy Policy you agreed to with each choice.

## Your data

Download a copy of your data or delete your account from Settings > Privacy & Data. See [Download your data](/help/legal-and-data/download-your-data) and [Delete your account](/help/legal-and-data/delete-your-account).
