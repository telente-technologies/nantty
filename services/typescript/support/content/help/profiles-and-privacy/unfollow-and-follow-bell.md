Following someone puts their posts in your **Following** feed. The bell lets you go further and get a notification whenever they post.

## Follow someone

Tap **Follow** on their profile, on a clip or in **Who to follow**. The button changes to:

- **Following** once you follow them, or
- **Requested** if their account is private and they haven't approved you yet.

## Turn on post notifications (the bell)

The bell appears on someone's profile once you follow them (and they've approved you, if they're private).

1. On their profile, tap the bell (**Post notifications**).
2. Choose what you want, under "Get notified when" they post:
   - **All posts**: "Posts and clips"
   - **Lives too**: "Also notify me when they go live"
   - **Off**: "No post notifications"

It starts as **Off** for everyone you follow. On the web the bell works the same way. See also [Post notifications bell](/help/notifications-and-settings/post-notifications-bell).

## Unfollow

1. Tap **Following** on their profile.
2. Zudomart always asks first: "Unfollow" them? "Their posts will no longer show up in your Following feed."
3. Tap **Unfollow**, or **Cancel** to keep following.

They aren't told that you unfollowed. Unfollowing also turns their post notifications back to **Off**.

## Cancel a follow request

If the button says **Requested**, tap it. You'll be asked "Cancel follow request?" They won't see your request any more and you can ask again later. Tap **Cancel request**, or **Keep request**.

## Good to know

- You can't follow yourself, and you can't follow someone who blocked you or whom you blocked.
- To stop one post's notifications without touching the bell, use **Turn off notifications for this post** in the post menu.

## If something goes wrong

- **"You can't follow this account"**: one of you has blocked the other.
- **"Follow this account to get post notifications."** Follow them first, and wait for approval if they're private.
- **"You can't get notifications from this account."**
- **"Couldn't update post notifications. Try again."** Check your connection.
- **"Couldn't unfollow"** or **"Couldn't follow"** followed by their username: try again in a moment.
