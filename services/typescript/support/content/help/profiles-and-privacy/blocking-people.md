## Block

From their profile or a chat, open the menu and choose **Block**.

A blocked person can't see your profile or posts, follow you, message you, mention you or add you to groups. They are not notified. Blocking works in both directions, so you won't see them either.

See everyone you've blocked in Settings > Privacy > Blocked accounts, where you can unblock them.

## Mute

Mute hides someone's posts and stories from your feed without telling them, and you keep following each other.
