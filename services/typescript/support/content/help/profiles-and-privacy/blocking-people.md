Blocking is the strongest way to stop someone reaching you on Zudomart. It works in both directions, so you won't see them either.

## How to block someone

**From their profile (app):**

1. Open their profile and tap **More options** (three dots).
2. Tap **Block** followed by their name.
3. Confirm. The message reads: "They won't be able to find your profile, see your posts or message you. They won't be told you blocked them."
4. Tap **Block**. You'll see "Blocked" and go back to the previous screen.

**From a chat:** open the chat menu and tap **Block**, or **Report and block** if they've broken the rules. From **Requests** and **Spam** you can also **Block** before accepting. See [Message requests](/help/chat/chat-requests-and-spam).

**From a followers or following list:** tap the person's options and choose **Block**.

Blocking isn't on the web yet. Use the app.

## What blocking does

A blocked person can't:

- find your profile, posts, clips, stories or store,
- follow you. Any follow between you is removed, in both directions,
- message you or add you to groups,
- mention you or tag you.

You won't see their content either. They are not notified.

## See and undo blocks

Your blocked list is in several places:

- **Settings > Security & Password > Blocked users**, where each person has an **Unblock** button,
- **Chat settings > Blocked accounts**,
- the **Blocked** tab in your **Connections** list (only you can see it).

Unblocking doesn't restore a follow. Either of you can follow again afterwards.

## Block or something softer?

If you just want to see less of someone, see [Muting accounts and quieter alternatives](/help/profiles-and-privacy/mute-accounts). If someone is threatening or harassing you, block and [report](/help/safety-and-reporting/report-a-problem) them.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Couldn't block. Try again."** or **"Couldn't unblock. Try again."**: check your connection.
- **"Couldn't load blocked accounts."** Pull down to retry.
- **"You can't block yourself."**
