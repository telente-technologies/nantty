The three-dot button at the top of a profile opens a menu. What's in it depends on whose profile you're on.

## On your own profile

Tap **More** (three dots) at the top of your profile. The menu shows, in this order:

1. **Seller dashboard**: only if you have a store. See [Seller dashboard](/help/selling/seller-dashboard-overview).
2. A creator shortcut, which changes with your status:
   - **Switch to creator account** if you haven't applied yet,
   - **Creator application status** while your application is being reviewed,
   - **Creator tools** once you're an approved creator.
3. **Follow requests**: only if your account is private or you have requests waiting. A badge shows how many, for example "3+". See [Private accounts](/help/profiles-and-privacy/private-account).
4. **Saved**: everything you've bookmarked. See [Saving posts](/help/posts-and-feed/bookmarks-and-saved).
5. **My orders**: track what you've bought.
6. **Wallet**: your balance, payouts and transactions.
7. **Verification**, or **Get verified** if you don't have a checkmark yet.
8. **Settings**.

Next to **More** there's a **Search** button. Under your name you'll find **Edit profile** and **Share profile**.

## On someone else's profile

Tap **More options** (three dots). You'll see:

- **Send in chat**: send their profile to one or more of your chats. See [Share to chat](/help/chat/share-to-chat).
- **Share or copy profile link**: opens your phone's share sheet.
- **Send coins to** followed by their name: shown when you can send them coins.
- **Report** followed by their username (in red).
- **Block** followed by their name (in red). See [Blocking](/help/profiles-and-privacy/blocking-people).

After a report you'll see "Thanks for letting us know" and "We'll review this account. You can follow the outcome in Settings › Support › My reports."

## On the web (app.zudomart.com)

- **Your profile** has icon shortcuts for **Saved items**, **Your orders**, **Notifications** and **Settings**.
- **Someone else's profile** has a **More options** menu with **Send in chat**, **Copy link** and **Report**. Blocking is only in the app for now.

## Good to know

- There is no QR code or profile insights screen. For post stats, see [Post analytics](/help/posts-and-feed/post-analytics).
- Muting accounts isn't available. See [Muting accounts](/help/profiles-and-privacy/mute-accounts) for what you can do instead.
