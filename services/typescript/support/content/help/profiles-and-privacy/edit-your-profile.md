title: Editing your profile
summary: Change your name, photo, bio, links and username from Edit profile.
tags: profile, bio, photo, edit
related: usernames, private-account, profile-types
---

Go to your profile and tap **Edit profile**.

- **Photo and cover**: upload a new picture. Large uploads continue in the background.
- **Name and bio**: shown at the top of your profile.
- **Username**: see [Usernames](/help/account-and-login/usernames).
- **Date of birth**: used for age rules and your birthday celebration. It isn't shown publicly.

Your faded @username appears under your name on posts and comments. Tapping your name anywhere opens your profile.
