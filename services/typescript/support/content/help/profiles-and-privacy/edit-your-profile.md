Your profile is the first thing people see when they tap your name. Keep it clear and up to date, especially if you sell or create.

## Edit your profile

1. Go to your profile.
2. Tap **Edit profile** under your name. The **Profile** screen opens.
3. Change what you need:
   - **Change profile photo**: pick a picture. It's cropped to a square and shows as a circle.
   - **Change banner image**: the wide picture at the top of your profile, cropped to 3:1.
   - **Name**: up to 50 characters. It can't be empty.
   - **Bio**: up to 160 characters about you or your business.
4. Tap **Save**.

Large uploads keep going in the background after you save.

## Change your username

Usernames are changed in **Settings > Account > Username**, not on the Edit profile screen.

1. Type your **New username**. Up to 20 characters, no spaces. It's saved in lowercase.
2. Tap **Save username**.

The screen shows how many changes you have left this year, or when your next change is possible. "Some usernames are reserved for Nantty and can't be used." See [Usernames](/help/account-and-login/usernames).

Changing your username also changes your profile link. See [Sharing your profile](/help/profiles-and-privacy/share-your-profile).

## Your birthday

You give your date of birth when you sign up. It isn't on your profile and isn't shown publicly. It's used for age rules and your birthday celebration. See [Celebrations](/help/getting-started/celebrations-birthdays-and-zudoversary). There's no screen to change it yet, so if it's wrong, [contact support](/help/contact-support/contact-support).

## How your name appears

Your name shows on posts and comments, with your username in lighter text next to it. Tapping your name anywhere opens your profile.

## Business and brand profiles

If you manage a business or brand profile, switch to it first, then edit it the same way. See [Profile types](/help/profiles-and-privacy/profile-types).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Couldn't save your profile"**: check your connection and try again.
- **"Couldn't change your username. Try again."** The name may be taken or reserved.
- **"You've used all your username changes for this year"**: wait for the date shown.
