Sometimes you want to see less of someone without blocking them. Zudomart doesn't have a single **Mute** button for accounts yet, but you can get the same result with the tools below. None of them tell the other person.

## See less of their posts

- **Unfollow them.** Their posts leave your **Following** feed. They aren't told. See [Unfollow and the follow bell](/help/profiles-and-privacy/unfollow-and-follow-bell).
- **Use Not interested.** On any of their posts or clips, open the three-dot menu and tap **Not interested** ("See fewer posts like this"). Your feed learns from it.
- **Hide a post.** **Hide this post** or **Hide this clip** removes that one from your feed straight away.

## Stop notifications from them

- **Turn the bell off.** On their profile, set post notifications to **Off**.
- **Turn off notifications for one post.** In the post menu, tap **Turn off notifications for this post**.
- **Limit mentions.** In **Settings > Privacy > Who can mention me**, choose **People I follow** or **No one**. See [Mentions](/help/posts-and-feed/mentions).

## Quiet a chat

Open the chat, then the chat's notification settings: **Notifications for this chat**. Choose **Mute for 8 hours**, **Mute for 1 week** or **Mute always**. Tap **Unmute** to undo. They aren't told. See [Chat notifications](/help/chat/chat-passcode-and-notifications).

## When to block instead

If someone is harassing you, threatening you, or you don't want them to see you at all, [block them](/help/profiles-and-privacy/blocking-people). Blocking stops them seeing your profile, following you, messaging you, mentioning you and tagging you. You can also [report them](/help/safety-and-reporting/report-a-problem).

## Good to know

- If you follow someone and want to keep the follow but see less, use **Not interested** on their posts. Over time your feed shows them less.
- Stories from people you follow still appear in your story tray. To stop that, unfollow them.
