Your Zudomart account is the login. Inside it you can run more than one public profile, so a shop owner can keep their personal life and their business apart without a second login.

## Profile types

- **Personal**: you, as a person. Everyone starts here. Personal profiles (including creators) can get the Blue checkmark.
- **Creator**: not a separate profile. When you're accepted into the [Creator Program](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-program), your personal profile becomes a creator profile. It unlocks **Creator tools**, going live and earnings.
- **Business or brand**: any organisation, from a small shop to a charity. Business profiles can get the Gold checkmark. See [Business profiles](/help/business-and-brands/business-profiles).
- **Government**: for official public bodies. These go through deep vetting before they can carry the Black checkmark. See [Government accounts](/help/business-and-brands/government-accounts).

## Create a business profile

1. Go to **Settings** and tap **Create business profile**.
2. Fill in **Business name** (2 to 80 characters), **Handle**, and **About (optional)**: "What does your organisation do?" (up to 300 characters).
3. Pick 1 to 5 categories.
4. Tap **Create business profile**.

You can have one Store or one Business page, never both, plus one Brand. With Blue Premium you can have up to 5 business profiles, still with only one Store. See [Stores, business pages and brands](/help/business-and-brands/stores-business-pages-and-brands). See [Opening a store](/help/selling/start-selling).

## Switch profiles

On your own profile, tap your profile name at the top (**Switch profile**) and pick the profile you want to use. Posts, comments and likes then come from the profile you switched to.

When you create a post you can also choose which profile to post as: **Personal**, **Business**, **Brand**, **Store** or **Government**, depending on what you run. See [Switching profiles](/help/business-and-brands/switch-profiles).

## Good to know

- Each profile has its own name, picture, followers and posts.
- Your login, password and security settings belong to the account, not to one profile.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Add your business name (2-80 characters) and a description of at most 300."**
- **"Pick between 1 and 5 categories."**
- **"Enter a valid website link."**
- **"You have reached the limit of business profiles."** You already have 10.
- **"You can't post as that profile."** You don't manage that profile.
- **"Couldn't load categories."** Try again in a moment.
