## Profile types

- **Personal**: you, as a person. Everyone starts here.
- **Creator**: not a separate profile. When you are accepted into the [Creator Program](/help/creators-and-earnings/creator-program) your personal profile becomes a creator profile. It unlocks Creator Studio, going live and earnings.
- **Business or brand**: any organisation, from a small shop to a charity. Choose one or more categories, or suggest your own (reviewed by our team). You can run up to several business profiles from one account.
- **Government**: for official public bodies. These go through deep vetting before they can carry the Black checkmark.

## Switching profiles

Tap your profile picture on your own profile and pick the profile you want to use. Posts, messages and likes come from the profile you have switched to.

## Create a business profile

From the profile switcher choose **Create business profile**, add a name and categories, and save. Selling is separate: see [Opening a store](/help/selling/start-selling).
