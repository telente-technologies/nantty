Stories are made to be seen, but you stay in control of who sees them through your profile settings.

## Who can see your stories

- **Public account**: anyone who can see your profile can watch your stories. People who follow you see them in the story tray at the top of their home feed.
- **Private account**: only followers you've approved can see your stories. See [Private accounts](/help/profiles-and-privacy/private-account).
- **Blocked people** can't see your stories, and you won't see theirs. See [Blocking](/help/profiles-and-privacy/blocking-people).

There is no separate audience setting for each story, and no close friends list. To keep stories to a smaller group, make your account private.

## See who viewed your story

1. Open your own story.
2. Tap the options button and choose **Story analytics**.
3. You'll see "Only you can see these numbers." and these totals: **Views**, **Unique viewers**, **Replies**, **Shares**, **Profile visits** and **New follows**.
4. Below that, **Viewed by** lists the people who watched, with when you posted it.

If nobody has watched yet: "No views yet. Share your story to get it seen."

Creators also see **How you earn**: "Story ads, gifts and subscriptions pay into your creator balance."

## When you watch someone's story

The author can see that you viewed it. This is different from posts and clips, where views are anonymous. See [About views](/help/posts-and-feed/views-and-analytics).

## How long stories last

Stories disappear 24 hours after you post them. They can't be reposted, quoted, pinned to your profile or downloaded by others.

## Removing a story early

There isn't a delete button for stories yet. If you need one taken down before the 24 hours are up, [contact support](/help/contact-support/contact-support).

## Report a story

On someone's story, open the options and tap **Report**. See [Report a problem](/help/safety-and-reporting/report-a-problem).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Only the author can see story viewers."** You can only see viewers of your own stories.
- **"This story isn't available."** It expired, was removed, or the account is private.
