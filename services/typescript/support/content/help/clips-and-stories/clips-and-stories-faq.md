## What's the difference between For You and Following?

**For You** picks clips from all of Nantty. **Following** shows only people you follow. See [For You and Following](/help/clips-and-stories/clips-for-you-and-following).

## How do I skip forward or back?

Double-tap the right side to skip ahead 10 seconds and the left side to go back 10 seconds. Keep tapping to skip more. See [Clip player controls](/help/clips-and-stories/clip-player-controls).

## Double-tapping didn't skip, it liked the clip. Why?

Double-tapping the middle likes a clip. Clips under 5 seconds, ads and locked subscriber clips can't be skipped, so a double-tap anywhere likes them.

## How do I jump to a part of a clip?

Drag the thin bar at the bottom of the clip. A bubble shows the time as you drag.

## Can I speed up a clip?

Press and hold to play at 2x. Or pause and tap the speed control to pick 0.5x, 1x, 1.5x or 2x.

## Where's the mute button?

Tap once to pause and you'll see **Mute** or **Unmute**. On the web, press M.

## How do I watch a wide video bigger?

Tap **View full screen** under it. See [Landscape videos](/help/clips-and-stories/landscape-videos).

## Why did the Follow button disappear?

Once you follow a creator, the button hides so it doesn't cover the clip. Unfollow from their profile.

## What is "friends liked this"?

People you know liked the clip. It shows for about 4 seconds, then fades.

## How long can a clip be?

Up to 3 minutes. Photo clips can have up to 10 photos. See [Making a clip](/help/clips-and-stories/create-a-clip).

## Can I see who viewed my story?

Yes. Open your story > options > **Story analytics** > **Viewed by**. See [Story viewers](/help/clips-and-stories/story-viewers-and-privacy).

## Can I see who viewed my clip?

No. Clip and post views are anonymous. You see totals in analytics.

## Can I hide my story from certain people?

There's no per-story audience. Make your account private, or block the person.

## Where do story replies go?

To your chat with the person, encrypted. See [Replying to stories](/help/clips-and-stories/story-replies-and-reactions).

## Can I post a story from the web?

No. Stories are created in the mobile app. You can watch them on the web.
