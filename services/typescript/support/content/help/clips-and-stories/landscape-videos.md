Clips are vertical, but some videos are filmed sideways (landscape). These play in the middle of the screen with bars above and below. To see them bigger, use full screen.

## Open full screen

1. On a landscape clip, tap **View full screen** just below the video. You can also pause the clip and tap **Full screen**.
2. The video turns sideways and fills the screen. Your phone's orientation switches automatically.
3. If it doesn't rotate, tap **Rotate** ("Rotate video to fill the screen").

## Full-screen controls

Tap the video to show or hide the controls. They hide by themselves after about 3 seconds while the video plays, and stay up while it's paused or while you're dragging the bar.

- **Top**: **Close video** (X) and the save button.
- **Middle**: **Skip back 10 seconds**, **Play** or **Pause**, **Skip ahead 10 seconds**.
- **Bottom**: the time (for example "1:05 / 3:00"), **Mute** or **Unmute**, **Exit full screen**, and a seek bar you can drag to any point.

## Leaving full screen

Tap **Exit full screen** or **Close video**. The clip carries on in the feed from where you stopped.

## Good to know

- The **View full screen** button only appears on landscape videos. Vertical clips already fill the screen.
- There's no speed control in full screen. Use the paused controls in the feed to change speed.

## If something goes wrong

If a video won't play or keeps buffering, check your connection and try again. See [Video won't play](/help/troubleshooting/video-wont-play).
