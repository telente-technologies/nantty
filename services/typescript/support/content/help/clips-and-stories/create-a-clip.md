Clips are made in the mobile app in four short steps: **New clip**, **Preview**, **Caption and sound**, and **Post settings**. Tap **Next** to move between them.

## 1. Choose your video or photos

Tap **+** and choose **CLIP** on the mode pill. Then pick one:

- **Record a video**: "Use your camera. Up to 3 minutes."
- **Choose a video**: "From your library. Up to 3 minutes."
- **Make a photo clip**: "Pick up to 10 photos to play as a slideshow."

A clip is either one video or a set of photos, not both.

## 2. Preview and trim

- **Video**: use the **Start** and **End** controls to trim. A clip must be at least 1 second long.
- **Photo clip**: each photo shows for 3 seconds by default. Change it to anything from 1 to 10 seconds per photo. Pick a transition (**No transition**, **Fade** or **Slide**; Fade is the default).
- **Looks**: add a colour look: **None**, **Warm**, **Cool**, **Rosé**, **Aqua** or **Sunset**.

## 3. Caption and sound

- Write in "Write a caption…". Type the at sign (@) to mention someone and # for hashtags.
- Tap the sound row to open **Add sound**: "Pick a sound for your clip". Browse **Trending** or **Saved**, or "Search sounds". The first option is **Original sound** ("Use the audio from your own video"), or **No sound** for photo clips.

## 4. Post settings

- **Who can reply**: **Everyone**, **People you follow**, **Followers only**, **Only people you mention** or **Off**. See [Who can reply](/help/posts-and-feed/reply-controls).
- **Subscribers only**: for approved creators with fan subscriptions.
- **Mark as sensitive**: pick at least one category.
- **AI generated**: turn on if AI made or heavily changed it. See [AI-generated content](/help/posts-and-feed/ai-generated-label).

Then post. If you leave partway you can save a draft ("Draft saved"). Drafts stay on this phone.

## Good to know

- Clips can be up to 3 minutes (180 seconds).
- Your original audio becomes a sound others can reuse with credit. You can turn reuse off. See [Sounds and copyright](/help/posts-and-feed/sounds-and-copyright).
- Clip authors can answer a comment with a video: in comments, tap **Reply with video**.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Add a video or photos to continue."**
- **"A clip uses one video or a set of photos, not both."**
- **"A photo clip can have up to 10 photos."**
- **"Clips can be up to 3 minutes long."** Trim it or pick a shorter video.
- **"Trimming isn’t available for this video. It will post at its full length."**
- **"A trim must end after it starts."**
