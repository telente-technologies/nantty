Replying to a story starts a private conversation about it. Your reply goes to chat, not under the story, so only you and the author see it.

## How to reply

1. Open someone's story.
2. Tap **Send a message...** at the bottom.
3. Type your reply and send it.
4. You'll see "Reply sent".

The reply appears in your chat with that person, together with a card showing the story you replied to. Like all chats, it is end-to-end encrypted. See [End-to-end encryption](/help/chat/end-to-end-encryption).

## Replies to your stories

When someone replies to your story, it arrives in your messages:

- From people you follow, it goes straight to **Chats**.
- From people you don't follow, it arrives in **Requests** (or **Spam** if the account behaves like a spammer), depending on your message request setting. See [Message requests and spam](/help/chat/chat-requests-and-spam).

The number of replies shows in your **Story analytics**.

## Reactions

Stories don't have quick emoji reactions. To react, send a short reply. Inside the chat you can react to the reply itself with an emoji.

## Good to know

- Once the story expires after 24 hours, the card in the chat shows "This is no longer available." Your messages stay.
- Stories can't be reposted or quoted.
- You can't reply to your own story.
- If the author limits who can send them message requests, you may not be able to reply.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Reply not sent. Try again."** Check your connection and try again.
- **"You can't reply to your own story."**
- **"Story not found."** The story expired or was removed.
- **"This account isn't accepting message requests from you."** The author only accepts messages from certain people.
- **"They haven't set up secure chat yet. Ask them to open Zudomart, then try again."** The author needs to open the app once so encrypted chat can start.
