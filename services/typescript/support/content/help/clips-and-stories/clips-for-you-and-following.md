The **Clips** tab has two feeds. Switch between them with the tabs at the top.

## For You

**For You** mixes clips from across Nantty, chosen from what's trending and what you watch, like and share. You don't need to follow anyone to use it. It learns from what you watch to the end, what you skip, and what you mark **Not interested**.

## Following

**Following** shows clips only from people you follow. If you're browsing as a guest, the web app asks you to sign in to see it.

If it's empty, you'll see "Nothing new from people you follow" and "Follow more creators to fill this feed." Tap **Watch For You** to go back.

## What's on each clip

From top to bottom on the left:

1. **Friends liked**: "2 friends liked this" when people you know liked the clip. It shows for about 4 seconds each time the clip starts, then fades away so it doesn't cover the video.
2. The creator's picture, name and username, with a **Follow** button.
3. An **AI generated** label, if the creator added one, and a tagged-people pill if people are tagged.
4. The caption.
5. The sound, under the caption. Tap it to see other clips using the sound.

On the right side: **Like**, **Comments**, **Share**, save, **Views**, **Tip** (on creator clips that aren't yours) and **More options** (three dots).

## Following a creator

The only button next to the creator's name is **Follow**. Tap it and the button disappears, so it never gets in the way of the clip. To unfollow later, go to their profile. Unfollowing always asks you to confirm. See [Unfollow and the follow bell](/help/profiles-and-privacy/unfollow-and-follow-bell).

You won't see a Follow button on your own clips or on creators you already follow.

## Views on clips

Tap **Views** on someone else's clip to see what views mean. Tap **Views** on your own clip to open its analytics. See [About views](/help/posts-and-feed/views-and-analytics).

## Tune what you see

Open **More options** and tap **Not interested** ("See fewer clips like this") or **Hide this clip**. You can also **Report** a clip or **Report copyright**.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Couldn't load clips."** Pull down to refresh or check your connection.
- **"No clips yet"**: "Check back soon for fresh clips." Tap **Refresh**.
- **"Sign in to see clips from people you follow."** Sign in to use Following.
