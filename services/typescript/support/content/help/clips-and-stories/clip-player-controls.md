Clips are controlled with simple gestures, like most short-video apps, plus a seek bar at the bottom like Facebook's.

## Gestures

| What you want | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Next clip | Swipe up |
| Previous clip | Swipe down |
| Pause or play | Tap once |
| Like | Double-tap the middle |
| Skip back 10 seconds | Double-tap the left side |
| Skip ahead 10 seconds | Double-tap the right side |
| Skip more | Keep tapping. Each extra tap adds 10 seconds, shown as -20s, +30s and so on |
| Play at 2x | Press and hold. Let go to go back to normal |
| More options | Tap the three-dot button beside the clip |

The screen is split in thirds: left third rewinds, right third skips ahead, middle third likes.

## The seek bar

A thin bar runs along the very bottom of each clip and fills as it plays. Drag anywhere on it to jump to a moment. While you drag, the bar gets thicker and a bubble shows the time, for example "0:42 / 1:30". The rest of the screen clears so you can see the video.

With a screen reader, swipe up or down on **Video progress** to skip 10 seconds.

## Paused controls

Tap once to pause. A large play button appears, along with:

- **Play** and the time left.
- **Playback speed**: tap to cycle 1x, 1.5x, 2x and 0.5x.
- **Mute** or **Unmute**.
- **Full screen**: for landscape clips, opens the full-screen player. See [Landscape videos](/help/clips-and-stories/landscape-videos).

## The tips card

The first time you open Clips, a **How clips work** card lists these gestures. Tap **Got it**, or tap anywhere, to close it. It won't show again on that device for your account.

## Good to know

- Skipping and the seek bar don't work on clips shorter than 5 seconds. On those, double-tap anywhere likes the clip.
- Ads, promoted lives and subscriber-only clips you haven't unlocked can't be skipped. Double-tap likes them instead.
- On the web (app.nantty.com) there's a **Mute clips** button, and you can press M to mute.
