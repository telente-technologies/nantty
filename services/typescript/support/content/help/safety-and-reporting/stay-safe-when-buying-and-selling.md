---
title: Staying safe when buying and selling
summary: Pay inside Zudomart with escrow, keep chats in the app and never share codes or PINs.
tags: scam, safety, fraud, escrow
related: how-escrow-protects-your-order, report-a-problem
---

- **Pay in the app.** Escrow only protects payments made through Zudomart checkout. Never pay a seller by direct bank transfer outside the app.
- **Keep chats on Zudomart** so we can help if something goes wrong.
- **Never share** passwords, one-time codes or card PINs. Zudomart staff will never ask for them.
- **Check the seller**: their reviews, success rate and checkmark.
- **Confirm delivery only when** you have the item and it matches the listing.
- **Report** anything suspicious from the menu.
