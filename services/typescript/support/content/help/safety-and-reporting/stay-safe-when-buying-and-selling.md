Most problems happen when a deal moves outside Zudomart. Keep the whole sale, from the first message to the payment, inside the app and you keep all of our protections.

## Who this is for

Buyers and sellers everywhere. Escrow is available in Nigeria only. Outside Nigeria, buyers pay the seller directly through checkout, so checking the seller matters even more.

## For buyers

1. **Pay through Zudomart checkout.** In Nigeria, escrow keeps your money locked until you confirm the order. It only protects payments made through checkout. See [How escrow protects your order](/help/buying-and-orders/how-escrow-protects-your-order).
2. **Never pay a seller by direct bank transfer**, gift card or crypto outside the app, even if they offer a discount.
3. **Check the seller**: their reviews, how long the store has been open and whether it has a checkmark. Unverified stores can only take escrow payments. See [Buying from unverified stores](/help/buying-and-orders/buying-from-unverified-stores).
4. **Confirm delivery only when** you have the item and it matches the listing. Once you confirm, the money goes to the seller.
5. **Open a dispute** before the order is released if something is wrong. See [Disputes and refunds](/help/buying-and-orders/disputes-and-refunds).

## For sellers

- **Ship only after the order shows as paid** in your orders. A screenshot of a "transfer receipt" is not proof of payment.
- **Never send money back** to a buyer who says they "paid too much". Refunds go through Zudomart.
- **Keep chats on Zudomart** so we can see what was agreed if there is a dispute.

## For everyone

- **Never share** your password, card PIN or any one-time code. Zudomart staff will never ask for them. See [What support never asks](/help/contact-support/what-support-never-asks).
- **Watch for pressure**: "pay now or lose it", "my account is blocked, pay my friend", or links to log in somewhere else are common tricks.
- **Use two-factor authentication** to protect your account. See [Two-factor authentication](/help/account-and-login/two-factor-authentication).

## If something goes wrong

- Report the account or listing from its menu. See [Report a scam](/help/safety-and-reporting/report-a-scam).
- If you shared a code or password, change your password straight away and see [Hacked account](/help/account-and-login/hacked-account).
- If you paid outside Zudomart, contact your bank at once and report the account to us. We can't reverse payments made outside the app.

## Stay safe

Zudomart will never ask for your password, card PIN or one-time codes, and will never ask you to pay into a personal account.
