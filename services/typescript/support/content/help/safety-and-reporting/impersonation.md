Pretending to be another person, brand, store or government body is not allowed on Zudomart. Parody and fan accounts are fine only when they clearly say they are not the real person.

## Who this is for

Anyone being impersonated, and anyone who spots a fake account.

## How to report an impersonating account

1. Open the fake profile and tap the three dots.
2. Choose **Report** (it shows their username).
3. Choose **Scam or fraud** if they are asking people for money, or **Something else** otherwise.
4. Then go to **Settings > Support > Contact support**, choose **Safety**, and tell us which account is fake and who it is copying. Add a link to the real profile and, if it's you, say so.

In a chat, open the chat menu, choose **Report user** and pick **Impersonation**.

## What proof helps

- **If it's you**: we may ask you to confirm your identity. Don't post your ID publicly; we'll ask through the support ticket.
- **If it's your brand or business**: proof such as a trademark registration or business documents.
- **If you act for someone else**: proof that you're authorised.

## What we do

We review the report. If the account is impersonating, we remove it or make it change its name and photo, and we may add a strike. Fake accounts used for scams are banned.

## Spot the real account

- Look for a checkmark. Blue is for verified people and creators, Gold for verified businesses and brands, and Black for government accounts. See [Checkmarks explained](/help/checkmarks-and-verification/checkmarks-explained).
- Check the exact username, the join date and the follower count.
- A real store takes payment only through Zudomart checkout.

## If your username was taken

Released usernames are held for 120 days before anyone else can use them. If a brand or public figure has a valid claim to a username, contact support. See [Usernames](/help/account-and-login/usernames).

## If someone took over your account

That's a hacked account, not impersonation. See [Hacked account](/help/account-and-login/hacked-account).
