## Will the person know I reported them?

No. Reports are confidential, and the person is never told who reported them. See [Reporting people and content](/help/safety-and-reporting/report-a-problem).

## How do I know what happened to my report?

Go to **Settings > Support > My reports**. Each report shows **Received**, **Reviewing**, **Action taken** or **No violation**. We also notify you when it closes.

## Can Zudomart read my chats when I report them?

Chats are end-to-end encrypted, so we can't read them. When you report a chat, your device sends only the messages you pick. See [Reporting chats](/help/chat/reporting-chats).

## I got a strike. What does it mean?

A strike means something you posted, streamed or sold broke our rules. It lasts 12 months and shows on **Settings > Account status** with the reason and expiry date. Three active strikes pause earning money and going live until one expires or an appeal succeeds. See [Account health and strikes](/help/safety-and-reporting/account-health-and-strikes).

## How do I appeal a strike?

Tap **Appeal** under the strike on your Account status page, explain why in at least 20 characters and tap **Send appeal**. A different reviewer decides. See [Appeal a decision](/help/safety-and-reporting/appeal-a-decision).

## How long can I appeal for?

Decisions can be appealed for 30 days, and each one only once.

## My account was suspended. What now?

Tap **Appeal this decision** on the suspension screen. Our team replies to your email within 3 working days. See [Account suspensions and appeals](/help/safety-and-reporting/suspensions-and-appeals).

## Will I lose my money if I'm suspended or banned?

Money that belongs to you is not forfeited. After any refunds you owe, it is paid to your verified account, unless the law requires it to be held.

## Do robots ban accounts?

No. Automated systems can flag content or pause a live for review, but a person always makes the final decision to suspend or ban.

## Someone used my song in their clip. What do I do?

Use **Report copyright** from the clip's menu. See [Send a copyright takedown request](/help/safety-and-reporting/copyright-takedown-request).

## A seller asked me to pay by bank transfer. Is that OK?

No. Only pay through Zudomart checkout. Paying outside the app removes escrow protection. See [Report a scam](/help/safety-and-reporting/report-a-scam).

## Someone is pretending to be me.

Report the account and contact support under **Safety**. See [Impersonation and fake accounts](/help/safety-and-reporting/impersonation).

## How do I stop someone contacting me?

Block them from their profile, limit message requests and reply settings, or make your account private. See [Harassment and bullying](/help/safety-and-reporting/harassment-and-bullying).

## I saw something that puts a child at risk.

Contact local emergency services if a child is in danger, then report it. See [Child safety on Zudomart](/help/safety-and-reporting/child-safety).
