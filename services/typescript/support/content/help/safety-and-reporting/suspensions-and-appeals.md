If we suspend your account, you'll see a screen that says **Your account is suspended** when you open the app. This article explains what that means and how to appeal.

## Who this is for

Anyone whose account has been suspended or banned.

## What a suspension means

- The screen shows the **Reason** and the date you were **Suspended on**.
- You can't post, message or sell while your account is suspended, and your profile and content are hidden from others.
- You can still read the Community Guidelines, appeal, download your data and log out.

If you try to sign in to a suspended or banned account, you may see **"This account has been suspended."** or **"This account has been banned."**

## How to appeal

1. Open the app and sign in. You'll land on the suspension screen.
2. Tap **Appeal this decision**.
3. This opens a support ticket for you. You'll see: "Appeal sent. Our team will reply to your email within 3 working days."
4. Watch your inbox for our reply. If we need more details, we will ask you there.

A person reviews every appeal. Automated systems can flag accounts and suggest a suspension, but they never decide a suspension or a ban on their own.

## What happens next

- **If your appeal succeeds**, your account is restored.
- **If the suspension stands**, your profile, posts, media and messages are deleted after 120 days. The screen shows the exact date. Payment and order records are kept in anonymised form for as long as the law requires.

## Good to know

- You can download your data from the suspension screen at any time before deletion. See [Download your data](/help/legal-and-data/download-your-data).
- Money that belongs to you is not forfeited. It is paid out to your verified account after any refunds you owe, unless the law requires it to be held.
- A ban also closes the account's stores, and new accounts aren't allowed.
- Smaller problems usually start with a warning or strike first. See [Account health and strikes](/help/safety-and-reporting/account-health-and-strikes).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Your account has been suspended for security reasons. You can appeal from the suspension notice."** We paused the account to protect it. Tap **Appeal this decision** or [contact support](/help/contact-support/contact-support).
- If the appeal button shows an error, check your connection and try again, or email support@nantty.com from the email on your account.
