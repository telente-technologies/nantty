## If your account is suspended

When you open the app you'll see the reason and the date. While suspended you can't post, message, follow or sell, and your profile and content are hidden from others.

## Appeal

Tap **Appeal this decision**. This opens a support ticket. Explain what happened and add anything that helps. A person reviews every appeal; automated systems can only suggest a suspension, never decide it.

## What happens next

- If your appeal succeeds, your account is restored.
- If the suspension stands, your profile, posts, media and messages are deleted after 120 days. Order and payment records are anonymised and kept for the legal retention period.

You can download your data at any time before deletion.
