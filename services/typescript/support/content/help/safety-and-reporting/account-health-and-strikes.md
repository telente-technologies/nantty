When something you post or sell breaks our rules, you may get a **warning** or a **strike**. Your **Account status** page shows every one of them, so you always know where you stand.

## Who this is for

Everyone, and especially creators and sellers, because strikes can pause earning and going live.

## How to check your account status

1. Go to **Settings > Account status**. On the web, open **Settings > Account status** on **app.nantty.com**.
2. The card at the top shows your standing and, for example, "1 of 2 active strikes".
3. Scroll down to see **Copyright notices**, **Active strikes** and **Warnings on record**.

Creators also see a summary in Studio, and sellers see one on the seller dashboard.

## What the standings mean

- **Good standing**: no active strikes and no takedown deadlines running.
- **At risk**: you have at least one active strike, or a copyright takedown deadline is running.
- **Restricted**: you have 3 active strikes of any kind, or 2 copyright strikes within 12 months (a repeat copyright infringement). The card lists what is **Paused on your account**: "Earning money (gifts, ads revenue share, subscriptions)" and "Going live". For copyright, posting and selling are suspended too, for at least 30 days. A third copyright strike sends your account for review.

## Warnings and strikes

Each item shows the policy (for example **Scams and fraud**, **Counterfeit goods**, **Spam** or **Harassment and hate**), the reason, when it was issued, when it expires and the content it relates to.

- **Warnings** are a heads-up. They don't count towards a restriction. A warning lasts 6 months, and a copyright warning lasts 12 months.
- **Strikes** count towards a restriction until they expire. A strike lasts 12 months, including copyright strikes. In rare cases our team sets a different expiry, so always check the date shown on each item.
- If we remove your content, warn you or suspend you after a report, that decision adds one strike. A warning given after a report is a strike and is listed under **Active strikes**.
- Expired or removed strikes stop counting straight away.

## Other restrictions

For some breaches our team can pause a single feature for a set number of days instead, such as posting, commenting, messaging, selling or going live. Serious or repeated breaches can lead to [suspension](/help/safety-and-reporting/suspensions-and-appeals).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Going live is paused on your account because of active strikes. See Settings > Account status."** You are restricted. Wait for a strike to expire, or appeal it.
- **"This strike has already been appealed, has expired or was removed."** You can only appeal an active strike once.
- **"Something went wrong."** with **Try again**: the page couldn't load. Check your connection and tap **Try again**.

## Good to know

- Tap **Appeal** under any strike or warning you think is wrong. See [Appeal a decision](/help/safety-and-reporting/appeal-a-decision).
- Copyright notices have their own rules and a counter-notice option. See [Copyright and account status](/help/safety-and-reporting/copyright-and-account-status).
- These limits can change. The Account status page always shows the current ones.
