You can ask us to remove or mute content that uses your work without permission. Our copyright team reviews every claim, and a person always decides.

## Who this is for

Rights owners and people authorised to act for them: musicians, labels, filmmakers, photographers, brands and their agents. You need a Zudomart account to claim from the app. If you don't have one, email copyright@zudomart.com.

## How to file a claim in the app

1. Open the post, clip, sound, live or product and tap the three dots.
2. Choose **Report copyright**.
3. Choose what your claim is about:
   - **The audio (music or sound)**: "If upheld, we mute the audio and keep the video up."
   - **The video itself**: "If upheld, the uploader is asked to remove it, and we take it down if they don't."
4. Fill in **Describe your copyrighted work**, for example the song title and release year, and add a **Link to the original** or upload it as your first attachment.
5. If it's a video or audio, add the start and end times where your work appears (for example 0:12 to 0:45).
6. Under **How do you own it?**, explain your ownership. Add **Proof links (one per line)**, such as a registration, distributor or release page, and **Proof documents** (certificates, contracts or screenshots). Only our copyright team can see them.
7. Enter your **Full legal name**, **Contact email**, and optionally **Phone** and **Company or label**.
8. Tick both declarations: that you believe in good faith the use isn't allowed, and that the information is accurate and you are the owner or authorised to act for them.
9. Type your full legal name as your **Electronic signature** and tap **Submit copyright claim**.

You'll see "Our copyright team will review your claim and contact you by email."

## What happens next

- **Audio claims**: the video stays up with its sound muted, and the sound can't be reused.
- **Video claims**: the uploader gets 48 hours to remove it. If they don't, we take it down and add a strike.
- **Lives**: acted on straight away.
- The uploader can send a counter-notice. If they do, we tell you, and unless you tell us within 10 business days that you've started court action, the content may be restored.

## Trademarks and counterfeits

For fake or counterfeit products using your brand, report the listing with **Counterfeit or fake product**, or email copyright@zudomart.com with proof of your trademark and links to the listings.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Your signature must match your full legal name"**: type your name exactly as in **Full legal name**.
- **"Enter a valid email address"**: check the contact email.
- **"You have filed a lot of claims. Try again in a little while."**: wait, then send the rest.
- **"Couldn't send your claim. Please try again."**: check your connection and try again.

## Good to know

Knowingly false claims can have legal consequences, and we may stop accepting claims from people who misuse the form.
