Keeping children safe comes before everything else on Nantty. Anything that sexualises, grooms or endangers a child is removed, the account is banned, and we report it to the authorities.

## Who this is for

Everyone, and especially parents, guardians, teachers and teens.

## If a child is in immediate danger

Contact your local emergency number or the police first. Then report it to us.

## How to report

- **A live**: open the live's report option and choose **Child safety**. It is marked **Urgent**, and "Urgent reports go straight to our safety team, who review them first."
- **A chat**: open the chat menu, choose **Report user** (or **Report a member** in a group), then **Child safety**. You'll see "Child safety reports are reviewed first by a specialist team." Pick the messages to include. Because chats are end-to-end encrypted, only the messages you choose leave your device.
- **A post, clip, comment or profile**: tap the three dots, choose **Report**, pick the closest reason (for example **Nudity or sexual content**) and write "child safety" and what you saw in the details box. You can also go to **Settings > Support > Contact support** and choose **Safety**.

Please don't download, screenshot or share the material yourself, even to report it. Report it where you saw it.

## What we do

- A specialist team reviews child safety reports before anything else.
- We remove the content, ban the account and do not allow new accounts.
- We report it to the relevant authorities and keep the evidence they need.
- Automated checks can blur or pause a live that shows severe harm until a person reviews it.

## How teens are protected

- You must be at least 13 to have an account, or older where your local law says so. Under-18s need a parent's or guardian's permission.
- Under-18 accounts never see nudity or dangerous content and are not shown personalised ads.
- Under-18s can't sell, receive payouts, join the Creator Program or run ads.
- Anyone can limit who can message them. See [Chat requests and spam](/help/chat/chat-requests-and-spam) and [Private account](/help/profiles-and-privacy/private-account).

## Tips for parents and teens

- Make the account private and limit message requests to people you follow.
- Never move a chat to another app because a stranger asks, and never send photos to someone you've only met online.
- Block and report anyone who asks for personal photos, gifts or secrets. See [Blocking people](/help/profiles-and-privacy/blocking-people).
