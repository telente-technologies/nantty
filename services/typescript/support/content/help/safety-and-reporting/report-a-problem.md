If something on Zudomart breaks our [Community guidelines](/help/safety-and-reporting/community-guidelines), report it. Reports go to our safety team, and the person you report is never told who reported them.

## Who this is for

Anyone with a Zudomart account. You need to be signed in to report; if you aren't, the app asks you to sign in and brings you back.

## How to report a post, clip, comment or product

1. Tap the three dots on the post, clip or product. For a comment, press and hold it and choose **Report comment**.
2. Choose **Report**.
3. Pick a reason: **Spam**, **Scam or fraud**, **Counterfeit or fake product**, **Nudity or sexual content**, **Hate or harassment**, **Violence**, **Copyright / intellectual property** or **Something else**.
4. Add details if you like (up to 500 characters), then tap **Submit report**.

You'll see "Thanks for letting us know".

## How to report an account

1. Open their profile and tap the three dots.
2. Choose **Report** (it shows their username).
3. Tap the reason that fits.

## Reporting lives and chats

- **Lives** have their own report sheet with urgent reasons such as **Child safety**, **Self-harm or suicide** and **Violence or dangerous acts**. See [Live safety](/help/live/live-safety).
- **Chats** are end-to-end encrypted, so you choose which messages to send us. See [Reporting chats](/help/chat/reporting-chats).

On the web (**app.zudomart.com**) the steps are the same: use the three-dot menu and choose **Report**.

## After you report

Track every report in **Settings > Support > My reports**. Each one shows a status:

- **Received**: we have it.
- **Reviewing**: someone is looking at it.
- **Action taken**: we found a breach and acted, with a short note.
- **No violation**: it didn't break our rules.

## Good to know

- Child safety, self-harm and violence reports are reviewed first.
- If several people report the same thing, it is reviewed sooner.
- To report your own stolen work, use **Report copyright** instead. See [Send a copyright takedown request](/help/safety-and-reporting/copyright-takedown-request).
- Don't want to see someone any more? You can also [block them](/help/profiles-and-privacy/blocking-people) or [mute them](/help/profiles-and-privacy/mute-accounts).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Couldn't send your report. Please try again."** Check your connection and try again.
- **"You have sent a lot of reports. Try again in a little while."** You've hit the reporting limit. Wait a while, then send the rest.
- **"Couldn't load your reports."** Pull down to refresh **My reports**, or tap **Try again**.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.
