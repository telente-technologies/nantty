## How to report

Open the menu (three dots) on the post, comment, clip, profile, product or live and choose **Report**. Pick a reason and add details if you like.

- Child safety reports are reviewed first.
- If you report self-harm, we also show you support resources.
- The person you report never sees who reported them.

## After you report

Track every report in **Settings > Support > My reports**: Received, Reviewing, Action taken or No violation, with a note about the outcome. We also notify you when a report is closed.

## Copyright and trademarks

Use the copyright or intellectual property option to report stolen work or counterfeit products.
