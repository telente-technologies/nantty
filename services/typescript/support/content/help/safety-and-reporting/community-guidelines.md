Our Community Guidelines apply to everything on Zudomart: posts, clips, stories, lives, comments, usernames, profiles, product listings, ads, boosts and chats. They apply to everyone, including sellers, creators, brands and Zudomart staff. The full version is on **zudomart.com/community-guidelines**. This is the short version.

## Who this is for

Everyone who uses Zudomart, in every country. Breaking the law where you live is always a breach too.

## Keep people safe

- **Children**: zero tolerance for anything that sexualises, grooms or endangers a child. We remove it, ban the account and report it to the authorities. See [Child safety](/help/safety-and-reporting/child-safety).
- **No threats or violence**, and no support for terrorist or violent extremist groups.
- **No harassment, bullying or doxxing** (sharing someone's private details), and no sharing intimate images without consent. See [Harassment and bullying](/help/safety-and-reporting/harassment-and-bullying).
- **No hate** that attacks people for their ethnicity, tribe, religion, nationality, sex, gender, disability or illness.
- **No promoting suicide, self-harm or eating disorders.** Recovery content is welcome.
- **Graphic or adult content** must be labelled. Under-18s never see it. See [Sensitive content and age](/help/posts-and-feed/sensitive-content-and-age).

## Be honest

- No scams, phishing, fake giveaways or "double your money" offers. See [Report a scam](/help/safety-and-reporting/report-a-scam).
- No pretending to be another person, brand, store or government body. See [Impersonation](/help/safety-and-reporting/impersonation).
- No fake reviews and no buying or selling followers, likes or views.
- Label AI-generated or edited media of real people and events. See [AI-generated label](/help/posts-and-feed/ai-generated-label).
- No asking people to pay or chat outside Zudomart to avoid escrow, fees or safety checks.

## Sell legally

No prohibited, counterfeit, stolen or unsafe items. Some items, such as food, cosmetics and electronics, have extra rules. See [Prohibited items](/help/selling/prohibited-items).

## Respect privacy and other people's work

Don't post other people's private information or their photos, music or videos without permission. See [Copyright and account status](/help/safety-and-reporting/copyright-and-account-status).

## What happens when rules are broken

We act in proportion to how serious the breach is and how often it happened:

1. **Content action**: the content is removed, age-restricted, covered with a warning or shown to fewer people.
2. **Warning**: recorded on your **Account status** page for 6 months. A warning given after a report also counts as a strike.
3. **Strike**: active for 12 months. Removing your content, warning you or suspending you after a report each adds one strike. 3 active strikes pause earning money and going live until a strike expires or an appeal succeeds.
4. **Feature restriction**: for example no posting, commenting, messaging, selling or going live for a set number of days.
5. **Suspension**, and for the most serious or repeated breaches, a **permanent ban**.

Serious harm, such as child abuse material, credible threats to life or serious fraud, can lead straight to a ban. Automated systems can flag or temporarily limit content, but a person always makes the final decision on banning an account. See [Account health and strikes](/help/safety-and-reporting/account-health-and-strikes).

## Good to know

- You can appeal decisions. See [Appeal a decision](/help/safety-and-reporting/appeal-a-decision).
- If you ever see someone in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first, then report it to us.
