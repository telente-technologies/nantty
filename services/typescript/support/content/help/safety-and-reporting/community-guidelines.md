The full Community Guidelines are on our website. In short:

- **Keep people safe**: no threats, violence, harassment or hate. Zero tolerance for anything that sexualises or endangers children.
- **Be honest**: no scams, fake reviews, impersonation, or misleading AI content.
- **Sell legally**: no prohibited, counterfeit or stolen items.
- **Respect privacy**: don't share other people's personal information.
- **Label sensitive content** and follow the age rules.

## What happens when rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is: the content is removed or limited, you get a warning, your account is restricted, or it's suspended or banned. You can appeal decisions.
