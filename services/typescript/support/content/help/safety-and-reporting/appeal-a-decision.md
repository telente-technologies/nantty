If you think we got it wrong, you can ask us to look again. A different person from the one who made the first decision reviews every appeal.

## Who this is for

Anyone who received a strike or warning, had a live paused, had their account suspended, or had content removed.

## Appeal a strike or warning

1. Go to **Settings > Account status**.
2. Under **Active strikes** or **Warnings on record**, find the item and tap **Appeal**.
3. In **Why should we remove it?**, explain what happened in at least 20 characters (up to 2,000).
4. Tap **Send appeal**.

You'll see **Appeal sent**: "A different reviewer will look at your appeal. We'll let you know the outcome by email and in your notifications." While you wait, the item shows **Appeal under review**. If you win, the strike is removed straight away.

## Appeal a copyright decision

Copyright takedowns use a counter-notice instead. On your Account status page, tap **Send a counter-notice** under the notice. See [Copyright and account status](/help/safety-and-reporting/copyright-and-account-status).

## Appeal a paused live

If our safety checks pause your live, you can tap the appeal option on the notice to open **Appeal this pause**. See [Live safety](/help/live/live-safety).

## Appeal an account suspension

Open the app and tap **Appeal this decision** on the suspension screen. Our team replies to your email within 3 working days. See [Account suspensions and appeals](/help/safety-and-reporting/suspensions-and-appeals).

## Appeal removed content or an ad

- For a removed post, clip, comment or listing, go to **Settings > Support > Contact support**, choose **Safety** and tell us which content it was and why it should come back.
- For a rejected ad, use **Appeal this decision** in Ads Manager. See [Ad review and policies](/help/ads-and-boosts/ad-review-and-policies).

## Good to know

- Decisions can be appealed for 30 days.
- You can appeal each decision once. Add everything that helps the first time: links, receipts, licences or context.
- A person always decides an appeal. Automated systems never ban anyone on their own.
- If your appeal succeeds, the action and any strike from it are removed.

## If something goes wrong

- **"You already appealed this decision."** Your first appeal is still being looked at or was decided.
- **"Decisions can be appealed for 30 days."** The window has closed.
- **"There is nothing to appeal on this decision."** The decision didn't remove anything or add a strike.
- **"Couldn't send. Try again."** Check your connection and send again.
