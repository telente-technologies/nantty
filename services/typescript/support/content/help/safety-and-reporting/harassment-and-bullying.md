Nobody should be bullied, threatened or targeted on Nantty. You have tools to control who can reach you, and our safety team acts on reports.

## Who this is for

Anyone getting unwanted comments, messages, mentions or attention, and anyone who wants to help someone else.

## What counts as harassment

- Threats, insults or repeated unwanted contact aimed at a person.
- Encouraging others to pile on someone.
- Sharing someone's private details (doxxing), such as their address or phone number.
- Sharing intimate images without consent. We remove these within 24 hours of being told.
- Attacking people for their ethnicity, tribe, religion, nationality, sex, gender, disability or illness.

## Take control

1. **Limit replies** on your posts: open your post's menu and choose **Edit reply settings**. See [Reply controls](/help/posts-and-feed/reply-controls).
2. **Control message requests**: choose whether everyone, people you follow, your followers or nobody can send you message requests. Unwanted chats land in **Requests** or **Spam**. See [Chat requests and spam](/help/chat/chat-requests-and-spam).
3. **Go private**: in **Settings > Privacy**, choose **Private** so only approved followers see your posts. See [Private account](/help/profiles-and-privacy/private-account).
4. **Hide content**: use **Not interested** or **Hide this post** from a post's menu.
5. **Block**: open their profile, tap the three dots and choose **Block**. "They won't be able to find your profile, see your posts or message you. They won't be told you blocked them." See [Blocking people](/help/profiles-and-privacy/blocking-people).
6. **On your lives**: hosts and moderators can mute or remove people from the chat. See [Live chat moderation](/help/live/live-chat-moderation).

## Report it

- **Comments**: press and hold the comment, then **Report comment**.
- **Profiles and posts**: three dots, **Report**, then **Hate or harassment**.
- **Chats**: chat menu, **Report user** or **Report and block**, then **Harassment or bullying**, and pick the messages to send.
- **Lives**: report option, then **Harassment or bullying**.

The person is never told who reported them. Follow your reports in **Settings > Support > My reports**.

## Good to know

- Keep evidence: note usernames and dates. In chats, you choose which messages to share with us.
- If you feel unsafe or someone threatens violence, contact local emergency services or the police.
- If you're struggling, talking to someone you trust or a local crisis line can help. You can find free helplines at findahelpline.com.
